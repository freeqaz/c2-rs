WHAT WAS REFUTED
  * The dispatched co-scheduling finding. The suite's ENTIRE exposure to the
    shared capture cache is one `c2rs gap --limit 1 --jobs 1` inside a test
    whose eight invocations total 0.461 s; pointing C2RS_GAP_CACHE at a fresh
    empty dir changes nothing measurable; a live gate beside the probe costs
    1.32x in wall AND in CPU-seconds. Co-scheduling is a confound, not a lever.
  * H4, mutant slot concurrency. Registered 5x with P(>=3x)=0.85; measured
    0.96x at 4 slots and 0.80x at 4 slots with a serial inner suite. A mutant
    is 88-92 % suite and 8-12 % rebuild, so slot-level and suite-level
    parallelism are ONE core budget. Rule: N x eff-par(inner) <~ free cores.
    NOT confounded: the by-name result set is identical between the N=1 and
    N=4 campaigns for all five items INCLUDING which tests are red.

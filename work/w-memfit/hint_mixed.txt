w-memfit — the TWO alignment hint bytes are per-operand IN THE IL
=================================================================
Four captures at size 96 differing only in the two pointee spellings, at the
workload's /O1.  All four .ex files are 2817 bytes and differ in THREE
positions.  `c2rs capture --keep-il`, work/w-memfit/probeH2.

   off     dd    cc    dc    cd
   2733    08    01    01    08
   2742    08    01    08    01
   2779    c1    f0    c1    f0

`dc` is (dst double, src char): 2733 reads 01 and 2742 reads 08.  `cd` is the
reverse.  So the two bytes are the SOURCE's hint and the DESTINATION's hint,
carried separately and UNCOMBINED, and offset 2779 is the type word that moves
with the destination.

GRID-G measures the effective divisor as the MIN of the two (G-MIN 56/56,
G-DST 38/56, G-SRC 38/56, G-MAX 20/56).  The IL does not carry that min, so
the combination happens inside c2.  wb-memcpy's `align = max(1, BYTE[node+0x38])`
names ONE byte; either c2 writes the min into that byte earlier than the
lowering, or the single-byte account is incomplete.  This lane does not read
the binary and does not choose between those; the OBSERVABLE is the min, at
56 of 56.

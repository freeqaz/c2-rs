WHAT IT BUILT, AND WHY IT HAD TO
A per-TU join gives Frechet bounds and nothing tighter, and it is wrong in a
known direction: objdiff attributes each linked function to one unit, so an
unfinished symbol emitted into fifty objs is charged to one of them — which is
every STLport template instantiation. So the lane built the missing side:
`symcensus.sh` compiles all 878 TUs with real c2 under wibo at the workload's
flags and `objsyms.py` reads each obj's .text COMDAT symbol names out of its
COFF symbol table (870 objs, 189,371 body instances, 90,897 distinct names),
and `namespace.py` intersects that name space with the decomp's per-symbol
verdict. All of it lives in `work/w-corpushealth/` and re-runs from the
committed scripts.

THE HONEST BOUND ON THE LANE'S OWN ANSWER
71.2% of the emitted-body population (134,791 of 189,371) carries a name that
is in NO objdiff unit — /Gy COMDATs the linker never selected anywhere.
`??6DebugFailer@@QAAXPBD@Z` is emitted into 732 of 870 objs and appears in the
shipped binary zero times. objdiff can never grade those, because it grades
what is in the target binary; decomp.db extends coverage by 1,217 names. So
2.95% is measured where the ruler reaches (28.8% of the population), the same
rate extrapolated to the whole is 10.24%, and BOTH are printed with the
ungraded denominator beside them rather than folded in — trap 0, #961, #1002.
Still a clean negative at either end.  (#3254)

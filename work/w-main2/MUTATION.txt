W-MAIN2 — the `_neg` fixture's MUST-FAIL MUTATION, run and reverted
==================================================================

Board #2698/#2699: a `_neg` cell is worth nothing until someone has watched the
clause it pins go RED, and for a MERGED clause the mutation has to delete the
WHOLE conjunction. `wmain2_eh_scope_neg.cpp` pins the tail conjunction of
`c2_il::func::ehscope::match_template`:

    m.lit(&[0x5E, 0x01, 0x21, 0x4B])?;      // the scope end, ONE sub-object
    m.lit(&[0x54, 0x02, 0x29])?;            // the exit label, immediately after

The two literals are adjacent, so relaxing only the first leaves the second
standing on the explicit `return 0;`'s own value bytes and the cell goes on
refusing for a reason the fixture is not about. The mutation deletes both:

    -    m.lit(&[0x5E, 0x01, 0x21, 0x4B])?;
    -    m.lit(&[0x54, 0x02, 0x29])?;
    +    m.p += body[m.p..].windows(3).position(|w| w == [0x54, 0x02, 0x29])?;
    +    m.lit(&[0x54, 0x02, 0x29])?;

Built, run against real `c2.dll` under wibo at `/nologo /c /GR /O1 /Oi /EHsc`,
and REVERTED (`git checkout -- crates/c2-il/src/func/ehscope.rs`).

    SHIPPED                              MUTATED
    [1/2] match     wmain2_eh_scope      [1/2] match     wmain2_eh_scope
    [2/2] vocab-gap wmain2_eh_scope_neg  [2/2] MISMATCH  wmain2_eh_scope_neg
                                         CORRECTNESS SIGNAL: 1 mismatching TU(s)

So the clause is live, the `_neg` cell reaches it, and the positive cell is
unaffected by the mutation — which is the second half of the demonstration and
the half that says the mutation did not simply break the recognizer.

WHAT THE WRONG OBJ WOULD BE, measured rather than asserted
----------------------------------------------------------
`work/w-main2/probe/n1.cpp` is the `_neg` source; its real obj is 1810 bytes and
its `.text`, both `.pdata` records and its 64-byte EH `.rdata` are byte-identical
to the positive class's. What differs is every one of the ten compiler labels:

    no `return`   seed 2551   __unwind$ +10   $M +15 +16   $T +17   $M +19 +20 …
    `return 0;`   seed 2552   __unwind$ +11   $M +16 +17   $T +18   $M +20 +21 …

An explicit `return` takes ONE more slot of the compiler-label counter. So the
mutated build emits ten symbol names one lower than c2's, in an obj that still
links — `docs/LABEL_COUNTER.md`'s own reason for existing, and the reason this
cell is a `_neg` and not a widening opportunity.

(`w-main` §5 measured the same fact as a COUNTER ADVANCE — `lab_dtor` +33
against `lab_main` +31, i.e. the no-`return` spelling is −2 — from four
counterfactual cells. This is the same fact read in `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §7.6's
in-the-middle form, where it is +1 per label rather than −2 per TU. The two
numbers are not the same quantity and neither is wrong.)

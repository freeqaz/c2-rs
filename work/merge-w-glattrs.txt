WHAT SHIPPED
------------

One match arm in `gl_function_attrs`, plus the constant it reads and four new
tests. The `.gl` function record's SIZE field escapes on `0x80` EXACTLY, with a
TWO-byte little-endian payload — three bytes total — so ATTR sits three past the
`0x80` and not one. SRCPOS's escape is five bytes because the two adjacent
fields are read by DIFFERENT readers: il-read-varint32 at 0x10c1f9e9 has a
four-byte payload where il-read-varint16 at 0x10c1f9a6 has two. The incumbent
stepped over both as if there were one width and refused the WHOLE FILE.

`0x81..=0xff` is a THIRD form — one sign-extended byte in c2 — and is STILL
refused. That was prereg decision D1, fixed before the count was taken, and the
count is ZERO witnesses in 1,461,374 workload records under both `.gl` framings.


WHY THE REFUSAL WAS CORRECT, AND WHY THE FIX IS NOT A LOOSENING
---------------------------------------------------------------

At the wrong width the attribute is an unrelated byte. The new test
`a_wrong_escape_width_reads_a_different_byte` puts it in the only terms that
matter: on a record whose ATTR is `noinline`, the width-1 misread hands back a
byte with bit 6 SET. A wrong width does not fail loudly — it gives `splice` a
PERMISSION on a body real c2 keeps a call to. The refusal was right while the
width was unknown. It is now known three ways.

(a) THE DISASSEMBLY, and it is the weakest of the three. il-read-varint16 at

FOUND AND NOT TAKEN
-------------------

1. gl_function_attrs ON #2783's RELAXED FRAMING — the biggest item, and this
   lane is what unblocks it. The reader currently sees 28,838 of 1,461,374
   records (2.0%), so every consumer of FN_FLAG_INLINABLE reads a 2% sample.
   Unpriced, and the price is real: a walk widening is a TIGHTENING of the
   fences that consume it (#2622/#2623 measured -1 fnbyte-exact). A lane taking
   it must also decide whether this reader follows GATE_BIND_FRAME's policy
   split or gets its own.

2. THE noinline_boundary.rs CELL GRID-D DESERVES. GRID-D cell B is a four-line
   source file that turns gl_function_attrs from answering to refusing on a TU
   whose small callee is noinline and whose call real c2 keeps. That file is
   w-witness7's seam and was NOT touched. It is the regression test for "a large
   function anywhere in the file blinds the port to every attribute in it", and
   it grades against the oracle rather than against a fixture.

3. THE ATTR VARU IS DOCUMENTED AND NOT DECODED (#3293). ATTR is a two-or-four
   byte value with a continuation flag in bit 15, not a byte.
   __declspec(noinline) takes it from 0x1068 to 0x801028 — crossing 0x8000 — so
   the record grows by two bytes, and THAT IS THE MECHANISM BEHIND w-target's
   long-unexplained nicmp2 observation "only .gl moves, and by 2 bytes". Reading
   the low byte is correct for bit 6 under both widths and nothing needs the
   value, so nothing was decoded. Open: the rest of the low byte. Vocabulary is
   28 48 4c 68 69 6c 88 c8 cc e8; 0x40 is noinline, 0x20 tracks static
   (0x68 -> 0x48), and 0x80 / 0x08 / 0x04 / 0x01 are undecoded. `c8` is 93% of
   all records and `68` is 85% of the ESCAPED ones — a distributional difference
   nobody has explained, and the place to start.

4. THE ESCAPED POPULATION IS ONE-ARMED, so a future inline-decision lane gets
   ZERO discriminating cells from it at either profile. A bound on a whole class
   of follow-ons, cheap to state and expensive to rediscover.

5. THE TWIN-GRID TRAP, which cost this lane a full grid run. `.gl` carries the
   source path and per-record source positions, so ANY .gl diff experiment must
   hold the source LENGTH and the PATH fixed or it is measuring the container's
   provenance fields. The first GRID-A run differed in 130 bytes and the one
   byte the experiment was about was invisible in the noise. Fix: pad the plain
   twin with 21 spaces, write both to one path.

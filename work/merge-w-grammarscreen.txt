FOUR FINDINGS, IN THE ORDER A READER SHOULD TAKE THEM
-----------------------------------------------------
1  A THIRD OF ANY SCREEN'S "REACHED" BUCKET IS EVALUATED AND NOT REFUSED.
   `.ok_or(X)` runs X on every pass, refusing or not, and a trailing `?` does
   NOT change that — the classifier must test the eager forms FIRST or it reads
   177 sites as diverging that are not.  186 of the 507 are eager, so a screen
   that does not separate them overstates its actionable half by 58 %.
   THIS READS BACK ONTO A LANDED ROW: #3276 prices all seven of w-deadsites'
   UNGUARDED sites as "a witness each, and cheap: the probe proves an input
   exists", which needs the site to diverge.  Six of the seven do.  CS4
   (census.rs's `bind_key.unwrap_or(STORE_RUN_BIND_NO_CARRIER)`) is EAGER, and
   that lane's own table records it firing "with `bind_key` Some" — i.e. with
   the default arm NOT taken.
   ROUTED, NOT DANGLING: flagged and not corrected here (#3117), and the
   coordinator has forwarded it to peer w-witness7 — mid-lane writing witnesses
   for exactly those seven sites — with the instruction to distinguish "the
   refusal fires" from "the expression is evaluated" before manufacturing a
   witness for any of them.  The correction happens in the lane that owns the
   consequence.  Settling CS4 is one probe placement inside a lane already there.

2  THE PARTITION DEGENERATES, STRUCTURALLY AND NOT BUDGETARILY.  w-deadsites
   split 26 rows by READING each quiet site.  That does not scale to 829, and
   the one unreachability proof that does — rustc's `dead_code` lint — runs on
   every build of this repo and is GREEN.  1 of 191 enclosing functions has no
   non-test caller and that one IS a `#[test]`.  A declared reading of the 11
   quiet sites sharing a ctx with a reached one (the exact shape L9/CA9/CA10
   came from) found 0 proofs.  So DEAD is not small; it is EMPTY.
   0 DEAD, NOTHING DELETED, NOTHING PROPOSED FOR DELETION, 829 UNKNOWN rows
   published untouched.  Returning zero here is the correct outcome and is what
   the "quiet is not dead" constraint was for.

3  QUIET IS A SPARSE TRAVERSAL OF EVERY PARSER, NOT AN UNVISITED ONE.  0 of 30
   files have zero reached sites; 26 of 30 are walked to within 15 % of their
   gate depth (28 of 30 to >= 83 %) while 17-47 % of their gates are ever
   touched.  float_walk_loop.rs: 24 of 89 reached, DEEPEST reached site the
   89th.  The story that would license deleting quiet sites is measured false
   file by file.

4  THE SCREEN COSTS THREE SOURCE LINES AND FOUR CORPUS RUNS, AND WHAT IT BUYS
   IS A PRICE.  `blk`, `blk_type` and `Block::refuse` marked `#[track_caller]`:
   one `&'static Location` per call site, so its ADDRESS is the site key and
   there is NO per-site edit.
   STATED PLAINLY: three #[track_caller] lines, no per-site edit, four corpus
   runs — against F1's costed ~20 runs and the original five-day estimate.
   Reach is a NECESSARY condition for a mutation to be killable, so the campaign
   w-mutcensus declined is now 507 runs (~34 h serial, ~2 h across 16

Four things fall out, none of which is a section-layout model:

* .text is 100% COMDAT on 7 of 7 objs — 117 sections, 0 packed. #232's MIXED
  obj (one COMDAT .text beside one packed .text) is a /Ox shape and does not
  occur at the workload's flags at all, so "COMDAT-style linkage against a
  packed single-.text writer" is true of the reproducer and false of the 380.

* the aux Selection byte, not the section kind, is what the writer has wrong:
  emit_comdat_obj hard-codes COMDAT_SELECT_NODUPLICATES (1) and c2 emits
  SELECT_ANY (2) on 99 of the 117. The .gl record's FLAGS byte at name_nul+5 —
  the byte record_is_plain_external already reads as FLAGS_PLAIN — predicts it
  117 for 117: flags & 0x20 gives ANY, flags == 0x00 gives NODUPLICATES.

* the 26 separator does NOT predict it. 80 of the 117 emitted records carry
  Selection=ANY and are NOT 26-introduced. The clause the commission was sent
  to price refuses on a byte that is not the one the layout depends on.

* EMITTED-but-NOT-NAMED is 0 on 7 of 7 (clause 3 discharged everywhere) and
  NAMED-but-NOT-EMITTED is nonzero on 6 of 7, up to 114 on urlencode. That is
  clause 4, that is factor A, and nothing in .gl discharges it.

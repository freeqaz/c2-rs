* gl.rs, the Name26Introduced clause.  Its opening sentence — "26 marks

  MIXED on 0 of 7; the aux Selection byte wrong on 99 of 117; and the .gl FLAGS

* bind.rs, Bindings::per_record.  selective states the over-emit obligation and
  refuses on it at clause 4.  per_record states nothing and has no such clause:
  it rests on the unstated premise that a record set covering every segment IS
  c2's emit set.  Measured over the 29 TUs gl_body_start_coverage reports n of
  n — CEILING §12's "full coverage of this acceptance path" — exactly one fails
  factor A, and HeadsetXferEffect.cpp would emit two functions absent from c2's
  obj entirely.  So 29 is not a sound bound on that path; 28 is.

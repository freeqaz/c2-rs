WHY THE SIZE VARIANT WAS NOT SHIPPED
------------------------------------
Two reasons, first decisive:

  (a) The prereg's frozen decision rule ENUMERATED the admissible container
      facts -- "emit binding, cflow_key, .gl attribute byte, the modelled set".
      Segment length is not among them. It was frozen at 682ae2a5 before any
      probe precisely so a fact discovered mid-lane could not be admitted after
      seeing that it flatters the number. Admitting it now is the goalpost move
      the freeze exists to prevent, and it is the lane's own registered bias #2
      verbatim ("keeping a small residual term to make the price come out flat,
      and calling the result a rule").

  (b) INLINE_DECLINE_BYTES (128) and INLINE_DECLINE_LOOP_BYTES (80) are tops of
      MEASURED obj-grid brackets in EMITTED bytes. This one is in IL bytes from
      sweeping the grader. It would be the first fitted constant in the tree
      wearing the same clothes as the measured ones -- on a corpus that moved 61
      commits DURING this lane.

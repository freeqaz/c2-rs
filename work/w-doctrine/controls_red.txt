ADDENDUM — TWO OF THE FOUR GUARD CLAIMS WERE FALSE, AND THE CONTROL
SET IS WHAT FOUND THEM.

`Surface::guards` is a **claim**: *this surface's domain exercises this
boundary constant*. `surface.rs` says at the field that "an entry here
that the domain does not reach is a false coverage claim and the control
set is what keeps it honest". Run against the four surfaces' seven
guards, the control set found **two** entries that were exactly that:

  POOL_TOP              re-spelled as a literal `9`      -> 0 lines moved
  FRAME_MIN_OUT_SLOTS   widened 8 -> 16                  -> 0 lines moved

Neither was a slip of the pen. They are the two failure modes a coverage
claim has, and they need different repairs:

  * **POOL_TOP is not an independent boundary at all.** Since `w-regsel`
    it is `GPR_DEFAULT.regs[0]` — a derived alias with no production use
    outside tests and comments. There is nothing for a domain to reach.
    Moved to `UNCOVERED` with the measurement as its reason; the thing it
    aliases, the order's head, is covered by `regalloc.select`. Ratchet
    12 -> 13.
  * **FRAME_MIN_OUT_SLOTS was a real boundary the DOMAIN was too narrow
    to see.** At `locals = 20_000` the floor moves the frame by 64 bytes
    and lands on the same side of every threshold, so raising it moved
    nothing. Repaired by choosing `locals = 20_360`, which straddles the
    `_RtlCheckStack12` threshold *through* the floor: at 8 the frame is
    20,448 and admitted, at 16 it is 20,512 and refused.

CONTROL CM (after the repair): the widening now moves **7 lines**, and 28
of 671 crate tests go red as well — this one was never a silent boundary,
which is precisely why it needed measuring rather than assuming.
CONTROL CS-2 (after the repair): FRAME_MAX_SAVED_NO_SPILL 17 -> 31 is
still `670 passed; 1 failed`, still E1 alone. The repair did not cost the
sharp control.

**This is the lane's own instrument catching the lane's own false claim,
and it is the reason a `guards` list has to be executed rather than
written.** A registry that reports coverage it does not have is #3470
with an extra step: the number goes UP and the grading does not.

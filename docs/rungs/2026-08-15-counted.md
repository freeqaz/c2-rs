## 3. The charge — `+7`/`+8` is `charge + 5`, and the real pair is 2 and 3

### 3.1 The instrument the published number used is the one #3148 refuted

`work/w-bdnz/LABEL_LEAD.md` measures the lead as *"the control puts a
`leaf-none` in the first slot, the test puts the cell there"* — the framed `$M`
of one TU minus the framed `$M` of **a different TU**. A TU's `.gl` label
counter depends on its own source text, so that difference is
`Δcharge + Δseed`. **Board #3148 refuted precisely this instrument on
`float_walk_loop` one day earlier**, and the seed-cancelling form
(`work/w-slots/lead.py`, each TU's own counter subtracted **inside** it) has
been generic since.

### 3.2 Re-measured, with the series and the zero-controls

`work/w-counted/lead/`, frozen by sha256; `work/w-counted/leads_modes.txt`. Each
cell is `[N counted-accumulate loops (all leaves), the SAME framed z9]`.

```text
                        /O1     /O2     /Ox     /Od
  lead_ctl              +0      +0      -6      -8     leaf-none  ZERO CONTROL
  lead_straight         +0      +0      -6      -8     two locals ZERO CONTROL
  lead_ctr1             +2      +3      -3      -8
  lead_ctr1u            +2      +3      -3      -8     unsigned counter
  lead_while            +2      +3      -3      -8     the `while` spelling
  lead_ctr2             +4      +6      -3     -11
  lead_ctr3             +6      +9      -3     -14
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CHARGE                 2       3       3       0
```

* **`/O1` and `/O2` close with residual 0** — `2n` and `3n` over one, two and
  three loops — and **both zero-controls read exactly 0**, which is what
  validates the base formula rather than assuming it. This is `w-slots`' `2n+1`
  lesson applied rather than quoted: the series is what separates a per-function
  charge from a per-TU slot, and here there is no per-TU slot.
* **At `/Ox` the absolute form is VOID and is marked so rather than quoted.**
  The zero-control reads **−6**: `/Ox` packs the functions into one `.text`
  instead of COMDATs, which is `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §7.6 step 3's *"base must be 5
  under `/Gy`, 4 packed; if it is not, the row is void."* The `/Ox` charge is
  therefore read only at **constant segment count** — `lead_ctl` against
  `lead_ctr1`, both two segments, both one leaf plus the same framed `z9`, so
  the layout term cancels along with the seed.
* **`lead_while` reads the same integer as `lead_ctr1` at all four modes.** That
  reproduces `w-bdnz` reading 3 seed-cancelled: the `for`/`while` confound that
  licenses three of the four other `None`s is **absent** for this class.

`+7 = 2 + 5` and `+8 = 3 + 5`: this lane's own cells differ by **6** in their
`.gl` counters and `w-bdnz`'s by 5. **The direction of the finding was right and
the magnitudes were both wrong** — which is the fourth published label number in
two weeks to be wrong in the direction that makes a fence look dearer than it is
(#3091, #3148, `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §4.2.1's `for` row, and this).

`#3062`'s standing counter reads `sole` 0 / `exact` 0 across all 23 causes, so a

* **`NARROW` is the proposal on its own**: reader gate `O1|Ox` → `O1`, nothing
  else. **−8**, and it buys nothing at all, because `label_slots` still returns
  `None`.
* **`NARROW+2` is the proposal at its best**: narrowed *and* the `/O1` charge of
  2 installed, which is the whole point of narrowing. **+1 on each of six `/O1`
  lanes and −1 on each of eight `Ox`-word lanes: −2 net.**
* **The withdrawn fixture is named, not inferred**: `wbdnz_ctr.cpp` goes
  `match → vocab-gap` at `/Ox` and `/O2` under the narrowed reader
  (`work/w-counted/narrow_named.txt`), while staying `match` at `/O1`.

**`NARROW` is also this decline's positive control.** A decline probe that cannot
detect a live arm cannot license a null (`w-slots` §4). This one moves 8
verdicts, so the null it would have reported — *"narrowing is free"* — is
excluded by measurement rather than by argument. Had it moved nothing, the
finding would have been that `/Ox` acceptance is unwitnessed on the tracked
corpus, which is the absence-read-as-success family, and it would have been
stated in those words.

**And a wrong emit is not what is being traded away here.** The narrowing does
not prevent a `mismatch`; every one of the 8 verdicts it costs is a `match`
today. That is the case where "a refusal that refuses more is never the
dangerous move" **does not apply**: the safe direction is safe against wrong
emits, and there are none on this arm to be safe from.

### 4.1 `/O2` is the eighth lane, and it was never named

`docs/OPT_MODE.md` §3: `/O2`'s optimization word is `00a00005`, which is
`OPT_WORD_OX` byte for byte. So `Some(OptWordMode::Ox)` in the reader admits
**eight** of the 18 gate lanes — six `/Ox`-family and two `/O2` — and every
statement anyone has made about this arm's "two modes" is a statement about
three lane families. The charge measures **3** at `/O2` as at `/Ox`, and the
two-pole probe reddens and greens them together, so the reading is consistent;
but a lane that had narrowed on a −6 estimate would have been two verdicts
short before it started. The prereg registered six and the measurement said
eight — **a miss, and it moved the price the wrong way for the proposal.**

### 5.2 The correction, recorded against the instruction that was wrong

> *"You were right and my instruction was wrong. … **A refusal becoming narrower

## 8. Found and not taken

Ranked, with the frame axis applied.

0. **`w-labeltable` and this lane agree on 2 and 3 from three constructions, so
   the number is no longer the open question — the METHOD is.** Two independent
   seed-cancelled measurements and one end-to-end oracle grading agree. What
   nobody has is a way to *use* a per-mode charge, and that is item 1.

1. **A charge this class can ship needs `label_lead` to know the optimization
   word, and `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §7.6 forbids exactly that** — *"`label_slots`
   needs NEITHER a mode parameter NOR a sub-shape parameter … a class may ship a
   measured constant obtained by steps 1–5, and nothing else."* §3.3 is the first
   evidence in this repo that the prohibition **costs something specific**: the
   two constants are measured, each is byte-exact at its own mode, and the only
   thing between them and a conversion is that no method may be told which mode
   it is in. That is a `w-labeltable`-shaped question and it is **not** the
   unsolvable one: the missing term for the *general* loop rule is the loop KIND
   (**#3127**, `R1′` 5 of 15), which no IL field carries — the missing term
   **here** is the optimization word, which the reader already reads
   (`opt_word_at`). Whoever revisits §7.6 owes: is the prohibition about modes in
   general, or about *unmeasured* modes? This class has both measured.

2. **`CountedAccumLoop` already carries a mode-derived field, which is the
   precedent §7.6 was not written against.** `counter_unsigned` exists because
   *"the answer is carried to the emitter rather than re-derived there"*. A
   `mode` field would be the same move, and it would not give `label_lead` a
   parameter — it would give it a **fact the recognizer already established**.
   **Deliberately not taken here**: the distinction between "a method with a mode
   parameter" and "a method reading a mode the shape recorded" is exactly the
   kind of re-spelling that looks like a loophole, and it belongs to the lane
   that owns §7.6, argued in the open, not to a lane that would benefit from it.

3. **`/O2` has been an unnamed member of every `/Ox` mode claim in this file
   tree.** This lane found it on one arm. `ptr_walk_loop`, `float_walk_loop`,
   `static_scan_loop` and `xtea_round_loop` all state mode facts as `/O1`
   against `/Ox`; each of those statements silently covers `/O2` as well, and
   at least one of them (`float_walk_loop`'s `+6` at `/O2` against `+0` at
   `/Ox`, `w-slots` §2.5) records a `/O2` number that **differs from its `/Ox`
   one while sharing its optimization word**. That is either a layout effect or
   a live inconsistency, it is one command to settle with the seed-cancelling
   lead, and **it is not settled here** because it is a peer's class.

4. **The `/Ox` absolute lead formula is void and nobody has written the packed
   one.** `lead.py`'s `base = counter + 9 + 3·segs + leaves` closes with residual
   0 at `/O1` and `/O2` and reads −6 on a zero-control at `/Ox`.
   `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §7.6 step 3 already knows why (*"5 under `/Gy`, 4 packed"*)
   but no instrument implements the packed variant, so **every `/Ox` charge in
   this repo is either a constant-shape difference or unsound**. Cheap to fix,
   and it would make §3.2's `/Ox` row absolute instead of relative.

5. **A check that cannot see the thing it exists to check — and this wave found
   two of them, in different layers.** `narrow_probe.sh` reports `match` and
   `mismatch` per lane and the mismatch column stayed 0 throughout, but it was
   designed for a change that could only *lose* matches; a lane pricing a fence
   in the other direction needs the per-fixture verdict diff. **`mode_lane.sh`
   does not emit per-fixture lines**: the `[n/N]` rows `w-slots`'
   `decline_probe.sh` greps for are not in its log, that grep produced **empty
   files** here, and the LANE-RESULT counts had to carry the whole result. So
   `w-slots`' probe has the same silent hole and neither lane noticed.

   **`#3156` is the same disease one layer out**, and the coordinator's
   cross-reference is right: none of the three funnel checks could see a tracked
   build artifact — `gate.sh` content-hashes only `crates fixtures scripts`,
   `board_audit.sh` audits the board, `rung_registry` reads header blocks — and
   the `git add -f` that lands a lane's legitimate `work/` evidence **silences
   the `*.obj` rule in the same stroke**. *(This lane used `-f` for 54 `work/`
   files and checked rather than assumed: **0** matching `*.obj`, `*.o`, `*.il`,
   `*.exe`, `*.dll`, `*.lib` or `_CL_*` in its whole diff against master.)*

   **A per-fixture-silent probe and an artifact-blind funnel are one family**: in
   both, the instrument's *output shape* excludes the failure it is supposed to
   catch, so it reports green in exactly the case that matters and no amount of
   running it more often helps. Worth one line in `mode_lane.sh`; the funnel half
   is `#3156`'s and is already filed.

6. **The six previously ungraded cells were green, and that is a datum about
   fixture design, not about this class.** A fixture that crosses one axis fully
   and another axis once reads, to every later lane, as though it crossed both —
   `w-bdnz`'s own header lists "the axes this file crosses" and both axes are on
   the list. Nothing in the tree measures *coverage of a recognizer's free
   fields*, and the recognizers state their fields in prose. That is a candidate
   instrument and it is bigger than one lane.

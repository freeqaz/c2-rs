## 2. THE HEADLINE — `expr-cmp-eq` IS A FALL-THROUGH KEY

`work/w-dclass/rerank.py`, re-run at this lane's start against its own committed
scan, reproduces the brief: `expr-cmp-eq` **converts 3** TUs where every other
frontier key converts 0, 1 or 2, and the three are `IPP_basicmath_xbox.cpp`,
`vswprnc.cpp`, `mmio.cpp` at prices 9 / 6 / 5. That arithmetic is correct and
this lane does not dispute it.

**What "closing a key" means is where it breaks.** A blocker key is the census
label on the **FIRST** refusal in a body. My brief demanded — correctly — that
any rung sized off a blocker-key census owe an argument for why it is not the
`expr-op-0x27` case. **The argument is a measurement, and it goes the other
way.**

### 2.1 The eight sites, and what they actually are

`c2rs census` over the three TUs, at the workload's own flags:

```
mmio.cpp                 8/11 in class   3 x expr-cmp-eq   cflow-if-2, cflow-if-n x2
vswprnc.cpp              0/1  in class   1 x expr-cmp-eq   cflow-if-n
IPP_basicmath_xbox.cpp   0/4  in class   4 x expr-cmp-eq   cflow-loop x4
```

All eight share one prefix, and the byte after the blocking byte is the finding:

```
  b9 <tok> <TYPE>       LOAD
  33 <TYPE> 00          LITERAL 0, same type
  1f                    ==     <-- the census stops HERE and says `expr-cmp-eq`
  38 <L> | 39 <L>       brfalse | brtrue
```

**The `==` is a branch condition, not a value.** `vswprnc`'s single blocked
function is **429 bytes** of IL and the parse stops at byte **22** — three `if`s
in a `||` chain, two calls, a loop, stores and a `return -1` are all downstream
and none of them is measured. `IPP_basicmath_xbox`'s four are
`if (n == 0) return;` followed by a **loop** over a float array.

`C2RS_SINK_REL=expr` consumes `0x1F..=0x24` so the census reports the

`IlOp` (a relational is not an `Add`, and a sink that lowered it as one would be
a wrong emit), and a walk that reaches the end with one in it refuses under
`expr-rel-sink-poison`. **Decoding is not accepting.**

## 3. WHY THE LADDER OVER-COUNTED — closing a key SUBSTITUTES a successor

`rerank.py` fixed a real defect and then assumed, in one line:

```python
def convertible(rows, closed):
    return [s for s, b in rows.items() if set(b) <= closed]
```

**that closing a key REMOVES it.** It does not. It **substitutes a successor**,
and the successor may already be a frontier key — in which case the TU does not
convert and the ladder's credit for it was never real.

`work/w-cmp/substitute.py` takes the two scans and prints it:

```
  closed mass 3298 · absorbed 3298 · net converted 0
  ladder-credited 5 TU · measured 0 TU
```

**Nine of the nineteen frontier TUs substituted**, by name:

| TU | lost | got |
|---|---|---|
| `osfinfo.cpp` | `expr-cmp-ge` | `expr-brfalse` |
| `undname.cpp` | `expr-cmp-ne` | `expr-brfalse` |
| `vswprnc.cpp` | `expr-cmp-eq` | `expr-brtrue` |
| `vsnprnc.cpp` | `expr-cmp-eq` | `expr-brtrue` |
| `Biquad.cpp` · `wordwrap.cpp` · `mmio.cpp` · `IPP_basicmath_xbox.cpp` | `expr-cmp-eq` | `expr-brfalse` |
| `xboxmem.cpp` | `expr-cmp-eq`, `expr-cmp-ne` | `expr-brfalse`, `expr-shl` |

The instrument is **deliberately not a predictor**: a successor key has no name
before its sink exists. The workflow it encodes is **sink first, re-rank second,
build third**, and it exits non-zero on a sink that moved nothing.

## 4. THE COUNTEREXAMPLE — the six relations are NOT one family in the guard position

`C2RS_SINK_REL` matches `0x1F..=0x24` in **one arm**. That is fine for a census
sink, which cannot emit. It is a **wrong emit** for anyone who reads this rung
and generalises, and my brief warned that the `!=`→`>` trap shape would be
waiting on the comparison seam. **It is.**

`work/w-cmp/gt_guard_rel.py`: 6 relations × {signed, unsigned} × k ∈ {0, 1, 2},
one TU, the **workload's** flags, **36 of 36 probes compiled**, graded against
real c2.

| unsigned | k = 0 | k = 1 | k = 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `<`  | **4 BYTES — `b sink`**, folded always-false | 16 B, LT | 16 B, LT |
| `>=` | **4 BYTES — bare `blr`, NO RELOCATION AT ALL** | 16 B, GE | 16 B, GE |
| `<=` | 16 B, **EQ** condition (`bclr 12,26`) — not LE | 16 B, LE | 16 B, LE |
| `>`  | 16 B, **NE** condition (`bclr 4,26`) — not GT | 16 B, GT | 16 B, GT |
| `==` · `!=` | 16 B, own condition | same | same |

**Signed folds at no literal**: all six keep their own condition at k = 0, 1, 2.

> ### The rewrite is a **three-way interaction of (relation, signedness, literal)** and it fires at **exactly** `k = 0` with both neighbours behaving normally. That is the 63-burner shape and w-dclass's 32768 bound, **third recorded instance**. A lowering of the form `cmp{l}wi cr6,r3,k ; b<cond>lr cr6 ; b callee ; blr` with the condition a table lookup on the relation emits **four wrong functions of the wrong SIZE**, one of them carrying a relocation real c2 does not emit — which shifts every COMDAT after it.

The reloc claim is checked, not asserted: `?g_u_ge_0@@YAXI@Z` is **4 bytes**,
`4e800020 blr`, and its `.text` carries **no relocation record** — the call to
`sink()` is gone. `?g_u_lt_0@@YAXI@Z` is 4 bytes, `48000000`, with the REL24 to
`?sink@@YAXXZ`.

**All 8 frontier sites this lane examined compare against 0**, and two of the
three TUs compare **unsigned** against 0. They are in the safe cell only because
their relation is `==`. **Prereg R7 is a HIT.**

This also corroborates, one position over, the tree's own committed measurement:
`CompareLeaf::label_slots` records the compiler-label stride as **1 for `==`/`!=`
and for every unsigned operand, 3 for signed `>`/`<=` and for signed `<`/`>=`
with a non-zero literal** — three groups in the *value* position, over a 60-point
grid. Two independent positions now say the six relations are not one family.

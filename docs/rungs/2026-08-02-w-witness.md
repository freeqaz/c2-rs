Board **#159** was minted by ROADMAP §10.13 with *"nobody has read what an
`ordinary` no-record symbol is"* as step one. §10.14 is the record of that step
failing: a private COFF reader keyed on a **different predicate** than the
instrument and missed the harness's known answer on the first witness TU. This
rung does step one the way §10.14 said it had to be done — **the harness prints
the names it already classifies** — and reads the result.

## What it admits, and what it refuses

Nothing. No emitter change, no acceptance change, no census movement. Two
additions to `crates/c2-harness/src/gap.rs`:

* **`C2RS_WITNESS=<path>`** — a witness list over the emitted-symbol residue:
  a ranked summary at `<path>` (per bucket: symbols, TUs, distinct names, and
  the top 40 names by frequency with an example TU) and one row per symbol at
  `<path>.rows.tsv`. **Rows are pushed from the same loop iteration that
  increments the counter**, and the summary prints both sides and whether they
  agree — on the workload all 8 buckets agree exactly (9,275 rows).
  Env-gated, not a CLI flag: the classification lives in this file and so does
  the precedent (`wall_dump`, `row_dump`), and the lane's seam is this file.
* **The two `.gl`-presence columns**, each labelled as a predicate that is *not*
  the bucketing one. There are two because one of them **cannot see half the
  residue**: `mangled_names` requires the run's second byte to be alphabetic and
  so drops every `??`-prefixed name — read alone it reports `0 of 947` for the
  `??_G…` bucket and that zero is an artifact of the reader, not a fact about
  `.gl`. `gl_symbol_index` is the second column and does see them.

Refused, deliberately: any second implementation of `mangling_class`, of the
COFF walk, or of the `.gl` framing. The one rule transcribed outside the harness
(`mangling_class`, in the analysis of a bucket the harness does not split) was
**validated against the harness's own labels first**: 4,591/4,591 no-record rows
agree, 0 disagreements.

## What the two buckets actually are

**`emit-unbound-no-record|ordinary` (2,809 symbols / 341 TUs) — base-class
virtual member functions.** The head of the ranking is
`?ClassName@RndPollable@@UBA?AVSymbol@@XZ` and `?SetType@RndPollable@@UAAXVSymbol@@@Z`
at 69 TUs each, `?Load@UIPanel@@UAAXAAVBinStream@@@Z` at 42,
`?ClassName@RndAnimatable@@UBA?AVSymbol@@XZ` at 39 — header-declared virtuals
that c2 emits as a `.text` COMDAT into every TU that includes the header. Not a
synthesis class, not EH, not a thunk family.

**`emit-unbound-no-record|special-generated` (947 / 365) — exactly what #152
says it is.** 100 % `??_G` scalar-deleting destructors, `??__F` atexit
destructors, `??__E` dynamic initializers, one `??_D`. The worry `wall_dump`'s
own doc comment registered — that `??_7` vftables, `??_R*` RTTI and `??_C`
string literals would be diluting this class — is **refuted** for the `.text`
COMDAT population: none of them is here.

**`emit-unbound-has-record` (4,684 / 509) — adjustor thunks.** 76.6 % carry an
`@@$4` / `@@$R4` / `@@W` marker (`??_ERndAnimatable@@$4PPPPPPPM@A@AAPAXI@Z`,
`?Handle@NavListSort@@W3AA…`). The "instrument defect" half of the wall and the
`ordinary` half of it are **the same object family seen twice** — virtual
dispatch, once with a framed record and once without.

### The result that outranks the description

**`.gl` names every symbol in the residue.** 9,274 of the 9,275 rows — all but
the single `undecorated` one — are bound to an operand token by
`gl_symbol_index`; for the `ordinary` bucket it is 2,809 of 2,809. What is
missing is the **framed `80 <LE32>` body-start record**, not the name.

So "no `.gl` body record" has been read as "c2 invented this symbol and a
segment-driven port must synthesize it", and that reading is not supported for
any of the 4,591 wall symbols. It changes what both items are:

* **#159 is a `.gl` framing question**, adjacent to #121 and #151<sub>w-vgl</sub>
  (which moved this same bucket 13,646 → 4,591 by widening one separator), not a
  Phase-7 synthesis question.
* **#152 still needs a body, but not a name.** Synthesizing the `??_` COMDAT
  family does not require inventing symbols; `.gl` already carries all 947.

**Predicate caveat, stated because this rung exists because of one.**
`gl_symbol_index` is `.gl`'s token→name index and includes *callee references*,
so what the 9,274 proves is "the name is in `.gl`'s symbol table", **not** "`.gl`
defines a body for it here". Three different questions have now been confused in
this area twice (§10.11, §10.14); this is the third and it is labelled.

### The board's own denominator for #159 is wrong

    emit-unbound-no-record|ordinary   2809 symbols over 341 TUs   <- the bucket #159 names
    emit-residue-unbound|ordinary     6271 symbols over 452 TUs   <- a different key, different axis

The symbol count was taken from `emit-residue-unbound|ordinary` (unbound = no

axis and stands. This is the same species of error §10.13 warned about in its
own re-price — a symbol denominator and a TU payoff are different quantities —
committed one section later.

## The splitter-anchor addition (coordinator's item), and its priced decline

`emit_set_reachable_tus` — the **25 of 871** ceiling that ROADMAP §10's
"at most 19 TUs, ever" rests on — filters on `fn_total == emit-emitted`, and its
doc comment asserts `fn_total` is the segment count `PortC2::build` consumes.
It is not: `fn_total` is `census_functions()`'s, anchored on `4C 4F 11`, while
the port consumes `IlBundle::functions()`, anchored on `4F 1F`.

Five new keys and an always-on printed block now count the ceiling **both ways**,
with both denominators printed and neither called "the" ceiling. The measurement:

    gate-side segment count KNOWN for 6 of 871 captured TUs; UNKNOWN for 865
    of the 6 known: 6 agree with `fn_total`, 0 disagree
    emit-set ceiling, LO-anchored, over ALL graded TUs: 25
    gate-anchored ceiling over the 6 TUs it is known for: 6 (+0 entering, -0 leaving)
    gate-anchored control on matching TUs: 0 violations over 6 matching TUs

**Declined, with the number: 865 of 871.** `IlBundle::functions()` is the only
public reader of the `4F 1F` split and it returns `None` for a bundle it cannot
parse — which is every `vocab-gap` TU. **Both TUs §10.11 names
(`TomCryptLicense.cpp`, `ZlibLicense.cpp`) are in the 865**, so the specific
defect that motivated the item is exactly what this instrument cannot see. The
headline claim remains **unverified on the anchor the port actually uses**, and
verifying it needs a segment-count accessor in `crates/c2-il/src/func/bundle.rs`
that reports the split even when the parse refuses — that file belongs to lane
`w-lo`, so it is handed back rather than taken.

On the control the coordinator asked about (item 4): **it can go red on exactly
one TU.** Five of the six matching TUs define zero functions (`fn_total` 0, gate
0, COMDATs 0) and agree trivially; only `src/system/utl/Spew.cpp` (2 / 2 / 2) is
a real check. Reported as that, not as "green".

## Found and not taken

1. **`C2RS_WALL_DUMP` already existed at `a091e37` and already dumped these
   names** (178,969 rows: 169,694 bound / 4,684 has-record / 4,591 no-record).
   §10.14 did not need a COFF reader; it needed to set an environment variable
   that was already in the file it declared out of scope. What the dump lacked
   was the **mangling class** and a ranking — which is what this rung added.
   The general lesson is narrower and sharper than §10.14 states it: before
   writing a second implementation, grep the harness for an env var.
2. **The 65 ordinary-only TUs have 1,200–9,228 more `.ex` segments than `.text`
   COMDATs** (median 2,966; computed on `fn_total`, the **`LO`-anchored** count —
   see the splitter section for why that qualifier is load-bearing). Workload-wide
   only **4** of 865 TUs have fewer segments than COMDATs. There is no counting
   pressure toward synthesis anywhere in this residue.
3. **Only 2.1 % of `ordinary` no-record names ever bind in any other TU** (49 of
   2,306 names, 63 of 2,809 symbols). So the family is not "the binding lost it
   here and got it there" — these names bind essentially nowhere in 878 TUs. The
   exceptions are worth one probe: `?CanSelect@UIListProvider@@UBA_NH@Z` **binds
   in 50 TUs and is no-record in 3**, which is a per-TU difference in the same
   symbol and therefore the cheapest possible witness for whatever the framing
   rule is.
4. **`mangled_names` is blind to every `??`-name** and its own doc comment says
   so, but nothing in the harness *reports* that blindness at a call site. Any
   future use of it as an "is this name in `.gl`" test will produce a confident
   zero. Not a board item yet; named here so the next lane does not rediscover it
   as a finding.
5. The witness list caps each bucket at 40 names. The tails are large (2,266 more
   names / 2,300 more symbols in `ordinary`) and the raw `.rows.tsv` is the place
   to work them; the cap is a display choice, not a measurement one.

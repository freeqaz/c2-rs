## What it admits, and what it refuses

**The rule.** `4C` is the `.ex` body-start token and `4F 11` is a **separate,
optional record** between it and the body's first `53`:

```text
  source body        …  46 <formals>   4C  4F 11  53  <stmts> …
  ??__E / ??__F      …  46             4C         53  <stmts> …
```

`c2-il` modelled the three bytes as one atom (`codec.rs` `ExToken::Lo`,
`bundle.rs` `LO_MARKER`). Now `ExToken::Lo` is the one byte and
`ExToken::LoRecord` is the record; `func::bundle::body_start` is the **one**
locator for both forms and `codec` calls it rather than keeping a second copy
(§10.14's rule — a private re-derivation is a second rule that agrees until it
matters).

**What the bare form admits.** A `4F 1F` region holding no `4C 4F 11` **at all**
is offered to a grammar walk that must complete

```text
  53 53   (53 | 26 <tok16>)*   46 (2D <tok16>)*   4C 53
```

from some `53 53` in the segment — candidate-and-verify, first completion wins.

**What it refuses,** and this is the load-bearing half, because `4C` is
overloaded (last byte of `IntCallEnd` `55 86 41 74 4C`, first of `VoidCallEnd`
`4C 4B`) and ~2 % of `4F 1F` hits on a real 1.5 MB `.ex` are payload collisions:

| refused | why |
|---|---|
| `55 86 41 74 4C 53` | an `IntCallEnd` tail before a statement — no prefix grammar in front of it |
| a bare `4F 1F 80 <word> … 4C 53` | payload collision — no `53 53` statement region |
| `… 46 4C 4B` | the byte after the formals list is a `VoidCallEnd`, not a body start |
| `… 53 53 26 <tok> 77 77 46 4C 53` | an unmodelled record inside the prefix — refuse, do not skip ahead to the next `4C` |
| **any region that already holds `4C 4F 11`** | the composed branch wins first, so every function that split before splits **byte-identically** |

**What it does not touch.** `parse_segment` (`body/mod.rs`) still requires the
composed marker, so every one of the 746 new census rows is honestly blocked at
`lo-marker:mid` and **not one of them reaches the emitter**. That was a
deliberate scope decision, not an oversight — see *Found and not taken*.

## The mistake the fixtures caught

The first implementation anchored the walk on the `4F 02 20 00 4F 01 NN`
block-start record. It passed `il_dyninit_static.cpp`, passed every unit test,
and was **wrong**: that record is per **module**, not per function. On
`wlo_dyninit_pair.cpp` — one object with both a constructor and a destructor, so
c2 emits `??__EsL` *and* `??__FsL` — the module opens block `18` at the first
segment and closes `19` at the end of the last, and the atexit thunk goes
straight from its FnHeader to `53 53`. The anchor found one of two bodies and
reported `fn_total = 1` for a TU with two functions.

That is §10.11's defect one level down — *a count is only evidence about the
predicate that produced it* — and on the 878-TU workload it cost **687 of the
746** bodies (it found 59). Nothing but a second thunk in one TU could show it,
which is exactly why `wlo_dyninit_pair.cpp` exists.

## Is it finding real functions? The independent bound says yes

The `.ex` **function tail** (`4F 12 47 54 01 54 00`) is a count neither splitter
produces, and `sy.rs` establishes it equals the `.sy` block count in all 871
graded TUs. Counted straight off the cached `.ex` bytes:

| | count | short of tails |
|---|---|---|
| function tails | **2,464,544** | — |
| segments, `a091e37` | 2,462,572 | 1,972 |
| segments, this rung | 2,463,318 | **1,226** |
| **TUs where segments exceed tails** | **0 before, 0 after** | |

So the rung recovers **746 of the splitter's 1,972 documented missing bodies
(37.8 %)** and overshoots in **no** translation unit. A spurious segment would
have to be paid for by a missed real body *in the same TU* to stay under that
bound. Two further checks corroborate it: `.sy`'s fail-closed rule (fewer blocks
than segments unbinds the whole file) did not fire in a single TU, and
`emit-bound` fell in **0** TUs while rising in 17.

**The remaining 1,226 are a different cause.** The bare `4C` is not the whole of
the splitter's shortfall — it is 38 % of it, and the rest is unread.

understanding before it is misread: a new segment **truncates its predecessor**,
whose segment used to run through the thunk's bytes into the next function. Those

control-flow byte that belonged to the thunk. Seven of them
(`param-width-undetermined`) stopped refusing altogether, which is where the
numerator's +1 comes from — **not** from any bare-`4C` body being in class. None
is.

**The emit-set ceiling moving 25 → 27 is the largest consequence of this rung**
and it is the number §10.2 derives "at most 19 TUs, ever" from. That headline is
now *at most 21*, and lane w-witness's §10.15 finding says the ceiling's filter
is keyed on the wrong splitter anyway. Do not compound the two: 27 is today's
instrument, recomputed, not a repair of it.

## Also landed, at the coordinator's request

`IlBundle::ex_segment_count() -> Option<usize>` — the `4F 1F` segment count as a
**pure reader**: no acceptance decision, no refusal, available on a bundle whose
`functions()` returns `None` (865 of 878 workload TUs). Until it existed,
`functions()` was the only public reader of that split, which is why `gap.rs`'s
emit-set ceiling filters on the *other* splitter's count (§10.15). Neither
splitter was altered to add it, and **this rung did not change
`split_functions_at`** — the re-tokenization is in `body_start` /
`split_function_bodies_at` and in the codec — so its value is byte-for-byte what
`a091e37` would report. It returns `Option`, not a flat `usize`, so that "no
`.ex`" cannot be read as "zero functions" by a ceiling comparing it to a COMDAT
count (STATUS trap 5); consumers take `.unwrap_or(0)` deliberately.

## Found and not taken

Ranked. The first is the one the next lane on #158 wants.

1. **Teach `parse_segment` the bare form — NOT TAKEN, and not merely for scope.**
   Every downstream reader takes the `LO` offset and assumes the operand stream
   begins at `lo + 3`: `parse_formals(seg, lo)`, `control_flow::scan_full(seg,
   lo)`, `mcall::mark_whole(seg, lo, …)`, and each shape recognizer. Handing any
   of them the bare `4C` offset does not make them refuse — it makes them read
   **two bytes into the body** and mis-parse, and a mis-parse in a body that
   reaches the emitter is a wrong-bytes emit, not a gap. Doing it properly means
   threading an ops-start offset (`lo + 1` or `lo + 3`) through the whole shape
   layer: a refactor with its own pre-registration, not an edit. **The 746 rows
   this rung admits sit at `lo-marker:mid` until that lands**, which is honest
   and gradeable, and it is why P6/P7 came out 0.
2. **The other 1,226 missing bodies.** Tails 2,464,544 vs segments 2,463,318.
   The bare `4C` was 38 % of the shortfall; nobody has read what the rest are.
   This is cheap to start — the same tail-count instrument used above localizes
   them per TU — and it is the natural successor to this rung.
3. **`??__E`/`??__F` is a *measured* boundary, not a proven one.** Every bare-`4C`
   body observed is one of those two thunks, and the one `??_` family member
   tested outside them (`??_G`, a synthesized deleting destructor) came out
   `4C 4F 11`. Whether the bare form generalizes further is still unknown and
   **must not be assumed** — the walk is written to refuse rather than guess.
4. **`is_empty_module` and `functions()`'s empty-module branch still key on the
   composed `4C 4F 11`.** Both are safe today (they require `4F 1F` to be absent
   too, and a body implies a start), but they are now reading a *composition* as
   if it were an atom. Left alone deliberately: changing either moves what the
   port emits for a TU it currently treats as empty, which is an emit-side
   change and belongs with the obj-shape half.
5. **The obj shape remains the larger half of #158.** `.rdata` + `.bss` +
   `.CRT$XCU` beside `.text` against a fixed four-section shell. Both license TUs
   need it *and* item 1; neither alone converts a TU.

## 5. THE MISMATCH: 30 OF 54 CELLS, AND WHAT c2 ACTUALLY DOES

### 5.1 How it was found, and that it is pre-existing

`w11_early_return.cpp`'s own header says `?mmioGetInfo` is `mm` *"plus a
two-word entry-block park"*. The first rung of §4.1 is therefore `mm` with a
two-formal argument swap, `work/w-clear/probe/m1.cpp`. It graded **`mismatch`,
bytes diverge** — on a first run, with **`--no-cache`**, so #1388's
cache-hit defect is excluded by construction.

Bracketed to the minimum in four probes:

| probe | shape | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `n1.cpp` | the swap, **no guard** | **match** |
| `n3.cpp` | two guards, **no swap** | **match** |
| `n4.cpp` | a 3-formal rotation, **no guard** | **match** |
| **`n2.cpp`** | **one guard + the swap** | **MISMATCH** |

### 5.2 The grid

`work/w-clear/probe/grid.txt` — guard count {0,1,2,3} × permutation {`[1,0]`,
`[0,2,1]`, `[1,2,0]`, `[2,0,1]`, `[2,1,0]`, identity, `[1,0,3,2]`} × trailing
call count {1,2}. **54 cells, every one graded against real `c2.dll`.**

```text
  guards = 0, non-identity      12 cells     12 MATCH        0 mismatch
  guards >= 1, non-identity     30 cells      0 match       30 MISMATCH
  identity, any guard count      6 cells      6 MATCH        0 mismatch
  two DISJOINT cycles [1,0,3,2]  6 cells     the parser refuses (`call-arg-multicycle`)
```

No exceptions in either direction. The port emitted **80 correct-looking bytes
with the wrong ones from offset 0x0c** on all thirty.

### 5.3 What c2 does — the park rule, characterized

```text
  f(p,q){ g(q,p); }                 mr r11,r4 · mr r4,r3 · mr r3,r11    <- the port MATCHES this
  f(p,q){ if(!p) return 5; g(q,p); }
      ENTRY   mr r11,r3 · mr r3,r4                 the PARK
              cmplwi cr6,r11,0 · bf 26,+12 · …     and the guard reads r11
      CALL    mr r4,r11                            only the REMAINDER
```

Two differences, not one: a **different cycle break** (the guarded form saves
the *lowest* slot's home, the unguarded form the *highest*), and a **split
across two blocks**.

Read off all five permutations at guard count 1, writing the cycle as
`r11 ← <lowest slot's home>` then the chain:

| perm | the whole move chain | hoisted to the ENTRY block | left at the CALL |
|---|---|---|---|
| `[1,0]` | `r11←r3 · r3←r4 · r4←r11` | 2 | 1 |
| `[0,2,1]` | `r11←r4 · r4←r5 · r5←r11` | 2 | 1 |
| `[2,1,0]` | `r11←r3 · r3←r5 · r5←r11` | 2 | 1 |
| `[1,2,0]` | `r11←r3 · r3←r4 · r4←r5 · r5←r11` | **3** | 1 |
| `[2,0,1]` | `r11←r3 · r3←r5 · r5←r4 · r4←r11` | 2 | **2** |

**The rule that fits all five: hoist the maximal prefix whose destination
register is strictly increasing, excluding the cycle-closing move; defer the
rest.** `[2,0,1]` is the only cell that discriminates it — its chain writes
`r3, r5, r4` and the descent at `r4` is where the split falls.

**This lane REFUSED rather than implemented it, and the reason is n = 1.** One
discriminating cell is exactly the evidence #260 warns about: an emitter built
on it writes a plausible-looking wrong instruction, which is the failure mode
that survives review. The rule is published here so whoever pays #275 starts
from a table and a grid instead of "three cells and no test".

### 6.1 The registered shape question, answered — and answered against me

`PREREG.md` §4 asked `w-4c`'s question explicitly: *is this rung bracket-shaped?*
It named **`cflow-if-n`** as the only candidate, cited #1346's "it still converts
zero", and registered **NOT bracket-shaped, therefore optimistic**.

**The bracket exists and it is not `cflow-if-n`. It is the GUARD ITSELF.**
Measured in §3.2: a member call on a global object is **free** alone and a rung
behind a guard; a `||` guard chain is **free** as a single guard and a rung as a
chain; an argument permutation is **byte-exact** alone and a **`Port=Mismatch`**
behind a guard. `SeqEarlyReturn` composes with almost nothing, and that one fact
sits under every cell of §4 at once — the same *"a floor under every call site
in the TU at once"* shape `w-4c` named for `0x4C`, in a different layer.

So the depths came back **shallower** than registered, which is the *second*
consecutive pessimistic miss after ten straight optimistic ones — and the reason
is the same reason both times: **a composition floor is not a sum of independent
refusals, and estimates keep treating it as one.**

**P-INV missed in the interesting direction too.** #1401's lesson is that carried
inventories are wrong; here **none of the carried items was wrong** — #483's
seven, #260's three, #410's correction and #505's 316 all reproduce. What the
record lacked was three items nobody had *taken* (§2.2). "Re-derive it" found
omissions, not errors, and the two failure modes want different searches.

## 0. The answer, in one paragraph

**The `0x80` in the `.gl` function record's `SIZE` field is a LENGTH ESCAPE with
a two-byte little-endian payload — three bytes total — and `0x81..=0xff` is a
separate one-byte sign-extended form that is not part of it.** `SRCPOS`'s escape
is five bytes and `SIZE`'s is three because they are read by **different
readers**: `il-read-varint32` at `0x10c1f9e9` has a four-byte payload where
`il-read-varint16` at `0x10c1f9a6` has two. The incumbent stepped over both as
if there were one width, and refused — correctly, while the width was unknown,
because at the wrong displacement the attribute is an unrelated byte and an
unrelated byte with bit 6 set is a **permission** `splice` acts on.

Confirmed three ways, and the disassembly is the weakest of the three (§2).
Shipped at `mismatch 0`, `fnbyte-exact` Δ **0**, and a gate whose every graded
count is identical at both ends (§6).

**What it bought is warranty, not reach, and the warranty is measured** (§4):
the refusal fired on **60 of 870** workload TUs and **56** now decode; among
them `src/xdk/nuiapi/headtracker.cpp`, where a `__declspec(noinline)` record was
invisible because *a different function in the same file* had a large `SIZE`.
GRID-D reproduces that from source and grades it against real c2 (§5).

## 2. The escape, confirmed three ways

### 2.1 The reader, off the image `[R]`

`il-read-varint16`, `0x10c1f9a6`, the address `ADDR.tsv` already labelled
*"1-or-3-byte reader"* before this lane:

```
10c1f9b1:  8a 11              mov    dl,BYTE PTR [ecx]      ; one byte
10c1f9ba:  80 fa 80           cmp    dl,0x80
10c1f9bd:  74 06              je     0x10c1f9c5             ; escape
10c1f9bf:  66 0f be c2        movsx  ax,dl                  ; else ONE SIGNED byte
...
10c1f9d8:  88 55 fc           mov    BYTE PTR [ebp-0x4],dl  ; payload low
10c1f9e0:  88 55 fd           mov    BYTE PTR [ebp-0x3],dl  ; payload high
10c1f9e3:  66 8b 45 fc        mov    ax,WORD PTR [ebp-0x4]  ; LE16
```

`0x10c1f9e9` is the identical shape with a **four**-byte payload
(`0x10c1fa07`…`0x10c1fa43`). That contrast is the whole finding: **two adjacent
fields of one record escape at different widths because two different readers
read them.**

### 2.2 A black-box twin grid — GRID-A, 18 cells `[O]`

No disassembly in the loop. Each cell is a pair of sources differing only by
`__declspec(noinline) ` versus **21 spaces**, so byte-length-identical, and
compiled from **one path** — otherwise the `.gl`'s embedded source path and every
record's `SRCPOS` shift and the diff is 130 bytes of noise instead of the one
byte the experiment is about. `__declspec(noinline)` clears exactly this
record's `FN_FLAG_INLINABLE`, so the twins must differ at `ATTR` and nowhere
else structural.

| `SIZE` | form | `ATTR` offset | offset − (`p`+5) | first differing byte past the source hash | XOR | `Δ` file length |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 55, 79, 103, 127 | direct | 236 | **2** | 236 | `0x40` | +2 |
| 139, 163, 211, 259, 379 | **escape** | 238 | **4** | 238 | `0x40` | +2 |

**18 of 18** at `/O1` and `/Ox` alike. **The escape moves `ATTR` by exactly
two**, measured directly.

**Endianness, also black box.** The family steps `SIZE` by 12 per statement and
the ladder does not break at the boundary: 55 → 79 → 103 → 127 → **139** → 163 →
211 → 259 → 379, every rung `19 + 12n`. Read big-endian the first escaped rung
would be **35,584**. Table: `work/w-glattrs/GRID-A.txt`.

### 2.3 The workload's own records — RIVALS, 99 records `[O]`

The 28,739 records whose `SIZE` is **direct** establish an `ATTR` vocabulary of
ten bytes independently: `28 48 4c 68 69 6c 88 c8 cc e8`. Scored on the 99
records whose `SIZE` escapes, under each rival width:

| assumed escape width | `ATTR` in the vocabulary | the byte it reads instead |
|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | **3 / 99** | the payload's low byte |
| 2 | **0 / 99** | the payload's high byte — `00:61 01:23 02:7 …`, i.e. `SIZE`'s top byte, exactly as predicted |
| **3 — shipped** | **99 / 99** | — |
| 5 (`SRCPOS`'s width) | **1 / 99** | two bytes past `ATTR` |

Background rate for a `.gl` byte to land in that vocabulary: **5.9 %**.
`work/w-glattrs/RIVALS.txt`. Re-run on GRID-C's 309-edge population (§3) the
same comparison reads **309 / 309** against **1 / 0 / 4**.

### 2.4 The third form, refused on a COUNT

`0x81..=0xff` is one sign-extended byte in c2, so admitting it is a one-line
change. **It is not made.** Prereg **D1** fixed the rule before the count was
taken: zero witnesses ⇒ keep refusing. The count is **0 of 1,461,374** workload
records, under **both** `.gl` framings. An unwitnessed shape is refused rather
than guessed at (`label_counter`'s rule one field over), and the byte stays a
desync canary — a walk that has drifted off a record boundary lands on a high
byte far more often than on a well-formed `0x80`.

## 3. GRID-C — the decode scored against real `c2.dll` on 7,667 call edges

`docs/rungs/README.md`: *"a predicate is priced against the oracle, on the
population it will apply to, or it is not priced."* The observable is `w-fence2`
GRID-W's, per IL call edge to a callee this TU defines: does the reference
caller's `.text` COMDAT carry a `REL24` naming the callee? **7,667 edges, 0
`unknown`** — the identical population `w-sizebracket` used, on the identical
corpus (§7.2).

### 3.1 The soundness test, in the consumers' own terms

`splice` and `comdat` read `FN_FLAG_INLINABLE` **clear** as *"c2 keeps this
call"*. So the falsifiable claim is `bit 6 clear ⇒ the edge is kept`, and a
`noinline` edge that c2 **inlined** is the decode wrong about c2 in the direction
that emits bytes.

| framing | records resolved | escaped edges | counterexamples |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| incumbent `gl_offset_framed` (what the reader walks) | 74 / 7,667 | **2** | **0** |
| `gl_offset_framed_relaxed` (#2783; what `w-sizebracket` measured on) | 7,660 / 7,667 | **309** | **0** |

The relaxed row reproduces `w-sizebracket`'s **309** exactly, which is the
cross-check that this is the same measurement.

### 3.2 The one `noinline` edge in the graded set

Under both framings there is exactly **one** call edge whose callee's decoded
`ATTR` has bit 6 clear, and c2 **kept** it. One edge is one edge; it is reported
because it is the entire arm-axis evidence the workload supplies.

### 3.3 ⚠ AND THE ARM AXIS HAS NO POWER ON THE ESCAPED POPULATION

All **309** escaped edges are `kept`; **0** are `inlined`. That is not a
coincidence and it is not a result: an escaped `SIZE` is `>= 128`, and 128 is
above the inline ceiling at **both** profiles (`w-sizebracket`'s `(97, 103]` at
`/O1` and `(121, 127]` at `/Ox`). **So `bit 6 clear ⇒ kept` cannot be falsified
on that population by construction** — every edge in it satisfies the consequent
whatever the decode says.

> **A green oracle test over a one-armed population is STATUS trap 0 in its
> purest form**, and this rung states it rather than banking the zero. What
> actually discriminates on the escaped population is §2.3's vocabulary score
> (309/99 vs 1/0/4) and §2.2's positional twin grid (18/18) — both of which
> **can** come out wrong and did not.

It also means something for the *value* of the fix: **the `FN_FLAG_INLINABLE`
bit of an escaped record is never itself the deciding fact for that record's own
call sites**, because c2 declines those calls on size anyway. The fix's whole
value is in un-refusing the **file**. Which is §5.

## 5. GRID-D — what the WHOLE-FILE refusal actually cost, constructed and c2-graded

The workload witness is **`src/xdk/nuiapi/headtracker.cpp`**: it holds
`??$sprintf_s@$0BAE@@@YAHAAY0BAE@DPBDZZ` with `ATTR = 0x88` — bit 6 **clear** —
in a **direct**-form record of `SIZE` 46. The incumbent refused the whole file on
a *different* record's escaped `SIZE`, so that bit was invisible and
`IlFunction::inlinable` read `None`.

**`None` is not conservative.** Its documented meaning is *"every consumer
behaves exactly as it did before this field existed"* — and before the field
existed `splice` expanded the body. So the refusal is not fail-closed for the
one consumer that matters; it is fail-*silent*.

Reproduced from source at the workload profile and graded by real c2
(`work/w-glattrs/GRID-D.txt`):

| cell | incumbent | new | small's `ATTR` | big's `SIZE` | **c2 keeps `caller → small`** |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| **A** small `__declspec(noinline)` alone | ok | ok | `28` | — | **yes** |
| **B** A **+ one function whose `SIZE` escapes** | **REFUSED** (`size-escape`) | ok | `28` | 187 escape | **yes** |
| **C** small **inlinable** + the same escaping function | **REFUSED** | ok | `68` | 187 escape | no — c2 inlines it |
| **D** B at `/Ox` | **REFUSED** | ok | `28` | 187 escape | **yes** |

**B is the loss and C is the control that makes it one.** Adding one large
function to a translation unit made the port blind to a `__declspec(noinline)`
on a small one — and real c2 keeps that call. In C the same refusal costs
nothing, so the loss is not a property of the shape.

`crates/c2-harness/tests/noinline_boundary.rs` cell `w10` is the record of what
that blindness has already cost once: *"a shipped, demonstrated wrong emit"*.
That file is peer `w-witness7`'s seam and is untouched here; the cell it needs
is §9.2.

M3 came back **GREEN in round 1, exactly as registered** — and it was
registered GREEN for the reason *"the shape has zero witnesses in the workload,

the time M3 ran this lane had added three **constructed** unit cells that assert

> **The lesson is sharper than "a test was weak".** A registered prediction that
> *matches* the observation is the case nobody re-examines, and here the match
> was a coincidence of two errors: the prediction reasoned about the corpus, the
> cell failed for truncation, and they agreed. It surfaced only as a
> **contradiction between two runs of one mutant** — which is precisely
> `w-mutcensus`'s #3231 pattern, arrived at from the other direction (there, two
> runs of one mutant disagreed; here, one run agreed with a prediction that was
> reasoning about something else).
>
> And `one_unreadable_record_refuses_the_whole_file`'s own comment —
> *"`_neg` cells have been confounded or inert in five of the last seven
> lanes"* — sits **four functions above** the cell this lane broke, in the same
> file, and was read by this lane while writing it. **Reading the warning did
> not prevent the defect; the mutant did.**

#### M2 is NOT EXPRESSIBLE, and that is the registered prediction in its strongest form

The shipped reader never *reads* the payload — it steps over it
(`q += 1 + GL_SIZE_ESCAPE_PAYLOAD`). There is no byte order anywhere in
`crates/` to mutate. Endianness is therefore graded by GRID-A against real c2 or
it is not graded at all, which is `w-sizebracket` §5.2's discipline applied to
this lane's own claim. The only place a byte order is pinned in the tree is the
**test fixture** `fn_record_sz`, which writes `w.to_le_bytes()`.

**Registered colours observed: 3 of 4** (control, M1, M4). **Contradicted: 1** —
M3, and correctly so. **Inexpressible: 1** — M2.

### 8.3 ⚠ A 17-for-17 prereg is a statement about the prereg

Two of the predictions were cheap and the rung says so rather than banking them:

* **P4's substance was already established when the prereg was frozen.** The
  image was disassembled *before* the prereg was written — that is why P4 is
  registered at **0.90** and not at 0.5. The prereg's own claim, *"frozen before
  any probe compiled, any scan ran, and any line of `crates/` changed"*, is
  true and is **not** the same as *"frozen before anything was known"*. Stated
  here because a reader scoring 17/17 deserves to know which rows were bets.
* **P5 inherits most of P4.** Given the width, the twin grid's position was
  nearly forced. What P5 genuinely risked was the *method* — that twins could be
  made to differ in one byte at all — and that took two attempts (§9.3).

The rows that were real bets and could have gone the other way: **P6** (0.55,
and its method did fail), **P9** (0.50, the realized size), **P14** (0.45, that
a live `noinline` was actually being hidden), **P17** (0.40, the framing
inflation) and **P11** (0.60).

### 8.4 GRID-B was registered and NOT RUN

The prereg froze three grids and **GRID-B — `.gl` byte-level mutation replay
through real `c2.dll`** — was not run. It was registered as the only instrument
that could decide the payload's endianness from c2's own output; GRID-A's ladder
continuity decided it instead, from a cell family the prereg had already frozen
for a different purpose. So the deliverable is met and the *registered
instrument* is not, and that distinction is recorded rather than smoothed over.
`work/w-roots/mutate.py` is the harness a follow-on would use (§9.4).

## 9. Found and not taken

### 9.1 `gl_function_attrs` on #2783's relaxed framing — the biggest item here, and it is now unblocked

`gl_offset_framed` pins `gl[o-5] == 0x10`, which board **#2783** measured as a
**value window on a rising counter**: 5 of 5 records on a 2.5 KB `.gl` and 36 of
811 on a 65 KB one. `gl_function_attrs` walks it, so it sees **28,838** records
where a relaxed walk sees **1,461,374** — **2.0 %**. Every consumer of
`FN_FLAG_INLINABLE` is therefore reading a 2 %-sample of the TU's records today,
and `codec.rs` already ships `gl_offset_framed_relaxed` with `GL_OFFSET_MAX`
pinned and a 876-TU superset measurement behind it.

**What this lane changes is that the widening is now possible at all.** Under the
relaxed framing the `SIZE`-escape refusal alone fires on **838 of 870** TUs — a
framing widening without this decode would have taken the reader from 801
answered files to **32**. With it, the relaxed framing answers **805**.

**It is not taken here, and the price is genuinely unpriced.** `NameFit`'s seam
(`w-decouple`) exists precisely because a walk widening is a *tightening* of the
fences that consume it, and #2622/#2623 measured one such widening at **−1
`fnbyte-exact`**. A lane taking this owes the same two-sided pricing this one
gave the escape, on a population 50× larger, and must decide whether
`gl_function_attrs` follows `GATE_BIND_FRAME`'s policy split or gets its own.

### 9.2 The `noinline_boundary.rs` cell GRID-D deserves

§5's cell **B** is a four-line source file that turns `gl_function_attrs` from
answering to refusing, on a TU whose small callee is `noinline` and whose call
real c2 keeps. `crates/c2-harness/tests/noinline_boundary.rs` is peer
**`w-witness7`**'s seam and was not touched. The cell is worth landing there:
it is the regression test for *"a large function anywhere in the file blinds the
port to every attribute in it"*, and it grades against the oracle rather than
against a fixture.

### 9.3 The twin-grid trap, and it cost this lane a full grid run

The first GRID-A run put `__declspec(noinline) ` in front of the callee and
compiled the twins from **two different paths**. Both `.gl` files then differed
in **130 bytes** — the embedded source path, a 16-byte source hash, and every
record's `SRCPOS` — and the one byte the experiment was about was invisible in
the noise. The fix is two lines: pad the plain twin with 21 spaces so the
sources are byte-length-identical, and write both to one path.

**Generalizable**: `.gl` carries the source path and per-record source
positions, so *any* `.gl` diff experiment must hold the source length and the
path fixed or it is measuring the container's provenance fields.

### 9.4 The `ATTR` varU, documented and not decoded

`ATTR` is a two-or-four-byte little-endian value with a continuation flag in bit
15 (`0x10c1f91b`), not a byte. `__declspec(noinline)` takes it from `0x1068` to
`0x801028` — crossing `0x8000`, so the record grows by two bytes. **That is the
mechanism behind `w-target`'s nicmp2 observation that "only `.gl` moves, and by
2 bytes"**, which has stood unexplained since it was recorded. Reading the low
byte is correct for bit 6 under both widths and nothing today needs the value,
so nothing was decoded; the `gl_function_attrs` docstring says so, so the next
lane does not discover it the way this one discovered `SIZE`.

**The other bits of that low byte are unread and there are at least eight of
them in use**: the workload's ten-value vocabulary is
`28 48 4c 68 69 6c 88 c8 cc e8`. `0x40` is `noinline`; `0x20` tracks `static`
(`0x68 → 0x48`) per `w-mmioclose`'s cells; `0x80`, `0x08`, `0x04` and `0x01` are
undecoded. `c8` is 93 % of all records and `68` is 85 % of the **escaped** ones,
which is a difference nobody has explained and which a lane wanting a second
attribute bit should start from.

### 9.5 The escaped population is one-armed, so a size lane cannot use it

§3.3: every escaped-`SIZE` callee in the workload is above the inline ceiling at
both profiles. A future lane looking for inline-decision evidence gets **zero
discriminating cells** from the escaped population, at either profile. That is a
bound on a whole class of follow-ons and it is cheap to state and expensive to
rediscover.

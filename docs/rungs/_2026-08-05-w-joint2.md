## 6. THE DECLINE — clause D1, fired at three times its threshold

Prereg D1: *"If R5 ≤ 10 … I decline to build the seven facts as a TU-match rung
and ship the measurement instead."*

**R5 = 8.** Seven independent facts — two of them coupled widenings in another
crate with a named red guard — for **+0 TU match**, against a standing decline
clause (board #269) that fires at **four**. The clause fires by a factor of
three and I am not softening it.

**What I am explicitly NOT saying:**

* **Not that the specification is wasted.** `OBJ_RDATA_R_SHAPE.md` §1–§9 is a
  byte-level measurement of a real section, with a DFS emission rule exact on
  63 of 63 objs and a derivability result of 3,570 of 3,570. It will be needed
  the day the codegen class reaches a polymorphic constructor. It is not needed
  *first*, and `0 of 676` is why.
* **Not that `w-reach` or `w-alias` were wrong.** Both labelled their quantity
  *reach* and both said in terms that match does not move. The alias decode is
  a zero-free-parameter reader agreed to the digit by two implementations; §3.4
  changes nothing about it.
* **Not that reach is worthless.** It is the right metric for an emit predicate.
  It is not the payoff metric, and a brief that quotes it as one will get a
  seven-fact build for `+0`.

### 6.1 What I recommend instead, with the number attached and split honestly

The only lever measured to move TU match without new codegen is **factor A** —
the emit-set model in `PortC2`. Its ceiling is **+2** (`B∧(D∨E)` **10** vs
`A∧B∧(D∨E)` **8**), on two named TUs that need **no section work at all**. But
the ceiling is not what an implementable model gets, and the split is already
measured — `w-reach` §4.1 scored all four models on exactly these two TUs:

| TU | `JFP` | **`JFP_ALIAS`** | `ORACLE` | `ALIAS_IN` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `src/system/decomp_pch.cpp` | exact | **exact** | exact | exact |
| `src/system/math/vec.cpp` | no | **no** | **no** | **no** |

> ### So the honest scoping number is: **an emit-set model in `PortC2` is worth `+1` TU match today — 8 → 9 — and `+2` only for a model that reaches `vec.cpp`, which NO published model does**, ceilings included. `vec.cpp` is one of the 11 `B∧C` TUs even `ALIAS_IN` misses (`w-reach` §4.3).

**That `+1` is nevertheless the largest TU-match rung on the board**, because
everything else is `+0` until codegen widens, and it is the first model whose

After that, **every** remaining TU needs codegen breadth — the FRONTIER's 19,

**I did not build it** (prereg D6). It is a load-bearing design piece:

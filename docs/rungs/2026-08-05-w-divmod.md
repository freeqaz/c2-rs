## 2. The discriminator

`w-hash` §5.1 published eight rows as **mnemonic multisets** and recorded two
`twi 6` placements with the discriminator unknown, declining to fit it because
*"a fit on one witness is not a rule"*. Two things had to be added before the
question was answerable:

* **register fields.** A mnemonic table cannot see an allocation, and the
  regimes turn out to differ in one.
* **the `TO` field read off bits 6..10 of the instruction word.** `w-hash`'s R6
  hit because it derived the trap semantics from the encoding rather than
  asserting them; the same undertaking is made here, and it is what found §2.3.

`work/w-divmod/twigrid.py` decodes every word itself into `(name, defs, uses)`,
so "was this operand computed in this block, and where" is answered
structurally. **The decoder is cross-checked against `llvm-mc` on every cell** —
instruction count and the position of every `twi` must agree — because a decoder
that silently drops a word shifts every index in the summary and still prints a
plausible table.

### 2.1 The rule

> **`twi 6` is emitted immediately after the FIRST instruction of the division's
> own basic block that is neither a multiply (`mulli`/`mullw`) nor a
> REGISTER-amount shift (`slw`/`srw`/`sraw`)** — provided that
>
> * **(b)** the dividend is produced by an instruction *in that block*,
> * **(c)** the divisor is *live-in* to that block, and
> * **(d)** that block is *not a loop body*.
>
> Otherwise `twi 6` stays inside the spine, in the slot the spine's own schedule
> gives it.

161 cells, three grids, every one graded. Board **#780**.

### 2.2 All three registered readings lose, and the pred-count reading loses twice

`PREREG` R3 registered three rivals and said plainly it expected the lead one to
lose. It did, and so did the other two:

| reading | killed by | what the cell does |
|---|---|---|
| **R3-a** the `divw`'s block is the **entry** block | `blk-if` | hoists out of an `if`-arm, which is not the entry block |
| **R3-b** the block has exactly **one predecessor** | `j-inblock` | hoists out of a **two-predecessor JOIN block** |
| **R3-c** it tracks the **divisor's last use** | `s-div-var` | `twi 6` precedes the `andc` that reads the divisor |

**The predecessor count is refuted a second time and in the more informative
direction.** `j-livein` and `p-join` do *not* hoist — and the reason is clause
**(b)**, not the pred count: their dividend is computed in a *predecessor* and is
live-in to the join block. Two cells that look like evidence for a control-flow
clause are evidence for an operand clause, and the pair `j-livein` /
`j-inblock` separates them cleanly.

### 2.3 What clause (a) is not

Four candidate mechanisms die on named cells:

* **not latency** — `lwz` and `lbz` are loads and do **not** block; `srawi` and
  `rlwinm` are single-cycle and do not block; the blockers are `mulli`, `mullw`,
  `slw`, `srw`, `sraw`.
* **not "there is a multiply in the block"** — `m-mul-then-add` (`mulli`, `add`)
  hoists, with the trap after the `add`. The blocker has to be *first*, not
  present.
* **not "the last dividend instruction"** — `dvd-3op` (`mulli`, `add`, `xor`)
  puts the trap at index 2, **before** the `xor` that finishes the dividend;
  `m-mulhi` (`addis`, `ori`, `mullw`) puts it at index 1, two instructions
  early.
* **not a fixed position** — the `blk2` group is nine cells built to move the
  first free instruction around, and the trap follows it every time: index 1
  when the block opens free, index 2 when it opens with one blocker, and no
  hoist at all when every pre-spine instruction is a blocker (`t-mul-mul`,
  `t-slw-slw`, `t-mul-slw`). **9 of 9.**

The sharpest loop witness is **`l-loopfree`**: a loop-*invariant* dividend makes
`c2` move the whole division into the preheader, and it **hoists there**. So
clause (d) is about the division's own block and not about the function
containing a loop — a distinction no amount of staring at `?HashString` would
have produced.

### 2.4 It is measured and it is NOT shipped, on purpose

Clause (a) is a **description of five blocking witnesses**, not a mechanism.
`codegen::div_mod_leaf` accepts only bodies whose operands are **both live-in**,
where the hoist clause is false by construction and the placement is a constant;
a computed operand is **refused**, not scheduled. A rule fitted here would have
joined the ten already refuted on this project (`w-pair` §4's six,
`leaf_store.rs`'s four).

## 3. `TO = 7` — a third trap form, and R2's registered half that missed

R2 registered two things. The first — that `TO` 6 and 5 are derived from the
encoding rather than paraphrased — **held**. The second was *"`TO` takes **only**
the values 5 and 6 across the entire grid"*, and it **MISSED**:

```text
  lit-zero    int P(int a){ return a%0; }     0ce00000    twi 7, r0, 0
```

`TO = 7 = 0b00111` is *equal* ∪ *unsigned-less* ∪ *unsigned-greater* — a
tautology over the unsigned order — so it traps **unconditionally**, and its
operand register is **`r0`**: not the dividend, not the divisor, because it reads
nothing. No division is emitted at all. Seven cells produce it and they are the
same value by seven different routes (`a%0`, `a/0`, `a%0u`, `a/0u`, a
`const int k = 0`, a namespace-scope `const`, an enumerator). Board **#781**.

**R6 held over 24 literal cells** — no *non-zero* literal divisor emits any trap,
for either sign, either signedness, `/` or `%`, including `INT_MIN`, `INT_MAX`,
the `simm16` cliff at 32768, and the same values reached through a `const` local,
a namespace-scope `const` and an enumerator. Seven distinct bodies come out of
that row: `addi ; divw ; mulli ; subf` for `%7`; `srawi ; addze ; rlwinm ; subf`
for `%2`; a single `addi` for `%1` and `%-1`; a bare `neg` for `/-1`;
`addis ; ori ; divw ; mullw ; subf` outside `simm16`; and the unconditional trap
above.

> The registered hunt was for a **non-zero** literal that keeps a guard. There is
> none in 24 cells, and saying so is worth more than a "clean grid" line — what
> the hunt found instead was a **new trap form** one column over. `a / -1` is the
> semantic cliff worth naming: with a literal `-1` `c2` emits a bare `neg`, which
> silently overflows on `INT_MIN`, while the same divisor arriving in a register
> traps. `c2` deletes the guard it can prove is the only reachable one, and the
> proof is wrong at exactly one input.

## 4. What shipped

Four constant bodies per optimization mode, 9 / 7 / 5 / 3 words, **no free
field at all**:

```text
  signed %                       signed /              unsigned %          unsigned /
    rotlwi r11,r3,1                rotlwi r11,r3,1       divwu r11,r3,r4     divwu r3,r3,r4
    divw   r10,r3,r4               divw   r3,r3,r4       twi   6,r4,0        twi   6,r4,0
    addi   r11,r11,-1              addi   r11,r11,-1     mullw r11,r11,r4    blr
    mullw  r10,r10,r4              twi    6,r4,0         subf  r3,r11,r3
    andc   r11,r4,r11              andc   r11,r4,r11     blr
    twi    6,r4,0                  twi    5,r11,-1
    subf   r3,r10,r3               blr
    twi    5,r11,-1
    blr
```

`/` is **not** `%` with a flag — it needs neither the `mullw` nor the `subf` —
and unsigned needs neither the three-instruction predicate nor the `twi 5` that
reads it, because `INT_MIN / -1` cannot arise. Four shapes, four lengths, and a
unit test asserts the lengths separately from the emitter so the two have to
agree.

**`Port=Match` on the first run**, at `/Ox` through `c2rs diff` and at `/O1`
through `c2rs gap`, with no adjustment.

### 4.1 Both modes ship, and `unsigned /` is the control

Board **#784**. `/Ox` emits the **same mnemonics in the same order** with a
different allocation — `mullw r8,r10,r4` against `/O1`'s `mullw r10,r10,r4`,
`andc r7,r4,r9` against `andc r11,r4,r11`. Three shapes move. **`unsigned /` is
byte-identical across the modes**, and that asymmetry is the control: a capture
artefact would not spare exactly one shape.

**This does not un-refute `OPT_MODE.md`'s register-field rule, and the pair is
the useful statement.** Board #763 stands — at `/Ox` the ptr-walk loop is a
different *body*, twenty-one words against twenty. The modes differ in a
**register field for a single-block leaf** and in the **body** once there is more
than one block. Two readings, two populations. That is why this file carries both
mode tables where `ptr_walk_loop` refuses outside `/O1`.

### 4.2 The refusals, each with a measured counterexample

Not one is conservatism; each names a cell where real `c2` emits something else:
a computed dividend (hoists the trap), a computed divisor (stays in the spine but
re-plans the registers), any literal divisor (seven other bodies), the operands
in the other order, a third formal, either operand twice, `short`/`char`/`uchar`
(which bracket the spine **and move the two traps adjacent**), `long long`
(a `divd`/`tdi` spine), a post-op, a local, a store, and two divisions in one
body (which batches **all four** traps together — a fifth placement).

**`long` and `unsigned long` are refused although `c2` emits byte-identical
code for them** (`IL_TYPE_TAGS.md` §3.1). `eat_int_like` would have taken all
four spellings for free; the two triples are matched literally instead, so **the
accepted set is exactly the graded set**. A unit test asserts the refusal, so a
later widening has to delete a test rather than relax a helper by accident.

Board **#782**. The consequence for whoever sizes the next rung: the payable
population is on the far side of exactly the thing this lane measured and

**plus** §2.1's rule made load-bearing — at which point clause (a) stops being a
description of five witnesses and has to be a model.

### 6.1 And 4,670 is an UPPER bound, for a reason worth naming precisely

Board **#783**. `expr-op-0x05` **cannot tell integer division from
floating-point division.** The key is a raw IL opcode byte rendered by
`Block::feature()` when `parse_expr_classed` has no arm — and it has no arm for
`0x05` at all. `IlOp::Div` is produced **only** inside `leaf_float.rs`'s own
local parse (grepped: four `IlOp::Div` sites in `c2-il`, all in that file), so an
FP division reaching the general expression parser renders the *same* key as an
integer one. The 1000× asymmetry between 4,670 and 4 is itself the evidence that
the two keys are not the same population.

> **The witness that would settle it costs one reader.** `census.rs` already
> captures a hexdump per blocking site (`FnCensus::hex`, `hex_mark`). Splitting
> the 4,670 on the operand TYPE triple immediately preceding the `05` —
> `86 41 74` / `86 42 75` against the float and double triples — partitions it
> exactly. Not done here: this lane shipped a leaf, and sizing the
> embedded-division rung is that rung's own first task.

## 9. Found and not taken

1. **`(a/c)%b` puts ALL FOUR traps adjacent** — `divw … twi twi twi twi subf` —
   a fifth placement, and definitive proof that no positional rule can hold. The
   grid's instrument **refuses to classify** two-division bodies rather than
   guessing (an earlier revision classified them off the *inner* division's
   divisor and printed a regime the note was not about).
2. **`a / -1` deletes a guard `a / b` keeps** (§3). A literal `-1` becomes a bare
   `neg`, which overflows silently on `INT_MIN`; the same value in a register
   traps. Not a defect to fix — it is what `c2` does — but it is the sharpest
   statement of what the two traps are *for*.
3. **The `short`/`char`/`uchar` widths are a bounded rung of their own**: the
   same spine bracketed by one `extsb`/`extsh`/`rlwinm` at each end, with the
   traps adjacent. Three widths × two operators × two signednesses, already
   captured in `work/w-divmod/run1.txt`.
4. **`long long` is a `divd` / `tdi` spine** with the same predicate shape, and
   `tdi` needs an encoder this lane did not write.

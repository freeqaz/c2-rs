## 3. The fold — 8 of 8, and why it is byte-neutral by construction

The obstacle was never the algebra. It was that four of the eight are `const
u32` items consumed from `coff/ehscope.rs` (outside this lane's fence), so their
**type and name had to survive**, and `mop::encode_op` is fallible and allocates
its error message — it cannot run in a `const` initialiser.

So the composition was split out and made const-evaluable:

- `mop::compose(base, &FieldPlan, &MachineOp) -> u32` — a `const fn` holding
  the field-placement loop. `encode_op`'s default path now **calls it**, so the
  compile-time word and the run-time word come from one function rather than
  two that agree. `plan`, `slot_of` and `mask_of` became `const fn` unchanged.
- `mop::const_word(MachineOp) -> u32` — row lookup + `compose`, at compile time.
  It takes **no `EncodeParams`**: a non-default parameter set is an instrument
  state, and an instrument state may not reach a `const` on the emit path
  (`rungs/README.md`'s decision-surface clause — grading is at the default and
  nowhere else).
- `const OPCODE_ROWS` backs `static OPCODES`. One list of literals, two names.
  `OPCODES` **stays a `static`** deliberately: `EncodeParams::row`'s O(1) fast
  path compares `self.rows.as_ptr()` against `OPCODES.as_ptr()`, and a `const`
  may be promoted to a different allocation per use site — which would have
  silently demoted every emit to the 85-row linear scan `w-s1` measured at
  **+10.67 % mean port time per obj** (#3336). A cost regression with a zero
  byte delta is exactly what that row is about.

`const_word_and_encode_op_agree` runs both entry points over every row and seven
slot patterns (588 pairs) and requires equality.

**`calls.rs:36` was PREREG P3's named risk and the risk was wrong.** The old rule
`disp & 0x03FF_FFFC` and form 6's `((disp >> 2) & 0xFFFFFF) << 2` are not merely
equal on the displacements that occur: they are the **same function on all of
`i32`**, because the mask's low two bits are zero (so both drop them) and its
24-bit width discards the sign extension the arithmetic shift introduces.
`mop.rs`'s own `DispWord` doc warns that a `>>` and a `&` "coincide only while
the displacement is a multiple of 4" — true of the *general* pair, and not of
this one, because the field is masked to 24 bits *before* the shift back. The
pin in `word_seam` checks it over 16,386 displacements including misaligned
ones.

`frame.rs:63` was the second-named risk and also folded cleanly: form 62 splits
the SPR five-and-five in the **arm**, so `mtspr`'s base word carries the field at
zero, and `mop_mtlr(12)` composes `0x7D8803A6` exactly. A new
`encode_mtlr`/`mop_mtlr` pair carries it — the port has always *emitted*
`mtlr r12` and never had an encoder for it.

## 4. The three refusals, priced two-sided

None of the three is a duplicate. `mop::OPCODES` has **no row** for `bl`,
`mfspr` or `stwux`, so nothing in this port can compose their words and **there
is no second rule to disagree with**. The fence is a missing transcription, not
a conflict.

| site | c2's row (`ref/ENCODE_OPCODES.txt`) | cost of folding | cost of leaving |
|---|---|---|---|
| `encode_call_branch` (`bl`) | `0x002b`, base `48000001`, **form 7**, arm `10bfa285` | 1 `OPCODES` row + **form 7's placement**, which is not among the 27 arms `w-read-r2` transcribed. Both are **adoptions**: they move `DISCLOSURE.md`'s `W-MOP-2` (85 rows) and `W-MOP-3` (27 arms) counts, and `DISCLOSURE.md` is `w-provaudit`'s fence this wave | one literal `0x4800_0000` in one function, composing a word no table row can reach. Risk is **future**: the day `bl`'s row lands, it becomes a real duplicate |
| `FRAME_MFLR_R12` (`mfspr`) | `0x00e6`, base `7c0002a6`, **form 54**, arm `10bfa76a` | same, plus form 54 is a *different form from `mtspr`* — `mtspr` (62) cannot stand in | one literal, three consumers |
| `FRAME_STWUX` (`stwux`) | `0x017f`, base `7c00016e`, **form 61** | **cheapest of the three: 1 row, NO new arm** — the port already plans form 61 (it emits `stdx`). Still an adoption that moves `W-MOP-2` | one literal, one consumer, on a shape `FrameLayout` refuses |

**The refusal is armed rather than merely recorded, and that is the part worth
having.** `word_seam`'s `no_inventoried_word_is_one_mop_can_compose` *runs* each
site every test run, takes the word it really emits, and asserts the table still
cannot compose it. Planting `bl`'s row into `OPCODES` turns it red with the
message *"THE REFUSAL … HAS EXPIRED — and that is good news"* (transcript, §5).
A refusal that notices when its own reason expires is worth more than the fold
it postponed.

## 5. The control, and watching it fail

`crates/c2-core/src/codegen/word_seam.rs`. Content is entirely `#[cfg(test)]`,
so it grades the crate, never runs in a build, and is invisible to
`scripts/provenance_census.py`, whose population is non-test code.

**The discriminator, registered in PREREG § P5 before the fold** so it cannot
have been back-fitted:

> A live word production outside the `mop` seam is a **DUPLICATE** iff the word
> it emits lies in the image of `encode_op` over some `OPCODES` row at the
> default `EncodeParams::C2`. It is **LEGITIMATE** iff no row can compose it.
> Decided by masking off every bit `plan(form)` places and comparing the
> remainder against the row's base word — a **superset** of the true image, so
> it over-reports toward RED and a LEGITIMATE claim must survive the generous
> test.

That one rule is what separates `encode_tail_branch` from `encode_call_branch`:
form 6's `DispWord` field lands at shift 2, so the low two bits stay **outside**
the mask, and `bl`'s bit 0 therefore fails the comparison. A
primary-opcode-only discriminator would have called `bl` a duplicate (P4
predicted this and it held).

**Two halves, because neither alone is the class:**

| half | scope | catches |
|---|---|---|
| **value** — `no_word_is_composed_outside_the_mop_seam` | `codegen/` only | a word spelled as a literal that `mop` already composes |
| **structural** — `every_big_endian_emission_is_inventoried` | whole crate | a *new* `to_be_bytes` site, however the word was built |

**The value half's scope is an evidence decision, not taste.** Crate-wide, **111
of 122** live 32-bit literals under `crates/c2-core/src` are "coverable" — and
almost all of them are COFF section characteristics (`CH_TEXT = 0x6040_0020`
decodes as an `ori`, `CH_PDATA = 0x4040_0040` as a `bc`). A value rule over
`coff/` is noise, and a control that cries wolf gets deleted. `codegen/` holds
**8** live 32-bit literals and exactly **1** is coverable (`encode_call_branch`,
inventoried).

### Watched failing — three times, and the first one was not planted

**0. Its own first run, before any plant.** `code_only` had no raw-string arm,
so this file's own `r#"…"#` fixture leaked its braces, `cfg_test_spans` closed
the enclosing `#[cfg(test)] mod seam` early, and the scanner reported **its own
fixture** as a live second producer. A real bug in the control, found by the
control, on run one. Fixed with `raw_string_hashes`; the arm carries the story.

**1. A planted eighth producer in `frame.rs`** (`work/w-mopfold/planted_red.txt`)
— one literal `sth` word and one emitter composed from a *variable*:

```
A SECOND PRODUCER OF AN INSTRUCTION WORD APPEARED.
  codegen/frame.rs:121  0xb181fff8  in `PLANTED_STH`  — mop::OPCODES already
  composes this word as `sth`
...
THE BIG-ENDIAN EMISSION INVENTORY IS STALE.
  NEW      codegen/frame.rs::planted_emitter  x1
```

The value half caught the literal; the structural half caught the
variable-composed emitter, which the value half is **blind** to. Different
defects, one each — which is the argument for two halves, made by the halves.
Reverted; green.

**2. A planted `bl` row in `OPCODES`** (`work/w-mopfold/planted_red_2.txt`) — the
armed refusal fired, and so did its own negative control:

```
THE REFUSAL ON codegen/calls.rs::encode_call_branch HAS EXPIRED — and that is
good news. ... `mop::OPCODES` can now compose 0x4bfffff5 as `bl`.
coverable() flagged the LEGITIMATE bl — it is saying yes to everything
```

Reverted; green.

**Armed self-tests ship with it** (`the_value_half_can_fail`,
`the_structural_half_can_fail`): they fabricate the defect in-process and
require the predicate to find it, and they check the *negative* side too — the
discriminator must not flag the three legitimate words, or it says yes to
everything and every green above is a green over nothing.

**Vacuity floors, because absence reads as success here:** ≥ 45 source files
walked, ≥ 5 literals scanned, ≥ 15 emission sites found, the seam
(`mop.rs::word`) present in the scan, and the one exemption's anchors
(`const OPCODE_ROWS` and its closing `];`) asserted to exist — an exemption
whose anchor has vanished exempts either nothing or everything, and both look
like a pass.

### What it cannot see — stated, because an unstated bound is a lie

1. A word composed with no literal at all and emitted through an **existing**
   inventoried site. Both halves blind. Nothing in the tree does this.
2. A word composed inside `coff/` from a literal — the value half does not run
   there.
3. `#[cfg(test)]` code, deliberately: `encode.rs`'s `mod incumbent` is a second
   producer *on purpose* with an armed cross-check, and it is what made
   `w-s1`'s required-zero byte delta provable in the portable lane.

## 6. Deliverable 3 — the pledges, and a fourth site `#3638` missed

**It is four sites, and the sharper fact is that two independent enumerations
each found three — different threes.**

| enumeration | named | missed |
|---|---|---|
| `#3638` (`w-encmap`) | `mop.rs:38-39`, `mop.rs:111`, `encode.rs:36` | **`mop.rs:680`** |
| `#3654` (coordinator's confirming grep) | `mop.rs:128`, **`mop.rs:680`**, `encode.rs:36` | **the module-doc site** |
| this lane | `mop.rs:53-54`, `mop.rs:127-128`, `mop.rs:680`, `encode.rs:36` | — |

The union is **four**. Neither prior pass was careless: they fail in opposite
directions for one reason. `mop.rs:53-54` and `127-128` **wrap the sentence
across a `//!` line break** (`…the port's *only* source of a
//! primary
opcode`), so a plain `grep` for the phrase cannot see them — that is what the
coordinator's grep missed. `mop.rs:680` is the only site where the sentence fits
on one line, which is presumably why the grep found it and a reading pass did
not. **A phrase that wraps is invisible to `grep` and a phrase that does not is
invisible to a skim**, and this claim happens to exist in both shapes.

Both line numbers `#3638` gave for `mop.rs` are also **stale** — `w-disclose`'s
`#3643` insertion moved the pair down by fifteen lines.

`mop.rs:680` is the doc comment on **`base_word` itself** — the site a reader is
likeliest to take at face value and least likely to check for a qualifier.

**After the fold the claim is STILL FALSE, and the repair says so.** `bl`,
`mfspr` and `stwux` remain, each sourcing a primary from a literal. All four
sites are struck in place and corrected to:

> `base_word` is the port's only source of a primary opcode **for instructions
> that go through `MachineOp`** — three the port emits are outside the table,
> and `word_seam` names them.

**Why the weaker sentence is the better one:** the strong claim was
unfalsifiable in the only way that matters — no test could express it, so it
stayed wrong for four days across two files *while being quoted as a reason*
(`DISCLOSURE.md`'s `W-MOP-1` closing note quotes it). The weak claim is
**checked**. A module doc on the emit path has now been wrong about its own
provenance twice (`#3632`: asserted a ledger row that did not exist; `#3638`:
asserted an exclusivity that did not hold) and both times the detector was a
lane reading it for another purpose.

## 9. Found and deliberately not taken

- **Transcribing `bl`, `mfspr`, `stwux` into `OPCODES`.** §4's table is the
  price. It is an adoption and `DISCLOSURE.md` was another lane's fence this
  wave. `stwux` is the cheap one (one row, no new arm) and is the natural first
  move for whoever takes it.
- **The value half over `coff/`.** 111 of 122 coverable; it would be noise. If
  it is ever wanted, the discriminator needs a second input — "is this value
  ever emitted big-endian into `.text`" — which is a dataflow question this
  scanner cannot answer.
- **A word composed entirely from variables into an existing emission site.**
  The one hole both halves share. Closing it needs a real dataflow pass or a
  newtype (`Word(u32)`) that only `mop` can mint; the newtype is the cheaper
  design and it is a construct rung of its own.
- **`mop.rs`'s module doc still says `encode.rs` "was 85 hand-written
  functions".** True as history. `encode.rs` is now **86** encoders and the
  population control pins that number; the two 85s were never the same
  population (`mtlr` and `mtctr` are two encoders over one `MTSPR` row) and the
  pin now says so.
- **`docs/whitebox/ref/P_ENCODE.md` §10.5's account of `#3637`** still carries
  "seven". Not this lane's fence; named for whoever owns that page.

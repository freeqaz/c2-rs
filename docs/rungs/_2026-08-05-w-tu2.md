## 4. The label-counter inter-function stride — a constant, on 36 cells

This is R3's miss and the lane's transferable result. `work/w-tu2/labelgrid.py`
(17 cells) and `work/w-tu2/stride.py` (36 cells), both through **real c2** at the
workload's own `/O1 /Oi /EHsc /GR`.

**Method, self-normalizing so the TU-level seed drops out.** Each cell is one TU:
a probe function whose interior control flow varies, then a *fixed* framed
function `int Z(int x){ return zz(x)+1; }`. A framed function contributes exactly
three symbol-table slots (`$M` at prologue end, `$M` at function end, `$T` on its
`.pdata`), so the measured quantity is `first($M of Z) − first($M of probe)`.

> ### **The stride is `5 + 1·(leaf or tail-call functions between) + 5·(framed functions between)`, and the probe's own interior control flow does not enter it at all.** **36 of 36 agree, 0 disagree**, with no free parameter. A framed function charges **5** — its 3 emitted labels plus **2 that are never emitted**; a leaf stub or a tail-call function charges **1**.

Measured invariant across twelve distinct interior shapes — `straight`,
`twocall`, `if1_ret`, `if2_ret`, `if3_ret`, `if1_join`, `ifelse`, `if_store`,
`call_test`, `call_test2`, `while`, `dowhile`, `ternary`, `and`, `or` — of which
`if3_ret`, `if_store`, `call_test2`, `and`, `or` and `stub5` were **held out
before any fit was attempted** (R7).

### 4.1 It predicts `mmio`'s own gaps, out of sample

`mmio`'s six labels are `$M3381,$M3382,$T3383` · `$M3386,$M3387,$T3388` ·
`$M3396,$M3397,$T3398`.

| gap | between them, in source order | predicted | **observed** |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `mmioGetInfo` → `mmioSetInfo` | nothing | `5` | **5** |
| `mmioSetInfo` → `mmioClose` | 5 leaf stubs | `5 + 5·1 = 10` | **10** |

Both exact. **The `+3` and `+8` I registered as underivable in the prereg are
fully accounted for**: the "extra" 2 is every framed function's own unemitted
pair, and the 7 is that 2 plus one slot per intervening stub.

### 4.2 What this does and does NOT do to #286/#287 — the qualifier matters

**It does not overturn #286.** w-label's result was about a *different quantity*
and stands. My grid shows where the two separate:

* **The INTER-function stride** — how far the counter advances from one framed
  function's labels to the next — is a **constant**, blind to interior control
  flow. **That is the part `mmio` needed and it is now measured.**
* **The INTRA-function charge** — where a function's *own* labels start relative
  to the `.gl` seed — **does** vary with interior control flow, and my own grid
  reproduces w-label's variation: relative to `straight`, `if1_ret` +1,
  `if2_ret` +2, `if3_ret` +3, `ifelse` +3, `dowhile` +4, `while` +5, `and` +1,
  `or` +2, `ternary` +2. **`ifelse` at +3 with one `if`/`else` against `if3_ret`
  at +3 with three `if`s is the cell that kills the obvious rule**, exactly as
  #286 records. That half is **still open**.

> **So the honest statement is that #286 is NARROWED, not closed.** A rung that
> needs to place a *later* function's labels no longer owes a new IL channel; a
> rung that needs to place a *control-flow-bearing* function's own labels still
> does. `mmio` needs both, which is why the stride result does not convert it.

### 4.3 A defect in my own generator, recorded rather than quietly fixed

The stride grid's first run read **16 agree / 12 DISAGREE**, every disagreement in
a cell whose "middle" was supposed to be a framed function. The rule was right and
**the generator was wrong**: `int f0(int x){ return h(x)+0; }` is emitted by c2 as
a **tail call**, not a framed function — no frame, no `$M`, charge 1 not 5. With
the index started at 1 the grid reads 36/36.

**This is the single-cell trap with the cells generated by the experimenter**, and
it would have been reported as a refutation of a correct rule. What caught it was
that the disagreements were *exactly* the framed-middle cells and the residuals
were *exactly* 4 per supposed frame — a structured error, not noise. A grid whose
failures are structured is telling you about your instrument.

## 6. `mmio`'s re-price — 17, and what it is actually blocked on

Counted off the three blocked bodies over and above the framed shape the port
already has. Six are selection/allocation/scheduling facts, §4.3's declared blind
spot — the **third** independent cross-check of the reprice table and the **third**
to come back dearer (w-dclass `negate_test` 10 vs 4, w-tu1 `xboxmem` 15 vs 4,
this lane `mmio` **17 vs 5**).

The load-bearing ones, none of which the blocker key `expr-cmp-eq` names:

1. a **materialized common epilogue** block reached by `b`, with early-return
   values (`li r3,5`, `li r3,11`) merging into it — all three bodies;
2. `std r31,-16(r1)` / `ld r31,-16(r1)`, and the *decision* to hold a formal in a
   callee-saved register across a call;
3. a **second CR-field regime** — `cmplwi cr0` + `bne cr0` in `mmioClose` beside
   `cmplwi cr6` elsewhere, and which one c2 picks;
4. member load/store at an offset (`lwz r11,28(r31)`, `stw r11,32(r31)`) and
   `cmplw` register-vs-register;
5. an **intra-TU REL24** (`bl mmioFlush`) — the target is a COMDAT in this obj,
   where every REL24 the port emits today goes to an external;
6. an indirect call `lwz r11,8(r31)` / `mtctr` / `bcctrl`;
7. the intra-function label charge of §4.2 — **#286, open**.

> **Board #198's ranking complaint is confirmed a fourth time.** `mmio` is ranked
> the frontier's best target by blocked-function count (3 of 11) and by #465's
> already-emitted count (8 of 11), and it needs a CFG step the port does not have.
> **w-cfgimpl's item B3 called this in advance and was right** — its list
> (frames, `.pdata`, `$M` pairs, member load/store, `cmplw`, a materialized
> epilogue, `mtctr`/`bcctrl`) reproduces from the obj almost verbatim. One
> correction to it stands, already on the board as #410: `mmio`'s `memcpy` is an
> ordinary REL24 `bl`, **not** an inlined intrinsic.

## 3. The exhaustive negative, run FIRST — and the fourth consecutive lane whose answer was outside the class

The prereg §3 class is every "sort the producers on their own features" answer:
a lexicographic key of up to 3 signed features over 10, applied as a stable
sort with first-use as the base order, **8,420 configurations**, scored on FIT
**conditional on the observed store order** so that a wrong store order cannot
contaminate the quantity #582 asks for.

```
FIT cells with >= 2 producers : 2604  (multi-symbol 2461)
TOP of the class              : 2580 / 2604 (99.1%)   +firstcons
SYMRANK        (+grank_min)   : 2107 / 2604 (80.9%)
ORDER rank     (-count,+fu)   : 2070 / 2604 (79.5%)
```

**The residual named the mechanism.** All 24 misses are three-producer cells,
and 16 of them emit the *rank* order — the shape where two producers share a
symbol group and their rank disagrees with their consumption order. That is an
**insertion/merge**, not a sort, exactly as `w-sched`'s 13,104 list schedulers
and `w-order2`'s 1,048,576 release times pointed outside themselves.

**So I built the merge, and the merge lost too.** `SYMMERGE` — each producer
belongs to the symbol group of its first consumption, within a group the
restricted global rank is a *precedence constraint*, and subject to that the
earliest consumption goes first — reduces to `ORDER` on one symbol by
construction and covers every cell I had hand-checked. It scores **96.4 % fit /
93.1 % holdout** and **is beaten by the plain case split**. It dies on
`x_split`, where both producers live entirely inside one symbol group and still
come out in consumption order. Three more variants (`QUEUE-G`, `QUEUE-L`,
`SYMMERGE-L`) are in `work/w-sym/model.py` and all four are worse. **Recorded
because the negative is the finding**: #582's two halves are not two views of
one mechanism, and I could not make them one.

## 4. What IS shipped

`docs/SYMBOL.md` is the write-up. In one paragraph:

* **The pin** (§1) is exact, 7,589/7,589, model-free. The store order can only
  permute *within* a symbol group.
* **The store order** (§2) is `ORDER`'s walk with the group rank, the pin, and
  one deletion: `w-sched` rule 1's *"if every remaining store is blocked,
  source order wins"* fallback is replaced by **lowering `u`**. Exact at ≤ 2
  producers — **1867/1867 fit, 1501/1501 holdout, 16/16 external, 0 wrong.**
* **The producer order** (§3) is the case split, exact in the same domain.
* **The layout** (§4) is **not modelled**, so `schedule()` still refuses.

`order.rs` has **one walk for both regimes**, which makes the reduction to
`ORDER` the code rather than a claim beside it: every single-symbol cell
`ORDER` was fitted on goes through the new code unchanged, and the 5,460-run
enumerating test still says the relaxation never fires there.

**The change is additive-refusal by construction.** `is_source_order` returning
`Some(false)` is a refusal; the pre-existing `schedule` guard runs
independently and is untouched. A wrong answer from the new path can only fail
to add a refusal — it can never turn a refusal into an accept. That is why the
scan is baseline-identical and why seventeen refuted rules have produced zero
wrong objs.

## 5. Three disclosures

**The grid was extended after the v1 residual was read.** Tiers A–F all force
≥ 2 symbols and tier S stops at 4 statements with no filler, so `xboxheap`'s
word **through one symbol** — the 35 cells that refute the first-consumer rule
— was in **no partition of the v1 grid**. That is precisely the trap the brief
names (#581; `w-sched`'s 184 conflicted cells), and I walked into it: the first
breakdown reported rank and first-consumption as *tied at 100 %* on
single-symbol cells because the population could not contain the counterexample.
Tier P (637 single-symbol cells with fillers) was added, the preregistered
partition rule was applied to it unchanged, and every number on this page is
from the extended grid.

**`SYMMERGE` and `SYMORDER-U` were found by hand-checking cells from FIT before
being scored on it.** They are *fitted* rules, not preregistered ones, and they
are labelled that way. `SYMORDER-U` earns its place on the holdout (1501/1501
in domain) and on the external cells; `SYMMERGE` does not and is not shipped.

**I first published the pin as `7,573 of 7,573` and it is `7,589 of 7,589`.**
The first number was fit + holdout; the 16 external cells satisfy the pin too
and I had not counted them. The wrong count is left here beside the right one
because a number whose population is unstated is the failure mode this project
keeps paying for — *every number names the population it is over*. Corrected in
`4e1d65b`, one commit after it was published.

## 6. The new wall, named precisely

`schedule()` returns the full interleaved sequence and an emitter needs it.
`ORDER`'s layout clause with #584's leading-run `u` is 1866/1867 fit and
1644/1645 holdout at two producers — and **12 of 16 external**, where the four
misses are one family:

```
 syms 000011   P0 · stw m0 · P1 · stw m1 · stw m2 · stw m3 · stw e4 · stw e5
 syms 001011   P0 · stw m0 · stw m1 · P1 · stw e2 · stw m3 · stw e4 · stw e5
```

Same statements, same store order, same producer order, same registers; **one
store moved to the other symbol and the second producer lands one slot later.**
Board **#602**. It is the next thing between the port and any multi-symbol
store-run emitter.

## 1. The one-paragraph answer

**There was no disagreement.** The `4` is a dated revision of the `8`
(2026-08-21 supersedes 2026-08-15) and `P_REGALLOC` §7 still said `8` because
the commit that edited §7 two days *after* the revision struck a different
bullet and left this one standing. That is a doc-maintenance defect, found
without opening the image.

**The read is the finding.** Both numbers rest on one enumeration —
`WB_ITEMF_FINDINGS.md` §6.1's eight named sub-items — and its scope is
measurably too small. Downstream of the register allocator the pipeline is
**7 stage drivers, 34 depth-1 passes, 10,714 B, 17 TUs**, with **27 of 34
mentioned by no document in this repository**. F0's eight sub-items name **one**
of the 34, and stage **S7 (`0x10b7e032`)** — a live merger run, a direct tuple
splicer that is another read's named open question, and the emit walk — has no
sub-item at all.

> **F0 ≥ 10 raw sub-lanes with two UNPRICED terms. Both published figures are
> floors.** And the number is not the deliverable: §6 of the findings is, because
> it says which parts are priced, which are UNPRICED, and against what
> denominator.

## 4. The two MISSES, which are the result worth carrying

`rungs/2026-08-15-itemfprice.md` §10 item 4, of the lowering band: *"**Reading
their sizes is a `grep` away and would tighten F0's 8.** Not taken: it is F0's
work, not a pricing lane's"*, and §2: *"**If any of the three is large, F0 is
larger.**"*

**None of the three is large — 425 B, the smallest stage in the pipeline — and
the inference is inverted.** The three addresses hold **zero transformation
logic**; they are pass drivers, and they drive **15 depth-1 passes / 3,101 B /
11 TUs** (11 live at `/O1 /EHsc`, 2,684 B, 9 TUs). The proposed `grep` returns
"small" and would have tightened F0 **downward**.

**This lane made the same mistake in its own prereg.** P3 bet *"> 2,000 B"*
(0.55) and P4 bet *"> 40 callees"* (0.50); the answers are **425 B** and **15**.
Both are honest MISSES. **A driver's byte size is anti-correlated with the work
it drives**, so size was not merely a weak instrument — it pointed the wrong
way. Only §5's enumeration discipline caught it. Fifth instance of `#3505`'s
family, and the first where the *lane's own preregistered instrument* was the
artifact.

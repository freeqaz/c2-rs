## 0. The one-paragraph answer

**The direction is UP, the magnitude does not move, and the reason the magnitude
does not move is the finding.** None of the three inputs wave 11 corrected was
ever an input to any published figure: `1.5–4.5 eng-mo` is the arch review's
top-down `3–9` for the pair halved by ordering, `7.5–22.5` and `15–45` are that
× 5, and the bottom-up `31–59` is `n_slices × flat 2–4 wk × 5` in which a
slice's mass never appears. **A figure whose inputs cannot be corrected cannot
be re-priced — it can only be withdrawn, or left standing with its derivation
printed.** All four are left standing, with their derivations printed for the
first time. What *did* move is the **denominator**: on the strength a general
decode is defined by, the residue is **50.5× larger in bodies and 25.1× larger
by byte** than the published `98.2 %` implied. Two upward terms appear in no
published price at all — the ~5:1 calibration is outside its fitted domain
while three *executed* rows missed **pessimistic** by 30×–1,200× on the unit
actually spent, and **I1's output is structurally unobservable**, which makes
4a(i) funded alone the unconsumable instrument row 4a exists to prevent.

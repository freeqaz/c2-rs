## 0. PREREGISTRATION (written before a single obj was decoded)

This section is committed **before** the corpus build finishes, so what follows
in §1 can refute it. `docs/PRIOR_ART.md` records the finding this lane acts on:
`gimli-rs/object`'s `src/pe.rs` carries the complete 23-row `IMAGE_REL_PPC_*`
table plus `TYPEMASK` and four modifier bits, establishing that COFF's `Type`
field is a **packed word, not an enum**. The lane's job is to port the table
(std-only, hand-written, `object` cited — never a dependency) and then to ask the
corpus whether the packed-word concern is live here or theoretical.

**What we know before measuring.** `crates/c2-core/src/coff/reloc.rs` held five
constants — `ADDR32 0x02`, `REL24 0x06`, `REFHI 0x10`, `REFLO 0x11`,
`PAIR 0x12` — and `RELOC_LEN = 10`. Those five are the writer's entire
vocabulary. **There is no relocation *reader* anywhere in the workspace**:
`crates/c2-obj` compares whole objs byte-for-byte and decodes section names and
COMDAT leaders but has never looked at a relocation table, and
`c2-core`'s `layout_sections` only steps *over* the records
(`cursor += n * RELOC_LEN`). So the honest statement of the prior is: we knew 5
of 23 types, we recognised no flag bit at all, and we could not decode a
reference obj's relocations to say what it contained.

**Registered predictions.**

| # | prediction | what would refute it |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | The corpus contains **6–9 distinct base types**. Named: `ADDR32`, `REL24`, `REFHI`, `REFLO`, `PAIR`, `SECREL 0x0B`, `SECTION 0x0C` (the last two from `.debug$S`); `ADDR32NB` possible | any count outside 6–9, or a type outside that named set |
| P2 | **Zero records carry a modifier bit.** `NEG` and `TOCDEFN` belong to the AIX/PowerOpen TOC model, which the Xbox 360 ABI does not use; `BRTAKEN`/`BRNTAKEN` are branch-prediction hints that MSVC's PPC backend is not known to emit. **So I expect the packed-word concern to be *theoretical on this corpus*** — and I register that in advance rather than discovering it and calling it a result | any record with `Type & 0xFF00 != 0` |
| P3 | **Every `PAIR` has `sym == 0`**, and 100 % of them, not "almost all" | a single `PAIR` with a nonzero `SymbolTableIndex` |
| P4 | Every `REFHI` and every `REFLO` is immediately followed by a `PAIR` at the same `VirtualAddress`; no other type takes one | a bare `REFHI`/`REFLO`, or a `PAIR` after anything else |
| P5 | **TU match moves by 0** and the port's emitted bytes move by 0. Factor A is the binding constraint at 28/871 and a relocation *reader* does not touch it | any change in the gate's per-lane counts |

**P5 is not a hedge, it is the point.** This lane's value is the correctness of
*reads* and the diagnostic power that follows; it is registered as a point mass
at zero on the payoff metric, and if the numbers move at all that is an alarm,
not a win.

**What would make me decline.** If P2 holds *and* nothing else in the table
appears in the corpus, then the only defensible product is the reader plus the
table, and I will say the packed-word finding is real-in-the-format and
inert-in-this-workload rather than dressing it up. I will not add a
speculative decoder for types the corpus does not contain beyond the table
itself, and I will not touch the emitter.

## 2. What we knew before, stated exactly

`crates/c2-core/src/coff/reloc.rs` was 24 lines: `RELOC_LEN = 10` and five `u16`
constants (`ADDR32 0x02`, `REL24 0x06`, `REFHI 0x10`, `REFLO 0x11`,
`PAIR 0x12`). Answering the brief's four questions against that file:

| question | answer at `c303ad0` |
|---|---|
| which types do we **recognise**? | those five, and nothing else — 5 of 23 |
| which do we **emit**? | the same five. Recognise and emit were the same set |
| which do we **refuse** on? | none — there was nothing to refuse *with* |
| is `Type` an enum or a packed word? | **neither, operationally.** The constants were bare `u16`s written into `Buf::u16` by `writer.rs`/`dyninit.rs` and compared as whole words in `coff/tests.rs`; no mask existed, and nothing ever *read* a `Type` word off a reference obj |

That last row is the crux the brief names, and it needs care. The whole-word
comparisons in `coff/tests.rs` are over records **the port itself just wrote**,
where the high byte is zero by construction — so they were never wrong, and they
were never a decoder either. The real gap was one level up: **there was no
relocation reader in the workspace at all.** `crates/c2-obj` decodes section
names, COMDAT leaders and section data and compares whole objs byte-for-byte;
`c2-core::layout_sections` steps *over* relocation tables
(`cursor += n * RELOC_LEN`) without looking at one. So the latent-wrong-read
class the brief worried about did not exist, for the unflattering reason that the
capability did not exist.

## 3. What landed

* **`crates/c2-obj/src/reloc.rs`** (new). The complete 23-row table, contiguous
  `0x00..=0x16`, plus `IMAGE_REL_PPC_TYPEMASK`, the four modifier bits and a
  derived `IMAGE_REL_PPC_FLAGMASK`. `reloc_type_name` returns `Option` — a value
  with no row is a *finding*, so there is no fallback string to hide it.
  `Reloc` keeps `ty` **raw and packed** and routes every derived question through
  `base()` / `flags()` / `unknown_bits()` / `sym_is_an_index()`, so no caller can
  reintroduce the whole-word compare. Provenance is in the module header:
  `gimli-rs/object`, `src/pe.rs` (Apache-2.0 OR MIT), which is the only public
  source for `TOCREL16 0x08`, `TOCREL14 0x09`, `IFGLUE 0x0D` and `IMGLUE 0x0E`;
  PE/COFF **rev 6.0** for `SECRELHI 0x14` and for `PAIR`'s displacement rule.
  **`object` is read, never linked** — the workspace stays std-only, zero
  external crates.
* **`ObjImage::relocations()`** — fail-closed exactly like the section and COMDAT
  walks (`None`, never a short list), and it handles
  `IMAGE_SCN_LNK_NRELOC_OVFL`. The overflow path is written because it is a real
  COFF rule, not because the corpus needs it; see §7.
* **`crates/c2-core/src/coff/reloc.rs`** now *re-exports* those five values
  instead of defining them, so the emitter and the decoder cannot drift onto two
  vocabularies. A test in that file pins all five against the literals the file
  held before the port, written out rather than referenced — it cannot be
  satisfied by the table it is checking.
* **`crates/c2-obj/tests/reloc_corpus.rs`** — the corpus instrument. Every
  assertion is a *positive* claim (a positive obj count, a positive record count,
  zero refusals, zero out-of-table types, zero out-of-range symbol indices), so
  it cannot pass by decoding nothing. **Set-but-empty is a FAILURE, not a skip**:
  the first run printed `SKIP: no .obj under work/w-reloc/objs` with 871 objs on
  disk, because `cargo test` runs from the *crate* directory and the path was
  relative. That is trap 5 firing inside the lane whose brief warns about it, and
  the guard is now an assert with the fix in its message.

No emitter, no fixture, no codegen. Nothing in `scripts/`, `fixtures/`,
`crates/c2-harness`, `crates/c2-reference`, `crates/c2-il` or the rest of
`crates/c2-core/coff/` was touched.

## 6. The two specific claims, answered

### (a) `Type` is a packed word — TRUE of the format, and THEORETICAL here

**No record in the corpus has a modifier bit set: 0 of 1,819,168.** The high byte
is zero everywhere, so `NEG`, `BRTAKEN`, `BRNTAKEN` and `TOCDEFN` are unexercised
and I cannot show a real reloc with a flag bit set. **There is therefore no
latent class of wrong reads in this workload today** — and I am saying that
plainly rather than shipping the alarm the brief hypothesised, because the number
does not support it.

Two reasons that is still worth the port rather than a shrug:

1. **It was registered in advance** (§0, P2) with the mechanism — `NEG`/`TOCDEFN`
   belong to the AIX/PowerOpen TOC model the Xbox 360 ABI does not use, and
   `BRTAKEN`/`BRNTAKEN` are hints MSVC's PPC backend is not known to emit. So
   "zero" is a confirmed prediction, not a discovery reframed as one.
2. **The counterfactual makes the claim falsifiable rather than merely true.**
   §7's BREAK 2 removes the mask in `Reloc::base()` — the exact enum-reading
   defect — and the corpus test **stays green**, because on a corpus with no flag
   bit masking and not masking are the same function. It is the *unit* test that
   fires. A reader who wants to know whether the packed-word concern is live here
   can read that pair of results and get a definite answer instead of an
   assurance.

The scope of "here" is one workload at one flag set. A different profile (`/Zi`,
a linked `.lib` from another toolchain, glue-bearing objs) can put bits in that
byte, and the reader now handles it by construction instead of by luck.

### (b) every `PAIR` has `sym = 0` — TRUE, over 540,521 of them

| | count |
|---|---:|
| `PAIR` records examined | **540,521** |
| …with `SymbolTableIndex != 0` | **0** |
| …following something other than a `REFHI`/`REFLO`/`SECRELHI`/`SECRELLO` | **0** |
| `REFHI` + `REFLO` records | **540,521** |
| …followed by a `PAIR` at the same `VirtualAddress` **and** section | **540,521** |

The last two lines are the same number as the first: the `HI`/`LO` population and
the `PAIR` population are in exact 1:1 correspondence, matched by position, VA and
section. `PAIR`'s field is a displacement (rev 6.0), it is always the zero
displacement in this workload, and **nothing may validate it as an index** —
`Reloc::sym_is_an_index()` is the single place that rule lives, and it is the only
type for which it returns `false`. The independent control that this is a real
property and not a mis-decode: over the **1,278,647** non-`PAIR` records, whose
`sym` genuinely is an index, **0** carry a value past that obj's
`NumberOfSymbols`. A misaligned 10-byte stride would scatter those across the
whole `u32` range.

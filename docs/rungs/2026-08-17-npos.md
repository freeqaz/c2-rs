## What it admits, and what it refuses

**Admits** (`IlBundle::provide_data_tu` → `coff::emit_provide_data_obj`): a TU
whose obj is the four-section shell plus one `Selection=2` COMDAT data section
per **provide-always** `.gl` data record — attr byte exactly `0xE0`, linkage
defined-external, `.rdata` on the record's `00 04` read-only pair and `.data`
on `00 02`, content from `.in` at exactly the record's size, witnessed widths
1/2/4/8 with the measured alignment-nibble table {1→1, 2→3, 4→3, 8→4} (the
u16 cell is ALIGN_4, **not** ALIGN_2 — the cell that separates the table from
the natural-alignment identity), aux CheckSum = init-0 reflected CRC-32 of the
content (verified 8/8 against c2's own objs), EXTERNAL symbol, declaration
(token-ascending) order — and whose **function emit set is witnessed empty**
by one of two shapes:

* **class A** — `.ex` positively declares an empty module (no body exists);
* **class B** — the **empty-main-file (pch-creator) shape**: segments exist,
  and every module-block intro (`4F 02 20 00 4F 01 <k>`) is followed by
  another full intro or a bare `4F 1F` segment — no statement material at
  module level. decomp_pch (1,242 segments, 0 emitted) is the witness. Plus
  defence in depth: no function record of the one measured frame
  (`… 05 04 <linkage>`) carries a non-COMDAT linkage byte.

**Refuses, by name**: any defined data record with attr outside {`E0` emit,
`A0` drop, `20` drop} (selectany-uninit `0x60`, plain non-COMDAT data —
`data_tu`'s class); thread-locals; `??_C@` literals in the emit set;
aggregates (size ≠ tag width); internal linkage; any `.in` reference on an
emitted object; alias involvement; conflicted tokens (refusal, not
`data_tu`'s poison-drop); a TU with module-level statements (`g12`'s explicit
instantiation — **the registered danger cell, and it FIRED**: c2 emits
`?m@?$C@D@@QAAHXZ` with zero ordinary roots, through a module block whose
`.gl` record is byte-identical in every measured field to the non-emitting
`x01` twin, so **only the `.ex` module-block shape separates them**); the
width-boundary TU (`wnpos_provide_sizes.cpp` — the `.in` reader does not
decode every witnessed width; the emitter already carries the nibbles).

**vec.cpp and NetworkSocket.cpp** (deliverable 5, measured at tip): both carry
the byte-identical `?npos@…` `0xE0` record — **their mechanism 5 is PAID by
this lane's emitter, prices 6 → 5 and 8 → 7** — and both are refused by the
module-block fence (statement blocks `4F 02 <token>` / `53`-lists);
NetworkSocket additionally carries **2 root-shaped function records**
(linkage `00` — its two `sel=1` bodies). Neither moved class; neither was
chased.

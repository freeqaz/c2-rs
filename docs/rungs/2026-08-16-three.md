## 0. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER

**All three are declined, and the decline is structural rather than budgetary.**
The three TUs `w-871` (#3184) and `w-vocabgap` (#3191) independently named are
not three units of work: **they stop at the same gate cause
(`gl-stop-26-introduced`), their `.gl` names 0 of 1,242 / 811 / 240 segments,
and every one of their reference objs contains the byte-identical four-byte
`sel=2` `.rdata` COMDAT `?npos@?$basic_string@…` = `ff ff ff ff` that no
emitter in `crates/c2-core/src/coff/` writes.** That file is peer **`w-section`**'s,
single-occupancy, and it is the **same terminal `w-bind16` (#3196) reached from
the binding side** — so this lane **stops and reports** rather than entering it.
`decomp_pch.cpp` prices at **5** mechanisms, `vec.cpp` at **6**,
`NetworkSocket.cpp` at **8**, after **two anti-inflation collapses that both
fired and both took a number out of the total**. The one mechanism that is in
this lane's own files — a front-of-gate emit set — **cannot be built without
guessing**: `.gl` carries **551 body records for a TU c2 emitted 0 functions
from**, and across the workload `.gl` **never once under-counts** the emit set
(0 of 865) while over-counting on **834**, so nothing in the input says which
records c2 kept. The brief's rule is explicit — *if a conversion would require
guessing, decline and price the decline* — and this is that case.

**Two findings land beside the decline and outlive it.** First, **the
FUNCTIONS-vs-BYTES series**: over **811** partially-exact TUs, the
function-count reading of *"how done is this TU"* exceeds the byte reading on
**811 of 811**, median **9.0×**, max **39.2×**, **zero exceptions** — so
`#3184`'s and `w-871`'s *"`NetworkSocket.cpp` — 2 of 4 bodies already exact"*
reads **50 %** in the unit that was quoted and **3.64 %** in the unit that

title and its enforcing line say different things**: the gloss in `factors.rs`
is *"**C** can the writer **write the containers** the obj needs"*, the

## 2. THE PREDICATE, as shipped

> **SPLICE-0-PORT.** The port's `/Gy` COMDAT body for `F` **is** the port's `/Gy`
> COMDAT body for the end of `F`'s splice chain — text, relocations and data
> references — when every clause below holds. `F` acquires **no** REL24 against
> its callee.

| # | clause | refusals on the workload |
|---|---|---:|
| **S1** | `select_function(F)` is `Selected::Tail` or `Selected::Seq` | `framed` **123** |
| **S2** | `F` names **exactly one** call site | `seq` **725** |
| **S3** | the port emits **nothing around the call** — for `Tail`, an empty setup; for `Seq`, an **identity argument mapping read off the IL** and a tail that is the ABI identity on r3 (`CallValue { add_k: 0 }` with nothing saved, or `SavedFormal { param: 0 }` saving exactly that formal) | `tail` **951** |
| **S4** | `G ≠ F` | 0 (unit-tested) |
| **S5** | the callee is defined in this bundle, unambiguously | 0 |
| **S6** | the port composes a complete body for the **chain's end**, unframed, and the chain **ended** rather than being cut off | `tail` 480 + `seq` 182 parse-refused; `chain-truncated` 6; `chain-open` 3 |
| **S7** | that body is ≤ **64 bytes** and the callee is not varargs | **0 — S7 never binds today** |
| **S8** | `F` materializes no data symbol of its own | `tail` **2** |
| **S9** | mechanism **E** does not fire for `F` | — |

**No clause reads the reference obj.** That is the whole difference between this
and `w-seq` §6's enumeration of 726, and it is why the rule generalizes to the
`fnbyte-exact` population without breaking it (§4).

### 2.1 What it emits, and the one thing that makes it unlike every other rule here

`F`'s body becomes the chain end's `ComdatBody`: same text, same `calls` at the
same in-section offsets naming the **end's** callees, same `data_refs`,
`frame: None`. **`F` acquires no relocation against `G`.** Every other emitter
rule in this port writes bytes; this one rewrites a relocation table, and §7 is
why that needed an instrument of its own.

### 2.2 S7 is the inline decision, taken where the decision is CATEGORICAL

`INLINE_PREDICATE.md` §2 is a cost model graded **0.9716** on a 9,993-callee
frozen hold-out, with a **2.84 %** residual its own §7 leaves NOT MODELLED. A
shipped emitter may not consult a 97 % rule. It may consult the *region where
that rule has no residual*, which is a different object:

```text
index(G) = s − 4(nparams−1) − 8[inline] − 48[leaf]      ≤  s

EXTERNAL: N_max = UNBOUNDED  if index ≤ 64            (§6.17.4)
STATIC:   i = index/4;  UNBOUNDED if i ≤ 16          (§6.18.9)   i.e. index ≤ 64
either:   N_max = 0          if varargs(G)           (§6.18.5)
```

Both linkage classes turn unbounded at the **same** `index ≤ 64`, every
correction term is subtractive, so `s ≤ 64` ⟹ `N_max` unbounded —
**independently of linkage, of `inline`, of `nparams`, of the site count, and of
the `leaf` bit §5 calls the model's one unreadable input.** The site-side
exceptions are excluded structurally, not modelled: an indirect site is a parse
refusal, a conditional site is a `cond_pair` or a `Seq` with a `guard` and S1/S3
refuse both.

`PREREG.md` P3 registered **"S7 never binds"** and it held: **0** functions
refused for size. The bound costs nothing today and binds the moment the port's
lowered class grows past 16 words.

clauses hold, including on functions that are **already** `fnbyte-exact` — and

**Why zero is not luck.** S7 is the reason: every callee the port lowers is far

external, refused, framed, or reached through a setup.

* **The workload, through the relocation check** the one-level rule forced into

## 7. THE RELOCATION CHECK — the instrument that decided the rung

FUNCTION BYTE MATCH compares a `.text` COMDAT's raw data, and a `.text` section's
raw data **does not contain its relocations**. Two bodies that are both the word
`48000000` against different targets compare `exact`: board **#882**,
`fnbyte-exact-relocated` = **4,664** credited functions, and `w-seq`'s `s12` is
the four-line reproducer.

This mechanism *replaces a caller's relocations with its callee's*. The one
thing FBM cannot see is exactly what it changes, for every function it moves. So
the check is an acceptance condition, registered in `PREREG.md` §3 item 4 before
any of it existed, and it compares — per spliced symbol, against the reference
obj's own records:

| side | what |
|---|---|
| port | the spliced `ComdatBody`'s `calls` + `data_refs`, as `(target name, in-section offset)` |
| reference | `ObjImage::text_comdat_relocs` for the **same COMDAT**, `PAIR` excluded (its `SymbolTableIndex` is a displacement, not an index) |

| version of the rule | spliced | no-relocs | ok | **disagree** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| one level | 945 | 723 | 72 | **150** |
| chain closed | 945 | 723 | 145 | **77** |
| chain closed + both truncation clauses | **723** | **723** | 0 | **0** |

**`no-relocs` is printed apart from `ok` on purpose.** "Both sides are empty" is
a much weaker statement than "both sides name the same targets at the same
offsets", and folding them would let an empty answer read as a pass.

`ObjImage::text_comdat_relocs` is new (**#994**): `text_comdat_reloc_counts` has
existed since #320 and a *count* could not have caught a single one of these.

### 7.1 The reason 150 is the number that matters

Every one of those 150 was `fnbyte-exact` at the time. A rung scored on the
partition alone would have shipped all three versions green — 726 conversions,
zero regressions, and 150 wrong relocation targets nobody was looking at.

### 7.2 Two retreats, and the 1 wrong relocation that bought them

Closing the chain fixed 145 and left 77, all of the same kind: the walk stopped
where the port cannot **read** the next link, not where c2 stopped.

* **`S6-chain-truncated`** (6 functions) — the chain's end still names a callee
  this TU's census carries: a parse refusal, a setup, a multi-call body. c2 is
  under no such restriction and does not stop there.
* **`S6-chain-open`** (3 functions) — the end carries *any* call. One function
  got past the clause above: `??1CharPollableSorter@@QAA@XZ` spliced to a body
  branching to `??1?$_Rb_tree@PAVObject@Hmx@@…` where c2 relocates against
  `?clear@?$_Rb_tree@…` one link further down. **That callee has zero census
  rows in `Character.cpp`** — `c2rs census` prints none — so it is in the 9,225
  `unbound` population and reads to the port as an external. c2 knows better:
  it inlined it, which is proof the function is defined in that obj.

**Refusing `S6-chain-open` costs 4 spliced functions of 727 — 3 whose
relocations the check verified CORRECT and 1 verified WRONG.** Keeping the three
means keeping the one, because nothing the port can read separates them. That
is the whole of the shortfall against `w-seq`'s 726, and `t10` is its cell.

## 9. Where the mechanism may NOT fire

`IlBundle::functions()` refuses any TU that defines one of its own callees — a
strict superset of S5 — so **no bundle that can splice ever reaches the whole-obj
emitter**. That refusal is not narrowed here and `crates/c2-il/` is untouched, so
`mismatch` cannot move.

`PortC2::build` refuses such a bundle anyway, on **both** paths, with the reason
named: a spliced `Seq` loses its frame, and with it its `.pdata` record and its
`$M`/`$M`/`$T` label slots, while `frame_label_counter` is computed from the IL
*before any body exists* and `plan_emit_order` orders on a call edge the emitted
obj no longer has. `elide.rs` refuses only the packed path, because mechanism E's
single `blr` never reached the label counter; this one does.

The refusal is asked through the same `splice_callee` the composition consults,
so a future narrowing of `functions()` turns it into a loud refusal rather than a
silent wrong obj — **#878**'s loaded gun, disarmed in the same commit that loads
it.

### 12.1 The first gate log was destroyed by this lane's own scrubber

`work/w-splice/scrub.sh` rewrites a file in place to replace absolute paths, and
it was run over `gate_tip.txt` **while the gate was still appending to it**. The
`sed -i` truncated everything after the point it had read, so a run that exited
**0** left a log ending mid-`mode cross` with no `GATE:` line — a green run that
looks exactly like a hung one. The gate was re-run from scratch at tree
`327ebcf` and that is the log this table quotes.

Worth writing down because it is `docs/GAPS.md`'s standing shape from a new
direction: **absence read as failure this time, but the same edit could have
truncated a log at a point that read as success.** A scrubber is a *destructive*
transform and must be run on a file nothing holds open.

1. **The 3 functions `S6-chain-open` refuses are recoverable** (§7.2) by giving
   the port a defined-name set that does not come from the census — the `.gl`
   knows which names a TU defines, and the census's `emit_name` binding does not
   reach 9,225 of them. That rung must then **re-grade the one** that is wrong
   today, not assume the other three generalize.
2. **`S3-tail-setup` is 951 functions and it is #969's three fields.** A source
   rename, a destination rename, a displacement fold. `w-seq` §10.2 already
   warns that the measurement says *what* changed and not *what decides it* —
   the `r11` in `?back@?$vector` was a register allocator's choice, not a field
   edit — and nothing here changes that.

   rule; a different hypothesis, and the one `w-seq` §10.6's 177 ungraded
   multi-call differs sit inside.

   was not written for it.** `elide.rs` covers only `Selected::Tail` callers, so
   mechanism E never claims such a caller; the splice reaches it, walks to the
   elided callee, and then **`S6-chain-truncated` refuses** — because that
   callee still *names* a same-TU function, even though c2 emits `blr` for it
   and no relocation at all. Traced through the code rather than measured: the
   population is **0 on this workload** (no `seq S6-chain-truncated` row in §5's
   census). The refusal agrees with `elide.rs`'s own decline of a `Seq` mid-node
   (#948) and is the safe direction, but **the two rules reach it for different
   reasons** and a lane widening either should know that before it widens.

## 0. The three answers

**1 — the mechanism.** The counter is **`DAT_10c2edd0`**, one 32-bit TU-global,
incremented at **exactly one instruction** — `inc DWORD PTR ds:0x10c2edd0` at
**`0x10b97de5`**, inside a 28-byte routine at **`0x10b97dd0`** that **faults with
internal error `0x37` if the counter is still 0**, which is the proof that it is
*seeded* rather than zeroed. That routine has **31 direct call sites**; one of
them is the generic label constructor at **`0x10b9a455`**, itself called **132
times from 86 distinct functions**.

And the sentence everything else follows from:

> **`c1xx` and `c2` share one symbol-id space.** The front end numbers the labels
> **it** creates, writes those ids into the IL, and hands c2 the next free value
> in `.gl`. c2's IL reader takes them **from the stream without touching the
> counter**; c2 allocates only for labels **it invents**.

**2 — why the published table is "wrong".** It is not. It is a **right model
measured wrongly**, and the wrong measurements are of a **different quantity**.
`w-json`'s counterfactual form measures `Δseed + Δcharge`, and the seed is a
function of the source text. The settling row:

> **Eight unused declarations, emitting not one instruction, move the
> counterfactual reading by `+16` while the true in-TU stride stays `1`.**

**3 — what a conversion lane should do.** Six steps,
`LABEL_COUNTER.md` §7.6 / findings §5.1, and the headline is *put the subject in
the MIDDLE and read the in-obj stride*. At `/O1` a construct-additive table
predicts the lead **6 of 6 on held-out compositions**, so a lane can predict and
confirm with **one** compile instead of four counterfactual builds. At `/Ox`
the same rule scores **1 of 6** — measure.

## 1. What it admits, and what it refuses

**It admits nothing into `crates/`. Not one line.** That is the deliverable, not
an omission: the standing precedent (#1761) is that a *fit* written into
`label_lead` is refuted by the next obj, one lane later.

**It refuses two routes, by measurement:**

* **the `/FAsc` listing** (#2432) — `p_dowhile` (stride 2) and `p_forever`
  (stride 4) emit **identical 24 `.text` bytes** and print the **same label name
  at the same index**. `$LN`/`$LL`/`$LC` numbers come from a *second*,
  per-function counter that counts label objects including the free ones;
* **a parameterised `label_slots`** (#2437) — neither a mode arm nor a sub-shape
  arm, because the quantity is *how many labels c2 invents beyond the IL's* and
  no IL field carries it, and because `w-blockir`'s "sub-shape dependence" was
  **the seed**.

## 2. Estimate vs outcome

**Frozen before the first `cl.exe`** (`WB_LABEL_PREREG_R2.md` §3), graded in
findings §4:

| cell | predicted `/O1` | actual | predicted `/Ox` | actual |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| X1 jump-tabled `switch` | 20 | **6** | 19 | **7** |
| X2 `for` with an `if` | 13 | **7** | 12 | **7** |
| X3 `while` with early `return` | 13 | **8** | 12 | **7** |
| X4 `try`, two `catch` | **28** | **28** | **25** | **25** |
| X5 `switch` in a `for` | 26 | **8** | 24 | **10** |
| X6 loop unrolled at `/Ox` | 12 | **7** | ≥13 | **11** |

**1 of 6 cells, 2 of 12 numbers.** The bias is **one-directional and large**: the
model over-predicted every non-EH cell by 2–3×, because it assumed c2 mints a
label per case arm and per join. It does not — those arrive pre-numbered. The
size of that miss is what identified the mechanism.

**The meta-predictions, registered before the builds, both landed:** P9.1 (*"≥2
of the six will MISS … a 6/6 is a reason to distrust the model"*) — five missed;
P11.2 (*"`/Ox` additivity scores ≤2 of 6"*) — it scored 1.

**Six retractions are filed** (findings §7): the five X-cell predictions, the
minted-equals-stride identity, the `.pdata`-section-symbol reading of the
`/Gy`−packed gap, the strong form of *"no closed-form rule"*, the listing as an
instrument, and the `/Ox` `minted` prediction.

## 4. Found and not taken

Ranked, with the frame axis applied.

1. **Enumerate the nine.** `LABEL_SEED_GAP = 9` is established as nine
   allocations rather than an offset (#2435); *which* nine is not read out.
   Cost: one focused pass over the 31 direct callers of `0x10b97dd0` plus a
   probe TU that varies the section set (a defined global, a string pool,
   `/GF`). Value: it would turn a fitted constant into a computed one — and
   **that is exactly the step that would incur a disclosure row**, so it should
   be taken deliberately, not incidentally (findings §8).
2. **The `/Gy` "+3 per function"** is re-confirmed on 22 of 22 rows (#2436) and
   *what the three are* is unread. Same pass as item 1.
3. **A third once-per-TU minting slot.** `_fltused` and `memcpy` are the two
   known; §7.1's mechanism says the list is **open**. The in-the-middle
   procedure finds them for the cost of one compile per candidate, and the
   candidates are the compiler-minted named symbols `coff/writer.rs` already
   enumerates (`CEILING.md` §11's NC-1 list).
4. **`w-json`'s 4**, reconciled in kind and not in arithmetic (findings §3.3).
   One re-run of that lane's cells in the in-the-middle form closes it.
5. **The `/Ox` loop charge** — four magnitudes (10, 3, 7, 10), no rule, and this
   lane proposes none. **Registered as low value**: `/Ox` is not the workload's
   mode, and the reason to know it is a mode-invariance check, not a conversion.
6. **The downward pool** `DAT_10c2ed40` and the crossing check are read but
   unmeasured. No probe here came near it and none of the frontier does.

## 5. For the next conversion lane, in one paragraph

Do not run four counterfactual builds. Put your subject in the **middle** of
`a0 · P · a1 · a2`, compile once at your mode, assert `base` in the same obj
(5 under `/Gy`, 4 packed), take `stride = first(a1) − first(a0) − base`, and
**subtract any `minted` surcharge** before you call the remainder a control-flow
lead. At `/O1` you can predict the lead first from `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §7.4's
six-construct table and use the compile as a confirmation. If your class is not
in that table, ship `None` — and `None` is not conservatism, it is the only
value that can be right for a quantity the IL does not carry.

**`w-mmioclose` (#2400–#2429) is converting `mmio.cpp` concurrently and needs
this channel; it should cite §7.6 rather than re-measure.**

## 0. The one-paragraph answer

`WB_RELATION_FINDINGS.md` §2's relation-code assignment is **wrong on eight of
its ten codes**, and it went wrong at one word: it named `0x10b189b8` a
*"strictness flip"* in its §1 and then used that name as a **premise** in its

code is 8 and 8 is `UGT`** — so `#2102`'s *"ULE"* and `w-c7`'s *"unsigned LT"*
are both wrong and `#2207` was right all along. `#2207` is nonetheless corrected

right through code 12 and off by one from 13. `#423`'s 36-cell grid is retired
**as a dispatch question and not as a byte prediction**, and §4.6 of the
findings draws that line explicitly.

## 2. The single most transferable result, and it is not about relations

`WB_RELATION_FINDINGS.md` §2 states its assignment is *"over-determined, which
is why it can be stated without a probe"*, on four independent constraints.
**Three of the four are satisfied by both candidate assignments.** Only
constraint 4 discriminates, and it discriminates by assuming what `b8` is.

> *The algebra of a permutation table determines the labelling only up to the
> automorphisms of that algebra.*

This relation lattice has an order-2 automorphism — exchange ↔ strictness on
`{3,4,5,6}` — and both candidates are involutions fixing `{EQ, NE}`, so no
amount of table-reading separates them. **A consumer or a name is required.**
Prereg **S3c** registered exactly that, before looking, which is why the miss is
diagnosable rather than lucky.

**The operational rule for the next lane: "over-determined by N independent
constraints" should be checked constraint by constraint for the one that
restates the conclusion.** Here one of four did, and it was the only one that
mattered. Board **#3518**.

## What it admits

```text
  addic  r11,r3,-1              the `!= 0` fold, unchanged from W6
  lis    r10,C>>16              the constant, materialized INTO the gap between
  subfe  rS,r11,r3                the carry producer and its consumer
  rlwimi r10,rS,SH,0,31-SH      the shift and the OR, ONE word
  mr     r3,r10
  blr
```

for `return ((unsigned)(P != 0) << SH) | C;` where `P` is the function's **only**
formal and `c2_il::shift_or_rlwimi` accepts `(SH, C)` — that is, `C`'s low 16
bits are zero and `SH > msb(C)`.

`rS` is **r11 at `/O1`** and **r9 at `/Ox`**. That is `docs/CODEGEN_W6_O1.md`'s
incumbent rule applied to one more spine, not a new fit (§4.1 of the main rung,
board **#464**).

## What it refuses, and why each refusal is a measurement

Every one carries its measured bytes in `w43_cmp_shift_or_neg.cpp`:

* **`SH <= msb(C)`** — c2 emits `slwi` + `oris`, four words and no `mr`. One
  column apart from an accepted cell.
* **`C`'s low half non-zero** — `lis` alone cannot make it; c2 reaches for `ori`
  or `li`, and the 288-cell grid does **not** separate which of those pairs with
  `slwi` and which with `rlwimi`.
* **`C = 0x80000000` and `C = 0x00030000`** — the two rows that break every
  boundary rule this lane could state, carried by name rather than smoothed.
* **The formal in any slot but r3** — c2 puts the constant in r3 and drops the
  `mr`, five words not six. Five measured cells.
* **Any relation but `!= 0`** — a different fold with a different live register.

## Estimate vs outcome

The prereg registered `rlwimi` as absent and predicted the boundary would not be
derivable from the workload's single cell. Both held: `rlwimi` had no encoder,
and a rule read off `SH = 30` alone would have been **wrong on 29 of the 32
columns of its own row** (`SH ∈ 0..=28` emit `slwi` + `oris`).

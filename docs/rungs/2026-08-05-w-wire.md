### §1.1 The two misses

* **Per-function census +4,869 against a predicted +8,000.** The prediction was
  sized off `disp-assign`'s 729,174 blocked bodies on the assumption that a
  multi-literal store body would be sitting there. It was not: `disp-assign` is
  **unchanged to the body**, and all 4,869 came out of **`disp-expr-load`**
  (401,009 → 396,140). The two arms are different branches of the ladder, keyed
  on the opening byte, and a store-shaped body that `try_parse_store_run`
  declined fell through to the *expression* parser, not to the assignment one.
  The estimate was wrong about the mechanism, not just the magnitude.
* **Factor D did not move.** Predicted 9 → 9…12. It read **9**. D is *every*
  emitted COMDAT of a TU being in the port's codegen class, which is a
  conjunction over the whole TU — 716 functions spread across many TUs moves no
  TU's conjunction from false to true. This is STATUS trap 8 in its own right:
  a per-function gain is not a per-TU one, and the lane predicted its own trap
  wrong in the optimistic direction.

### §1.2 TU match is 9, and that was the honest prediction

Six lanes' honest zeros are why these models are trustworthy, and this is a
seventh. `xboxheap` is blocked on a parse chain at least two operators deep
(board #622) and no emitter work reaches it; the frontier is priced at ≥ 6
independent refusals per TU (board #269). **Expect the function number to move
and the TU number not to** — the brief said so and it was right.

### §1.3 A correction to this lane's own brief

The brief states baseline **FBM 0.16251, 29,084 exact, 9,374 partial**. Measured
at `2ed0030`: **0.16254, 29,085 exact, 9,375 partial**, which is what
`docs/STATUS.md`'s generated block reads. The brief's figures are one commit
behind; every delta above is against the measured baseline.

## §2 What was wired, and what was refused

### Wired

`crates/c2-core/src/codegen/leaf/store.rs::scheduled_gpr_run_text` claims any
`ops` stream that is **entirely value-simple GPR groups** — every group a
`[Load(base), Lit(k) | Load(formal), StoreInd]` — and emits:

| decision | model | holdout |
|---|---|---|
| which store goes where | `order::store_order` | 561/561 |
| which producer comes first | `order::producer_order` (#582) | case split, measured |
| where producers sit among stores | `order::layout_slots` (#602) | 24,891/24,891 |
| which register each takes | `alloc::allocate` | 250/250 |
| the symbol pattern | the PIN inside `walk` (#601) | 7,589/7,589, model-free |

`crates/c2-il/.../leaf_store.rs::try_parse_store_run` now admits **up to three
distinct literals** and **literals mixed with formals**.

### Refused, and why each refusal is a measured regime

* **More than 3 distinct literals** — c2 reuses a register freed by an emitted
  store rather than taking a fresh one, and **the two modes disagree about
  which** (§3.2). Board #541.
* **More than one base symbol** in the widened class — with one symbol every
  producer's `nsw` is identically 0, so `MAX_SYMBOL_CROSSINGS` holds by
  construction. Board **#621** measured a rival layout clause that answers the
  multi-symbol case at 99.44 % fit / 97.30 % holdout and deliberately did not
  ship it. **This lane did not resurrect it to widen coverage.** (The
  *pre-existing* one-value class keeps its multi-symbol admission untouched.)
* **A pool too small for the run** — c2 descends into registers freed by
  emitted stores, and the boundary probe shows it reusing **r10, a formal's own
  register**, with 8 formals live. Unmodelled.
* **A multi-word literal beside another producer** — §4. The counterexample
  that fired.
* **Everything the old walk still owns**: load-valued groups, FP groups, mixed
  streams, and any residue. `scheduled_gpr_run_text` declines the whole stream
  rather than a prefix, so those fall through unchanged.

### §2.1 The invariant, stated per change

> *A change in a model's answer can add a refusal, but can never turn a refusal
> into an accept, outside the region that model is exact on.*

**The two commits do not have the same property and they are not described as
if they did** — the distinction `w-frame2` drew for `schedule()` and asked this
lane to keep visible.

* **`8cad19a`, the emitter (#640) — mostly additive-refusal, with one named
  exception.** `schedule`/`allocate` answering `None` becomes `out_of_class`. It
  cannot accept anything the parser did not hand it. **The exception**: the
  emitter used to refuse a literal beside a formal as a *second lock* on the
  parser's gate, and now schedules it. That is an accept where there was a
  refusal, it is stated in the code comment and in the commit message, and it is
  safe because the answer is graded against real `c2` (probe cell `M2`). It
  moved **no byte** — the workload scan after this commit is **identical to
  baseline in every `gap-metric` and in the whole dispatch histogram**, because
  the parser was still the live lock.
* **`bb89894`, the parser (#642) — additive-ACCEPT, and that is said plainly.**
  Its safety does **not** come from the guards. It comes from a *syntactic* gate
  landing strictly inside a region measured exact against real `c2` bytes, plus
  the five constructed counterexamples of §4.

The claim that the emitter rewrite cannot *delete* accepted bytes is proved by
**enumeration, not argument**: `the_schedule_never_refuses_a_run_the_parser_admits_today`
walks 4,000+ runs to length 7 over 3 base symbols across both admitted families
and asserts `schedule` answers, answers **source order with the producer
leading**, and that `allocate` says `r11` at every formal count.

## §3 `/O1` and `/Ox` — the question no prior lane asked · #641

Every grid behind SCHED, ALLOC, ORDER, SYMORDER and LAYOUT was compiled at the
**workload's** `/O1 /Oi /EHsc` (`work/w-alloc/alloc_lib.py:26`,
`docs/ALLOC.md`, `docs/ORDER.md`). The **fixture gate runs `/Ox`**, and
`leaf::store` already carries a mode split for its load-valued arm. A lane
shipping an emitter has to know whether the models hold at `/Ox`. Nobody had
asked.

`work/w-wire/mode_probe.py` compares the two modes' emitted permutations **to
each other** — model-free — over 18 cases spanning both killer-cell families,
the interleaved layouts and the pool boundary:

```
MODE AGREEMENT: 18 of 18 cases
```

The load-bearing cells are the ones no source-order emitter can reach:

| case | source | both modes | the model |
|---|---|---|---|
| `A6` | `1,1,2,2,2` | `li r11 ; li r10 ; S8@r11 S0@r10 S4@r10 S12@r11 S16@r11` | store order **[2,0,1,3,4]** |
| `A8` | `1,2,2` | `li r11 ; li r10 ; S4@r11 S0@r10 S8@r11` | store order **[1,0,2]** |
| `M5` | `1,2,u,v` | `li r11 ; S8@r4 ; li r10 ; S12@r5 ; S0@r11 S4@r10` | `"01.."` → `P0 S2 P1 S3 S0 S1` |
| `M7` | `u,1,2,v` | `li r11 ; S0@r4 ; li r10 ; S12@r5 ; S4@r11 S8@r10` | order `[0,3,1,2]`, `u=2` |

### §3.1 `/O1` implies COMDAT and `/Ox` does not · #643

The same probe TU lists as `PROC NEAR ; X, COMDAT` at `/O1` and `PROC NEAR ; X`
at `/Ox`. **Every inherited listing parser requires the `, COMDAT` suffix**
(`work/w-alloc/alloc_lib.py:63` and its copies), so **any `/Ox` listing parses
to zero functions**. Caught here only because `compile_cod` raises on `0 PROC
NEAR` — STATUS trap 5 (*absence reads as success unless something forbids it*)
firing exactly as designed. `work/w-wire/wirelib.py` makes the suffix optional.

### §3.2 The agreement is a property of the DOMAIN, not of store runs

Quote 18/18 with its scope. One step outside the modelled region the modes
**disagree** (`work/w-wire/boundary_probe.py`, `{a=1;b=2;c=3;d=4}`):

```text
  /O1   li r11 ; li r10 ; stw r11,0 ; li r9 ; li r11 ; stw r10,4 ; stw r9,8 ; stw r11,12
  /Ox   li r11 ; li r10 ; li r9 ; stw r11,0 ; li r8 ; stw r10,4 ; stw r9,8 ; stw r8,12
```

`/O1` **reuses r11** once its store frees it; `/Ox` takes a fresh **r8**. That
is a stronger reason to keep `MAX_MODELLED_PRODUCERS = 3` than the one board
#541 recorded.

## §4 The constructed counterexample that FIRED · #644

Five were registered in the prereg §4, built to fail. **One did**, and the
prereg had registered the *opposite* prediction — item 4 read *"NOT a boundary:
this is in domain and must be answered with the pair kept whole."*

Real `c2`, identical at `/O1` and `/Ox`:

```text
  { a=100000; b=1;      }   lis r11 ; li r10 ; ori r11 ; stw r10,4(r3) ; stw r11,0(r3)
  { a=100000; b=200000; }   lis r11 ; lis r10 ; ori r11 ; ori r10 ; stw r11,0 ; stw r10,4
```

Two independent failures at once:

1. **A producer is not one contiguous instruction.** c2 *interleaves* the halves
   of two wide loads, so `layout_slots` — which places producers by index —
   cannot express the sequence at all.
2. **`store_order` is refuted on the first cell**: c2 emits stores `[1, 0]`
   where the model says source order.

**Every ORDER/ALLOC grid used single-word `li` values, and neither
`docs/ORDER.md` nor `docs/ALLOC.md` said so.** Both now carry the restriction as
a banner. The port refuses more than one producer where any literal needs more
than one word; a run whose **only** producer is wide is untouched, and the test
asserts both halves so the gate cannot over-refuse.

**Had the widening shipped without this probe it would have been a live wrong
emit** — board #232's exact shape, found by a constructed counterexample instead
of by a scan 255 commits later. That is the whole argument for the method.

The other four behaved as registered: 4 distinct literals refuse, 2 symbols with
2 distinct literals refuse, the pool boundary refuses (and c2 is measured
reusing a formal's register there), and `x_split`'s `nsw = 3` mask is refused by
`layout_slots` before any emission.

## §5 How it was graded — the sole judge, not a model

Every prior lane in this family fitted a model in python and scored a grid with
it. That is the right instrument for *finding* a rule and the wrong one for
shipping an emitter, so nothing here was scored against a transcription:

| instrument | population | result |
|---|---|---|
| `work/w-wire/grid.py` + `c2rs diff` | **360** probe functions — every word of length 2..4 over `{formal, 1, 2, 3}` plus the length-5/6 shapes | `ReferenceReplay=ByteExact`, **`Port=Match`** at `/Ox` |
| the same TU + `c2rs gap` at the **workload's own** `/O1 /Oi /EHsc /GR` | 360 | **`match`** — whole obj byte-exact |
| `fixtures/cpp/w45_store_run_scheduled.cpp` | 16 curated discriminating cells | **`Port=Match`** at `/Ox`, `/O1`, `/O2`, `/Ox /Gy`; honest `codegen-gap` at `/Od` (unmodelled opt word, pre-existing for every fixture) |
| `fixtures/cpp/w45_store_run_scheduled_neg.cpp` | 6 cells, one per refused regime | **`Port=NotImplemented`**, **0 of 6** in class, `ReferenceReplay=ByteExact` — the negative half, graded 0/N |
| the 878-TU workload scan | 178,975 emitted functions | **`fnbyte-differs` 0**, `mismatch` 0 |
| `scripts/gate.sh --jobs 6` | 18 lanes + sweep + mode cross | §6 |

The fixture is 16 cells and not 360 because the gate grades each fixture in 18
mode lanes; the exhaustive version stays a lane instrument. The cells were
chosen to separate models that a uniform grid cannot: `t_perm2` is where the
**rank** (forward first-use) and the **allocation** (reverse first-use among
shared constants) disagree in sign, so a port using one order for both passes
every unique-count cell and fails there.

## §7 What this does not claim

* **It is not a TU.** TU match is 9 before and after. Per-function conversion is
  not per-TU conversion, and this lane predicted factor D would move and it did
  not.
* **`fnbyte-differs 0` is not a proof.** It is the alarm not firing over the

  not where the docs implied.** A wide-value grid does not exist for any of
  them; a lane widening past this one owes it.
* **`codegen::schedule` still has only a guard consumer.** The emitter consumes
  `codegen::order`, which subsumes it. `schedule.rs` is unchanged.

## 7. The method results worth carrying off

* **V1 missed by 23 opcodes** because the bracket was anchored on
  `expr.rs::chain_skip_form` — the port's *narrowest* reader, poisoned and
  environment-gated — while its *widest* had not been opened. That is
  `#3505`'s "ranking instruments measure themselves" turned on a prediction,
  and it is the **second** instance in two lanes on this subject (`#3415`'s
  own P1.2 was the first). The counter-move costs one grep: enumerate the
  **entry points** before bracketing anything about their reach.
* **Two scanner defects, both self-inflicted, both found by reading its output
  back against the source** — brace-depth `fn` attribution defeated by doc
  comments containing fenced code blocks, and single-line arm matching that
  reported `1F..24` as unread when they sit on a continuation line
  (`control_flow`'s reach **39 → 40 of 61**).
* **A prereg defect**: `NARROW`'s limb 2 required a read the lane did not
  budget, so V6 is `UNRESOLVED` rather than scored. Recorded rather than
  resolved by relaxing the definition after the fact.
* **The operand class works as a consistency check on the PORT**, with no
  knowledge of what a class means: opcodes sharing a class share a payload
  grammar, so a class read two ways is a place the port's widths were pinned
  per opcode from witnesses. That is what found `0x28` and the `0x43` escape.

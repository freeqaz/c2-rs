## 2. The finding that decided the lane

The frontier is advertised as *"17 TUs whose only remaining blocker is codegen
breadth"*. That is true, and it is also the wrong picture of the work, because of
**where** that breadth is missing and **which crate** it is missing in.

### 2.1 It is missing in `crates/c2-il`, not in `crates/c2-core/src/codegen/`

Every one of the 17 TUs reports the same TU-level reason:

```text
  reason: il function decode failed
  detail: .ex NNNN B, K .gl names — c2_il::functions() and dyninit_tu() both None
```

`c2_il::functions()` returning `None` means no `IlFunction` is ever built, so
`c2_core::codegen::select_function` is never reached. And the scan's own
cross-check agrees from the other direction — measured over the **whole 878-TU
workload**, not just the frontier:

```text
  census/gate disagreement: 0  (the port accepts every function above)
  fn_gate_refusals across all 878 TUs: NONE
  fn_gate_refusals across the 17 frontier TUs: NONE
```

`function_gate` — which runs `select_function` itself, not a copy — refuses
**zero** functions anywhere in the workload. There is no live refusal in this
lane's seam to widen. Widening `crates/c2-core/src/codegen/` cannot move the
frontier by one TU, because the shape never arrives.

Prereg §4 **B2** predicted exactly this and it holds.

### 2.2 15 of the 17 need control flow, which this lane was told not to build

The 17 TUs carry **35 blocked functions**. On the decode-only control-flow axis:

| cflow class | blocked fns | TUs containing ≥1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| `cflow-straight` / `cflow-straight+expr-modeled` | 8 | 8 |
| `cflow-if-1` | 5 | 2 |
| `cflow-if-2` | 1 | 1 |
| `cflow-if-n` | 10 | 8 |
| `cflow-loop` | 8 | 5 |
| `cf-expr-0x05` (body did not decode end to end) | 3 | 3 |
| **total** | **35** | |

A TU converts only when **every** one of its blocked functions converts.
**Exactly two** frontier TUs have all their blocked functions in the
straight-line rows. The other **15** each contain at least one `cflow-if-*`,
`cflow-loop` or `cf-expr-0x05` function, and no ordering of leaf-shape widenings
reaches them. Prereg **B1** holds.

So the honest restatement of the frontier is: *the 17 cheapest TUs in the project
are cheap because their section shape, binding and segments are already solved —
but 15 of them are gated on the CFG step, which `docs/ROADMAP.md` §G4 schedules
for when a block/instruction IR is forced, and which the brief for this lane
forbids on the grounds that doing it here would put every existing byte at risk.*
That is a scheduling fact worth having in writing before the next lane picks the
frontier up expecting leaf shapes.

### 2.3 The biggest bucket is not the cheapest conversion

Across the 35 blocked frontier functions the leading blocker is `expr-cmp-eq`
with **14** functions in **7** TUs. Granting it alone converts **zero** TUs: 13
of the 14 sit behind `cflow-if-*` / `cflow-loop` / `cf-expr-0x05`, and the
fourteenth (`vsnprnc.cpp` fn 0) shares its TU with a straight-line function that
is blocked on something else. Prereg **B4** holds.

This is the same trap `docs/BOARD.md` #150 already recorded for `expr-op-0x27`
("22,759 emitted / 407,016 bodies; granting its named token converts **6**
emitted functions — the board should carry 6, not 22,759"), reproduced on the
frontier: **bucket size and conversion yield are different questions**, and the
frontier is the place where they diverge most sharply.

### 3.1 The caveat that must travel with this table

**32 of the 35 rows read `complete-none`.** Per `c2_il::Complete::NoSignal`, a
keyed byte refusal "says nothing about whether anything is behind it". So no row
in this table licenses the claim "grant this one construct and the function
converts" — it licenses only "this is the first thing that stops the parse".
Only three rows carry a completeness signal at all: `Main.cpp` fn 0 and
`vsnprnc.cpp` fn 1 read `partial:segment-end` (there **is** more segment behind
the refusal), and `Biquad.cpp` fn 1 reads `unmeasured:grammar`.

Anyone who converts a row here should expect to hit a second blocker and should
budget for it. That is a property of the instrument, not a defect in it.

## 4. The cheapest-first ladder for whoever owns `crates/c2-il`

1. **`src/xdk/nuispeech/xboxheap.cpp`** — the single cheapest TU on the frontier.
   One blocked function, `cflow-straight`, `eh-none`, one blocker
   (`expr-op-0x27`), at this site (`>` marks the refused byte):

   ```text
   53 4f 01 04  b9 0f 0a a6 43 81 20  33 86 41 74 10  >27<  a6 43 f5 08
                └ LOAD tok 0x0a0f, ptr type ┘ └ LIT int 0x10 ┘  └ re-typed designator ┘
   ```

   `docs/IL_EXPR_LAYER.md` §4 already reads `0x27` as the byte-offset pointer add
   that *re-types the designator and carries a TYPE* — distinct from the ordinary
   `02` ADD. Residue behind it: **unmeasured**. `docs/BOARD.md` #150 records the
   whole-corpus yield of granting the token as **6 emitted functions**, so this
   is a one-TU win and should be scoped as one.

2. **`src/Main.cpp`** — also one straight-line blocked function, but *not* one
   widening away: it reads `eh-state1`, so the whole EH record must be modeled
   too, and it carries a `.rdata` section. Its blocker
   `param-width-undetermined:mid` is a `disp-formals-width` refusal — the `.sy`
   layer withheld a formal's width — and it reads `partial:segment-end`, so
   there is definitely more behind it. Prereg **B3** predicted this is not the
   cheap one; it holds.

3. **Then the CFG step, and `cflow-if-1` is its minimal instance** — one
   conditional branch and one label. Two frontier TUs are built entirely from
   `if-1` plus straight-line functions:

   * `src/xdk/nuispeech/xboxmem.cpp` — 3 × `expr-cmp-eq` @ `if-1`, 1 ×
     `expr-cmp-ne` @ straight. Four small functions, all `eh-none`, no `.rdata`
     or `.bss` in the emit set.
   * `src/system/utl/Pool.cpp` — 2 × `if-1`, but its third function is
     `cf-expr-0x05` (did not decode end to end), which is a strictly harder
     problem than a branch.

   `src/xdk/nuispeech/mmio.cpp` deserves a separate mention: **8 of its 11
   functions are already in class**, and all three blocked ones are
   `expr-cmp-eq` (at `if-1`, `if-2`, `if-n`). It is the TU with the most
   already-solved mass sitting behind a single construct.

4. Loops last. Five TUs are gated on `cflow-loop`, and
   `src/system/synth_xbox/IPP_basicmath_xbox.cpp` is the purest specimen — four
   functions, all `cflow-loop`, all blocked on `expr-cmp-eq`, no EH, no data
   sections. If a loop lowering is ever built, that TU is its natural first
   target and converts four functions at once.

## 5. Cross-seam items found and declined

1. **The frontier is unreachable from `crates/c2-core/src/codegen/`** (§2.1).
   Every widening any of the 17 TUs needs is a `crates/c2-il` shape-recognizer
   change — `crates/c2-il/src/func/body/shapes/` and `…/body/mod.rs`. That crate
   is lane w-r2's. **Declined, not taken**, per the brief and per prereg §1.
   *This is the item that decided the lane.*

   The structural reason is worth stating for whoever schedules the next round:
   a shape widening is intrinsically a **paired** change — `c2-il` must recognize
   the shape and populate the `IlFunction` field, and `c2-core/codegen` must
   lower it (`shapes/leaf_compare.rs` ↔ `codegen/leaf/compare.rs`,
   `shapes/leaf_store.rs` ↔ `codegen/leaf/store.rs`, and so on). Splitting those
   two crates across two lanes makes *every* conversion a cross-seam change.
   A future fan-out should assign the **pair** to one lane, or assign lanes by
   shape rather than by crate.

2. **`fn_gate_refusals` is empty over the entire 878-TU workload.** Not a defect
   — it is exactly the "one decision procedure, not two" property
   `codegen/select.rs` documents — but it means the census/gate-disagreement
   counter is currently a constant 0 and carries no information about
   `c2-core/codegen`. Any future claim that "codegen is the blocker" should be
   checked against this number first. `crates/c2-harness/src/gap.rs` is lane
   w-fifth's; **not taken**.

3. **`docs/BOARD.md` / `docs/ROADMAP.md` / `docs/STATUS.md` unedited**, per the
   brief. Proposed rows in §6.

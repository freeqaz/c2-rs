## 0. What the lane was asked and what it answers

Three consecutive conversion lanes found their **last** blocker was not codegen.
The commission was to find the rest of that class *before* the next three lanes
each discover one at the end, and — the standing alternative — to say honestly
if the three known instances were the only ones.

**Both halves of the answer are real, and they point opposite ways.**

* **The whole-obj half (NC-1/NC-2) is EXHAUSTED at exactly two TUs.** Over all
  871 graded TUs there is **one** TU whose every emitted function is
  `fnbyte-exact` and which does not match (`src/system/math/vec.cpp`) and
  **one** whose reference obj contains no code at all and which does not match
  (`src/system/decomp_pch.cpp`). They are the same two TUs board **#213**
  already names, and what is new is that the claim is now **byte-verified**
  rather than a reachability argument. `w-blockir`'s `_fltused` was not the
  first of many; it was the second-to-last.
* **The mislocated-clause half (NC-4) is SYSTEMATIC, unrepaired, and board
  #1416 said so a day ago.** The reported refusal key of an emitted function
  routinely names a layer the body does not contain. This lane's sharpest
  instance is `void WordWrap_SetOption(unsigned int option) { g_uOption =
  option; }` — **twelve bytes of PowerPC, three instructions, no control flow
  of any kind** — reported blocked at **`expr-jump`**.

**And the lane found a third thing it was not looking for: the corpus is not
pinned.** `dc3-decomp` advanced **23 commits** between this lane's first scan
and its last, `fnbyte-denominator` fell **178,977 → 162,092 (−9.4 %)**, and
**FBM rose +0.0183 from a change nobody in `c2-rs` made** — larger than the
+0.026 that `w-fnbyte`'s entire instrument widening bought. §5.

## 2. The class and its detector

Both are written up where a lane will hit them:
[`docs/CEILING.md` §11](../CEILING.md). §11.1 is the class, §11.2 the detector,
§11.3 this sweep, **§11.4 the checklist**.

**Why `CEILING.md` and not the rung template.** `CEILING.md` is the page a lane
reads to *price* a TU — before it writes an emitter arm — and its §10 addendum is
already the "how far is this" page. The rung template is read when a lane
*writes up*, which is after the money is spent. The template gets a four-line
pointer into §11.4 and nothing more, so a lane copying it still sees the rule at
the point where it fills in "estimate vs outcome".

**The detector, in one line each:**

* **T1 ALL-EXACT-NO-MATCH** — `fnbyte-denominator > 0 ∧ fnbyte-exact ==
  fnbyte-denominator ∧ class != match`. `work/w-nc/sweep.py`.
* **T1b ZERO-BYTE** — `fnbyte-denominator == 0 ∧ class != match`.
* **T2 emitted-side refusal keys** — the census verdict key of every
  `fnbyte-refused` **emitted** function. `work/w-nc/keys.py` + §6.1's scratch.
* **T3 the type-tag regex** — `(type|target)-[0-9A-F]{4}` over T2.

**T2 did not exist.** `gap`'s published `fn_blockers` histogram is over every IL
body (2.4 M) and `w-inlfence` (#2220) showed it is fail-open on 845 of 871 TUs;
the frontier's codegen column is four totals with no keys. T2 sums exactly to
`fnbyte-refused` and is per function, per TU, by name. Board **#2384**.

## 3. The sweep

### 3.1 The byte-distance-zero population is 2, and both are hand-checked

**`src/system/math/vec.cpp` — factors `-BCD-`, `exact 2/2`.** The two emitted
functions, and their bytes from the real obj (`work/w-nc/ref/vec.obj`,
`scripts/gt_dump.py`):

```text
-- .text #6 (16 B) ??0Vector3@@QAA@MMM@Z
   d0230000  stfs 1, 0(3)      d0430004  stfs 2, 4(3)
   d0630008  stfs 3, 8(3)      4e800020  blr
-- .text #7 (20 B) ??0Vector4@@QAA@MMMM@Z
   d0230000  stfs 1, 0(3)      d0430004  stfs 2, 4(3)
   d0630008  stfs 3, 8(3)      d083000c  stfs 4, 12(3)
   4e800020  blr
```

Both are `fnbyte-exact`. The obj's other eight sections are what stands between
it and a `match`, and **not one of them is an instruction**: a 4-byte `.rdata`
COMDAT carrying `?npos@?$basic_string@…`, a 112-byte `.data` carrying **seven**
defined externals (`?sX@Vector3@@1V1@A` … `?sW@Vector4@@1V1@A`), a 32-byte `.bss`
carrying two, and — symbol `[17]`, immediately after `??0Vector3`'s own —
**`_fltused`**. The TU has **811 `.ex` bodies and 2 emitted functions**, so
factor A fails 811-to-2.

**`src/system/decomp_pch.cpp` — factors `-BCD-`, the purest instance in the
workload.** Its entire reference obj is **933 bytes**:

```text
   1 .drectve  132   2 .debug$S  180   3 .XBLD$W  16   4 .XBLD$W  16
   5 .rdata      4   =  ff ff ff ff
  [13] ?npos@?$basic_string@DV?$char_traits@D@stlpmtx_std@@…  sec=5  EXTERNAL
```

**1,312 `.ex` bodies, zero emitted functions, zero code bytes.** There is
nothing here for codegen to get wrong and the port does not match it. The whole
distance is the emit predicate plus one 4-byte `.rdata` COMDAT.

**This pair is exactly `CEILING.md` §2.5 and board #213's** *"the two TUs inside
`B∧C` that fail A and that the port already accepts
(`src/system/decomp_pch.cpp`, `src/system/math/vec.cpp`) — modelling the emit
set would reach them without any codegen"*. That was published as
**reachability**. It is now published as **bytes**: `vec.cpp`'s two bodies are
byte-identical to c2's and `decomp_pch.cpp` has none to be wrong about. Boards
**#2381**, **#2382**, **#2383**.

**And it independently reproduces §10's invariant from the other side.** §10
reads *"the non-codegen headroom has been exactly 2 at every reading"*, derived
from `|D∨E| − match`. This lane derives **2** from `fnbyte-*` verdicts with no
factor algebra in it at all. Two instruments, two derivations, same number.

## 5. THE CORPUS IS NOT PINNED, AND IT MOVED 23 COMMITS UNDER THIS LANE

Not commissioned, found while chasing a 16,885 discrepancy between two of this
lane's own scans.

The capture cache's context includes the workload tree token (`capture_cache.rs:366`,
`context.push_str(&format!("tree {}\n", tree.token()))`), so a dc3 commit
invalidates the whole cache and every cached entry is genuinely from the
revision it claims. The two readings are therefore both correct, for two
different corpora:

| metric | dc3 `8423cba1` | dc3 `eb40a361` | Δ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `fnbyte-denominator` | 178,977 | 162,092 | **−16,885 (−9.4 %)** |
| `fnbyte-exact` | 36,232 | 35,774 | −458 |
| `fnbyte-refused` | 131,117 | 114,687 | −16,430 |
| `fnbyte-differs` | 1,879 | 1,879 | **0** |
| `fnbyte-reloc-differs` | 532 | 532 | **0** |
| **FBM** | **0.20244** | **0.22070** | **+0.01826** |
| TU match / mismatch | 19 / 0 | 19 / 0 | **0 / 0** |
| TUs whose class changed | — | — | **0 of 878** |
| TUs whose emitted-function count changed | — | — | **451 of 878** |

**23 dc3 commits, 7 changed files under `src/`, two of them headers**
(`src/system/obj/Object.h`, `src/system/math/Easing.h`). **FBM — the only
continuous number on `STATUS.md` graded by the oracle's own bytes — rose by
+0.0183 from a change nobody in this repo made.** For scale, `w-fnbyte`'s entire
instrument widening (board #322) is recorded on `STATUS.md` as buying
"+0.026 of ratio".

**What this does and does not mean.** The payoff metric did not move and no
verdict flipped: `match` 19, `mismatch` 0, and **0 of 878 TUs changed class**.
Nothing is wrong with the port. What is wrong is that `docs/STATUS.md`'s
generated block quotes `fnbyte-exact 36232 … of 178977` and **that denominator
no longer exists in the tree**, and there was no instrument that would have said
so — the block records a `workload` stamp and the drift check
(`STATUS.md` §6.1's *"0 of 878 workload source blobs differ, checked"*) is a
thing a lane did by hand once, not a standing row. Board **#2392**.

I did **not** regenerate `STATUS.md`. A docs/`work/`-only lane rewriting the
project's headline block on a corpus that moved mid-session would be publishing
a number nobody asked for; the row says what moved and the next lane that runs
`scripts/status.sh --write` picks it up with everything else.

## 9. Found and not taken

### 9.1 The byte-distance-zero pair — **+2 TU conversions for ZERO codegen**

Population **2** (`vec.cpp`, `decomp_pch.cpp`), hand-checked in §3.1, stems 2/2
distinct. **Cost: factor A — an emit-set model (Phase 7) — plus, for `vec.cpp`,
a writer path that composes `.text` COMDATs with non-COMDAT `.data`/`.bss` and a

today (`emit_gy_obj` for TUs with functions, `emit_data_obj` for functionless

statics only. `decomp_pch.cpp` is the cheaper of the two by a wide margin — it
needs the emit predicate and **one** 4-byte `.rdata` COMDAT, and no `.text` at
all.

**This is the highest-payoff row this lane found and it is still not cheap.**
The point of ranking it first is that its codegen price is now *measured* at
zero rather than assumed, which no prior reading of #213 could say.

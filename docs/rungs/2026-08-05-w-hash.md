## 3. What shipped, said plainly

### 3.1 It is a **transcription**, and the module doc says so in those words

Twenty words, two immediate fields, no scheduler, no register allocator, no CFG
builder:

```text
  0000  lbz    r11,0(r3)      the PEELED load: iteration 0's character,
  0004  mr     r9,r3          before the loop is entered at all
  0008  li     r10,<K0>
  000c  cmplwi cr0,r11,0
  0010  bt     2,+0x38        -> the exit
  0014  mulli  r8,r10,<K>     LOOP:
  0018  lbzu   r10,1(r9)      iteration k+1's character, and the induction
  001c  add    r8,r8,r11      the accumulate, over iteration k's character
  0020  mr.    r11,r10        carry k+1 forward AND set cr0 — the loop test
  0024  rotlwi r10,r8,1       \
  0028  divw   r7,r8,r4        |  the signed `%` with its two trap guards,
  002c  addi   r10,r10,-1      |  the predicate INTERLEAVED between `divw`
  0030  mullw  r7,r7,r4        |  and `mullw`
  0034  andc   r6,r4,r10       |
  0038  twi    6,r4,0          |
  003c  subf   r10,r7,r8       |
  0040  twi    5,r6,-1        /
  0044  bf     2,-0x30        the BACK EDGE, on cr0
  0048  mr     r3,r10
  004c  blr
```

Both branch displacements are **constants of the class**, because the body
length is. They still go through `encode_bc` so a body of a different length
cannot silently keep them. Board **#760**.

**No new `Selected` variant was needed.** Every branch is self-relative, the body
takes no relocation, mints no symbol and defines no label, so it reaches the
emitter as an ordinary `Selected::Plain`. That is worth stating because
`w-loop` §5.3's objection to building the label relaxation early was that *there
is no caller* — and the caller, when it arrived, needed nothing from the branch
machinery at all.

### 3.2 The refusals, each with a measured counterexample

Not one of these is conservatism; each is a cell where real `c2` emits something
else, and the rung records what:

* **the pointer formal anywhere but slot 0**, the divisor anywhere but slot 1, a
  third formal, or a **directly-walked formal** with no local copy. `swap`,
  `divfirst`, `p3` and `nocast` each re-plan the whole block layout — the
  accumulator coalesces into `r3`, the guard becomes a `beqlr`, and the closing
  `mr r3,rA` disappears. Those are three different **block plans**, not three
  register fields;
* `K` a **power of two** (`rlwinm`), `K == 1` (nothing at all), `K == 0` (`li`),
  `K` **negative** (`c2` rewrites `x + a*(-3)` as `x - a*3`, so the `add` becomes
  a `subf`), `K` **above `simm16`** (`addis`/`ori`/`mullw`);
* an **unsigned** divisor (`divwu` + **one** `twi`, no predicate) or a **literal**
  one (`li` + `divw` + `mulli` + `subf`, no `twi` at all) — both measured, both
  a different spine, neither shipped;
* `/` in place of `%`, a stride other than 1, a wider element type, an
  accumulator init outside `simm16`, and `*u > 0` in place of `*u != 0`;
* **`/Ox` and `/O2`** — §5.2.

### 3.3 The label gate, and why `None` rather than a number

`IlFunction::label_slots` returns **`None`** for the shape. `w-loop` measured
seed-free, 17 probes, 5/5 anchor control on every row, that a **leaf** loop
charges the compiler-label counter `+1..+4` — never 1 — and that `?HashString`'s
own pointer-walk shape charges **+3**.

`Some(4)` would have been a rule fitted to one source spelling of a class whose
members are indistinguishable where this port can look: `do/while`,
`for(;;)`+`break` and a backward `goto` emit the **identical 24 bytes** and
charge **+1, +3, +1**. So the three-valued gate refuses instead, and
`IlBundle::functions` turns that into *a TU pairing this shape with a framed
function is rejected; a TU without one is admitted* — exactly the boundary
`w-loop` §5.1 measured. Board **#746**, discharged.

### 4.1 R8 is the row worth reading, and it lost for a reason worth stating

R8 predicted no conversion and named #10 and #11 as the stoppers. **It is wrong
about the outcome and right about the mechanism**, and both halves matter.

It is wrong because the question it answered — *can the loop's register
allocation be derived as a rule* — is **not** the question a conversion needed.
The answer to that is still no: `hashgrid.py` shows `c2` re-planning the whole
block layout on four separate axes, and §3.2 refuses all four. What converted
the TU is that the class was drawn narrowly enough for the allocation to be a
**constant** rather than a function, and then graded over the axes that remain
free. That is `w-tu1`'s technique and it is the second time it has worked.

**The registered bias also lost, in the direction I wanted.** R3 was written as
*"I want this to win"* because it is the reading under which the trap machinery
is a bounded rule; it won. R8 was written as *"I want this to lose"*; it lost.
Both are on the page.

### 4.2 Three claims of my own that the grid refuted, corrected in place

An earlier draft of the **shipped module doc** asserted that the sentinel
spelling (`*u` vs `*u != 0`), the accumulate's operand order and the pointer's
source spelling were *"the same IL"*. `c2` emits **byte-identical `.text`** for
all three — which is exactly why the assumption was easy, and exactly why it had
to be graded. Each is a different IL production, all three refuse, and the doc
now carries the three byte sequences that separate them **instead of** the old
claim. Board **#768**.

> *"Emits the same bytes"* is not *"is the same IL"*. A lane that widened on the
> byte evidence would have shipped three unmeasured productions.

## 5. The measurements, and the three that are worth more than the conversion

### 5.1 The signed `%` spine is separable — and its schedule is **not** universal

31 cells, `work/w-hash/divgrid.py`, two anchor controls (`plain-add` must be two
words; `s-mod-var` must carry `Sort.cpp`'s own vocabulary). Board **#766**.

```text
  s-mod-var    a % b        rotlwi divw addi mullw andc twi subf twi   <- Sort's own order
  s-div-var    a / b        rotlwi divw addi twi andc twi              <- predicate shared,
                                                                          SCHEDULE not
  u-div-var    unsigned /   divwu twi                                  <- ONE trap, no predicate
  u-mod-var    unsigned %   divwu twi mullw subf
  s-mod-k7     a % 7        li divw mulli subf                         <- NO trap at all
  s-mod-k2     a % 2        srawi addze rlwinm subf                    <- power of two
  s-madmod     (c+r*127)%i  mulli add TWI rotlwi divw addi mullw andc subf twi
  s-mod-lhsk   100 % b      li TWI rotlwi divw addi mullw andc subf twi
```

The last two are the finding. **A computed dividend in a straight-line body
hoists `twi 6` to the front of the spine; `?HashString`'s loop, whose dividend is
also computed, does not.** Two placements, discriminator unknown, and the order
is emitted **as measured for this class and claimed for no other**. A rule fitted
to two placements here would be the eleventh refuted placement rule, joining
`w-pair` §4's six and `leaf_store.rs`'s four.

### 5.2 `/Ox` emits a **different body**, and the refusal had to move into the census

Board **#763**. Twenty-one words against `/O1`'s twenty:

```text
  /O1  … bc  · mulli lbzu add mr.        · rotlwi divw addi mullw andc twi subf twi · bc · mr blr
  /Ox  … bclr· rlwinm lbzu twi subf add mr. · rotlwi divw addi mullw andc subf twi  · cmpli bc bclr
```

`ret * 127` strength-reduces to `rlwinm` + `subf`, the zero-divisor `twi` hoists
to the third slot, and the loop closes on an explicit `cmpli` instead of the
record form — **so the branch reads a different condition-register field**.
Emitting the `/O1` body there would be four wrong words and a wrong `BI`.

**The refusal is raised in the census, not only in codegen, and that is the
load-bearing half.** In codegen alone it was a real census/gate disagreement —
`census_gate.rs` went 1 → 3 and said so, i.e. an error term on the published
numerator. It has its own key `ptr-walk-loop-not-o1` beside `opt-mode`, because
the two say different things: `opt-mode` is *never verified at this mode*, this
is *verified, and `c2` emits a different body*.

This is the strongest witness yet for `OPT_MODE.md`'s already-refuted
register-field rule: outside a straight-line chain the modes differ in the
**body**, not in an allocation.

### 5.3 `.sy` kind `0x03`, and the `tid` gate that would have refused the very local it needed

Board **#764**. 21 cells, one type axis at a time, each record read with nothing
left over. `sy.rs`'s own module header warns hardest about this: *six widths were
wrong there and every one of them "agreed on small, simple, hand-written
declarations."*

```text
  local           tag kind size flags   tid
  int              86   01    4  0001   0x74
  unsigned         86   02    4  0001   0x75
  const int        86   01    4  0001   0x1000
  float            86   05    4  0001   0x40
  unsigned char*   86   03    4  0001   0x0420   <== the shape this rung needs
  const char*      86   03    4  0001   0x1001
  int*             86   03    4  0001   0x0474
  void*            86   03    4  0001   0x0403
  char**           86   03    4  0001   0x1000
  int(*)(int)      86   04    4  0001   0x1002   <== kind 4, a FUNCTION pointer
  int[4]           86   06   16  0001   0x1000   <== kind 6
  struct{int;int;} 86   06    8  0081   0x1002   <== kind 6
  int,  &x taken   86   01    4  0021   0x74     <== flags bit 5
  char*,&u taken   86   03    4  0021   0x0470   <== flags bit 5
```

> ### The `tid` is the **POINTEE**, and gating it was the natural mistake. The header says constructed types live above `0x1000`; `const char*` (0x1001) and `char**` (0x1000) agree — and `unsigned char*` reads **0x0420**. A rule read off two witnesses in one file would have **refused exactly the local this rung exists to admit.**

That is board **#644**'s shape — *a population with an unstated restriction* —
caught by varying the axis the first probes held fixed. `ptr_locals` is a
**separate list**, not a widening of `int_locals`: `assign.rs` folds an `int`
local into the expression that reads it, and a pointer walked by `lbzu` is not
folded at all. Merging them would hand the substituting parse a pointer.

### 5.4 `a * k` over the whole constant axis

Board **#767**. 38 constants × both commutations at `/O1`:

```text
  k == 0                  li rD,0
  k == 1                  nothing at all
  k == -1                 neg
  k == 2^n                rlwinm
  k == -(2^n)             neg ; rlwinm
  fits simm16, otherwise  mulli            <== the only regime shipped, and only
  wider                   addis ; ori ; mullw     inside the loop class
```

Shipped with a **second clause**: `c2` emits one `mulli` for a negative
non-power-of-two too, *and* rewrites `x + a*(-3)` as `x - a*3`, so the
accumulate's `add` becomes a `subf`. Two facts, two gates — the predicate is
about the multiply, the clause is about the fold. **Lifting `mul-by-lit` in the
general chain is not done here**; `chain.rs`'s stated reason is now known to be
right for six of seven regimes and wrong for the largest, and repairing it is a
rung with its own cross product.

## 6. #747, discharged — the fixture, the control, and the mutation that proves the gate

Board **#769**. `w-loop` §6 registered before anything shipped that the breaking
shape is **a loop leaf followed by a framed function**, and that **neither
standing instrument can produce it**: `expr_sweep.sh` generates single-function
TUs and `mode_cross.sh` crosses that same corpus, so **both would grade a wrong
label charge green**.

`fixtures/cpp/whash_loop_then_framed.cpp` is that TU, and
`fixtures/cpp/whash_ptr_walk_loop.cpp` — the identical loop with no framed
function beside it — is its **separating control**.

> ### The must-fail mutation was RUN, not described. Replacing `label_slots`' `None` with `Some(1)` turns the fixture from `NotImplemented` into a **live `mismatch`** against real `c2.dll`, while the control stays `match`.

Real `c2` mints `$M2564`/`$M2565`/`$T2566` for the framed `?z9`. The mutated port
charges the loop 1 where `c2` charges 4, so the triple lands **three low** — six
wrong bytes in an obj that still links, which is board **#263**'s exact shape.
The mutation is recorded in the source at the gate it proves.

This is the **fourth** instance of *"the corpus cannot express the failure"* and
the **first found ahead of the defect rather than after it**. The other three:
the sweep's three-leaf enumeration that let a live wrong emit sit on master for
255 commits; a grid whose tiers all forced ≥ 2 symbols; and `w-convert`'s guard
that no corpus could separate from its absence.

  their full cross product and everything else is a constant.

## 9. Found and not taken

1. **The `twi 6` placement has two regimes and no discriminator** (§5.1). It is
   the smallest unexplained residual this lane produced and it blocks the div/mod
   leaf.
2. **`chain.rs`'s `expr-out-of-class-mul-by-lit` reason is wrong for the largest
   regime** (§5.4). The repair is a bounded rung with a 38-cell grid already
   measured.
3. **`/Ox`'s loop body is a different, larger class** — its multiply is
   strength-reduced by a rule that depends on `K`'s bit pattern, so it is not a
   second transcription.
4. **The three refused spellings emit identical bytes from different IL** (§4.2).
   Whoever widens them should note that a byte comparison cannot tell them apart,
   so the grid has to be an IL grid.

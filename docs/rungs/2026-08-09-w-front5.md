## 5. THE COUNTERFACTUAL — the repair is a NET REGRESSION

Registered in PREREG §2.1 **before the build**. One clause removed
(`gl_defined_names_framed`'s `runs[k].2.len() <= INLINE_NAME_MAX` refusal),

tip binary confirmed bit-identical to the base binary afterwards. Full output:
`work/w-front5/COUNTERFACTUAL.txt`.

### 5.1 What it does NOT do

`match` **22**, `mismatch` **0**, and **0 of 878 TUs change `class`**. No TU
converts, and none regresses to a wrong emit. P1 and P5 hold.

### 5.2 What it costs

| key | base | counterfactual |
|---|---:|---:|
| `fnbyte-exact` | 35,798 | **35,797 (−1)** |
| `fnbyte-refused` | 114,634 | 114,635 |

**One named TU carries all of it: `src/system/synth_xbox/FFT.cpp`**, whose
`fnbyte-exact` goes 1 → 0 and whose `fn_in_class` goes **116 → 15**.

**The mechanism, in code.** `gl_defined_names` is read in two places, not one.
`Bindings::per_record` is the binding; `bind::defined_name_set` is the ground
set the `W-INLFENCE` callee test uses, and its own doc says why the stop
clauses are load-bearing there: *"The set is a subset of the truth and never a
superset, which is what makes a membership test sound in the refusing direction
on a TU that does not fully bind."* Letting the walk run further makes the
subset **larger**, so `callee_defined_here` matches more callees and the fence
refuses more bodies. **A widening at one call site is a tightening at the
other.** Any repair of the name clause must move both sites together or pay
this; that is a two-site coupled change, not the one-line widening the clause's
own comment reads like.

### 5.3 What it reveals — a SECOND gate behind the first, on both TUs

Under the counterfactual `src/Main.cpp` and `src/xdk/nuispeech/mmio.cpp` bind,
and both come back with `gate_causes = [body-out-of-class,
unclaimed-gl-symbol]`. **`unclaimed-gl-symbol` is new on both** and appears in
no published price for either. This is `w-pool` #2560 — *"a gate-cause set is
not a price; `body-out-of-class` being a TU's only cause means the reader is
the only gate that FIRES, not the only thing OWED"* — arriving from the
binding side: on a TU that does not bind, four of `decode_causes`' gates are
**vacuous**, and paying the binding is what makes them speak.

Of the other thirteen, **none** binds: seven fall through to
`gl-stop-26-introduced` and six to `bind-record-count-ne-segments`. **2 of 15**
is the whole reach of the repair.

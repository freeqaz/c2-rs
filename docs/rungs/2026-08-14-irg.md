### 1.0 The success criterion, stated before the claims

**Required-zero byte delta.** This lane converts zero TUs *by design*. A
conversion would mean behaviour changed, which is this kind's failure mode, not
its prize. Grading is a line-for-line identity diff of the 878-TU scan's
`gap-metric` keys, its 878 per-TU verdict lines, and every gate lane's
fixture-verdict counts, base against tip. **Any byte that moved = FAILED**,
whatever else the lane achieved, reported in that word with the moved byte
named.

Absence is not the check. Every row below is graded **positively on content**:
the scan must print its `gap-metric` block and be digit-for-digit equal; the
gate must print `graded > 0` per lane and the per-lane counts must be equal; the
workspace run must print a pass count **and** a target count. A missing number
is a MISS, never a zero.

### 1.2 What item G is, predicted before it was written

One new module `crates/c2-core/src/codegen/fold.rs`, **emitting nothing**, plus
a call from each of **two already-byte-exact classes that sit in two different
bands** — which is what makes the predicate a shared one rather than a
tautology at its single call site:

1. **`FoldBand`** — §3.5's three measured bands and no fourth: `Branchless`
   (band 1, an arithmetic select), `ConditionalReturn` (band 2, `bclr`),
   `Branch` (band 3, a real forward `bc`).
2. **`BandVerdict`** — what this port can *decide*, which is deliberately not
   the same type. `Is(FoldBand)`, plus `BranchlessOrConditionalReturn` (board
   **#187**'s declined cost model, carried as **one** value so it can never be
   narrowed by accident) and `Unmeasured` (an arm-end combination §3.5's table
   has no row for).
3. **`FoldShape`** — the inputs §3.5's own band rules are stated over: the two
   arms' ends, whether both arms are constants, and whether the relation is an
   equality. Nothing else, because nothing else appears in the rules.
4. **The gate** — `FoldShape::admit(band, site)`: the class states the band it
   was drawn in and the shape must decide to exactly that band. Both non-answers
   refuse, with different messages, and neither is ever narrowed to a band.

**Predicted clients, both already byte-exact against real `c2.dll`:**
`codegen::cond_tail` (band **3** — both arms end in a tail call to a different
external, §4.1's thirty-six published bytes) and `codegen::pool_free_list`
(band **2** — `bclr 12,26`, §3.5's own `?Pool::Alloc`/`?Pool::Free` rows). A
disagreement in either direction would be a defect in the new mechanism, visible
immediately against published bytes.

## 2. What it admits, and what it refuses

### 2.1 Item G, in five lines

`crates/c2-core/src/codegen/fold.rs`, one new module, zero new dependencies,
**emits nothing and decodes nothing**:

* **`FoldBand`** — §3.5's three measured bands (`Branchless`,
  `ConditionalReturn`, `Branch`) and no fourth, each with §3.5's own number and
  §3.5's own words. This is the *record* item G's title asks for: the bands as a
  thing the port can name, instead of a sentence in five module headers.
* **`FoldShape`** — the four facts §3.5's band rules are *stated over*: where
  each arm ends (`Transfer` / `Epilogue` / `Join`), whether both arms are
  constants, and whether the relation is an equality. Nothing else, because
  nothing else appears in the rules. **Symmetric in the two arms**, asserted
  rather than assumed.
* **`FoldShape::band`** — those rules read back as a decision, in §3.5's own
  clause order: band 3's two clauses first, then the unmeasured combination,
  then band 1's exclusion.
* **`BandVerdict`** — what this port can *decide*, deliberately **not** the same
  type as `FoldBand`. `Is(band)`, plus `BranchlessOrConditionalReturn` (board
  **#187**) and `Unmeasured`.
* **`FoldShape::admit(class_band, site)`** — the gate. The class states the band
  it was drawn in; a shape that does not decide to exactly that band is refused
  **before a byte is emitted**.

And at the two call sites: `cond_tail::cond_pair_parts` admits at
`FoldBand::Branch` as its first act, before `plan_cond_pair` is even asked; and
`pool_free_list::pool_free_list_text` admits at `FoldBand::ConditionalReturn`,
straight after the mode clause.

### 2.2 Why "band 3 or refuse" is this item's *sentence* and not its *rule*

Item G's concrete demand is written for `cflow-if-1`, where band 3 is the
interesting answer. Its **title** is *"per accepted shape"*, and that is the half
that binds, because this crate ships **two byte-exact classes in two different
bands**:

| class | band | witness |
|---|---|---|
| `codegen::cond_tail` (`?MemFree`) | **3**, a real forward `bc` | §4.1's thirty-six published bytes |
| `codegen::pool_free_list` (`?Pool::Alloc`, `?Pool::Free`) | **2**, `bclr 12,26` | §3.5's own last two `bclr` rows, and `work/w-pool2/ref/Pool.obj` |

A module-wide "band 3 or refuse" would have **refused a shipped, graded class**
— which would have moved bytes and failed this lane on its own criterion. So the
band is a **parameter** of the check. That is also what stops the predicate being
a tautology at one call site: the same rules must come out `Branch` for one
client and `ConditionalReturn` for the other, off different inputs, and each must
be refused by the other's band.

### 2.3 The one thing this lane refuses to decide, carried as a value

Board **#187** — the band-1 ↔ band-2 boundary — is a c2 cost model, **OPEN and
DECLINED**, §3.5's eighteen rows *"fitted by every cell and tested by none"*.
This lane does not settle it and did not try. What it does is make the decline
**representable**:

* §3.5 states band 1 as a **necessary** condition — *"reached **only when** the
  relation is `==`/`!=` **and** both arms are constants **and** the constant pair
  is cheap"* — and declines the third conjunct. A necessary condition can be
  falsified by one failing conjunct and never verified without all three.
* So `band()` **never returns `Is(Branchless)`**. An ordered relation or a
  non-constant arm *falsifies* band 1 and, with both arms at the epilogue, leaves
  band 2 — **decided, with no cost model read**. An equality with two constant
  arms leaves `BranchlessOrConditionalReturn`, which refuses.
* `BranchlessOrConditionalReturn` is **one** variant on purpose. Two would be a
  place for a later lane to write #187's fitted rule with the fitting left out.

**This turns #2596 from a paragraph into a check.** That row's claim is that
`pool_free_list`'s band 1 is unreachable *by the class's own precondition*, so
the class never has to read #187. `pool_free_list`'s new test asserts its verdict
is band 2 **and is not** the undecided pair, plus the counterfactual: the same
shape with two constant arms *is* the declined pair and refuses outright. A
widening that ever admitted such a body surfaces there, not in an obj.

### 2.4 What it refuses, by name

| refusal | why it is a refusal and not caution |
|---|---|
| a shape that decides to a band **other than** the class's | §3.5's headline: **6 of 7** leaf `cflow-if-1` bodies emit no branch at all (board **#186**). A band-3 lowering run on a band-2 body emits a branch a real obj does not carry |
| an equality with two constant arms, both at the epilogue | board **#187**'s declined cost model. `?f_eq59` (`a==b → 5:9`) takes a `bclr`, `?f_eqzk` (`a==0 → 5:9`) folds to nothing — the same constant pair, opposite bands. Refused rather than guessed |
| one arm joining, the other returning | §3.5's eighteen-row table has **no row** for that combination. An unmeasured band is not a band, and this refusal message says so in a different sentence from the one above — the two non-answers are never merged |

The third is the absence-is-not-evidence rule this crate already applies to
`CondSource::Unknown` and `CrEffect::Unmodelled`, applied to a table instead of
to a decoder.

### 2.5 What it delegates rather than restates

* `cond_tail::branch_sense` stays the only reader of *"which `(BO, bit)` an IL
  relation becomes"*, and `Relation::of` reads §3.5's equality clause **through
  it** — the relation is `==`/`!=` exactly when the branch it becomes tests the
  `EQ` bit. The six relations are not enumerated a second time;
* `select::out_of_class` stays the one refusal constructor;
* `codegen::labels` is untouched: this module has no notion of a branch site, a
  target or a displacement, and adds no second fixup list;
* the two `c2_il` shape headers that carry this fact in prose — including the one
  that calls itself *"a band predicate spelled as a syntactic one, which is what
  §6.2 item G asks for"* — are **not edited**. `crates/c2-il` is peer lane
  `w-deaccept`'s, and the emitter-side check is a second *asking* of one rule,
  not a second *statement* of it (`pool_free_list_text`'s mode clause, #1638/
  #1710, is the precedent).

### 2.6 What it does NOT build

| item | state at tip | why not here |
|---|---|---|
| board **#187**'s band-1 ↔ band-2 rule | **still OPEN, still declined** | it needs the ~30-cell probe varying only the constant pair over a fixed relation that #187 has asked for since 2026-08-04. This lane represents the decline; it does not remove it |
| **F** — values live across block boundaries | absent, **and not re-priced** | §1.4. `w-dagorder` (#3067) and `w-dagclients` (#3099) moved its price twice in two days with a fourth merger unread (#3103) |
| a band-1 emitter | absent | nothing in this crate emits an arithmetic select, and `FoldBand::Branchless` is a name in the record, not a lowering |
| a loop's fold record | absent | §3.7's rotations are behind board #3082/#3089's fence (`label_slots → None`, #746), and item A still has one client class |
| a third client | absent | `guard_ret_chain`, `close_call_chain`, `alloc_init_or_fail`, `osf_handle_guard` and the four loop emitters all branch on a guard; none was re-expressed here. See §5 item 1 |

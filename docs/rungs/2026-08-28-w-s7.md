## 1. The three deliverables, answered

**1. What S7 does.** `FUN_10b7e032` @ `0x10b7e032`, 225 B, called once per
function from `0x10b7e701`. Ten depth-1 passes, and it is **two stages wearing
one address**: `test DWORD PTR [eax+0x20],0x1000` at **`0x10b7e03a`**, over the
*symbol* record reached through `func+0`, selects between two complete unwind
routes. `FUN_10b3421b` branches on the same bit at `0x10b3424e`, and the unwind
emitter `0x10c217fd` has exactly one caller on each side. Full table with per-row
gates: `WB_S7_FINDINGS.md` §1.1, or `f0_pipeline.py --s7`.

**2. What it can reorder.** In the image: a lot — `0x10c21b03` moves `0x1b`
label tuples with `0x10bd38b0`, `0x10b35c78` moves them with
`0x10bd3852`+`0x10bd3815`, the mode-0 merger runs. **On this project's
configurations: three passes, none of them the two splicers** —
`0x10c12099` (mints a `0x284` through the `0x10bd3824` *pointer*, deletes the
original), `0x10c275a7` (mints a `0x2eb` through the `0x10bd3815` pointer), and
`0x10b36169` → `0x10c1dc58`, the late jump-table expansion. §4.1.

**3. Is `0x10b35c78` reachable here?** **YES — and this rung's first draft said
NO on an unchecked premise.** Its only two callers are `0x10b7e032` and
`0x10c21b03`; `0x10c21b03`'s only caller is `0x10b7e032`; both sites are inside
the gate. The gate is measured **clear on 2,946 of 2,946 functions over 384
fixtures** (`w-restim`'s `sched0` site `0x10b7e00c` sits inside the range
`0x10b7dfea` skips when the bit is **set**, and `sched0` fired on every
function). **But the bit is settable, at the workload's own flags, by `__try`**
— three cells through the existing `c2rs stage counts` tap, `WB_S7_FINDINGS.md`
§4.3:

| cell (`/O1 /Oi /EHsc /GS- /c`) | fns | `sched0` | `after0` | bit set |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| control, three ordinary functions | 3 | 3 | 3 | **0** |
| `ctl_a` verbatim + `__try/__except` + `__try/__finally` | 3 | **1** | 3 | **2** |
| `ctl_a` verbatim + C++ `try/catch` | 2 | 2 | 2 | **0** |

Per-function (`ctl_a` is byte-identical source in the control and in the SEH TU
and still reaches `sched0` there); **not** C++ EH; obj-corroborated
(`s7_seh.obj` carries `__C_specific_handler`/`.pdata`/`.xdata`, `s7_ctl.obj`
carries none of the three).

> **The splicer executed zero times in 2,946 function compilations, and so did

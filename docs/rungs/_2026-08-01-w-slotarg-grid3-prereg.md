# W-SLOTARG — grid 3 is the OUT-OF-SAMPLE test, predicted before capture

`scripts/slotarg_rule.py` was refined **four times** against capture grids 1 and
2 (360 cells) until it agreed with c2 on all 360. A rule refined against the data
it is then scored on is **fitted, not measured** — §9.14.7's disease — so the
claim "this is c2's rule" is worth nothing without an out-of-sample test.

Grid 3 (`scripts/slotarg_grid3.py`, **402 cells**) varies four axes that appear
in neither earlier grid:

* the base formal **clobbered by a MIDDLE walk word** — grids 1–2 always parked
  the base at the lowest slot, so the clobbering `li` was always the *last* walk
  word and "hoist ahead of the walk" was never distinguished from "hoist ahead
  of the clobber";
* offsets **straddling the signed-16-bit boundary from both sides** — `0x7ffc`,
  `0x8000`, `0x8004`, `0xfffc`, `0x10004`, rather than only `0/8/0x8000/0x10000`;
* **arities 6, 7 and 8**, past the 5 both earlier grids stop at;
* a **two-step designator whose steps straddle the boundary** (`0x7ffc + 8`),
  which no single-step cell can produce and which is the shape §9.17.5 showed is
  summed rather than repeated.

Predictions for all 402 cells were generated by the rule and frozen **before
`grid3.cod` existed**:

```

```

|---|---|---|
| **O1** | cells the rule predicts exactly, mnemonic + registers + immediate | **370 / 402 (92 %)**, [300, 402] |

| **O3** | the boundary offsets `0x7ffc`/`0x8004` need no new arm (they are just wide/not-wide) | YES |
| **O4** | arity 6–8 needs no new arm | YES |

**If O1 lands below its floor the rule is refused, not patched again** — that is
the decline rule registered in `_2026-08-01-w-slotarg-prereg.md`, and a fifth
refinement against a third grid would be fitting with extra steps.

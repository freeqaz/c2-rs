## 1. What was wrong, and what was actually at risk

`crates/c2-core/src/coff/label.rs` shipped

```rust
pub const LABEL_SEED_GAP: u32 = 9;
```

Read **R3** (`w-read-r3`, board #3388) measured the quantity over 22 cells as

```text
gap = 7 + 2·[/Og] + 1·[/GF ∧ a string literal pooled in the data phase]
```

so `9` is one cell of a formula, presented as a compilation-independent
constant.

**Nothing the port emits today is wrong, and this lane re-checked that rather
than inheriting it.** What was live is the **licence**: the constant *reads* as
compilation-independent, and every caller inherits that reading. The repair is
therefore not a value change — it is making the dependency impossible to
inherit.

## 2. The three questions, answered before the fix was written

### Q1 — how does the port learn `/Og` and `/GF` at the seam where the seed is computed?

Traced end to end. `plan_labels` has **two** live call sites, `writer.rs:224`
(`emit_comdat_obj`) and `writer.rs:936` (`emit_obj`), plus one further consumer
of the constant in `ehscope.rs:179`. Neither writer receives any struct carrying
argv; `coff::Function`'s every field is a per-function fact derived from IL body
content.

* **`[/Og]` IS derivable, and from the IL alone.** The per-function optimization
  word is in `.ex` (`c2_il::IlBundle::opt_words`), and `opt_word_mode` admits
  exactly `/Ox` (`0x00a00005`) and `/O1` (`0x00200005`) plus their
  `fp_contract`-off spellings. **Both imply `/Og`.** `/Od` (`0x00800005`) and
  `#pragma optimize("",off)` are refused upstream. So the shipped `9` is the
  formula at `og = true` — correct today, and correct *because of a refusal*
  that the constant did not mention.
* **`/GF` is NOT derivable and never reaches the port.** It is not a field of
  `IlBundle`, not a bit of the optimization word, and not a member of
  `plan::PlanInputs` — which carries exactly one field,
  `function_level_linking`, and whose own doc states the pattern: the argv-derived
  facts the IL does not record. `/GF` is that same kind of fact and nobody has
  plumbed it. In the harness, `gap/scan.rs:97` collapses the whole flag vector to
  a single `gy` bool before the port is constructed; **the raw flags reach only
  the reference side.**

### Q2 — is "a string literal pooled in the data phase" decidable at seed time?

**No, not soundly, and the obvious detector is wrong.** `gl_string_comdat_names`
(`crates/c2-il/src/func/gl.rs:2058`) returns every `??_C@…` record in `.gl` —
but `docs/LABEL_COUNTER.md` §8.1 records that a literal **in a function body
costs 0** while one **in the data phase costs +1**. A non-empty set therefore
does not mean the charge is taken: the detector **over-approximates**.

The sound signal is named in #3404 so the next lane does not re-derive it: a
data-phase pooled literal needs a **TU-scope data object holding a relocation
against a `??_C@…` COMDAT** — `DataTu`'s `relocs`, not the `.gl` name set.
Nothing here builds that detector, because nothing here can grade it: every such
TU is refused before the writers, three independent ways.

So the term stays a **caller-supplied input** whose doc says the caller must not
guess it. **A formula with a stated undecidable term beats a guessed one.**

### Q3 — what can byte-judge the repair today?

Two things, and neither is a fixture:

1. **Required-zero** on everything currently matching — `scripts/gate.sh`, base
   re-measured on this tree at `9b9530791` and compared to the tip (§4).
2. **The 22-cell grid**, as portable arithmetic. R3's instrument reads the seed
   straight out of the captured `.gl` (`u32_le(.gl[7..11])`) and subtracts it, so
   the seed cannot hide inside the answer; this rung pins the *formula* those
   cells produced, cell by cell, in `cargo test`. The instrument's own confound
   (#3390 — the first grid put a function ahead of the subject and read
   `9 + the functions in front`) and its `before == 0` / VOID guard are
   untouched.

## 3. What shipped

* **`SeedGapModel`** — the three read coefficients (`7`, `2`, `1`), with
  `READ` as the measured instance and `gap()` a `const fn`.
* **`SeedGapInputs`** — the two compilation facts, as named settable
  parameters (`docs/GOAL_DECISION_2026-08-21.md` § AMENDED). Its
  `PORT_ADMITTED` constant carries, in its own doc, **the two upstream refusals
  that make `9` right today** — not as prose about the future, but as the reason
  the value is correct now.
* **`LABEL_SEED_GAP`** is now `SeedGapModel::READ.gap(&SeedGapInputs::PORT_ADMITTED)`.
  Still exactly `9`.
* **`global_optimizer_of_opt_word`** — `[/Og]` from the IL's own word.
  `Some(true)` for the accepted set, `Some(false)` for `OPT_WORD_OD` and
  `#pragma optimize("",off)` in **both** spellings, **`None`** for anything
  unread. `/Os`, `/Ot`, `/Oy`, `/Ob2` stay `None`: R3 read their *gap*, nobody
  has captured their *word*, and mapping them would be inventing a reading.
* **`plan_labels_with_gap`** and **`EhLabels::of_single_function_tu_with_gap`**
  — the settable forms. The old entry points are these at `LABEL_SEED_GAP`, so
  **no call site moved** and the diff stays inside the label machinery.
* **One `debug_assert` in `PortC2::build`** tying the resolved mode to
  `PORT_ADMITTED.global_optimizer`. Deliberately not a refusal: it can only fire
  on a word the mode loop already accepted, so in release it duplicates an
  existing check — but the gate's **DEBUG-lane row runs all 18 lanes through a
  debug-profile `c2rs` (6,948 verdicts)**, so it executes on every graded
  fixture. That is what converts #3388's documentary warning into something the
  gate can fail on.
* **`c2_il::OPT_WORD_OD` / `OPT_WORD_PRAGMA_OFF`** — values its own word table
  has documented since the table was read, which nothing could refer to.
  `opt_word_mode` is untouched and still refuses both. **Naming a word admits
  nothing.**

## 5. What this rung did NOT do

* **`/GF` is not plumbed from argv.** Declined, priced two-sided in the prereg
  before any code changed, and recorded as **#3404**. It would need a new field
  on `PlanInputs` and `PortC2` threaded through `build` into both writers and set
  at four harness call sites — one of them `gap/scan.rs`, adjacent to lane
  `w-s0`'s live surface — and it buys **zero coverage today**, because the shape
  is refused upstream on every path reaching `plan_labels` and **no lane in
  `scripts/lanes.txt` passes `/GF` explicitly**.
* **No detector for the data-phase-pooled-string conjunct** (§2 Q2). Shipping an
  over-approximation would replace a fitted *constant* with a fitted *rule*,
  which is strictly worse — the constant is at least visibly wrong.
* **The `/Gy` `+3` is untouched.** It is still fitted from 11 TUs with an unread
  identity — and it is a **charge**, which is exactly what #3387 says is not a
  constant to be substituted (`charge(TU)` = the number of objects c2 constructs
  itself; 42 of 163 sites sit on loop back edges). The module banner now says so
  explicitly instead of grouping it with the gap.
* **The mechanism is still unread.** `P_LABEL.md` §4 bounds the candidate sites
  to five once-per-TU allocations and shows the gap is mode-dependent — which
  refutes "nine fixed allocations" but does not attribute a single unit.
  Attributing them needs a live tap on `0x10b97de5`, which no lane has built.
  **`SeedGapModel` is knowingly a fit to a read grid, one level better than the
  literal `9` and one level short of the mechanism**, and its own doc says that
  rather than presenting the arithmetic as understood.

## 6. Corrections to the dispatch brief

Past lanes have corrected every coordinator-supplied figure; this one is no
exception, and the corrections were registered in the prereg **before** the
work, not selected afterwards.

1. **Two cited paths do not exist.** The brief names
   `docs/whitebox/LABEL_COUNTER.md` and `docs/whitebox/scripts/gt_label_seedgap.py`;
   the real paths are **`docs/LABEL_COUNTER.md`** and
   **`scripts/gt_label_seedgap.py`**.
2. **`/O1` and `/O2` imply `/GF`** — board **#3403**. The brief frames the
   defect around `/Od` and treats the third term as a corner case. `/Od` is
   indeed the exposed lane for `[/Og]`, but the third term's **first conjunct is
   ON in 8 of the 18 graded lanes**, and the workload itself compiles `/O1`.
   What holds the charge at zero is the **second** conjunct — a thinner margin
   than the brief's framing implies.
3. **The formula's terms are not disassembly-derived**, so the DISCLOSURE row the
   brief asked for could not be written as instructed. All three coefficients come
   from `gt_label_seedgap.py`'s obj grid against real `cl.exe`; **no address,
   value, bit position or field width is copied into `crates/`.** Row
   `W-SEEDGAP-1` is filed as **`route:`** and says exactly that — which is what
   `DISCLOSURE.md`'s own closing rule §5 asks for.
4. **The brief expected the third term might be undecidable and asked me to say
   so precisely.** It is — but the reason is not the one implied. It is not that
   the port lacks *any* signal; it is that the available signal
   (`gl_string_comdat_names`) is an **over-approximation**, which is a more
   dangerous shape than an absent one, because it looks like a decision
   procedure.

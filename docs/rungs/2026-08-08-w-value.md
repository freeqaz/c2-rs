## 3. What ships, and the theorem it rests on (#1940)

`mcall::eat_call_value` is a **bracket walk**, which is board #1534's own
prescription word for word — *"a sink that consumes `26 … 4C` **entire** — a
bracket walk in `mcall.rs`, not a width row"*. `BD` and the intrinsic call token
`40` each open a region, `4C` closes one, and the production ends at the `4C`
that closes a **`BD`-opened region at depth 0**. It reports one of three things:

| | what it means | what `parse_expr` does |
|---|---|---|
| `Value(c)` | the return TYPE is a class `ValueClass` names | push `c` |
| `Void` | class nibble 7 — the call leaves nothing | push nothing, **and `cstack_ok` stays true** |
| `Opaque` | a value is left, its class is not modeled | push a placeholder, clear `cstack_ok` |

Three variants and not two, for the reason §4.4 measured.

> ### THE ACCEPTANCE THEOREM
>
> Every path that reaches byte `0x26` inside `parse_expr` returned `Err`
> **unconditionally** before this lane. The new arm pushes no `IlOp` — `IlOp`
> has no call variant and this lane did not add one — and the end-of-walk poison
> re-raises the *same* `mcall::classify` block the old line returned. So the arm
> can only replace one `Err` with another `Err`: **`parse_expr` cannot return
> `Ok` on any body it refuses today.**
>
> It is registered in the PREREG as a claim to be falsified by measurement, and
> the three levels that could have falsified it are in §1's table. It is also a
> **test** rather than a comment: `the_value_model_never_turns_a_refusal_into_an_acceptance`
> re-runs nineteen captured probes through `parse_segment_detail` *and*
> `parse_segment`.

**549 pre-existing `c2-il` tests pass with zero edits, and two of them drove the
design.** `a_chain_is_a_chain_in_both_statement_positions` went red on the first
draft, which returned at the inner `4C` of a chain and handed `parse_expr` a
`99` it has no arm for — `binds_the_result` is the repair.
`a_branch_target_is_a_label_defined_later_in_the_segment` went red on the
second, and is why the control-flow bytes are in the fence (§4.2).

## 4. The measurement (#1941, #1942, #1943, #1944, #1945)

### 4.1 The redistribution, and the key redefinition it is (#1948)

`expr-call-in-expr` **449,274 / 36,751 → 423,925 / 35,583**. 120 of 678 body
keys moved, 113 of 655 emitted keys; 45 sub-keys vanish and 7 appear; the totals
are equal on both sides (1,751,949 bodies, 130,567 emitted — checked by the
script, not assumed).

**The key's meaning has changed and both sides are published.** It counted *"a
body containing a member call"*; it counts *"a body whose member call is the
last thing its expression contains, or whose production the walker cannot
tokenize"*. Any figure quoted against it from a scan at or before `285a94d7` is
on the old definition. That is trap 0's generalisation (#1002) applied on
purpose: a denominator is not published until it has been printed on both sides
of a change.

The alternative — an **env-gated sink** — was considered and rejected. Six
sinks have shipped in this crate and #1534 exists precisely because this
counterfactual went unrun for a year. The permanent form also makes the key
*more* truthful: the old one over-claimed, saying "blocked by a member call"
about 1,168 bodies that are additionally blocked by something the reader can
name.

### 4.2 THE ANSWER to #1534 — a three-way split, and the fence that makes it readable

The lane ran the redistribution **twice**, and the difference between the runs
is the second deliverable.

**Run 1 — no fence.** Any byte behind the call could take the head:

| recipient | emitted |
|---|---:|

### 4.4 The `Opaque` stack-depth defect (#1944)

The first draft treated an `Opaque` return as "push nothing", on the reasoning
that a class the model does not know must not be guessed. That reasoning is
right about the **class** and wrong about the **depth**, and the difference is a
number: `expr-convert-no-value-0x2C` — the key board #1462 is written about, and
whose 4,973 witnesses closed #1469 — went **4,973 → 5,790 bodies / 371 → 378
emitted**. A `2C` converting an opaque call's result found an *empty* stack and
reported "a conversion with nothing to convert" about a stream that plainly has
something to convert.

Pushing a placeholder and clearing `cstack_ok` — the discipline the
`C2RS_SINK_CHAIN=type` arms already use, in the same file, with the same stated
reason — puts the key back at **4,973 / 371 exactly**.

`GAPS.md` §6's "two facts sharing one field", in the form where the
honest-looking choice is the wrong one. It was caught only because a key that had
been stable across two lanes was **diffed** rather than eyeballed.

### 4.5 The pre-armed fence order, and it fired (#1945)

end-of-walk guards"* before a line of code was written, on w-park's finding that

## 6. `0x2C`'s width — a DECLINED adoption, priced at zero (#1946)

`WB_READER_FINDINGS.md` §3.4 records c2 reading **one raw byte** after `2C`'s
TYPE where the port reads a **varint**, calls it a latent desync, and §5.4
designs the check as *"a query, not an experiment"*. `WB_EH_FINDINGS.md` §4.3
scores its own **P3.5 a MISS**: seven decoded sites, every payload `0x00`.

**The black-box discriminator, run here, needs no disassembly and no `cl.exe`.**
The two rules are identical below `0x80`, so flip the port's own reader and
re-scan: any site with a payload ≥ `0x80` desynchronises one and not the other.
`shapes::control_flow::operand`'s `s.vint("cf-convert-payload")` was replaced by
a bounds-checked one-byte step and all 878 TUs re-scanned:

| observable | delta |
|---|---:|
| first-blocker keys (635 of them, 1,751,949 bodies) | **0 moved** |
| `gap-metric` keys (251) | **0 changed** |
| control-flow decode classes (1,244, over 7,308,883 bodies) | **0 moved** |

> **PREREG decline clause D5 fires.** The reading is *unwitnessed on this
> corpus*, `DISCLOSURE.md`'s grey-zone rule prefers a black-box derivation that
> does not exist, and the clause says publish the zero rather than adopt a width
> nothing can grade. The patch is reverted; **no `DISCLOSURE.md` row is carried
> by this lane.**

**And a correction to the source, found on the way.** *"The port reads a
varint"* is true of **one of three readers**. `parse_expr`'s `2C` arm consumes
**one byte** and requires it to be `00`; `mcall::walk_detail` reads `tw + 1`,
also one byte; only the control-flow scanner reads a varint. The three have
disagreed with each other since before this lane, on a field whose corpus
witnesses are all `0x00` so the disagreement cannot show. Unifying them is a
one-locator repair with **no corpus witness behind either spelling** — it is
filed, not done.

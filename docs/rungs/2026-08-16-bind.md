## 0. The answer, in one paragraph

Board **#3177** publishes the reachable widening order and names its head *"call-argument /
data-symbol **BINDING** — 1,825 of 3,062, 59.6 % — **not grammar at all**"*. The
first half of that sentence is right and the second half names a **symptom**.
Lifted rung by rung on the 878-TU workload, the head is **one fence wearing four
names in series**, and **binding is not what it is**:

```
   call-arg-multi-sym   1,296  ──L1──▶  call-arg-sym-permuted  1,008
                                            │
                                           L2
                                            ▼
                          data-sym-unresolved  1,824   (1,295 of the 1,296 land here)
                                            │
                                           L3
                                            ▼
                          data-sym-not-extern  1,458   (79.9 % had a .gl name all along)
```

Ranks **1**, **2** and **3** of the published reachable order are not three
problems. They are one problem seen through three masks, and the thing at the
bottom is **a `.rdata`/`.data` COMDAT SECTION EMITTER** — `IL_CALL_IN_EXPR.md`
§17.2 item 7's *"puts a `.data`/`.bss` section into the middle of the section
table"* — which is an **emit-side** object, not a reader-side binding one.

**And every graded column moved by exactly zero at every rung**:
`fnbyte-refused-parse` **113,612 → 113,612 → 113,612 → 113,612**,
`fnbyte-exact` **35,734** flat, `match` **25** flat, `mismatch` **0** and
`codegen-gap` **0** throughout.

## 5. The `/Ox`-vs-`/O1` split — the reason the rung was declined does not apply at the workload's flags

Registered in the prereg **before** the freeze (§3.5, P8–P10) after reading two
measured records that disagree. **Both are real `c2.dll` captures; they disagree
because they were captured at different profiles.** Reproduced here from **one
source at two profiles** via `c2rs listing`:

`void d2(const char*, const char*); void f2(){ d2("aa","bb"); }`

**`/Ox /GS- /c`** — the fixture default, and the profile §17.3 was captured at:

```text
   ?f2@@YAXXZ PROC NEAR                          ; NOT a COMDAT
     lis   r11,$SG2536                           ; ONE packed .rdata pool
     addi  r4,r11,$SG2536
     addi  r3,r4,4                               ; the other string, BY POOL DIFFERENCE
```

**`/nologo /c /GR /O1 /Oi /EHsc`** — **the workload's own flags**:

```text
   ;  COMDAT ??_C@_02DFENNNBM@aa?$AA@            ; each literal its OWN section
   .rdata  SEGMENT
   ;  COMDAT ??_C@_02BMCGDAIG@bb?$AA@
   .rdata  SEGMENT
   ?f2@@YAXXZ PROC NEAR                          ; f2, COMDAT
     lis   r11,??_C@_02BMCGDAIG@bb?$AA@
     lis   r10,??_C@_02DFENNNBM@aa?$AA@
     addi  r4,r11,??_C@_02BMCGDAIG@bb?$AA@
     addi  r3,r10,??_C@_02DFENNNBM@aa?$AA@       ; NO pool difference anywhere
     b     ?d2@@YAXPBD0@Z
```

§17.2 **item 4** already carries the mechanism, one subsection above the place it
is not applied: *"`/O1` implies `/GF` and `/Gy`, so string literals become
`??_C@_…` **COMDAT** `.rdata` sections … **The two profiles need two different
emitters**."* At `/Ox` all literals share one ordinary `.rdata`, so offset
differences exist and c2 exploits them; at `/O1` each literal is its own COMDAT,
so **there is no pool to take a difference in**.

**The consequence.** §17.3 is titled *"What is NOT established, and it is what
stopped the rung"*, and what stopped it is **(b)** — *"which symbol is the anchor
is offset-dependent … a **HYPOTHESIS with no mechanism behind it**"*, a
**14-witness fit**. **(b) is `/Ox`-only.** At `/O1` there is no anchor to choose
because there is no pool to anchor in. Corroborated by the `/Ox` n=3 cell, which
anchors **r5 with POSITIVE offsets** (`addi r4,r5,4` · `addi r3,r5,8`) where
§17.3(a)'s own witness anchors **r3 with negative** ones — **even the sign is
layout-dependent**, which is (b)'s point and confirms it is about the pool.

**A rung was declined on a property of a profile the graded workload is not
compiled at.** This is `#3160`'s mode-dependence and `#3165`'s
title-versus-enforcing-line in one object.

### 5.1 The SERIES — four cells, n = 1…4, at the workload's profile

Publishing a number without its series is what **#3147** cost `w-slots`: told to
read the charge off the fixture's own obj, it did, **the objs read 3, and the
series was `2n+1`, not `3n`**. So the series, not a cell:

| n | emitted at `/O1 /Oi /EHsc /GR` | words |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | `lis r11,s1` · `addi r3,r11,s1` · `b` | 3 |
| 2 | `lis r11,s2` · `lis r10,s1` · `addi r4,r11,s2` · `addi r3,r10,s1` · `b` | 5 |
| 3 | `lis r11,s3` · `lis r10,s2` · `lis r9,s1` · `addi r5,r11,s3` · `addi r4,r10,s2` · `addi r3,r9,s1` · `b` | 7 |
| 4 | `lis r11,s4` · `lis r10,s3` · `lis r9,s2` · `lis r8,s1` · `addi r6,…` · `addi r5,…` · `addi r4,…` · `addi r3,…` · `b` | 9 |

**Closed form.** For `i = n … 1`: `lis r(11-(n-i)), s_i`; then for `i = n … 1`:
`addi r(2+i), r(11-(n-i)), s_i`; then `b callee`.
**Temps descend from `r11`; argument registers descend from `r(n+2)` to `r3`;
all `lis` precede all `addi`; word count = `2n + 1`; no anchor, no difference.**

That is a **closed recognizer AND a series at four cells** — both of the
prereg's §5 build conditions, met.

## 2. The two independent confirmations

The standard is board **#820** and it is **two**: a captured token stream graded
against real `c2.dll`, and a workload-wide check that the rivals are excluded by
the bytes. **And the obligation `w-bd` added and `w-4c` discharged — ask what
your population EXCLUDES before you grade it.** §3 is that question, asked and
answered honestly in both directions.

### 2.0 What the search found before the first capture, and what it settles

`docs/EH_RECORDS.md` §7.1 (lane WEH, 2026-07-31) **already measured this width**
— `5C <TYPE> <varint>`, with the TYPE varying in length and the second field a
varint that really does escape — and
`crates/c2-il/src/func/body/shapes/control_flow.rs`'s `operand()` **consumes it
today**, reading `cf-eh-live-type` then `cf-eh-live-state` and incrementing
`eh.live_stmts`. Four `ctor_dtor.rs` recognizers eat `5C <TYPE> <state>` inside
shapes the differential grades byte-exact.

**So this is `0xBD`'s diagnosis one family along, and the half that made `0xBD`
hard was already solved.** `chain_skip_form`'s `None` at `0x5C` meant *"no row
was written"*, not *"no evidence exists"* — and `SkipForm::TypeVarint` could
already spell the width (`2C`, `33` and `99` use it), so unlike `0xBD` this was a
plain omission and not an expressiveness one. That distinction is the whole of
board **#1425** and it is why `5D`/`5E` are **not** shipped here.

It does not excuse skipping the confirmations. A width that three readers agree
on can still be wrong in the same way in all three, and the rung's own standard
is a capture and a population, not a citation.

## 3. THE POPULATION QUESTION, ASKED BEFORE THE GRADE

`w-bd` declined `0x4C` because its 26,701 sites were **zero-argument calls
only**. `PREREG.md` §3 named the four classes a `5C` grid could wrongly exclude,
in advance. Three are present and one is not.

| class | present in the anchored grid? |
|---|---|
| a 4-byte object TYPE | **yes** — 64,437, and 5-, 6- and 7-byte ones besides |
| `5C` in OPERAND position | **yes** — 60,604 (18.05 %), reported as its own row |
| generated-destructor sub-object **and** ordinary-function local | **yes** — both in the probe, both in the workload |
| **the ESCAPED state varint** | **NO — 0 of 335,716.** D3, fired |

### 3.1 D3 fired, and it is DISCHARGED by a population rather than waved at

`TypeVarint` and a hypothetical `TypeByte1` (`5C <TYPE> <one raw byte>`) **agree
at every state value below `0x80`**. So the 335,716-site result above, decisive
as it is against P/T/V/TT, says **nothing** about that pair. That is `0xBD`'s
§2.2 situation exactly, where the corpus excluded neither reading and the tie was
broken by matching the accepting parser.

**Here it does not have to be.** The escaped sites exist — they are inside bodies
whose walk abandons at one of the unpinned opcodes above before it reaches them.
So they are located with an **over-inclusive raw byte scan** (every `5C` byte
with a readable TYPE after it), with the bias stated rather than hidden and the
**BASE RATE printed beside the result**, because a raw `5C` may be a payload byte
and not a token at all:

```text
  `5C <readable TYPE>` byte positions in the workload      544,783
  BASE RATE — the varint reading lands on `4B`             330,485   60.66 %
  sites whose state byte is `80` (the ESCAPE)                9,744

    varint (`80 <LE32>`)  lands on 4B  9,645 / 9,744   98.98 %   legal opener  99.70 %
    one raw byte          lands on 4B      0 / 9,744    0.00 %   legal opener   0.97 %
```

**Decided.** The varint reading is 38 points *above* the base rate on that class
and the byte reading is 61 points *below* it; the byte reading's landings are
`01` at 9,645 of 9,744, i.e. the second byte of the LE32 the reading refuses to
consume. And it agrees with the ACCEPTING side — `operand()` reads a varint
there too — so the sink and the reader beside it cannot report successors the
other can never reach.

### 3.2 One caution the same measurement carries, stated because it is a bound

**All 9,645 escaped sites are the SAME byte sequence** —
`5C 86 41 74 80 01 01 00 00 4B`, state 257, in 812 TUs. So the corpus witnesses
the escape's **width** at 9,645 sites and the escape's **value range** at
exactly one point. A reading that got the escape's length right and its
interpretation wrong would be invisible here, and this table is width-only, which
is why that is a bound and not a defect. (`read_varint`'s own header carries the
adjacent one: for tag-`0x88` types the escape payload is 8 bytes, not 4.)

It is also the reason §2.1's `many_locals()` probe is reported as a **failure to
produce the class** rather than quietly dropped: 100 live objects all read state
`01`, so the escape is not reachable by piling up locals and the probe could not
have produced it.

## 6. THE TWO `0x5C` RUNGS, AND WHY ONE OF THEM CANNOT BE LIFTED BY A WIDTH

Board **#1390** published the caution that this lane had to test rather than
repeat: *"`5C` surfaces as a `parse_expr_classed` refusal a sink grant would
open, not as a `noform`, so no ladder row exits there. A lane sizing this floor
off ladder exits would price it at zero."* Measured on both sides of the width,
the caution is **sharper than filed**, because the two keys are two productions:

| key | raised by | size | this lane |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `expr-op-0x5C` | `parse_expr_classed`'s fall-through, reachable only with `4C` sunk | **36,477** fns / 827 TUs | **CLOSED** |
| `expr-call-in-expr-*-op-0x5C` | `mcall::classify`/`walk`, the **diagnostic** call classifier | **1,212** fns / 810 TUs, on the DEFAULT scan | **moved by 0** |

The second family does not consult `chain_skip_form` at all — it is
`Blocker::Op(0x5C)` inside a shape recognizer that runs *before*
`parse_expr_classed` — so a width in this table structurally cannot lift it.
`work/w-front3/ladder.py`'s speculative tail-opcode rule (board **#1357**) says
so out loud, and it says it at **both ends**:

```text
  Main.cpp, hatched, base   rnd3  TAIL_INERT: expr-call-in-expr-recv-object-then-op-0x5C:op:5C
  Main.cpp, hatched, tip    rnd3  TAIL_INERT: expr-call-in-expr-recv-object-then-op-0x5C:op:5C
```

`op:5C` is TRIED, the blocker set does not move, the candidate is discarded and
named. `w-one` measured that at base with no width behind the token; this lane

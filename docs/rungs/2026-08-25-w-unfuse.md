## 2. What was fused, and what the split is

`docs/DECISIONS_2026-08-22.md` decision 13 funds row 4a(i) and quotes the
obstacle: *"`BodyShape` starts at 35 whole-function grammars **that are
simultaneously the admission gate**"*. Concretely, at base there was one call —

```rust
pub(crate) fn parse_segment(seg: &[u8], sy: SyView) -> Option<BodyShape>
```

— whose `None` meant two different things at once: *"this port cannot read this
IL"* (a **decode** fact about the bytes) and *"this port may not emit this
body"* (an **admission** fact about the port). Because they were one value,
**widening what the port could read was the same edit as widening what it would
emit.** Lane `S0` measured what that ships when done naively: blind-differs
**96.1 %** of what it reached.

After the split, in `crates/c2-il/src/func/body/decode.rs`:

* **`Decoded`** — total over segments, constructed for every body, **carries no
  admission verdict**. It answers reach (`reached_shape`), the decode's stopping
  point (`block`, `feature`), and two segment facts admission provably does not
  consult (`call_tokens`, whose own doc at base already read *"Diagnostic only.
  Nothing here is consulted by the emitter or by acceptance"*; `body_start`).
* **`AdmissionPolicy`** — the named decision surface, `DEFAULT =
  RecognizedShape`, which is today's rule byte for byte.
* **`Decoded::is_admitted_under(policy)`** — the admission question as the
  yes/no it is; **`into_admit(policy)`** — its emitting form.

### 2.1 The mechanism is a VISIBILITY, not a comment

`parse_segment_detail` is **`pub(in crate::func::body)`** now. `func::bundle`,
`func::census` and `func::diag` **cannot** reach the raw parse; a future call
site that wants to re-fuse the two questions does not compile.

That matters because of what it replaces. The invariant *"the census and the
gate cannot disagree about what is in class"* was held at base by a sentence in
a doc comment — *"acceptance is identical — `parse_segment` is `.ok()` of
this"*. A sentence is not a mechanism, and `docs/STATUS.md`'s standing
`census/gate disagreement: 0` line rests on it. The same invariant now holds
because there is **one decode, one admission predicate, and no second route to
either**.

`parse_segment` itself survives `#[cfg(test)]` only — ~40 pinned-segment tests
are written in that spelling — and is *defined as* `Decoded::of(..)
.admitted_default()`, so it cannot become a second implementation of either
half.

### 2.2 The five production call sites, all re-expressed

The prereg's §0.1 inventory was frozen before the change and held: **2 fused
entry points, 5 production call sites, 0 out-of-crate callers.**

| # | site | at base | at tip | which question it means |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `bundle.rs` `IlBundle::functions` | `parse_segment(seg, locals)?` | `Decoded::of(..).admitted_default()?` | **admission** — it emits |
| 2 | `bundle.rs` `IlBundle::dyninit_tu` | `parse_segment(&segs[0], ..)?` | same | **admission** — it emits |
| 3 | `diag.rs` `bodies_out_of_class` | `parse_segment(seg, ..)` | same | **admission**, and the counter's name was always about the port's *class* |
| 4 | `census.rs:607` `tu_modelled_callees` | `parse_segment_detail(..)` | `Decoded::of(..).into_admit_default()` | **admission** — it converts and splices |
| 5 | `census.rs:939` the census verdict | `parse_segment_detail(..)` | `Decoded::of(..).into_admit_default()` | **both**, off one parse |

Site 5 is the one worth reading twice. The census's `InClass` verdict is an
**admission** claim, not a decode one — `gap`'s census/gate cross-check (roadmap
#44) grades every `InClass` function against `PortC2`'s own selector and its
known answer is 0 — so `shape` there holds the admission predicate's *answer*.
The ~120 match arms below it **name the grammar the decode reached**, which is
the decode reading of the same parse. One `Decoded`, two readings, and the file's
own pre-existing comment (*"two readings of one parse, never two parses"*) is now
true of two different questions rather than one question asked twice.

### 2.3 `IlBundle::decode_bodies` — the surface `w-decodereach` measures

One `Decoded` per `.ex` segment, over the **census** population
(`split_function_bodies_at` + `Bindings::census`) and deliberately **not** the
gate's. The two splitters disagree on 634 of 871 workload TUs, so a reach
number taken over one and compared against a census taken over the other would
be a ratio of two different denominators. A pure reader: it makes no acceptance
decision, licenses no emit, and is available on a bundle whose `functions()`
returns `None` — which is the whole population it is about.

## 3. THE FINDING — the admission layer cannot own a refusal REASON

**Board #3556, and it is the sharpest thing this lane produced.**

The prereg registered `AdmissionPolicy` as a *named, settable parameter*
(`docs/rungs/README.md`'s DECISION-SURFACE CLAUSE). It ships with **one
variant**, and the reason is a constraint that was discovered by building the
second one, not an omission.

The second variant was `Nothing` — *admit nothing*, an instrument state in the
**refusal** direction, which `docs/PROGRESS_METRIC.md` orders strictly above a
wrong emit. It does not work, because:

> A **`Block` says where the READ stopped.** A policy that refuses a body the
> decode read **whole** has no such point — the read did not stop. Every
> consumer of a refusal wants the reason (`census.rs` renders `Block::feature`
> from it and that histogram *is* the widening order), so a policy that can
> decline a decoded body must mint a `Block` — and minting one publishes a
> `:eof` census key **that no scan can ever reach**.

That is not an aesthetic objection. It was caught by an existing repo guard:
`crates/c2-harness/tests/fence_site_census.rs` counts `Block::at_end(`
production sites in `c2-il` and went **7 → 8** with the variant in, its message
reading *"Every one renders a `:eof` key a scan reports, so one landing unscored
is a published key nothing measured."* The guard is right, and my site would
have been the first one its rationale is **false** of — the key is unreachable
in production because only a non-default policy constructs it.

**Consequences, and they are inputs to 4a(i)'s later slices:**

1. **An admission verdict is a yes/no. Every refusal KEY in this port belongs to
   the decode.** That is now the shape of the API: `is_admitted_under` returns
   `bool`, and `into_admit`'s `Err` side carries the *decode's* block.
2. A future policy that can decline a body the decode read whole **owes its own
   scored census key** before it may reach `into_admit`. The `match` in
   `into_admit` is exhaustive over `AdmissionPolicy`, so a second variant cannot
   be added without that site being made to answer the question.
3. The one-line change such a lane needs is named here so nobody re-derives it:
   `EXPECTED_AT_END_SITES` 7 → 8 in
   `crates/c2-harness/tests/fence_site_census.rs`. **This lane did not make it**
   — that file is peer lane `w-guard`'s this wave, and the fence is that a lane
   which needs a peer's file stops and reports.

**Two-sided price, because a fence is never priced one-sided (`#1042`,
NC-5/`#2691`).** What shipping one variant costs: `AdmissionPolicy` is named and
defaulted but not *settable*, so the decision-surface clause is met in name and
not in effect, and a permuter has no knob here yet. What shipping two would have
cost: a published census key no scan can reach, plus a guard constant moved by a
lane that does not own the guard — i.e. exactly the *"a green instrument is a
statement about the population it can reach"* failure (trap 0), installed
deliberately. The refusal is the cheaper side and it is also the one that leaves
a *measured* fact behind instead of a decorative enum.

### 3.1 The one duplication, and its fence

The policy is matched in two places — `is_admitted_under` (the yes/no) and
`into_admit` (the emitting form) — because Rust cannot derive an owning return
from a borrowing one. Two matches over one enum is exactly the *"two predicates
that can drift apart"* shape this lane was forbidden to ship, so it is **fenced
rather than argued away**: `the_yes_no_and_the_emitting_form_cannot_disagree`
asks 16 pinned segments both ways and requires agreement, with an explicit
assertion that the population is **not one-sided** (both an admitted and a
refused arm are non-empty — `docs/STATUS.md` trap 0 applied to the fence's own
corpus).

## 7. What this lane did NOT do, on purpose

* **It widened nothing.** Not by one function — and that is checked per symbol,
  §1. `AdmissionPolicy::RecognizedShape` is *the identity on the decode result*;
  the split's value is not that the function does something, it is that there is
  now **a named function for it to be the identity of**, so widening the decode
  and widening the emit set became two edits instead of one.
* **It decoded nothing new.** Row 4a(i)'s general op-level decode is priced at
  I1 raw 1.5–4.5 engineer-months and **15–45 engineer-months as a lower bound**
  for 4a as a whole. This is its prerequisite, not a slice of it.
* **It did not touch `select_function`'s dispatch order**, any clause of
  `IlBundle::functions`, or what `Block::feature` renders for any refusal.
* **It did not touch `crates/c2-harness` or `scripts/`** (peers `w-decodereach`
  and `w-guard`), and it stopped rather than editing `w-guard`'s guard — §3
  item 3.
* **It did not fix the `0x43` escape hazard** peer `w-ilarms` flagged (the port
  reads `0x37`/`0x42` as sub-opcodes of a `0x43` escape that does not exist in
  the real dispatch; class `00+02+00` reproduces its `+4`/`+2` advance exactly,
  so it is right by coincidence, and a wide `varU` walks into the payload).
  **This lane's seam does not touch that site** — it is in
  `shapes::control_flow`'s walk, reached from `census.rs:448` through
  `scan_full`, and no byte of that path changed. Recorded here so the hazard has
  a second home; a fix is an emit change and was out of fence.

## 8. The correction this lane carries — the fusion is REAL but LOCAL

Decision 13 dispatched this lane on *"decode and admission are fused"*. Peer
lane `w-ilarms` measured, and this lane verified against the source, that the
statement is **true of `parse_segment_shape`'s ladder and already false of
`shapes::control_flow`**, whose own first line has read *"The control-flow
statement layer — DECODE ONLY"* since it landed, and *"Nothing in this file can
make a function in class"*. `census.rs` reaches it on every body through
`scan_full`, with no admission gate in the path.

So the right description of this lane is **not** that it invented a decode
layer: it applied `control_flow`'s already-shipped, already-byte-exact pattern
at the one layer that *is* the admission gate, and the module doc says so and
cites it. A lane that had believed the dispatch brief literally would have built
a second abstraction beside an existing one — the `#3098` family (*the correct
answer was already on the board, unconsulted*) at its seventh instance.

**And the number that goes with it must not leak.** `w-ilarms`'s *"68 of 95
opcodes"* is a statement about **cursor advance**, not decode coverage — the
same lane found that no site in any of the five crates mints an IR node for the
`≥ 0x2af` node opcodes. A width reader is not a decoder. The reach measurement
is `w-decodereach`'s, and it is taken off `IlBundle::decode_bodies`.

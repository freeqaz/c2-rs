The brief: *"extend `PORT_WRITER_SECTIONS` and the COFF writer so the port can
emit `.rdata$r` sections/symbols/relocations byte-exactly **where its content

### §3.1 REFUTED — `??_8` is not `??_7`, 0 of 4 (board #928)

The spec's §6 calls it *"the vftable/vbtable block"* and its §3 gives the block
Selection **6** and `Value` **4**. That is the vftable's rule. All four `??_8`
**vbtables** in the grid — `??_8Vd@@7B@`, `??_8R@@7B@`, `??_8Dia@@7BLft@@@`,
`??_8Dia@@7BRgt@@@`, i.e. every virtual-inheritance cell — read Selection **2**
and `Value` **0**, with Characteristics `0x40301040` 4/4. The two records differ
in **exactly the two fields a writer sets**, which is the worst possible place
for a one-rule-for-both spec to be wrong.

**And the vftable's own rule is `/GR`-conditional (board #929).** At `/GR-` the
same 27 `??_7` vftables read Selection **2** and `Value` **0** — **0/27** on
both. The complete-object locator at `vftable[-1]` is what displaces the symbol
and earns LARGEST; without RTTI there is none. The spec only ever measured `/GR`
cells and recorded a conditional fact as an unconditional one. The same column
confirms `§10.20`'s *"it dies at `/GR-`"* at **0 `.rdata$r` sections and 0
`??_R0` COMDATs on all 18 cells**.

### §3.2 CORRECTED — the group is cut at the vftable run, not the `.text` (board #930)

§5's DFS pre-order and §6's non-interleave are individually right and **compose
wrongly**. Three models, three modes, counted:

| DFS model | `/O1` | `/Od` | `/Ox` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| one global walk rooted at every vftable, reverse section order | 16/18 objs | — | — |
| a walk per group, group cut at each **`.text` COMDAT** | 21/21 | 17/19 | 16/18 |
| **a walk per group, group cut at each VFTABLE RUN** | **21/21** | **22/22** | **21/21** |

The `.text` cut fails because **the `.text` is not always there**.
`f11_three_classes.cpp` defines three polymorphic classes and their three
constructors; at `/O1` the obj carries three `.text` COMDATs and three
vftable+RTTI blocks, and **at `/Od` and `/Ox` it carries ONE `.text` COMDAT and
still three blocks** — `??0C2@@QAA@XZ` is emitted and `??0C3`/`??0C1` are not,
while all three vftables and all twelve records are. Block order is unchanged
across all three modes and equals the order the constructors are **defined** in
(C2, C3, C1 — deliberately not the declaration order), so the ordering key is
the *function*, not the section it did or did not get.

**§2's *"a `.rdata$r` TU always has at least one `.text` COMDAT"* survives.** It
is true on all 72 objs, and four probes in `work/w-rtti/probe/` went looking for
a zero-`.text` counterexample on purpose — a namespace-scope object of a
polymorphic class turns out to be **statically** initialized here (vfptr
relocation and all, no `??__E`, no `.text$yc`) and *still* drags the constructor
COMDAT in. What does not survive is the stronger reading that the `.text`
COMDATs and the RTTI blocks are in bijection, and a writer built on the `.text`
cut is wrong on two of eighteen cells at the workload's own mode's neighbours.

### §3.3 CONFIRMED and narrowed — §7's `/Od` divergence (board #933)

`OBJ_RDATA_R_SHAPE.md` §7 records board #295's two-vftable `/Od` divergence and
notes it did *not* reproduce on `w-rdata`'s simpler grid. **It reproduces here.**
`f14_vdtor_derived.cpp` — virtual destructors on base **and** derived — reads
**4 records / 1 vftable at `/O1` and at `/Ox`**, and **8 records / 2 vftables at
`/Od`**. It is the **only one of the eighteen** sources whose record count moves
with the optimization mode (`f08` and `f11` move their *section* counts per
§3.2, with record counts fixed). So the shape it needs is narrower than "base
and derived": it needs the virtual **destructor** on both levels.

### §3.4 The reader found its own bugs first, and that is why the misses are trustworthy

`verify.py`'s first run reported **7 refuted claims of 19**. Four were the
reader's, and each is now a comment in the file rather than a lesson to relearn:

* `IMAGE_REL_PPC_ADDR32` is **`0x0002`**; `0x0006` is i386's ADDR32 and PPC's
  REL24. Hardcoding it read **0/268**. It reads the name through
  `gt_dump.reloc_name` now, i.e. from `crates/c2-obj/src/reloc.rs`.
* The section aux record's **CheckSum is at +8**, not +4 (+4 is
  `NumberOfRelocations`). Reading +4 read **0/174** on a claim that is 174/174.
* The record fields are **big-endian** and `gt_dump.u32` is the container's
  little-endian reader: `??_R3.numBaseClasses` read `33554432` for `2`.
* The DFS model, §3.2 — which turned out to be a genuine spec correction rather
  than only a reader bug, and would have been read as "the spec is fine" if the
  reader had been written the other way round.

**Three of the four made the spec look wrong when it was right, and the fourth
made it look right in a way it is not.** A verification pass whose first run is
clean has usually verified its own reader.

### §4.2 Item 2 is not what the spec says it is — and the finding makes the next lane cheaper (board #931)

`w-rdata` reported item 2 to `w-vocab` as *"a reader for the `??_R*` record
graph"*, and `OBJ_RDATA_R_SHAPE.md` §4 framed it as a **derivation** problem:
3,337 of 3,570 records rebuild from mangled names, and `??_R4.offset`,
`??_R4.cdOffset`, `??_R3.attributes` are *"not recoverable from a mangled symbol,
which is why they are the reader's job and not the writer's"*.

**They do not have to be recovered. They are literally in `.in`.**

```text
  ??_R1 …  07 00 f7 09 00 · 02 f4 09 00 04 · 01 02 04 00 · 01 01 04 00
           · 01 01 04 80 ffffffff · 01 01 04 00 · 01 02 04 40 · 02 f5 09 00 04
                                    ^^ pdisp = -1        ^^ attributes = 0x40
  ??_R3 …  07 00 f5 09 00 · 01 02 04 00 · 01 02 04 00 · 01 02 04 01 · 02 f6 09 00 04
                                                          ^^ numBaseClasses = 1
  ??_R0 …  07 00 f4 09 00 · 02 f8 09 00 04 · 01 03 04 00 · 03 08 ".?AUA@@" 00
                             ^^ reloc to ??_7type_info@@6B@   ^^ tag 03, the name
```

Element tags `01` (scalar) and `03` (byte string) are **already read**, by
`crates/c2-il/src/func/ininit.rs` and `inlit.rs`. The one missing tag is
**`02` — `<token> 00 <n>`, the address of another symbol** — which `ininit.rs`
names `InInitResidue::SymbolAddress` and refuses by design, citing
`OBJ_DATA_BSS_SHAPE.md` §8.6's *"unexercised"*.

**And what is actually missing is measured, not argued.** A throwaway spike over
`gl_data_objects_ordered`, with a positive control so a zero is a reading rather
than a broken probe:

| capture | records the `.gl` data reader returns |
|---|---:|
| `wsect_data_two.cpp` — the positive control | **2 of 2** |
| the minimal RTTI TU — 11 sections, 6 of them data | **0** |
| `struct A{virtual void f();int a;}; A g;` — 12 sections | **1 of 12** (the `$initializer$`) |

Zero of eleven: not the four `??_R*`, not the `??_R0` in `.data`, not the
vftable. And in the third row not even the plain `?g@@3UA@@A`, because its
initializer carries a **relocation** — the same element tag `02`.

**The count of seven does not change** — a cheaper fact is still a fact — but the
*shape* of the harder binding one does, and it is now ordinary reader work with
a graded consumer (`emit_data_obj`) already in the tree. **Tag `02` is worth
measuring on its own, before any RTTI**: it is the gap between the `.data`
writer that exists today and every initializer that names a symbol.

The spike was **removed before anything landed** — `git diff --stat crates/` was
empty at that point, checked and not assumed.

### §4.3 There is no cheaper slice, and it was looked for

The one route that would have made this rung small is a `.rdata$r` TU with **no

Four probes (`work/w-rtti/probe/z01`–`z04`) tested it directly; §3.2 has the

## §5 What DID ship: board #301 is closed, and BREAK 2 is why it had to be

`w-rdata` §10 filed the hole and priced the closure: the vocabulary test
reconciles `PORT_WRITER_SECTIONS` against `Section { name: … }` literals, and
**a literal inside an emitter that nothing calls satisfies it and still inflates
C**. `container::bss_deferred_layout` was that — a `.bss` layout the differential
had never graded one byte of, wrong on the walk *and* on the free list the moment
a real caller was written — and board #278 deleted it.

`coff::tests::every_production_emitter_has_a_lib_rs_caller` closes it: **every
`pub fn emit_*` present in a non-test build must be named by `lib.rs`.**
`emit_mvp_obj` — #301's named live instance, whose only caller was
`coff/tests.rs` — is `#[cfg(test)]` rather than exempted, because an allow-list
is the thing that goes stale. The test asserts its own population size (**5**),
so a run that scans nothing cannot read like a run that scans everything.

**Measured by breaking the tree twice** (`work/w-rtti/counterfactual.sh`, which
refuses to start on a dirty `crates/` so the restore check cannot be vacuous):

```text
### BASE
  the_writer_vocabulary_…  ok        every_production_emitter_…  ok
### BREAK 1 — the constant only, no Section literal
  the_writer_vocabulary_…  FAILED    every_production_emitter_…  ok
### BREAK 2 — the constant PLUS an uncalled emitter that builds the Section
  the_writer_vocabulary_…  ok        every_production_emitter_…  FAILED
### RESTORE
  git status --porcelain crates/ = 0 path(s)
  the_writer_vocabulary_…  ok        every_production_emitter_…  ok
```

BREAK 1 re-runs `w-rdata` §5's known-answer control and reproduces it. **BREAK 2
is the new one**: a real `.rdata$r` `Section` literal in an emitter nothing
calls, the constant at 11 names, and the vocabulary test **green**. That is
+421 of factor C, no writer, one test away, and until this commit nothing said
no.

### §5.1 The guard caught its own author first (board #932)

The test read `pub fn emit_*_obj` for one commit. BREAK 2's emitter was named
`emit_rtti_obj_counterfactual`, which does not **end** in `_obj`, so the
population excluded it and **BREAK 2 passed both tests on the first run** — the
exact inflation the test exists to catch, waved through by the test's own naming
convention. A suffix filter is an allow-list wearing a different hat. It reads
every `pub fn emit_*` now, and this paragraph is in the record because a guard
whose first version was blind is a guard whose *second* version deserves the
counterfactual it was given.

  priced: it belongs with factor A.
* **`docs/STATUS.md`'s generated block is not regenerated here.** Nothing this
  lane did moves a metric in it — every one of the 77 `gap-metric` lines is
  byte-identical — so it is not stale *because of this lane*. The coordinator's
  call, from the merged tree.

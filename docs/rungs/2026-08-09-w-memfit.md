### 3.1 `align` is MEASURED, and GRID-M's own manifest is wrong about it

`work/w-memfit/hint.py` differences the captured `.ex` across eight sources
that differ **only** in the pointee type. Exactly two byte positions —
**offsets 2733 and 2742** — carry a value equal to `alignof(pointee)` for all
eight:

```text
  char 01   int 04   double 08   S16{double;double;} 08
  void 01   long long 08   S4{int} 04   S32{double[4]} 08
```

That is `wb-memcpy` §5.3's positional identification of "two alignment hints",
re-done over the whole type axis of both grids, and it is a **black-box**
observation: the `.ex` is c1xx's output and the port's own input format, decoded
by `crates/c2-il`. No disassembler.

**GRID-M's manifest records `align = 16` for `S16 { double a; double b; }`.**
That is the type's *size*; its alignment is 8. Scoring the rule with the
manifest's field instead of the IL's byte gives **224 / 232** — eight misses at
sizes 48, 56, 64 and 72, every one in the *inline* direction
(`work/w-memfit/s16.py`). Eight of 232 is small enough to have been written up
as "the rule is nearly right", and that is the shape a wrong emit hides in.

### 3.2 Two label traps, both of which manufacture a refutation

**(a) The relocation is consulted FIRST.** The obvious three-valued verdict —
`nbytes == 4 ⇒ none` — mislabels the four `memcpy_*_var` cells. A non-constant
size at `/O1` is a **tail call**: one instruction, `b memcpy`, **four bytes AND
a REL24**. Four cells on GRID-M, and with it every one of the eight published
scores came out exactly 4 low. This is the mirror image of the bug `w-memcpy`
§6.2 recorded (reading the relocation *instead of* the byte count), one lane
later and in the other direction. The correct order is: relocation, then byte
count.

**(b) The committed `probeM2/measured.json` carries the KNOWN-BAD labels.**
`gridm2.py`'s verdict function is two-valued — it is the very function
`w-memcpy` §6.2 records as the bug — and all 176 of its rows read `call` or
`inline`. **44 eliminated bodies are labelled `inline` in the committed file.**
Scoring against that field grades the reading at 132/176 and produces a
refutation out of a label somebody already knew was wrong. The three-valued
verdict is recomputed from the recorded `nbytes`. **This trap was written into
the PREREG §5 before the first score**, precisely because it is the failure this
shape has.

### 3.4 What `w-memcpy`'s cells decide that GRID-W cannot

Two reciprocal facts, and this is the part that makes the reconciliation worth
more than either lane's number alone.

**The threshold.** GRID-M's cells separate `T = 5` from `T = 10` on **34 cells,
34 to 0**; GRID-M2's live cells on **42, 42 to 0**. So `T = 5` at `/O1` was
never a coin-flip in `w-memcpy`'s data — it was already decided there, by 76
cells, and nobody had asked.

**The truncation.** `Q-QUOT` and `Q-QUOT-CEIL` differ only on a size the
alignment does not divide.

```text
    GRID-W /O1     separating cells   0   truncating 0, ceiling 0
    GRID-W all     separating cells   0   truncating 0, ceiling 0
    GRID-M         separating cells   4   truncating 4, ceiling 0
    GRID-M2        separating cells  18   truncating 18, ceiling 0
```

GRID-W's `n` axis is `{4,5,6,9,10,11}` **elements**, i.e. exact multiples of the
alignment, so it cannot see the rounding at all. `w-memcpy`'s size axis is
absolute and includes 44, 46, 47 — which is the whole of the `double*` 44/48
boundary `wb-memcpy` §3 called "the one thing no size-only rule can produce".
**The whitebox lane's grid could not have graded its own sharpest claim.**

### 3.5 The hold-out — why the row is `route:` and not `adoption`

`work/w-memfit/holdout.py` never reads an address. It fits, by exhaustive
search over four candidate quantities (`size`, `floor(size/align)`,
`ceil(size/align)`, `ceil(size/4)`) × every threshold `0..2048`:

```text
  fit on GRID-W `/O1` only (72 cells): Q-QUOT, T = 5, 72/72
      -> scored on GRID-M   (never fitted): 232 / 232
      -> scored on GRID-M2  (never fitted): 176 / 176
  fit on GRID-M + GRID-M2 only (408 cells): Q-QUOT, T = 5, 408/408
      -> scored on GRID-W `/O1` (never fitted):  72 / 72
      -> scored on GRID-W O1Ot:  18 / 36   (its own best fit is T=10 at 36)
      -> scored on GRID-W O2  :  18 / 36   (its own best fit is T=10 at 36)
      -> scored on GRID-W O2Os:  36 / 36   (its own best fit is T=5  at 36)
      -> scored on GRID-W Ox  :  18 / 36   (its own best fit is T=10 at 36)
```

Both constants are recoverable from obj cells alone, on either population, and
each transfers to the other at 100 %. What the disassembly supplied is the
**search space** — `size / align, truncating` is a quantity nobody enumerated
before reading it, and `w-memcpy` froze six rivals over 408 cells without one of
them being a quotient. `DISCLOSURE.md`'s grey-zone rule is exactly this case,
and it says `route:`.

The three flag sets that refuse `T = 5` at 18/36 are the favor-speed reading
re-confirmed from the other side, on cells this lane did not compile.

### 5.3 And four is for 84 of 380 bytes

some `mmio.cpp` function is ≤ 1 independent refusal and that refusal is inside
the memcpy expansion.* The residual is **4**, and only one of the four is inside

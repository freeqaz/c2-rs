## 0. What this lane is, in one paragraph

`docs/ARCHITECTURE_PROPOSAL_2026-08-20.md` §5 step 1: build a **lossless
framing** of the IL bundle (IR0), re-express `IlBundle::functions()` and the

## 3. What IR0 v1 is — and the one thing it is NOT

**IR0 v1 is one locator for `.ex` framing, holding totality, with the two
existing splitters re-expressed as named views over it.** `.gl`, `.sy`, `.in`
and `.db` are **one `Opaque` record each**. `.gl` record framing is IR1
(proposal §5 step 4) and is out of scope: three walkers disagree today
(`func/gl.rs`), `parse_gl`'s 1:1 gate is FALSE on 811 of 878 workload TUs, and
any `.gl` framing in this lane loses the required-zero delta.

**IR0 does NOT unify the splitters.** The gate framing (`split_functions_at`,
every `4F 1F`) and the census framing (`split_function_bodies_at`, `LO`-anchored
plus the additive bare-`4C` pass) stay two. They are pinned apart on purpose
(`func/bind.rs`) and their disagreement is published (`emit-splitter-disagree`).
Unifying them moves the census numerator, which is what everything else is
differenced against. **IR0 gives them one input, not one answer.**

### 3.1 Honest note on the invariant, registered now so it cannot be inflated later

The brief states IR0's invariant as "totality + byte-identical
re-serialization". For a framing whose records are **extents** (half-open byte
ranges) and whose re-serialization is *concatenate `bytes[extent]`*, **byte
identity is implied by index totality**: if the extents tile `[0, len)` exactly,
the concatenation *is* the input. So I1 and I2 are **one invariant computed two
ways**, not two invariants. They are still both computed and diffed, because
that is exactly the #3288 second-derivation discipline — but this rung will not
claim two independent checks where there is one.

The genuinely-independent invariant already exists and is not IR0's:
`crates/c2-il/src/codec.rs` (`IlModel::parse` / `encode`) frames `.ex` into
**typed tokens** and fails closed with `CodecError::CannotRoundTrip` if the
re-encode differs. That is a strictly stronger claim than IR0's, and **it has
never been run on the 878-TU workload** — only on the 386 fixtures
(`crates/c2-harness/tests/il_roundtrip.rs`). Running it is S1 and is the
highest-information, lowest-cost item in the lane.

## 5. Grading criterion

**Primary — REQUIRED-ZERO BYTE DELTA.** A line-for-line identity diff of
per-lane gate counts before/after. A construct rung that changed any emitted
byte **FAILED**, whatever else it did.

1. `scripts/gate.sh --jobs 16 --require-graded` — all 18 lanes of
   `scripts/lanes.txt`, result table identical line for line, 18/18 PASS,
   `graded > 0` on every row.
2. `c2rs gap`: `match 26 · mismatch 0 · codegen-gap 0 · vocab-gap 844 ·
   capture-fail 8` unchanged.
3. The whole `gap-metric` set diffed key-for-key, base vs tip. The count delta
   must equal **exactly** the number of new `ir0-*` keys, each enumerated by
   name; every pre-existing key identical in value.
4. Census numerators identical (`706960/2403977`, `39365/162226`) **and the
   whole blocker histogram key-for-key**.
5. `fnbyte-exact` non-decreasing, expected **exactly equal** (base **35912**).
   Base and tip read back to back at the same workload stamp.
6. Suite `C2RS_REQUIRE_TOOLCHAIN=1 cargo test --workspace --release
   --no-fail-fast`: base counts held, plus the new tests **enumerated by name**.
7. Sweep and cross rows at mismatch 0.

**Workload stamp.** Re-read at lane end and `diff` against §2's string. If it
moved, the base is re-read at the current stamp before anything is published.

exist"**, never `0`. `docs/STATUS.md` trap 5 is that absence reads as success;
fabricating a base column of zeros is that trap with the mask on.

## 7. Decline conditions, registered

**D1.** The views cannot be made byte-identical to the incumbent splitters
without changing them. Then IR0's "views, not a rewrite" premise is false and
this is a reader change wearing a construct rung's clothes. DECLINE, price the
reader change, hand it back.

**D2.** The gate is not identical at the `functions()` switch and the difference
cannot be removed by reverting to the incumbent line. Report **FAILED** in that
word.

**D3.** Framing costs > 10% of scan wall clock at comparable load. DECLINE the
switch; keep the standalone pieces; re-price.

**D4 — the most likely honest decline, and this lane must actively look for it.**
If every `ir0-*` denominator turns out to be derivable from keys that already
print, then IR0 v1 is a rename of `split_functions_at` with no new checkable
invariant. `ir0-seg-*` was already removed from the plan for exactly this reason
(`emit-gate-segments` / `emit-splitter-*` publish it). If the remaining keys go
the same way, **say so and DECLINE the rest.** (P9.)

**D5.** No quiesce window. The switch inside `bundle.rs`/`census.rs` conflicts
mechanically with everything in flight (363 registered worktrees; `w-objplan`
observed live at lane start). Land what is additive, park the switch, say so.

## 3. What IR0 v1 is — and the one thing it is NOT

**IR0 v1 is one locator for `.ex` framing, holding totality, with the two
existing splitters re-expressed as named views over it.** `.gl`, `.sy`, `.in`
and `.db` are **one `Opaque` record each**. `.gl` record framing is IR1
(proposal §5 step 4) and is out of scope: three walkers disagree today
(`func/gl.rs`), `parse_gl`'s 1:1 gate is FALSE on 811 of 878 workload TUs, and
any `.gl` framing in this lane loses the required-zero delta.

**IR0 does NOT unify the splitters.** The gate framing (`split_functions_at`,
every `4F 1F`) and the census framing (`split_function_bodies_at`, `LO`-anchored
plus the additive bare-`4C` pass) stay two. They are pinned apart on purpose
(`func/bind.rs`) and their disagreement is published (`emit-splitter-disagree`).
Unifying them moves the census numerator, which is what everything else is
differenced against. **IR0 gives them one input, not one answer.**

### 3.1 Honest note on the invariant, registered now so it cannot be inflated later

The brief states IR0's invariant as "totality + byte-identical
re-serialization". For a framing whose records are **extents** (half-open byte
ranges) and whose re-serialization is *concatenate `bytes[extent]`*, **byte
identity is implied by index totality**: if the extents tile `[0, len)` exactly,
the concatenation *is* the input. So I1 and I2 are **one invariant computed two
ways**, not two invariants. They are still both computed and diffed, because
that is exactly the #3288 second-derivation discipline — but this rung will not
claim two independent checks where there is one.

The genuinely-independent invariant already exists and is not IR0's:
`crates/c2-il/src/codec.rs` (`IlModel::parse` / `encode`) frames `.ex` into
**typed tokens** and fails closed with `CodecError::CannotRoundTrip` if the
re-encode differs. That is a strictly stronger claim than IR0's, and **it has
never been run on the 878-TU workload** — only on the 386 fixtures
(`crates/c2-harness/tests/il_roundtrip.rs`). Running it is S1 and is the
highest-information, lowest-cost item in the lane.

### The four findings

**F-1. The container codec round-trips the entire workload, and nobody had ever
asked.** `IlModel::parse`/`encode` has held IR0's stated invariant since K1 —
total by construction, fail-closed on a re-encode mismatch — but had only ever
run on the 386 fixtures. On dc3: **870 of 870 bundles re-encode byte-identically,
0 broken, residue empty.** This is the strongest lossless claim in the lane and
it is strictly stronger than IR0's own: a wrong *token* boundary breaks it and
cannot break a framing built from extents. Cost to obtain: one `if` in a scan
that already held every bundle in hand.

**F-2. The fixture corpus contains ONE instance of the disagreement the whole
lane is fenced against.** The gate framing and the census framing differ on
**685 of 870** workload TUs and on **1 of 386** fixtures. The single most likely
and most flattering way this lane could have failed — quietly unifying the two
splitters — would have been **684/685 invisible** on the corpus the suite runs
against. So the property is asserted directly (`the_two_views_are_not_the_same_
segmentation`, plus 9 portable cases of which 4 differ) rather than left to a
corpus that does not contain it.

**F-3. The `.ex` header is 68.65% of a fixture and 0.39% of the workload — the
same bytes, a factor of 176.** The opaque head no incumbent splitter has ever
covered totals **2,543,450 B** across 870 TUs, ~2.9 kB each, which is the
documented ~0x0A54 header/index region. A fixture `.ex` averages 3.9 kB and a
workload `.ex` averages 743 kB. **And that 2,543,450 was byte-for-byte
IDENTICAL across two workload stamps** while the framed total moved by 6,115 B —
the header is invariant to source changes and the bodies are not, which is what
makes it a clean denominator. Anyone sizing reader work off the fixture corpus
is reading a population 176× off on this axis.

**F-4. Routing the production readers through the framing costs 8–14% of the
port's geomean, and the cause is structural.** All eleven production call sites
were switched. It compiles; it cannot change a byte. Five paired interleaved
`c2rs perf` trials, every one in the same direction:

| trial | incumbent | via IR0 | via IR0, `records` suppressed |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 526× | 503× | — |
| A2 | 645× | 558× | — |
| A3 | 580× | 547× | — |
| B1 | 606× | 520× | **700×** |
| B2 | 559× | 510× | **536×** |

The B column isolates it: suppress the `Vec<Record>` allocation and the cost
disappears. The price is **the framing's own records** — 24 B × (functions + 1)
per call, at seven `bundle.rs` sites, several on `PortC2::build`'s path, on a
1.5 MB `.ex` with ~5,340 functions. (Box load 45–112 on 32 cores throughout;
only the paired *direction* is claimed, never the absolute ratios.)

**The reason is structural, not an implementation slip: IR0 v1's records carry
no information the gate view reads.** `gate_segments` needs `starts`; it touches
`records` only because routing through the framing is the point. The production
path pays to build a description it then ignores.

> **The conclusion is about migration ORDER.** Re-express the readers as views
> when the records become **load-bearing** — folded into IR1
> (`ARCHITECTURE_PROPOSAL_2026-08-20.md` §5 step 4, where a record carries a
> binding) — rather than landed alone as step 1. Until then IR0's value is the
> layer it already is: a totality control, an opaque denominator about the
> input, and a fence that makes a silent unification impossible.

Note what could **not** have caught this: the gate is identical, `mismatch` is 0,
every census number is unchanged, and `c2rs perf` is **reported, never gated**.
A required-zero *byte* delta is silent about a required-zero *cost* delta, and
this lane's own primary criterion would have passed the switch.

"one index serves both views" rationale — made all seven do **two**. Fixed
before it shipped by computing the `LO` index inside `body_segments`. The
sharing was buying nothing anyway: **no call site wants both segmentations.**

**DD6 — the plan graded the switch on byte-identity alone.** See F-4. Byte
identity held; the switch was still the wrong move, and no criterion in the
plan or in `docs/rungs/README.md`'s construct-rung definition could express why.

**DD7 — `emit-splitter-*` is printed but not published.** The plan's correction
(c) said these *"already exist"* and warned against minting `ir0-seg-*`
duplicates. The warning was right and was followed. The premise is half true:
`emit-gate-segments` / `emit-splitter-agree` / `-disagree` / `-gate-sees-more` /
`-census-sees-more` reach the screen through the `SPLITTER ANCHORS` prose block,
but **none of them is a `gap-metric` key**, so no script can re-derive them —
which is precisely the failure mode `GapReport::metrics`' own doc comment says
that block exists to prevent. Recorded, not fixed: minting them is a different
lane's call, and this one owed a zero delta.

### Mutation evidence — the tests are not vacuous

Four mutants against the views: removing the never-reuse-a-start rule
(`bundle.rs:571`) — **caught**; removing the additive bare-`4C` pass —
**caught**; changing the `4F` walk's miss step from 1 to 2 — **caught**;

## Found and not taken

1. **Publish `emit-splitter-*` as `gap-metric` keys** (DD7). They are computed
   on every scan and reach the screen only as prose, so no collector can
   re-derive the single most load-bearing disagreement in the reader. Cheap; a
   different lane's call, because this one owed a zero delta.
2. **`ir0-bytes-framed == ir0-ex-bytes-framed` is currently forced** — only
   `.ex` has non-`Opaque` records. Kept as a control that separates the moment
   IR1 frames `.gl`; today it is a tautology and is labelled as one.
3. **The `.gl` framing (IR1) is untouched and is where the real work is.**
   `parse_gl`'s 1:1 gate is false on 811 of 878 workload TUs, three walkers
   disagree in `func/gl.rs`, and 39,170 row-conflicts are already published.
   None of that moved and none of it was supposed to.
4. **The switch, when the records are load-bearing.** F-4's table is the price
   to beat; the branch that did it is in this lane's history, reverted, with
   the isolation experiment beside it.
5. **`c2rs perf` is reported and never gated.** F-4 is a regression class the
   entire warranty stack is blind to. Gating a throughput floor is a real lane
   and this one did not price it.

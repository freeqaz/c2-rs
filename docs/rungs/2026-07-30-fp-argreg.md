An FP parameter's register number is counted over the **FP parameters alone**,
not over its positional index. With a single non-FP leader the two models agree
on every case, which is why the old sweep could not separate them; what
separates them is a non-FP parameter *between* the FP ones, more than one of
them, and the two FP widths interleaved. `scripts/sweep.d/32-fp-argreg.py`
generates exactly that cross product.

ninth live wrong-bytes emit; mismatch 0 and disagreement 0 after.

## 0. The decision this lane propagates

Owner, 2026-08-21, quoted in `docs/GOAL_DECISION_2026-08-21.md` because the
wording carries the ranking:

> *"the goal is: the perfect reproduction that gives us a clear understanding
> of the MSVC internals, to help us with decomp, and also to get parity so that
> we have a 100% open source implementation."*

Two ends, ranked equally. **The verifier-throughput thesis is retired** —
demoted to a property. `CLAUDE.md` and `README.md` were updated in the deciding
commit `b3f131b31`; this lane is everything else.

## 1. What it admits, and what it refuses

**Admits:** a doc that uses throughput to argue what the project should **do**
gets a dated, in-place supersession banner pointing at
`GOAL_DECISION_2026-08-21.md`.

**Refuses, and this is the load-bearing half:**

* **A doc that MEASURES throughput keeps its measurement, verbatim.** Not one
  number was edited or withdrawn anywhere in this lane. `STATUS.md`'s perf cell
  keeps every misreading warning it had — the population-change caveat, the
  623×/653×/689× wall-clock spread, the 674× → 481× load sensitivity, "quote
  the order of magnitude with the count", "never rank two lanes by it".
* **No dated record is rewritten.** House style `765095b42`: strikethrough in
  place with the supersession beside it, so a superseded claim stays readable.
* **Four things are explicitly untouched** and every banner says so where it
  could be misread: the one correctness rule; the port staying I/O-behavioral;
  `PROGRESS_METRIC.md`'s rule that a wrong emit scores strictly below the
  refusal it replaced; and two-sided pricing of every new fence.
* **Nothing in `crates/` changed behavior.** Three comment texts moved, in
  three files, and `git diff` on `crates/` and `scripts/` is comment-only —
  reproduced in §5.

## 2. The distinction that did the work: SUPERSEDED, not SATISFIED

The 2026-08-13 NO-GO against downstream use, and `ARCH_REVIEW_2026-08-21.md`
§7's economics behind it — consumer capped at ≈2.4× with an infinitely fast
c2, its wall-clock bound moved off compilation onto generation, the shipped
hybrid prefilter never once enabled, work-weighted coverage 46 of 162,147
emitted functions — carried a two-clause reopen tripwire.

**Nobody met that tripwire.** The tripwire is guarding a question the project
no longer asks. Every place in this repo that cites it now says **superseded,
not satisfied**, because the two read identically from a distance and lead to
opposite behavior: "satisfied" invites re-checking whether it is still
satisfied; "superseded" says the check has no consumer.

**And the asymmetry is written down everywhere it is stated**, because it is
the half that is easy to lose: throughput arguments can no longer *justify* a
lane, **and they can no longer forbid one either.**

## 4. Estimate vs outcome

No forward estimate is owed — this rung admits nothing and converts nothing.
The one prediction worth recording, because it was **wrong**:

**Predicted:** the sweep is a `docs/` sweep. **Measured:** three sites carrying
the retired thesis live **outside `docs/` entirely** — a Rust module doc, an

sweep `docs/` and stopping there would have left the module doc of the very

## 6. What this lane found that is worth carrying forward

**Amending a heavily cross-cited doc costs line-number stability, and this repo
cites by line.** `STRATEGY_REVIEW_2026-08-13.md:251`, `:271` and `:485-489` are
cited from five files including `CLAUDE.md`. **§251 was therefore struck
through *on its own line*, adding no line**, and §9 was appended at the end of
the file where it shifts nothing. Where a shift was unavoidable — the
`GAPS.md` §5 banner — the two citations that pointed past it were **re-anchored
to `§`**, which is `#3188`'s own prescribed remedy for drift-prone raw line
anchors:

| citation | was | now | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| `STRATEGY_REVIEW` → GAPS | `GAPS.md:1006-1008` | `GAPS.md` §5 | shifted by this lane's banner |
| `STRATEGY_REVIEW` → PRIOR_ART | `PRIOR_ART.md:107-137` | `PRIOR_ART.md` §2 | shifted by this lane's banner |
| `ARCH_REVIEW` → GAPS | `docs/GAPS.md:2601` | `docs/GAPS.md` §8 | **already drifted before this lane** — the target is at 2626 and carries its own 2026-08-21 amendment |

The third row is the point: a raw line anchor had **already** gone stale
without anything noticing, which is exactly `#3188`'s family — *a citation that
resolves to something plausible is undetectable by every check we have*.
`board_audit.sh` reports `RAW LINE-NUMBER ANCHORS: 0` because its check is
scoped to `rungs/<lane>:<line>` refs; **cross-doc `file.md:NNN` citations are
outside it and are unchecked by anything.** Named here, not fixed — building
that check is a real lane with its own price.

**One pointer this lane got wrong in its own first commit and repaired:**
§9's table cited *"board #3357-area"* for the unowned-goal-question row. It is
**#3355**. Caught by reading the board rather than by any instrument, which is
the detection mechanism `#3188` says a wrong pointer does not have.

## 8. What this lane does NOT claim

1. **That the sweep is exhaustive.** It is a grep-driven sweep over `docs/`,
   `crates/`, `scripts/` and `c2host/` for the thesis's vocabulary. A doc that
   argues from throughput without using the words is invisible to it.
2. **That the 209+ dated rung records were swept.** They were **deliberately
   not** — they are frozen records, and rewriting them is what the house rule
   forbids. A rung that argued from throughput was correct when written.
3. **That any economics measurement is withdrawn.** None is. Every figure in
   `ARCH_REVIEW` §7, `GAPS` §5, `PRIOR_ART` §2 and `STRATEGY_REVIEW` §2 stands.
4. **That the still-open decision is settled.** It is not:
   `ARCHITECTURE_PROPOSAL_2026-08-20.md` §8 **decision 0** — fund rows 4a and
   4b, or declare step 5 characterization-only — remains the owner's. The goal
   decision constrains it (parity is unreachable without 4a; understanding is
   not) without answering it.
5. **That `CLAUDE.md` § "Units of work" was re-worded.** It was not — the
   owner's own § "The goal" already carries the characterization promotion, and
   this lane treats `CLAUDE.md` as out of its scope per its brief.

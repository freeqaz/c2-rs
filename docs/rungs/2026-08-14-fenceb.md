### 2.3 The falsifiers fired, and they name one thing

PREREG §5 registered three, in advance. Two fired.

**§5.2 — the break term is not a constant.** `w-c0b1` and `f-c0b1` carry one
break each over the same base and residual **2 and 1**.

**§5.3 — the feature vector is falsified again**, and this time with five
witnesses rather than `w-backedge`'s three:

```text
   same (bwd_targets, bwd_uncond, bwd_cond, break-jumps, named), different charge

     w-c1b0   f-c1b0     3  vs  2      a `while` with 1 continue vs a `for` with 1
     w-c2b0   f-c2b0     4  vs  2                    2 continues
     w-c0b1   f-c0b1     4  vs  3                    1 break
     w-c0b2   f-c0b2     5  vs  4                    2 breaks
     w-c1b1   f-c1b1     5  vs  3               1 continue + 1 break
```

**Every collision is a `while` against a `for`, and the `while` always charges
more.** Read as a mechanism: a `while`'s extra backward reference (a `continue`)
charges **1** and a `for`'s charges **0**; a `while`'s **first** break charges
**2** and a `for`'s charges **1**. The loop **kind** is a term, and it is exactly
the term `R1′` — which is a function of backward branches alone — cannot hold.

**§5.4 did NOT fire.** `g-sep2` (two independent named back edges) charges
exactly twice `g-back1`, so the *named* half of `R1′` survives its own falsifier
— but `g-back2` (two backward references to **one** named target) charges **2**
where `R1′` says 1, so it is `while`'s +1-per-extra-reference again, on a named
target.

`C0` — that the `.sy` declaration token space and the `.ex` label token space
coincide, so `named(t)` is readable at all — is **HIT**: `g-back1`/`g-back2`
report the name `top`, `g-sep2` reports `t1;t2`, and every backward target of
those cells is a declared name.

## 3. TASK 2 — the two-sided price, and the paragraph that matters

**`R1′` failed, and the lift shipped. That is not a contradiction, and this is
the argument.** It is stated at the top of the price because a reader who skips
it will read this rung as a lane that ignored its own negative result.

recovered from the emitted bytes; `do/while`, `for(;;)`+`break` and a backward

indistinguishable where the port looks.*

**That argument is about LOOPS IN GENERAL and it is now stronger than when it

**It is not about this CLASS.**

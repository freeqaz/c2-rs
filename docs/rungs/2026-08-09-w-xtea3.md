## 4. THE LABEL CHANNEL — paid in two terms, and `plan_labels` is untouched

`w-xtea2` §4.3 measured the charge and this lane **declared it as a prior**
rather than re-deriving it: `2721 (.gl) + 9 + 3·5 = 2745` seeded, `1 + 2 + 1 + 3
+ 4 = 11` consumed, `= 2756 == $M2756`, zero residual, every stride read out of
its own probe obj in `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §7.6's **in-the-middle** form.

What this lane found is that paying it is **one layer and not three**:

```rust
// c2_il::IlFunction::label_lead
+ 2 * u32::from(self.xtea_round_loop.is_some())     // the leaf loop: stride 3
+ 4 * u32::from(self.xtea_encrypt_loop.is_some())   // the framed loop: extra 4
```

* **`coff::plan_labels` needed no arm.** It already does `cur += f.label_lead`
  before looking at the frame at all, and `PortC2::label_lead_of` already
  carries the value onto `coff::Function`. Two lines of arithmetic, no new
  concept.
* **`IlBundle::functions`' gate needed no edit.** It asks
  `label_slots(false)? != label_lead() + 1`, and `label_slots`' leaf arm returns
  exactly `label_lead() + 1`. A leaf whose lead is 2 satisfies it by
  construction — which is precisely the reading `w-bdnz` #1983 wrote into that
  gate ("does the charge AGREE with what `plan_labels` will advance") and which
  nobody had used.
* **`label_slots` needed no `None` removal**, because the two loop classes here
  are the first that never had one.

**The first LEAF class with a non-zero lead** (#2694). Every other term in that
sum belongs to a framed class, where the lead moves the function's own `$M`
triple; a leaf has no triple, so lead and total are one number and what it moves
is every LATER function's labels.

**And the number is confirmed a second time, at a mode the workload TU is never
compiled at.** `fixtures/cpp/wxtea3_encrypt_loop.cpp` is a FOUR-function TU
(`?SetKey` is absent — see §6.3) whose charge is `1 + 1 + 3 + 4 = 9`, and it
matches at plain `/O1`. Two independent objs, two different totals, both from
the same two terms.

**`/Ox` is a different world and is unreachable**: the same grid reads 13 for
the leaf loop and 41/37 for the framed one, `label_slots` has no mode parameter
and `wb-label` §7.6 forbids giving it one. The recognizers' mode gates are what
make that safe, which is #1638 doing work it was not written for — `w-xtea2`'s
own reading, used.

## 5. WHAT ACTUALLY BLOCKED THE TU, AND WHY NO PER-FUNCTION INSTRUMENT COULD SEE IT

With all five classes shipping, the TU read:

```text
  [1/1] codegen-gap  src/system/utl/EncryptXTEA.cpp
        (inlined-callee ?Encipher@XTEABlockEncrypter@@AAA_K_KPAI@Z:
         this TU defines it and its lowered body is <= 128 bytes,
         so the port cannot prove c2 kept this call)
  gap-metric fnbyte-exact 5   fnbyte-differs 0   fnbyte-refused 0
```

That is `CEILING.md` §11.2's **T1 ALL-EXACT-NO-MATCH** firing — and NC-1's six
obligations and NC-2's four are all satisfied. There is no missing symbol. The
port knows everything it needs and is refusing on purpose.

**The fence is right in general.** `comdat::fenced_inlined_callee` exists
because a `bl` the port emits and c2 replaced with the callee's body is a wrong
obj (board #232's shape), and its threshold was raised from 64 to 128 by
`w-fence2` precisely to cover GRID-W's **mixed band** at 64–95 emitted bytes,
where 146 calls were kept and 570 inlined. A region no rule may answer.

**`?Encipher` is not in that region, and the project already knew why.**
`docs/whitebox/WB_INLINE_FINDINGS.md` §7's MAY table — five categorical arms,
*"each obj-established here"* — lists

| clause | evidence | port-side use |
|---|---|---|
| a **loop-bodied** callee **> 80 bytes** ⇒ never inlined at `/O1` | F9 + the anchor, 62 cells | the safe *decline* side: the port may keep the call |

and GRID-J measures the loop family's boundary at `(56,80]` against the
straight-line family's `(96,120]`, **identically at the workload flags and at
`/O1 /GS- /c`** — which is what refutes `R7-FLAGS`. §5 of that doc gives the
mechanism: the ceiling is applied to a pre-codegen tuple COUNT and emitted bytes
are a proxy that over-credits a loop by roughly 1.55, because the induction
variables, the compare and the branch collapse into one `bdnz`.

`?Encipher` is **116** emitted bytes with a `bdnz` — 36 above the top of the
loop bracket. Four of §7's five arms were already in
`callee_is_one_c2_expands`; the fifth was not, and adding it is the conversion.

```rust
if g.body_has_loop() && gb.text.len() > INLINE_DECLINE_LOOP_BYTES { return false; }
```

**This widens acceptance, which is the dangerous direction**, and the rung says
so. It is bounded three ways: it is one of the five rows the whitebox lane
licensed *for exactly this use*; it fires only above a bound three times tighter
than the one it replaces, so the straight-line side of the mixed band is
untouched and only the top 15 bytes of it are entered on the loop side; and it
is graded by the oracle over 354 fixtures × two modes and 878 workload TUs,
where a mis-prediction is a `mismatch` and not a silence. The gate found none.

`IlFunction::body_has_loop` is the new locator — an **enumeration, not a
derivation**, because a class silently absent from it costs reach and never a
byte.

### 9.1 The calibration reading, and it is not flattering either

**Sixteen graded, sixteen HIT, one VOID.** A clean sweep is not a good
calibration result; it is evidence the probabilities were **too low**. P1 at
0.30, P4 and P5 at 0.40 — the three rows that carried real information — were
priced for a lane that would convert one or two of three bodies, and the reason
they were priced that way is the base rate (`w-xtea2` converted one of four,
`CEILING.md` §10.30's rate is ~1 TU per lane at best). What the base rate did
not include is that **`w-xtea2` had already paid the expensive part**: the label
term was measured, the frontier inventory was confirmed, the mode facts were
recorded, and the probe method was written down. A lane that inherits a
completed measurement should price its execution higher than the population it
came from.

**The row this lane should have registered and did not** is the one that
actually decided the outcome: nothing in the PREREG anticipated that the last
blocker would be the inline fence. P1a (*"if it declines, N ≤ 6"*) is void
because the lane converted, but a decline at the fence would have been N = **1**
— a row worth more than the four encoder-count rows put together, and one the
PREREG could have carried had it run `CEILING.md` §11.4 item 2 as a *forecast*
rather than as a checklist.

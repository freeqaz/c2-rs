## 2. The vocabulary, and what "graded" means

For a rotated sentinel walk the loop **body** is the words from the loop top up
to but excluding the back edge — a merge of the **chain** `c1..cN` (the
accumulate, in data-dependence order) with the **induction pair**: the
update-form `lbzu` and the **record form** (`extsb.` signed, `mr.` unsigned) that
writes the CR field the back edge tests.

```text
    a    chain words BEFORE the lbzu                      (a == L)
    R    body index of the record form
    p    chain slot of the last chain word READING the physical register CHAR
    pv   chain slot of the last chain word reading the char's VALUE — a SOURCE
         fact, available before allocation, where `p` is not
    M    number of PRODUCERS (board #644: a producer is not one instruction)
```

Two **regimes**, read off which register `lbzu` writes:

```text
    TWO   lbzu writes a scratch; CHAR is live across it; entry form ROT
    SAME  lbzu writes CHAR itself; ONE register; entry form JUMPIN (#773)
```

Every one of these is decided from the bytes. `N` is taken from the **emitted**
body, so a cell whose source chain constant-folded is still gradeable — and
`emitted N != intended N` is a **printed count per grid**, never absorbed. Every
rate is `n of m` with `m` beside it; **reached and graded are separate counters,
printed even when equal; a cell that fails to capture is a FAILURE with its own
counter**; and a cell excluded from a rate prints its reason.

## 3. The rule

### 3.1 S1 — the load slot, and the part of it that is startling

> ### `a <= 1`. **At most one chain word ever precedes the induction load**, at every body length the grid reaches — up to a ten-word body.

**131 of 131 cells, 84 of 84 held out.** This is the half of S1 worth having: it
says the schedule does not grow a prologue as the body grows, so an emitter never
has to search a position. The full form —

> `a = 1`, except `a = 0` when `N <= 2` and `p = 0`

— is **80 of 84 held out**, and **all four misses are cells with a split
producer** (§5). On the 74 held-out cells whose producers are single words it is
**74 of 74**.

### 3.2 S2 — the record form's slot

> ### TWO regime: `R = max(p + 1, a + 2)` — the earliest slot at which the carried char's register is dead *and* the load's result has landed. SAME regime: `R = N + 1`, the last word of the body.

**82 of 84 held out**, and **67 of 67** once the two cells in which c2 hoisted a
wide literal into a register are dropped (§5.2). Both terms of the `max` are
load-bearing and both are exercised: `g-644-1` and `j-str1..3` are decided by
`a + 2` and the whole `c-last`/`c-every` family by `p + 1`.

### 3.3 S3m — the regime, and it must be counted in PRODUCERS

> ### SAME iff `pv = 0` **and** `M >= 4`. The induction load reuses `CHAR` exactly when the char's value dies at the chain's first producer and the chain has at least four producers.

**84 of 84 held out, 131 of 131 overall.** This is the rule that decides the
entry form, so it closes the loop on **#773**: c2 flips to `JUMPIN` at `M = 4`
because the peel and the induction load then write the same register.

**And it carries a registered NEGATIVE prediction that was counted rather than
assumed**: no TWO-regime cell can exist with `pv = 0` at `M >= 4`. **37 cells
reached the predicate and 0 violated it** — printed as a count, because an
absence nothing tried to violate is not a graded prediction.

### 3.4 S4r / S4n — the allocation, split into structure and names

> ### **S4r (structure).** TWO regime: the last producer writes the accumulator's home; every producer entirely BEFORE the record shares one register `T1`; every producer entirely AFTER it shares a different `T2`; and when `a = 1` and `pv = 0`, producer 0 takes `CHAR` itself. SAME regime: the last producer writes the home and every other producer shares one register.
> ### **S4n (names).** `T1 = r8`, `T2 = r9`, home `= r3`.

**S4r is 81 of 84 held out and 9 of 12 on Grid J, the only set registered before
it existed. S4n is 79 of 84.** Restricted to the port's own signature class,
**both are 67 of 67**.

**The split is the finding, and it was registered as a split before it was
measured.** S4n's two non-#644 misses are `i-slot1n3` (`11 9 3`) and `j-pool1`
(`11 9 3`) — cells where the walked pointer moves off formal slot 0 and the whole
register pool shifts under roles that do not move. That is **w-hash's #770
mechanism 11 arriving inside the body**, and `PtrWalkModLoop` already refuses
every one of those signatures, which is why the restricted number is the one a
lowering faces.

### 3.5 The bytes, side by side

```text
  N=3, pv=0, M=3 -> TWO             N=4, pv=0, M=4 -> SAME
  add    r11,r11,r3   chain0        add    r9,r11,r3    chain0
  lbzu   r9,1(r10)    a=1           lbzu   r11,1(r10)   a=1, writes CHAR
  xori   r8,r11,3     chain1        xori   r9,r9,3      chain1
  extsb. r11,r9       R=3           addi   r9,r9,5      chain2
  addi   r3,r8,5      chain2        ori    r3,r9,9      chain3
  bf     2,-20                      extsb. r11,r11      R=5 = N+1
                                    bf     2,-24
  guard: bclr (GUARDRET, ROT)       entry: b .+24 (JUMPIN)
```

One source op added, and **the entry form, the allocation and the record's slot
all move together** — which is exactly w-rotate's `SCHEDULE -> REGISTER NEED ->
ENTRY FORM` chain observed end to end in one pair of objs.

## 5. Board #644 is the boundary, and it was probed deliberately

The prereg registered a #644 probe in §5.1 **before any grid existed** — *"at
least one cell's chain must carry a constant too large for `addi`, so that a
two-word producer sits inside the chain."* **13 of 131 graded cells carry a split
producer and 4 of them have the record form scheduled BETWEEN a producer's two
halves.** The counts are printed by the script.

**The two units are needed in opposite directions, and no rule works in both.**

| rule | over SLOTS | over PRODUCERS |
|---|---:|---:|
| **S1** the load slot | **74 of 74** (no split) | 77 of 84 |
| **S3** the regime | 125 of 131 | **131 of 131** |
| **S4** the temps | 108 of 131 | **115 of 131** |

So *"the schedule is a function of the chain's length"* is false in both
readings: the load slot is decided by the chain's **words** and the regime by its
**producers**. A lowering must carry both counts.

### 5.1 A registered prediction that came true on four cells, not one

ADDENDUM 2 §A2.1.1 registered S4r **without** a clause for a straddling
producer, named `g-644-1` as the single cell that would miss it, and said in
terms: *"Grid J mints four more of them to find out whether the miss reproduces
or whether `g-644-1` is the single-cell trap."*

**It reproduced. `j-str1`, `j-str2` and `j-str3` are three fresh straddle cells
and all three miss S4r in the same direction, and they are exactly S4r's held-out
misses.** So the straddle clause is real and `g-644-1` was not the Nth cell —
which is the first time in this project's record that going after the Nth cell
found a *family* rather than an exception.

### 5.2 The other boundary, found by a positive check

S2's only two misses inside the port's signature class are `h6-two` and
`j-pool3`, and **both are cells in which c2 HOISTED a wide literal into a
register before the loop** — taking one out of the pool the body would otherwise
use and moving the record's slot with it. The script detects the hoist from the
preamble's bytes and prints `2 of 69 cells hoist one`, so this is a counted
boundary and not an excused miss. `PtrWalkModLoop`'s IL recognizer refuses wide
literals (`is_mulli_literal`, `simm16`), so the boundary sits outside the
admitted class by construction rather than by choice.

## 6. Board #747's fixture, in its own shape

w-rotate §7.1 states the shape in advance and ADDENDUM 2 repeats it before the
code existed: **two bodies of DIFFERENT lengths in the same class, in ONE TU.**

Neither `scripts/expr_sweep.sh` (single-function TUs from an enumerated axis set)
nor `scripts/mode_cross.sh` (that same corpus crossed with the lane registry) can
produce it, so **both would grade a one-length schedule GREEN**. Grid K compiles
it and reads both loops out of the TU's `.text` COMDATs:

```text
  k-1-3   P: N=1 a=0 R=2 TWO  | Q: N=3 a=1 R=3 TWO   -> SEPARATING
  k-2-6   P: N=2 a=0 R=2 TWO  | Q: N=6 a=1 R=7 SAME  -> SEPARATING
  k-3-8   P: N=3 a=1 R=3 TWO  | Q: N=8 a=1 R=9 SAME  -> SEPARATING
  k-same  P: N=1 a=0 R=2 TWO  | Q: N=1 a=0 R=2 TWO   -> identical  (CONTROL)
```

**3 of 3 separating pairs.** `k-2-6` is the sharpest: one TU, one set of flags,
and its two functions take **different register regimes and different entry
forms**. A model that hard-codes one interleave is wrong about at least one
function of an obj that links, and **no single-function corpus can see it**.

**The reader's widening is itself graded**, which is the part that makes this a
fixture and not a demo: `all_loops` refuses any cell whose back-edge ranges
overlap, `k-same` is a control that would go red if the reader paired an edge
with the wrong target, and every single-loop cell reproduces its earlier verdict
unchanged in the same run.

### 6.1 What is NOT discharged, and why the rung says so

**The port emits no variable-length body, so there is no `crates/` mutation for
this fixture to bite**, and no `fixtures/cpp/*.cpp` is added. The prereg said
this in advance: *"a fixture that cannot be turned red by breaking the rule it is
supposed to guard is not a fixture"*, and shipping one whose mutation was never
run would be the ungraded-code-path refusal of w-loop §5.3 reason 2 wearing a
different hat. **#747's shape is demonstrated and its port-side discharge is
owed by whichever lane builds the lowering** — filed as **#792**.

## 8. Why `cflow-loop` still did NOT enter `PORT_CFG_CLASSES`

w-rotate gave three reasons. **Two of them are answered by this lane and the
third is not, and the third is the one that governs.**

1. ~~The entry form is not evaluable from IL~~ — **answered.** S3m decides the
   regime from `pv` and `M`, both IL facts, at 131 of 131; #773 then gives the
   entry form.
2. ~~The interleave is unstated~~ — **answered.** S1, S2, S4r, at 67 of 67 in
   the port's class.
3. **The list cannot express a restriction — UNCHANGED, and now it is the whole
   blocker.** `PORT_CFG_CLASSES` is a flat `&[&str]` matched against a census key
   that is the bare string `cflow-loop` (`crates/c2-harness/src/gap/factors.rs:44`).
   The claim this lane can support is *"`cflow-loop`, restricted to the sentinel
   walk at `/O1`, pointer formal at slot 0, chains of single-word producers with
   no hoisted literal"*, and **that sentence has no representation in a list of
   strings.** The only available move is the wholesale claim, and the wholesale
   claim is still false. **Board #778 stays open and is now load-bearing rather
   than latent** — it was *"becomes wrong the moment a lane wants to report
   partial coverage of a class"*, and this is that lane.

**Widening the list would have been World D.** Closing #778 means a sub-class
predicate — an instrument change that must itself be graded — and it is not made
here, because an instrument change landed in the same rung as the measurement
that motivates it has nothing independent to grade it against.

## 8.1 What a lowering costs, so that "reachable" is not read as "cheap"

Surveyed before the rules were known, so the price is not a rationalisation:

* `PtrWalkModLoop` (`crates/c2-il/src/func/mod.rs:1269`) carries `params`,
  `acc_init`, `mul_k` — **three scalars and no operation list at all.**
* `try_parse_ptr_walk_loop` (`.../body/shapes/ptr_walk_loop.rs:221`) consumes the
  accumulate with literal `eat_byte(0x04)` / `eat(&[0x04,0x02])` / `eat_byte(0x06)`
  calls at fixed cursor positions. **It is a byte-pattern match, not a walk.**
* `ptr_walk_loop_text` hand-writes twenty words behind
  `debug_assert_eq!(t.len(), 80)`.

So a body-parameterized lowering needs a chain representation minted on the IL
side, a generic recognizer where a fixed pattern is today, and a producer-aware
emitter — and it needs the **producer** count as a first-class quantity, because
§5 shows two of the five rules cannot be stated without it. **Reachable means the
facts are measured. It does not mean small.**

## 8.2 What was deliberately not built

* **No `Selected` variant, no widening of `codegen::ptr_walk_loop`.** The rules
  are stated and graded against real `c2`; nothing in `crates/` moved.
* **`cflow-loop` did not enter `PORT_CFG_CLASSES`** (§8).
* **No `fixtures/cpp/*.cpp`** — §6.1.
* **The `%`/`div` spine was not touched.** Lane w-divmod owns it and its `twi`
  placement discriminator; **no cell in this lane contains a `/` or a `%`**, and
  `Sort.cpp`'s own modulo is treated as a black box.
* **S1's `a = 0` clause was not extended to cover the four #644 cells.** Their
  behaviour is on the page as a residual shape (§5), not as a fitted clause —
  four cells is the arity a placement rule dies at here.

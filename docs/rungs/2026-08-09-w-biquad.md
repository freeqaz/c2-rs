## 3. The correction this lane exists to make: `expr-op-0x27` is a DISPATCH FACT

The commission is explicit that the family's size is context and not a promise,
and it is right, but the reason is sharper than "the key is not its worth".

**`expr-op-0x27` is raised by `parse_expr`'s fall-through arm.** It is reached
when the shape ladder has declined a body and the straight-line expression
parser then meets a byte it does not model. So the key says *"no recognizer
claimed this body"* — a statement about the **ladder**, not about the operand
stream. That is structurally the same position `expr-brfalse` occupies, which
`expr.rs`'s own doc-comment already says in as many words.

**The offset-add grammar has been implemented since `w-34`.** Both recognizers
this lane ships call `designator::eat_offset_adds`, which consumes

```text
   33 <int-like> k   27 <PTR>          a member offset, re-typing the address
   33 <int-like> k   28 00 00          a subscript offset, not re-typing it
```

sums them, enforces the `28 00 00` payload and reports the last re-type. **This
lane added zero bytes of grammar to either opcode.** `w-readpx`
(`WB_READER_FINDINGS` §3.3) had priced the grammar cost at NONE and ranked it a
lowering with **0 TUs**; `w-dclass` §6.1 measured its head worth six functions
and zero TUs; `w-band` found it reads `NoSignal`. All three are confirmed here,
from the other side: a lane that *converted* the TU the key was supposed to be
about moved the key by **zero**.

`expr-op-0x28`'s width disagreement (`WB_READER_FINDINGS` §3.4) is untouched and
unclaimed: all 28 witnesses read the literal `28 00 00`, which is exactly what
`walk_offset_adds` already requires.

## 4. What ships

### 4.1 `shapes::fp_store_diamond` — the 35 words (#2530)

A two-armed `if`/`else` over a null-guarded pointer formal, whose then-arm and
join are pooled-constant float member stores and whose else-arm is a CSE'd
division run. Three measured rules decide its words, and the emitter's header
carries the byte table:

* **B-RULE** (`WB_CHOOSER_FINDINGS` §3.3; 3 entry-block witnesses, 6
  block-local): one `lis` per pool per function, at the top of the **earliest
  block that dominates every use**; the `lfs` is **at the use**. `A` (0.0f) is
  used in the then-arm and after the join, so its `lis` is word 0; `B` (1.0f) is
  used once, so its `lis` is the then-block's first word and its `lfs` five
  words below it.
* **B′-RULE** (§4.1; 5 flip witnesses, 15 non-flip): the CSE'd divisor loads
  **first** in every statement of the run except the last.
* **f0 is live across the whole diamond**, which is why the else-arm takes
  f13/f12.

**B-RULE-2 is not consulted, by decision.** The compare/branch separation slot
is `medium` at exactly three witnesses; the entry block's word order is
transcribed from this class's own obj and no clause anywhere in the lane asks a
separation question. PREREG decline clause **D6**, honoured rather than tested.

| 2 | **A framed function that introduces a pooled constant.** `emit_comdat_obj` refuses it: where its `.rdata` sits relative to its own `.pdata` COMDAT, and where the symbol pairs sit among `$M`/`$M`/`$T`, are both uncaptured. `OBJ_GY_SHAPES` §2 has pools only on leaves and §3 has the `.pdata` group only without them. |
| 3 | **`emit_obj`'s packed pool order.** Its comment says first-reference; §2.3 measures the reverse; no packed obj has ever had two constants in one function, so the disagreement is unreachable and this lane refuses both new classes on that path rather than repairing it — a repair is a claim about every packed FP obj the port already matches. |
| 4 | **A third pooled constant, and `double` pools.** `WB_CHOOSER`'s B5 gives two pools in one block taking r11 then r10; a third has no witness and the emitter has no register to hand out. |

| 6 | **`w-park`'s fifteen**, for `Biquad.cpp`, are paid — §11 counts them. `mmio.cpp`'s eleven are untouched. |

## 11. Which of `w-park`'s fifteen are paid

`w-park` §6.2 priced `Biquad.cpp` at fifteen without enumerating them; its
ladder `lad_bq.cpp` is six cells, 0 of 6 in class. At this tip **all six cells'
constructs are in class**, and the fifteen resolve into three groups:

| group | count | disposition |
|---|---:|---|
| the two designator opcodes (`0x27`, `0x28`) | 2 | **NOT PAID — never owed.** Implemented since `w-34`; §3 |
| the reader rungs (`expr-cmp-eq` on a pointer, `expr-brfalse`, the arm scopes, the join label, the ctor's `return this` plumbing, the FP literal, the float store, the float load, the `fdivs`) | 9 | **PAID**, by the two recognizers, all inside their own grammar |
| the chooser rules (B-RULE, B-RULE-2, B′-RULE, M-RULE) | 4 | **B-RULE, B′-RULE and M-RULE PAID; B-RULE-2 DECLINED by name** (§4.1) |

So the honest count is **thirteen paid, one declined, and two that were a
misreading of the census key** — and the misreading is the finding, not the
arithmetic.

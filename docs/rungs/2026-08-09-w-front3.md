## 4. Four things the lifting found that the counting could not

### 4.1 `Pool.cpp` — the published price came out of a round that measured nothing

`work/w-depth/chain.py`'s `sink_token` predates board **#816**, which refined
`expr-op-0xNN` into `expr-op-0xNN-TTTT` (the operand TYPE) for DIVIDE and
MODULO. The driver strips `expr-op-0x` and hands the rest to the sink, so
`expr-op-0x05-8641` becomes the token `op:05-8641`. `ChainSink::parse` rejects
it and sets `bad`; `chain_step_with`'s **first** statement after the enable
check is `if c.bad { return Some(Err("expr-chain-badtoken")) }`, before it even
looks at the byte. So the whole sink is off and **every** function in the TU
refuses at `expr-chain-badtoken`, at whatever byte the cursor happens to be on.

Round by round, on this master (`work/w-front3/depth/`):

```text
  0  expr-brtrue 1 · expr-op-0x27 2
  1  expr-op-0x30 1 · expr-op-0x32 1 · expr-op-0x53 1
  2  expr-op-0x4B 2 · expr-op-0x4F 1
  3  expr-call-in-expr-other 1 · expr-jump 2
  4  expr-op-0x05-8641 1 · expr-op-0x54 2      <- the malformed token is minted here
  5  expr-chain-badtoken-0xB9 3                <- ALL THREE functions, sink OFF
```

`w-front2` published `Pool.cpp` at READER **6** with exit `badtoken-0xB9`, and
its §4.2 table records `Pool.cpp  D=8 EXIT noform-0x05 → D=6 EXIT
badtoken-0xB9` as a *result*. The `-0xB9` suffix is the byte under the cursor
and carries no information at all.

`work/w-front3/ladder.py` fixes the token (`op:%s` over `rest.split("-")[0]`)
and — more importantly — **carries the positive control `chain.py` lacks**: the
blockers are re-read after every round and `expr-chain-badtoken` anywhere is a
hard stop reported as `CONTROL-RED`. A control that cannot go red is not a
control; this one goes red on precisely the defect that produced the number it
replaces.

Repaired, `Pool.cpp` climbs to **9** and stops at `noform-0x10`.

## 5. Unreachable rungs — one confirmed, one propagated, and the screen that says where the rest cannot be

### 5.1 `value_bound` — CONFIRMED, and there are TWO independent reasons, not one

`w-mrslot` §5.1 gives one: the only producer of `IlOp::BoundAddr` in
`crates/c2-il` is `bind_run_ops`' discharge loop, which rewrites the BASE
position. That reproduces here — `work/w-front3/reach.py` finds **exactly one**
producer, and the loop's own body pushes the rewritten `b` and then `*v` and
`*st` untouched.

The second is upstream of it and is in the same function's comment: *"Only the
BASE position can hold a bound name at this point — **clause 1 refused every
value one**"*. So even a lane that widened the discharge to rewrite the value
position would find nothing to rewrite: `STORE_RUN_BIND_ADDR_PRODUCER` /
`STORE_RUN_BIND_MIXED_KIND` have already refused the body.

**Two empirical checks, because a code reading is what put `value_bound` on the
roadmap in the first place:**

* with the mixed-kind clause lifted, `xboxheap` reports `store_run_call`'s
  sentence on **both** the replica and the real TU (§4.3) — never
  `value_bound`'s *"a store run whose value is a bound reference"*;
* that message string is witnessed in **zero** committed files under `work/` or
  `docs/` — no scan log, no sweep tally, no rung, in the project's whole
  history.

### 5.2 A SECOND inert rung, and its published cause cannot be its cause

`crates/c2-core/src/codegen/leaf/store.rs`, on `alloc::Root::roots` (board
**#1231**'s carrier), states why it is `None` at every producer:

> *"every producer that reaches here is a literal (`s.producer` is `s.lit`), so
> the pair is refused. Board **#840** / `w-mixed` §5 is the reason that is not a
> defect: no register-derived producer reaches `alloc::allocate` from today's
> emitter at all, **because a bind-valued store is refused upstream as
> `value_bound`**."*

**`value_bound` cannot fire, so it cannot be refusing anything upstream.** The
carrier is genuinely inert, and the reason is `bind_run_ops`' clause 1 and
`parse_simple_gpr_run` declining `Load(<bound local>)` in the value position —
both of them in `crates/c2-il`, both of them one layer higher. The consequence
is concrete: **a lane that lifted `value_bound` to reach #1231's carrier would
move nothing**, and the sentence above is what would send it there.

`docs/ALLOC.md` §148 carries board #1218's four-rung wording as well and is
equally stale; it is named here rather than edited, for the reason `w-mrslot`
§5.1 gives — the file's semantics are shared with two concurrent lanes and a doc

### 5.3 The screen, its answer, and its blind spot

`work/w-front3/reach.py` runs the brief's own procedure over all 21 `IlOp`
variants: find the refusal's input, find every producer of it in
`crates/c2-il` (test code excluded, brace-counted from `mod tests` — the two

```text
  VARIANT-LEVEL UNREACHABLE: 0 of 21
```

**So there is no second `BoundAddr`-shaped hole at the variant level.** Every
remaining unreachability of this kind must be POSITIONAL, and the screen prints
the eight candidates — the variants with ≤ 3 producers, where one position can
account for all of them:

```text
  LoadInd 2 · LoadIndSized 2 · AddrOf 3 · SymAddr 2 · StoreIndFp 2
  LoadIndFp 1 · BoundAddr 1 · FpLit 1
```

`BoundAddr` is the only one of the eight whose consumers read **two different
positions**; the other seven are read only where they are produced.

**The screen went red on its own first run and the failure is left in the
source rather than deleted.** It reported six unreachable variants — `And`,

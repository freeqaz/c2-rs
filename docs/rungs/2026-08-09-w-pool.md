## 0. THE COMMISSION, AND THE ONE SENTENCE IT TURNS ON

> The brief: *"`Pool.cpp` and `EncryptXTEA.cpp` are yours. For these two, **the
> reader is the whole blocker**: no binding gap, no writer-composition gap, no
> whole-obj obligation standing behind it. That is a materially different
> situation from every frontier TU declined this week, each of which had a
> second layer behind the first."*

The first clause is **true and this lane confirms it byte for byte**. The second
does not follow from it, and the gap between them is this lane's deliverable.

> ### **`{body-out-of-class}` IS NOT A PRICE, FOR THE SAME REASON A FIRST-CAUSE HISTOGRAM IS NOT.** `w-vec` established that `gl-stop-26-introduced` fires first on 811 TUs and converts zero, because `body-out-of-class` fires on 851 of 851 and the ALSO column is what prices a repair. One level up, the same shape: **`body-out-of-class` being a TU's ONLY cause means the reader is the only gate that FIRES — it does not mean the reader is the only thing OWED.** `IlBundle::functions` refuses before an emitter is asked, so on these TUs *every* emitter question is unasked, and four of `decode_causes`' own gates are **vacuous** because they are evaluated over the functions that built, which is 0 of 3 on `Pool.cpp`. **Board #2560.**

**`Pool.cpp` DECLINES at five mechanisms (§3). `EncryptXTEA.cpp` DECLINES at
eight (§4). TU match 20 → 20.** The two prices are re-derived from an empty
chain sink and from the reference objs' own bytes, they replace three inherited
figures (§5), and the smaller TU is smaller **by every axis this lane measures
and by a wider margin than any published ranking said** (§4.3).

### 1.1 `fn_names < fn_total` on all three, and it is NOT `mmio.cpp`'s trap

That row is exactly the shape #2406 fired on — one `.gl` name against eleven
`.ex` segments — and here it means something else. `fn_names` is
`c2_il::mangled_names(gl).len()`, whose acceptance requires
`bytes[1].is_ascii_alphabetic()` and therefore **drops every `??`-prefixed
name**: constructors, destructors, the `??__E` thunks. Both TUs have a
constructor; `Biquad.cpp` has one too.

The **gate** does not use it. `Bindings::per_record` calls `gl_defined_names` =
`gl_defined_names_framed(gl, sep26 = true, codec::gl_offset_framed)` and applies
exactly two checks — `bound.len() == segs.len()`, and every record's offset equal
to its segment's start, in order — which are precisely the two clauses
`decode_causes` publishes as `bind-record-count-ne-segments` and
`bind-offset-ne-segment-start`. **Neither fires on any of the three, and no
`gl-stop-*` fires either.**

> ### **`Bindings::per_record` RETURNS `Some` ON ALL THREE READER-ONLY TUs AND BINDS EVERY RECORD 1:1 TO ITS SEGMENT.** Two consecutive lanes priced codegen on a TU whose gate bound nothing (`w-mmioclose` #2406 by `looks_mangled`, `w-vec` #2503 by the `.gl` walk stop). These three are the other case, and it is worth naming because the check is one line either way: **`fn_names` is a census field and says nothing about the gate — on `mmio.cpp` it under-read for one reason and here for another, and only the absence of the two `bind-*` causes settles it.** **Board #2561.**

### 1.2 …and the cause set UNDERSTATES, by construction

`decode_causes` evaluates `shape-token-unresolved`, `label-stride-mismatch`,
`label-counter-unreadable` and `locally-defined-callee` over **the functions that
DID build** — **0 of 3** on `Pool.cpp`, **1 of 5** on `EncryptXTEA.cpp`. Those
four are therefore **vacuous** here and can fire the moment the reader admits a
body. Its own module doc says so (*"a gate that cannot fire on a partial function
list is not evidence that it would not fire on the whole one"*), and this is the
population where that sentence has teeth.

**`unclaimed-gl-symbol` is the exception and is *strengthened* by an empty
function list**: `accounted` is then the bound names and nothing else, the
strictest form of the test, so its pass is real. That asymmetry is why §3 and §4
count it at zero and the other four as unmeasured.

### 5.1 The ladders, and the instrument that produced them

`work/w-pool/ladder.sh` walks one TU from an **empty** `C2RS_SINK_CHAIN`,
adding at each rung exactly the token the census reported, until the reported key
is not sinkable. The sink is **poisoned**, so no rung can move an obj byte and
the whole walk is a measurement.

```text
Pool.cpp        rung 0  expr-op-0x27      -> op:27
                rung 1  expr-op-0x32      -> op:32      (board #622's successor,
                rung 2  expr-op-0x4B      -> op:4B       re-derived under the poison)
                rung 3  expr-op-0x4F      -> op:4F
                rung 4  expr-op-0x30      -> op:30
                rung 5  expr-op-0x53      -> op:53
                rung 6  expr-brtrue       -> op:39
                rung 7  expr-op-0x41      -> op:41
                rung 8  EXIT: expr-jump   (expr-call-in-expr-other still standing)

EncryptXTEA.cpp rung 0  expr-op-0x27 · 1  expr-op-0x28 · 2  expr-op-0x30
                rung 3  expr-load-type-8882 · 4  expr-intrinsic-memcpy
                rung 5  expr-op-0x55 · 6  expr-op-0x4C · 7  expr-op-0x4B
                rung 8  EXIT: expr-jump   (expr-convert-target-A882 still standing)

Biquad.cpp      rung 0  EXIT: expr-cmp-eq   (owned by lane w-biquad; not touched)
```

**Two things the first version of this script got wrong**, both recorded:

1. it counted `expr-chain-sink-poison` as a terminal, which stops the walk at the
   first body that finishes and leaves the others unwalked — `EncryptXTEA`'s
   ladder read **4** instead of 8 under that rule. A terminal that is an artefact
   of the stopping rule is `w-bd` #1316's own error, one instrument over;
2. the ladder counts **reader** depth only, and this lane's whole finding is that
   reader depth is the axis that does *not* separate these two TUs (§4.3). The
   number is published with that beside it, never alone.

**`expr-jump` as a terminal is NOT confirmed against either body**, and is
reported as a ladder terminal only. `w-nc` found a 12-byte body with no control
flow at all reported blocked at `expr-jump` (CEILING §11.4 item 5), so the key is
a known fall-through and a lane taking it as a construct would be repeating that.
`expr-op-0x27` **is** confirmed, on a 2-statement `cflow-straight` body (§3.1).

### 5.2 …and from an EMPTY sink neither walk reaches a `noform-*` key AT ALL

Not `0xBD` (`w-xlr` §10's band bound), not `0x00` and not `0x4F` (`w-xtea`'s
#2337/#2338, on this same TU). **Sixteen rungs across two TUs and zero `noform`
keys anywhere in either walk.**

That is not a refutation of the published depths — it is the third independent
route to what #2337 already established. A `noform-0xNN` is what a **desync**
looks like: the sink steps a token whose width the tree has not pinned *for the
bytes actually there*, and the refusal then lands on whatever byte the cursor
reaches, *"indistinguishable from an unpinned opcode"*. A walk seeded with 37
tokens can sink a token this stream does not contain; a walk that adds only the
token this TU's own census just asked for **cannot**. The from-empty ladder
removes the mechanism rather than working around it.

**Neither TU carries a floating-point literal**, so #2343's specific nine-byte
defect is not what is at work here — which is why these two depths are quotable
at all, and it is checked rather than assumed: `Pool.cpp` and `EncryptXTEA.cpp`
are integer throughout, and `?Encipher`'s `0x9E3779B9` is materialized as
`addis`+`addi`, not as a constant pool entry.

### 9.2 Thirty claims, zero misses, and that is a calibration problem

Board #770's tally is about lanes over-estimating. This lane registered
thirty-one claims and missed **none**, with the two lowest probabilities (C1 at
0.06 and C2 at 0.02) correctly declining to fire. That is not a good score; it is
a sign the claims were mostly **restatements of things already measured before
the PREREG was frozen** — §0 of the PREREG says so explicitly for eight of them,
and C5–C8, C10, C13 were all decidable from artifacts that already existed on
disk at freeze time.

The two claims that carried real risk were **C15** (0.45, distinct blockers on
two cells not yet cut) and **C17** (0.60, the test delta). Both landed. The
claims that carried *no* risk and still shipped as predictions are the ones a
successor should refuse to register: a prediction whose evidence is already in
`work/` is a finding wearing a probability.

## 2. The question this lane was given, answered

> the exit-value merge charges a **sixth label slot**, because c2 branches
> **backwards** into the first arm. […] the label counter and the codegen class
> fail at the same place, which is either a coincidence worth understanding or
> the shape of the real rule. **Find out which.**

### 2.1 The instrument

`work/w-label/cflabels.py` — `scripts/gt_label_stride.py`'s seed-free
construction (three in-TU anchors, every number a difference *inside one obj*,
so the `.gl` seed, the mangled-name lengths and the `/Gy` per-function surcharge
all cancel) with a probe list that varies **only the control-flow shape** and
holds the function class fixed at framed Class A. It **imports** the shipped
instrument rather than copying it, so the anchor control, the group walker and
the `minted` counter are the same code that produced `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §1.
`scripts/` is lane w-modes'/w-shapes' seam and was not edited.

`cftargets.py` reads the same objs' bytes back and counts intra-section branch
targets — discriminated by **the absence of a relocation**, not by the opcode
(`CFG_SHAPE.md` §3.3). `cfdis.py` prints one probe's `.text` with every target's
predecessor count. `ilseed.py` reads the `.gl` label seed back out of the IL.

**Controls: 0 failed on 33 rows** across the registered and held-out sets. The
anchor base came back 5 in every obj, measured rather than assumed.

### 2.2 The answer, in one paragraph

**Not a coincidence, and not one rule.** The two boundaries coincide **by
inclusion, not by identity**, and the surviving half of the inclusion is the
half the port needs:

> **Every body containing a BACKWARD intra-section branch charges the counter
> ≥ +1 — 11 of 11 cells. The port's `plan_labels` charges 0.**

The other direction is **weaker than this lane registered and its own held-out
cell refuted it** (§2.4). Forward-only is *necessary* and nowhere near
sufficient: three forward-only cells charge — `cf-ifelse` +1, `cf-merge-tail`
+1, and the held-out `ho-ternary` **+2**.

So the operational statement is one-sided, and that is exactly what an
accept-or-refuse gate wants: **a backward reference is a refusal this lane can
justify from bytes; a forward reference is not thereby safe**, and the map
therefore refuses one and asserts nothing about the other.

### 2.3 The table (`/O1 /GS- /c`, the workload's own mode)

`sur` = stride, less the base, less `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §1.1's *minted*
surcharges (`minted − 5`), so a row paying for a `__savegprlr_N` pair is not
credited with it.

| probe | stride | minted | **sur** | backward? |
|---|---:|---:|---:|:--:|
| `cf-none`, `cf-if2`, `cf-if3`, `cf-ifelse-val`, `cf-goto-fwd` | 5 | 5 | **+0** | no |
| `cf-ifelse` (if/else with a join block) | 6 | 5 | **+1** | **no** |
| `cf-merge-tail` (guard returning the sequence's own literal) | 6 | 5 | **+1** | **no** |
| `cf-merge2` / `cf-merge3` / `cf-merge-mixed` (the exit-value merge) | 6 | 5 | **+1** | yes |
| `cf-goto-back`, `cf-dowhile` | 6 | 5 | **+1** | yes |
| `cf-while`, `cf-for`, `cf-for-continue` | 7 / 9 / 9 | 5 / 7 / 7 | **+2** | yes |
| `cf-forever`, `cf-for-break`, `cf-dowhile2` | 8 / 10 / 8 | 5 / 7 / 5 | **+3** | yes |
| `cf-for2`, `cf-fornest` | 11 | 7 | **+4** | yes |

Four rows (`cf-if`, `cf-void-guard`, `cf-switch-dense`, `cf-switch-sparse`) came
back **stride 1**: c2 made those bodies *leaves*. They are a different class and
are excluded rather than averaged in — and that an 8-arm **dense** `switch` with
a call in every arm is a leaf at `/O1` with **no jump table** is itself worth
recording, matching `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §4's reading and its caveat.

**Two candidate rules were fitted to this table and both are refuted by it**,
stated and killed here rather than shipped:

* *"+1 per distinct interior branch target"* — **15 of 24 registered rows miss**.
  `cf-if3` has three interior targets and costs 0.
* *"+1 per distinct interior JOIN (≥ 2 predecessors)"* — **6 of 24 miss on the
  registered set and 11 of 33 with the held-out cells in**, and the misses run
  **both** ways across the zero boundary, which is the only direction an
  accept-or-refuse gate cares about:

  | cell | interior joins | surcharge |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | `cf-goto-fwd` | 1 | **+0** |
  | `ho-and` (`if (a && b)`) — **two** branches naming one target | 1 | **+0** |
  | `ho-or` (`if (a \|\| b)`) — likewise | 1 | **+0** |
  | `ho-compose`, `ho-compose2` | 1 | **+0** |
  | `ho-ternary` | 1 | **+2** |

  `ho-and` and `ho-or` are the sharpest: **two branches naming one interior
  target with two predecessors, at zero cost** — which is the exact structure
  §6.2 item B's fixup list exists for, and it is free.

Refusing to ship either is `CFG_SHAPE.md` §3.5's precedent applied to this lane's
own work: an 18-row table fitted by all its cells and tested by none was
declined, and these were tested by theirs.

### 2.4 The held-out cell that refuted this lane's own §1.4 — and why it is the best result here

Prediction **L6** was registered *specifically to be scored* on seven cells
chosen to break the boundary, committed at `afe735e` before they ran. Six came
back as registered. **One refuted it:**

```text
  ho-ternary   int P(int a,int b){ return gp(a ? b : b+1) + 1; }     sur +2
```

It is **forward-only** (`cfdis.py`: one `bc` at 0x14 → 0x1c, no backward word)
and it charges **+2**. `work/w-label/PREREG.md` §1.4's second half — *"no body
without a backward branch charges more than +1, 13 of 13"* — is **REFUTED at
14**. The first half, which is what the shipped assertion rests on, is untouched.

**And it is worth more than the six that held**, because of what it is next to.
`cf-ifelse` is the *same emitted shape*: a `bc` over a two-word block into a
**shared `bl`**, i.e. `CFG_SHAPE.md` §3.4.1's tail-merge-plus-hoist, `d_join`
exactly. Same block skeleton, same branch kind, same predecessor structure —
**+1 against +2**. So:

> **The control-flow surcharge is not a function of the emitted object.** Two
> bodies with near-identical emitted CFGs charge differently, so no amount of
> reading the port's own output can recover it.

That is the shape lane w-order established for the emit *order* one level up —
*"the ordering edge is not in the obj"* — and the brief asked whether the same
IL-not-obj lesson applies here. **It applies, and this lane then looked for the
IL channel and did not find it.** `ilseed.py` reads the `.gl` label seed
(`u32(.gl[7..11])`, `coff::LABEL_SEED_GAP`) for the decisive pairs:

```text
  cf-none      2547     cf-if2      2550  (+0)     cf-dowhile   2551  (+1)
  ho-ternary   2548     cf-ifelse   2550  (+1)     cf-while     2551  (+2)
```

`cf-if2` and `cf-ifelse` **share a seed and differ by +1**; `cf-dowhile` and
`cf-while` **share a seed and differ by +1**. The seed is one TU-level number and
it does not carry the per-function surcharge. **Ruled out, by measurement, not by
argument.** Whether a per-function `.ex` field carries it is **open** — the
port's `IlFunction::label_lead` derives every surcharge it knows from body
*shape*, never from a field, so if such a field exists nobody has found it.

**The consequence for the port is stronger than the refusal it was gathered
for**: the surcharge is derivable neither from the obj (`ho-ternary` vs
`cf-ifelse`) nor from the seed (`cf-if2` vs `cf-ifelse`), so **the port cannot
model it from anything it currently reads.** Any rung that wants a charging shape
owes a new IL channel first, and "read it off the blocks" is closed as a route.

### 2.5 Two disagreements with the record, stated not reconciled

* **w-conv §4 reads "label lead 2" for the in-class cells and "lead 3" for the
  merged ones. This lane measures `extra` 0 / `stride` 5 and `extra` 1 /
  `stride` 6.** The **delta** — +1 for the merge — reproduces exactly and is the
  load-bearing half; the absolute does not. My `extra` is
  `first(P) − first(a0) − base` with `base` read per obj from
  `first(a2) − first(a1)`. I do not know which quantity w-conv's "lead" names and
  did not guess. Both documents' deltas agree and the delta is what the port
  consumes.
* **`LABEL_COUNTER.md` §4 records `for` at +2 and nested `for` at +4.** Raw, this
  worktree reads **+4** and **+6**. The whole difference is the
  `__savegprlr_29`/`__restgprlr_29` pair those loop bodies oblige — §1.1's +2,
  visible as `minted 7` against `minted 5`. With it removed the numbers are §4's
  exactly. **§4's rows are right and are easy to re-derive wrong**; the `minted`
  column is what keeps them honest, and this lane found that out by getting +4

## 3. The construct — `crates/c2-core/src/codegen/labels.rs`

`CFG_SHAPE.md` §6.2 item **B** asks for *"a label identity, a map from label to
block, and a fixup list of (word offset, label, form)"*. That is what this is.

### 3.1 What was there before, and why Z-c overstates it

Board row **Z-c** (W11) reads *"The port emits an intra-section `b` and resolves
a real label→offset map — DONE"*. What was in `calls.rs` is a fixup list with
**one implicit target**:

```rust
let mut early_fixups: Vec<(usize, Option<usize>, u8, u8)> = …;
…
let epi_start = text.len();
for (bc_at, b_at, bo, bi) in early_fixups { … }
```

— every entry resolved against `epi_start`, the epilogue's identity carried by
*the shape of the tuple* (`Some(b_at)` means "an arm's `b`", `None` plus a
still-zero word means "a void arm's `bc`"), and `calls.rs:519` saying so in its
own words four lines above the block that resolved it: *"there is no fixup list
and no label map"*. **The lowering was right and the row is stronger than the
code.** This rung does not restate Z-c; it builds the thing and leaves every byte
alone.

### 3.2 What it enforces

Five invariants, each an **ordinary `Err`** rather than a `debug_assert` — the
release build the gate actually runs compiles those out, and the port must
degrade to `NotImplemented` honestly rather than panic:

| # | invariant | why it is not decoration |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | every referenced label is **defined**, and an undefined one **names itself** | a refusal that cannot say which label it is about is one somebody re-derives |
| 2 | the site is **in range** of the finished text | |
| 3 | the site still holds the **zero placeholder** | catches a caller that emitted over its own pending branch — a branch patched on top of an instruction |
| 4 | **every reference is FORWARD** | **§2.2. This is the ordering rule.** |
| 5 | the displacement **fits the form's field** | `CFG_SHAPE.md` §3.3.1's long-branch expansion is measured and **not built** |

and one at define-time: **a label defined twice is refused**, not overwritten.
Two blocks claiming one target is a lowering defect; silently keeping the last
would emit a legal-looking branch to the wrong place, which is the same failure
mode as a truncated `BD` that the encoders already refuse rather than round.

**`Form` has exactly two variants and deliberately no third.** `bc` and the
intra-section `b` carry a **true self-relative displacement** and take **no
relocation**; an external `b`/`bl` carries `−(own offset)` and takes a `REL24`.
`48000008` and `4bffffec` are the same instruction (board **#191**). An external
branch is **not a label reference — it is a relocation**, and admitting it into
this map is precisely the corruption §3.3 warns a single "patch the branch" path
causes. `encode_tail_branch` stays where it is.

### 3.3 The ordering assertion, and why it is a `coff/` fact

The brief's standing rule is that a rung touching `coff/` adds a portable
assertion per ordering rule. **This rung deliberately does not touch `coff/`** —
and its assertion is still a `coff/` assertion, which is the interesting part:

```
  a BACKWARD branch to label `X`: c2 charges the compiler-label counter at
  least one extra slot for every body with a backward intra-section branch
  (11 of 11 cells, work/w-label/PREREG.md §1.4) and `coff::plan_labels`
  charges none, so the obj would carry a wrong $M as well as a wrong block.
  The magnitude is measured (+1/+2/+3/+4) and NOT modelled.
```

A lowering that emitted a backward branch would be **one label slot low for this
function and every later one in the TU** — six wrong bytes in a symbol table, in
an obj that still links. That is the defect class `LABEL_COUNTER.md` exists for
and that `coff/` has shipped twice. **The refusal lives in `codegen/` and its
justification lives in `coff/`, and this is the seam where the two are tied
together in one place instead of two.**

**Eleven portable unit tests**, all running with no toolchain: the two-references-
to-one-label happy path, the `bc` form, the backward refusal, the self-reference
boundary (`target <= at`, not `<`), the undefined label, the double define, the
written-over site, the `bc` range refusal *and* the matched control that the same
distance is in range for the wider `LI` field (so the range test is a statement
about the field and not about the length), an unreferenced label being legal
(the `/Ox` arm duplicates the epilogue instead of branching to it), and a label
used against a different map.

## 7. What was deliberately not done

* **No `coff/` edit.** The licence this lane measured for itself is explicitly
  *"widen inside forward-only control flow without touching `coff/`"*, and the
  counter is where both six-wrong-bytes defects came from. `plan_labels` is
  read, measured against and asserted about — not changed.
* **No `fixtures/cpp/`, `scripts/`, `BOARD.md`, `STATUS.md` or `ROADMAP.md`
  edit** — other lanes' seams. Rows proposed in §9, lettered.
* **No new emitted shape**, as registered. The exit-value merge stays refused,
  now with the counter measurement *behind* the refusal instead of beside it.
* **No long-branch expansion** (`CFG_SHAPE.md` §3.3.1). No fixture body is 32 KB,
  so it has no bytes for the oracle to compare and building it would be an
  ungraded code path by construction (F-c).
* **No counter model for the charging side.** Four magnitudes over eleven cells,
  published and not interpolated.
* **No attempt on any FRONTIER TU.** All 19 were priced by w-conv at ≥ 6

## 8. Found and not taken

1. **`ho-compose` and `ho-compose2` cost the counter ZERO — so the shape
   `calls.rs:415` refuses by name is counter-free.** A guarded early return and a
   guarded call in one body (`if(a) return 5; if(b) gp(b); gp(c); return 0;`)
   reads `stride 5 / extra 0`, at one and at two early returns. w-conv measured
   c2 *composing* the two block plans (`probe3::x6`) and refused it because *"two
   block plans in one body is a second production"* — a `codegen/` judgement,
   which stands. **What is now known is that it is not also a `coff/`
   judgement**: the composition is inside the counter's zero set, so the rung
   that builds it needs no counter work. Priced here so the next lane starts with
   the number instead of ending with it. It still needs the IL parser's refusal
   lifted (`c2-il`), which the coverage sweep cannot see.
2. **The `.ex` per-function label channel — open, and now the only route.**
   §2.4 closes "read the surcharge off the emitted blocks" and closes "read it
   off the `.gl` seed", by measurement. If the counter is ever to charge
   correctly for a loop or a merge, the quantity has to be found in the IL, and
   `IlFunction::label_lead` today derives every surcharge it knows from body
   *shape* rather than from any field. This is w-order's `26`-unwind-action
   question asked of the counter, and nobody has looked.
3. **Short-circuit `&&` and `||` put TWO branches on ONE interior join for
   free.** `ho-and` and `ho-or` read `stride 5 / extra 0` with two `bc`s naming
   one two-predecessor interior target. That is the multi-reference case the
   label map was built for, at zero counter cost, in a shape the workload is
   full of — the most promising forward-only widening this lane can see, and it
   is a `c2-il` decode question rather than a `coff/` one. Alongside
   `cf-goto-fwd` (one interior join, zero charge, and the port refuses it) it is
   also a standing counterexample to using *"the counter charges"* as a proxy for
   *"the port must refuse"*.
4. **`cf-ifelse` at +1 against `ho-ternary` at +2, same emitted shape.** Not
   worth building on, but it is the sharpest single pair in the table and any
   future counter model has to explain it before it explains anything else.
5. **The dense `switch` is a leaf with no jump table at `/O1`** — eight arms,
   each a call, `stride 1`. Recorded because a jump-tabled switch is still
   unknown and a lane that meets one will not be able to tell from this.

## What it admits, and what it refuses

**Admits.** The final-expansion switch `FUN_10c0d57e` (3,899 B) read to **29 arm
bodies over 69 discriminated opcodes**, each with a word count; the prologue
family read to **five** arms with the driver chain named; the peephole
`FUN_10c182b4` read to **18/18 arms, 659/659 opcodes**; and the prologue's word
count **obj-confirmed on 12,610 framed functions**.

**Refuses.** Everything about the **767 opcodes that reach the dispatch tail**
`0x10c0e30b`. They are dispatched by a per-opcode byte table at `0x10c3afd8`
that this lane located and did **not** read. *"Opcode X is not expanded"* is
outside this lane's reach unless X is one of the 69. The 10 shared fall-through
bodies are excluded by a width rule, not by reading them, so the arm map is a
subset of the truth.

**The deliverable came back a different shape than the read plan asked for, and
that is the finding.** The plan wanted "which opcodes expand to how many words",
picturing a switch that fans pseudo-ops out. **26 of 29 arm bodies emit 0 or 1
words**, and the three that produce a prologue or epilogue emit **zero
directly** — they delegate. Expansion is not spread across the switch; it is
concentrated in four helpers.

## Found and not taken

Ranked, and the first is the one a follow-up should buy.

1. **Read `0x10c3afd8`** — one per-opcode byte table, **767 opcodes**, the
   largest hole in this lane's denominator. Small, addressed, and it converts
   "reaches the tail" into "is or is not expanded".
2. **Resolve the `0x10b1d180` index mapping.** That table is a stride-16
   `{name, machine_opcode, BO, BI}` simplified-mnemonic table that decodes
   perfectly (`beq → bc,12,2`) and is reached **only** by string compare from
   the assembler name-lookup path. **This lane deliberately did not publish the
   opcode mapping**, because under the obvious hypothesis `0x2f0` decodes to a
   trap mnemonic while `0x2f0`'s arm demonstrably calls the prologue driver.
   Both cannot be true. Cheap to settle; expensive to get wrong.
3. **The S1 bijection may be repairable rather than demotable.**
   `ROADMAP_SLICING` §5 demotes it to a ratio because the prologue pseudo-op is
   one tuple that becomes many words — **and that correction term is a field of
   the obj**, `.pdata`'s `prolog_words`. Not validated here; three live caveats
   (leaf functions emit no record, the epilogue term is UNGRADED, and both
   `WB_INLINE_FINDINGS.md:286`'s loop breaker and `nopalign`'s unbounded arm are
   independent breakers).
4. **`ADDR.tsv:1124` presents a callee count as "41 arms."** A defect in a
   navigational file; this lane is docs-fenced to the pages it owns.
5. **`0x2f5`** is minted by IL arm 48 (`P_ILRECORD.md:254`) and handled by **no
   arm** of this switch. Either another pass consumes it or it is dead.
6. **Do not build another source-shape grid for a register-allocation
   quantity.** This lane's grid held its own independent variable constant
   because c2 saved two GPRs regardless of the source — board **#3052**
   ("framing is a consequence of allocation, not a cause") had already said so.
   The corpus, which contains real variation, is the right instrument.

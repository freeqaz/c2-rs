count**, and both my brief and the coordinator's mid-task message single out the

**A blocked function is a unit of counting. A refusal is a unit of work.** A TU
converts only when **every one** of its distinct blocker keys is closed, so the
published ranking is a **marginal over a conjunction**. `work/w-dclass/rerank.py`
does that arithmetic, which nothing in the tree did:

> ### The frontier is **22 distinct blocker keys over 52 blocked-emitted-function sites**, not 19 rows.

### 1.1 The trap in the bar fired on me, on the first reading of my own baseline

`docs/STATUS.md` and my brief both warn that `cargo test --workspace` **stops at
the first failing target, so a truncated run reports fewer passes AND fewer
targets and reads as a smaller passing run**. My first summary of my own
baseline log read **14 targets / 325 passed** — because the log was still being
flushed when I summarised it, not because a target failed. The correct read of
the same file moments later is **27 / 806 / 0**.

**The failure mode is one step wider than the bar states it.** A truncated read
of a *complete* run is indistinguishable from a complete read of a *truncated*
run: both show fewer targets and fewer passes and both say `ok`. The mitigation
is the one already on record — **compare a count, never a status** — and the
count that matters is the **target count**, which is the only one of the three
that cannot be raised by a partial read looking healthy.

## 2. THE RANKING IS A MARGINAL OVER A CONJUNCTION — the frontier is 22 keys

The FRONTIER is published as 19 rows ranked by **blocked emitted function
count**, and both my brief and the coordinator's mid-task message single out the
nine TUs blocked by exactly one function as *"the highest-density target."*

**A blocked function is a unit of counting. A refusal is a unit of work.** A TU
converts only when **every one** of its distinct blocker keys is closed, so the
published ranking is a **marginal over a conjunction**. `work/w-dclass/rerank.py`
does that arithmetic, which nothing in the tree did:

> ### The frontier is **22 distinct blocker keys over 52 blocked-emitted-function sites**, not 19 rows.

| key | **converts alone** | appears in |
|---|---:|---:|
| `expr-cmp-eq` | **3** | 7 |
| `assign-store-type-0x86` | **2** | 3 |
| `expr-jump` · `expr-op-0x27` | 1 | 3 each |
| `expr-brfalse` · `expr-cmp-ne` | 1 | 2 each |
| `assign-rhs-call-0x26` · `expr-cmp-ge` · `param-width-undetermined:mid` | 1 | 1 each |
| 13 further keys | **0** | 1 each |

**`converts` and `appears` are different numbers and the gap is the finding.**
`expr-cmp-eq` appears in 7 TUs and converts 3; the other 4 each carry a second
distinct key. Thirteen keys reach **17 of 19**; `keygen_xbox.cpp` (10 keys) and
`EncryptXTEA.cpp` (3 keys) are unreachable by any single-key ladder.

**Prereg R8 is a HIT, exactly and by name**: closing `expr-cmp-eq` alone
converts `vswprnc.cpp`, `mmio.cpp` and `IPP_basicmath_xbox.cpp`, and no others.

### 2.1 The greedy re-ranking, and the step that proves it re-ranks

```
 1. +expr-cmp-eq             +3 TU   IPP_basicmath_xbox · vswprnc · mmio
 2. +assign-store-type-0x86  +2 TU   Sort · negate_test
 3. +expr-cmp-ne             +2 TU   undname · xboxmem
 …
13. +assign-rhs-call-0x26    +1 TU   xlrcimpl            (cumulative 17 of 19)
```

Step 3 is the whole argument for re-ranking: **`expr-cmp-ne` converts 1 TU
alone and 2 once `expr-cmp-eq` is closed**, because `xboxmem.cpp` carries both.
A lane that ranked once and worked down the list would have mispriced it. My
brief warned that this lesson *"cost the section ladder a lane"*; here is the
same lesson with the numbers attached, one metric further in.

The script prints three known-answer controls as **counts** (19 of 19 frontier
TUs located; **0** with an empty key set, which would be vacuously convertible;
52 sites) and **exits non-zero when it ranks zero TUs**.

### 2.2 The ceiling, labelled as definitional so nobody reads it as a finding

Closing all 22 keys converts all 19 by construction: match `8 + 19 = 27 =
A∧B∧C`, at which point **`A∧B∧C` becomes the binding constraint and D stops
being one.** That is w-joint2's arithmetic, not a new result.

## 3. w-conv's PRICE IS STALE — and this is the finding that matters most

Board **#269**'s standing decline clause — *a frontier TU at ≥4 independent
refusals is not a target* — is the standing reason a lane declines a frontier
TU, and **no lane has ever converted one** (TU match has been 8 across every
rung since, and every conversion on record came from the whole-TU `??__E`
recognizer, not from codegen). Its entire evidential base is `w-conv` §2's
hand-count: minimum **6** over the (then) seventeen, clause fires on all of
them.

*(I have deliberately NOT written "no lane has attempted one since". That is a
causal claim about other lanes' choices that I did not measure, and `w-cfgimpl`
did attempt `xboxmem.cpp` — converting 3 of its functions and 0 TUs. The
defensible statement is about the metric, which is 8.)*

**That count was taken against the port as it was that day.** `w-conv` §3 also
ranked the frontier by missing **mechanism**, and said of its own top two rows,
verbatim: *"The top two are one rung and the port had emitted **neither**."*
Those two rows covered **14** and **10** of the seventeen TUs.

**The port emits both now.** `work/w-dclass/mechanism_age.sh` checks it and is
re-runnable:

| w-conv §3 mechanism | TUs | today |
|---|---:|---|
| a real label→offset map | 14 | **BUILT** — `codegen/labels.rs`, driven from `calls.rs:506` |
| the intra-section unconditional `b` (#191) | 10 | **BUILT** — `Form::B`, referenced from `calls.rs` |
| a REFHI/REFLO data-symbol pair | 6 | **BUILT** at the reloc layer — `coff/reloc.rs` |

| a CTR loop (`mtctr`/`bdnz`) | 4 | MISSING |

**Ancestry is checked by git, not by date**: `git merge-base --is-ancestor` puts
`b662bc6` ("codegen: the label->offset map, with the backward reference refused
by name") **after** the w-conv rung commit `11743d2`. w-conv's own merge message
says it left board #191 *half*-closed; `b662bc6` closed the other half.

> ### So every price in w-conv's table was counted against a port missing two mechanisms it now has, and those two covered 14 and 10 of seventeen TUs. **The prices are stale in the direction of OVER-pricing** — and nobody could notice, because `work/w-conv/frontier_dis.txt`, the dump the count was taken off, **was never committed.** `docs/STATUS.md` flags this pricing as unverified only on *"the two newest frontier members"*, which understates it badly.

### 3.1 Each probe separates NAMED from EMITTED, and that changed four rows

Three of the seven mechanisms appear in the tree as **the exact opposite of
built**:

* **`cr0`** occurs only in an `encode.rs` **comment** explaining that `addic.`
  writes cr0 and that c2 branches on it there. A comment is not an emitter.
* **`savegprlr`** occurs inside `frame.rs`'s **`out_of_class_ctx`** — it is the
  string of a **refusal reason** (`"frame-savegprlr-helper"`), returned when
  `saved_gprs >= 3`.
* **`cmplw`/`cmpw`** exist only in their **immediate** forms (`cmplwi`,
  `cmpwi`); the register-register encodings are absent.

**A grep that merely found the word would have reported 6 of 7 built and been
wrong about four rows.** This is why every probe in the script is chosen to
separate the two, and why the separation is printed in a note column rather than
trusted.

### 3.2 I checked the obvious weakness in my own finding, and it does not survive unqualified

An **encoder** is not a **capability**. Both `LabelMap` and `Form::B` are
reachable from exactly **one** place — `codegen/calls.rs`, inside the
early-return / guarded call-sequence family. `calls.rs:507` mints the epilogue
label; the `Form::B` reference sits in an `OptMode::O1` arm of that shape's own
loop. **Nothing else in the port mints a label.**

So what landed is a byte-graded **encoder** plus the **plan for one shape
family**, not a general CFG facility. A frontier TU prices one lower **only if
its block structure reaches that wiring**; for any other block structure the
encoder is reusable and the plan is not. In particular **every `cflow-loop`
frontier member needs a BACKWARD reference, which `labels.rs` refuses by name**
(measured: ≥ +1 on the compiler-label counter in 11 of 11 cells).

The staleness stands. What does not stand is reading the two BUILT rows as 24
TU-refusals already paid for.

> **The project's most-repeated defect is *absence read as success* (16
> instances). This is its neighbour — *presence read as coverage*.** The fix is
> the same shape: **a positive check that names WHERE the mechanism is reachable
> from**, not merely that the symbol exists. Both the finding (`0a6f329`) and
> this correction (`c27f62e`) are committed separately, in that order, on
> purpose.

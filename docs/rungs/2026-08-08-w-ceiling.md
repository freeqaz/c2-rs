### 3.4 The 450-wall, re-derived — a count, not a ceiling

The scan's own line and its own assertion:

```

  421 would if `bind.rs` lost none;
  450 carry an emitted symbol with NO `.gl` body record and are a wall
  nesting invariant, must hold: today <= repaired (true), repaired + wall == graded (true)
```

`421 + 450 == 871`. So **450 is the complement of 421 inside the graded
population** — the number of TUs a segment-driven model can *never* reach — and
the ceiling it implies is **421**. The three figures are one decomposition of
**factor B**: 338 bind today · **+83** reachable by a perfect `bind.rs` · **450**
needing COMDAT *synthesis*, which no binding repair reaches and no phase in the
plan builds. Supporting counts: `emit-unbound-has-record` 4,632 +
`emit-unbound-no-record` 4,585 = the 9,217 FBM reports as `unbound`.

**Verdict on the prereg's question: the 450 SURVIVES as a measurement and is
SUPERSEDED as a ceiling.** It is renamed, not deleted. `scripts/status.sh`'s
registry label — `Emit-set MODEL ceiling (today / repaired / wall)` — puts the
word *ceiling* in front of all three, which is how the misreading is available.

## 4. The elision and the two parks — as shipped, with their sources

### 4.1 The elision — `w-ifn`'s D2 firing as registered, a lane later

**The rule** is `w-ifn` #2351, obj-derived, **ten cells at `/O1` and again at
`/Ob0`**: *a call whose RESULT IS UNUSED and whose callee is defined in this TU
with a body that has NO SIDE EFFECT is deleted.* Explicitly **not**
`__declspec(noinline)` (`e5` elides without it), **not** empty-body (`e6`'s
callee emits `addi r3,r3,1 ; blr`), **not** tail position (`e9`), and identical
at `/Ob0`, which is what separates it from the inliner.

**It ships as a clause of this class and NOT as an `elide.rs` widening**, which
is `w-ifn`'s D2 decline clause honoured rather than re-argued. Three seams:

| what | where |
|---|---|
| the statement's result is unused — a BARE call statement, no `26 <dst>` in front and no `32` tail behind | the body parser |
| its ARGUMENTS are side-effect-free — literals or bare formal loads only | the body parser (`eat_args`) |
| the callee is one of this TU's own bound names **and its own body is pure** | `IlBundle::functions` |
| nothing is emitted | `codegen::close_call_chain` — there is no word for the statement |

`IlFunction::is_pure_expression_leaf` is the purity predicate and it is an
**ALLOWLIST**, because the direction of an error here is a wrong emit — a call
deleted that c2 kept. It admits D2's whole measured accept side and refuses its
measured decline side (`e2` stores → `StoreInd`/`global_store_leaf`; `e7` calls
→ `callees`) **plus everything the ten cells did not separate**, including an
indirect READ (`LoadInd`) and every FP op, the latter because an FP leaf mints
`_fltused` and may pool a constant and both move the label counter.

**The argument clause is this file's own contribution and is not in D2.** D2's
ten cells vary the CALLEE and say nothing about an argument that is itself a
call; a bare call statement whose argument is a call is a statement with a side
effect in it, and it is refused.

### 4.2 The two parks — M-RULE, and the sub-rule that does NOT apply

`WB_CHOOSER_FINDINGS` §2.3 (#1881, obj-derived from 16 manufactured cells, no
DISCLOSURE row needed):

> For each call in a body, c2 computes the callee's *register footprint*. For a
> direct call to a function defined **anywhere in the same translation unit**
> that makes no call of unknown footprint, the footprint is the exact set of
> registers that callee writes. For every other call — external, indirect
> (`bctrl`), or same-TU-but-itself-calling-unknown — the footprint is the whole
> volatile set.

That predicts **both** of this body's parks and neither is a default:

* **`u` → r5**, a VOLATILE, across a `bl`. It crosses exactly one call, that
  callee is a same-TU pure leaf writing only r3, and r5 is the register its next
  consumer — the `bctrl`'s **third argument** — wants. That is M-RULE's first
  sub-rule, *"coalescing beats allocation"*, whose three witnesses are this
  body's r5, M9-b and M14-b.
* **`h` → r31**, callee-saved. It crosses the `bctrl` **and** an external `bl`,
  both of which have the whole volatile set as their footprint, so no volatile
  qualifies.

**Sub-rule #1882 — `r11` when the value does not cross a call, `r10` when it
does — does NOT apply to either park here**, and the code says so where a reader
would reach for it. That sub-rule decides which volatile a *move* picks; both
parks here are settled one rule earlier. The commission named it for this body
and the body does not use it. Board **#3005**.

**The second instrument, and it moves in the predicted direction.** `w-ifn`'s
`probe/park.cpp`, five cells at the workload's own flags: `p1` reproduces the
124 bytes exactly; `p2` and `p4` replace the crossed callee with an external one
and the park moves to **r30** with the frame growing **96 → 112**. So the
transcription is not fitted on one obj, and `wmmio3_close_extern_neg.cpp` is the
port-side control on the same axis.

### 4.3 What is fenced, since the port does not implement M-RULE

The bundle proves, before any byte is emitted, that the crossed callee is a
sibling **pure expression leaf of at most two formals** — so its footprint
cannot reach r5, and the reader has separately pinned that this call passes
exactly two arguments, so r5 is not an argument register at it either — and that
the **void callee is EXTERNAL**, which is what makes the r31 park forced rather
than chosen. M-RULE is the explanation and the fence's justification; it is not
the code.

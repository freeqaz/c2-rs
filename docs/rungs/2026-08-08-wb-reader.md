## §1 What it admits, and what it refuses

**It admits nothing into the port.** This is a docs lane; its output is a
reading, an obj-check of that reading, and a measurement of what adopting it
would be worth.

What it **refuses** is the premise it was dispatched on. The campaign's Lane WB-A
brief says the 48 reader-refused frontier functions are the distance to
conversion and that *"nothing black-box can price them because the port's IL
reader refuses the body before anything downstream runs."* The second clause is
false in the way that matters:

* the reader that refuses is the **accepting** parser (`shape_to_function`);
* the port's **width scanner** (`shapes::control_flow::scan_full`) does not
  refuse — it walks **48 of 48** of those bodies from the `4C 4F 11` marker to
  the seven-byte function tail with the depth invariant intact;
* so every one of the 48 reports a `cflow-<shape>` key and **not one** reports a
  `cf-<production>-0xNN`.

There is no token in any of the 48 whose width the port does not already know.
The 48 are held by the **modeled expression class** — acceptance, i.e. codegen
semantics — and by CFG breadth (33 of the 48 also sit on `cflow-loop`,
`cflow-if-n` or `cflow-if-2`). Board **#1590**.

## §3 The reading

c2's `.ex` operand grammar is **one table**: `movzx eax,[opcode+0x10b25e48]` at
**`0x10b3d626`**, bounded to 29 classes at `0x10b3d631`, dispatched through the
jump table at **`0x10b3d954`**, under the token fetch at `0x10bbc9ab`. All 29
classes are decoded with arm VAs in the findings doc §3. Board **#1591**.

For the three largest families:

* **`expr-cmp-eq` (11)** — opcode `0x1F`, **class `00`: zero operand bytes**.
  Same for all six relationals `0x1F`–`0x24`.
* **`expr-jump` (10)** — opcode `0x3A`, **class `02`: one `varU`, resolved through
  the TU symbol table** (`0x10b3d669`). A branch target is a *symbol token*.
  Same for `0x38`/`0x39`.
* **`expr-op-0x27` (4)** — **class `01`: exactly one TYPE**, and `0x27` is the
  **only** opcode the TYPE reader special-cases: `0x10b3d581` flags it and
  `0x10b3d5b9` returns before the whole classification tail, so its type is read
  for width and never decoded. Board **#1595**.

And the byproduct that is worth more than any of them: **nine positions where the
port's published width table and c2's disagree** (board #1592), three of them
latent desyncs in the live corpus (`0x2C`, `0x54`, `0x28`) and one of them a
documented `UNKNOWN` (`IL_STMT_GRAMMAR.md` §12.1) now resolved. Plus **ten
independent confirmations** of widths the port reached from captures alone —
including `0x9A <TYPE>` with no trailing field, where c2 sides with the
13,024-body corpus measurement and against the analogy to `0x99`.

## §5 The obj-checks

Real `c2.dll` under wibo on real captured bundles, one-and-two-byte `.ex` edits,
replay, compare. Full grid and predictions in the findings doc §5.

**Confirmed, surviving a designed check with its rival refuted:**

1. **The class table discriminates, at one site, one byte, three ways.**
   Substituting a class-`00` opcode at `?Add_InPlace`'s `1F` changes exactly one
   function body (`Δleaders 0, Δbodies 1`, twice); substituting the class-`02`
   opcode `26` **breaks the obj's COMDAT structure**. This is the success floor
   for the largest frontier family.
2. **`varU` is little-endian with a bit-15 continuation.** Byte-swapping a jump
   token breaks the obj. Big-endian predicts byte-identical; plain-2-byte-LE
   predicts a merely-different obj. Both refuted. Board **#1596**.
3. **The TYPE word has a one-byte short form and the port's
   `<tag><kind><LEB id>` grammar is REFUTED.** `A6 43 8B 20` → `26 43 8B 20`
   breaks structure (the port's rule says it is still four bytes and aligned);
   `26 C3 8B 20` is **byte-identical**. Three cells, two TUs. Board **#1594**,
   DISCLOSURE draft **W-EXT-1**.
4. **A `0x27`'s TYPE is never classified.** Its class nibble `43` → `41` is
   byte-identical in 2 of 2 TUs. Board **#1595**, draft **W-EXT-2**.

**Refuted / not established, reported as such:** the cell `1F` → `27` is
**not discriminating** and is scored a miss rather than converted into support;
and one compensating control (`R2-A4`) failed on my own off-by-one arithmetic and
is reported as a failed control.

**And the fact that cost four registered predictions:** **c2 does not ICE on a
desynchronised `.ex` operand stream** — nine cells shifted the stream and all
nine produced an obj. Board **#1599**. The usable outcome code is the COMDAT
leader/body delta, with `c2-obj`'s fail-closed walk as the strongest cell.

## §7 Found and not taken

Ranked by what the next lane can do with it.

1. **`0x2C`'s payload is one raw byte, not a varint** (`0x10b3d694`). The
   discriminator needs **no `cl.exe` at all** — it is a query over the captured
   corpus for a convert whose payload byte is ≥ `0x80`, where the port's scanner
   desyncs and c2 does not. Cheapest open item this lane produced.
2. **The one-byte TYPE short form** (W-EXT-1). Obj-confirmed, drafted, not
   adopted. It changes `crates/c2-il`'s `read_type`, which lane `w-mass` owns.
   **The corpus cannot falsify the port's current rule** — no workload TU emits a
   one-byte word — so this is a case where the disassembly is the only route and
   the disclosure row is the price.
3. **`0x14`, `0x31`, `0x07`, `0x08`, `0x1D`, `0x1E`, `0x25`** — seven opcodes the
   port refuses as "unwitnessed" and whose class the table names. Each is one
   substitution cell to confirm. Their census mass is not measured here.
4. **`0x43` is not an escape.** The port's sub-opcode table (`42` → +4, `37` →
   +2) is two ordinary tokens in c2's reader, and its refusal of every other
   sub-opcode is unnecessary. One cell to check.
5. **The per-opcode `u16` attribute table at `0x10b25f10`** (`0x10b3d56e`, bit
   `0x400`, gated on type-word bit `0x1000`). Read, meaning **not** established.
   Left `unknown`, which is a respectable value here.
6. **Deliberately not drafted as a DISCLOSURE row: the class table itself.**
   Copying 192 entries would be the largest single adoption this project has
   contemplated, §4 shows it converts nothing, and only three of its cells are
   obj-confirmed. A future lane should want the **nine rows of §3.4**
   individually, each with its own check and its own row.
7. **`C2RS_SINK_REL` and `C2RS_SINK_BRANCH` do not compose** (board #1600) — a
   defect in a live instrument, found in passing, reported rather than worked
   around.

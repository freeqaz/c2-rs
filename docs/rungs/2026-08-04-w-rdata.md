## §2 What `.rdata$r` is, from real objs

The full specification is [`OBJ_RDATA_R_SHAPE.md`](../OBJ_RDATA_R_SHAPE.md).
Measured on a **22-source hierarchy grid (25 objs)** and **38 real workload
objs** captured through `work/w-frame/refobj.sh` at `work/dc3-workload/flags.txt`
verbatim — **2,668 `.rdata$r` sections, 902 `??_R0` `.data` COMDATs, 3,570
records**, spanning 4 to 156 `.rdata$r` sections per obj. Over the census's 871
graded TUs: **676 TUs, 24,163 sections**, and factor C goes **169 → 590** if the
name is added. The census reproduces the ladder to the TU.

Four things worth carrying forward:

**2.1 The trigger is a VFTABLE, and the controls are sharper than w-gr's.**
`rungs/2026-08-04-w-gr.md` §4 established that `dynamic_cast` and `typeid` mint
zero; both re-derive here (7 sections and **5** respectively, the `typeid` probe
not even minting a `??_R0`). The new control is
**`__declspec(novtable)`** — an XDK idiom that is *in this workload*. It takes a
TU with every other RTTI ingredient from 11 sections to **5**: no vfptr write, no
vftable, no RTTI. That is the cleanest statement of the rule in the file.

**2.2 Therefore every `.rdata$r` obj has at least one `.text` COMDAT**, because
the trigger *is* a function body. This is the fact that decides the rung's shape:
`emit_data_obj`'s entire TU class is *"defines no functions"*, so the section is
permanently out of its reach and the step cannot be paid the way `.data`/`.bss`
was.

**2.3 The record bytes are DERIVABLE, and the residue is three integers.**
`work/w-rdata/synth.py` rebuilds each record from nothing but the mangled symbol
names and the reference's relocation targets:

```
  grid      (25 objs,   203 records)     194 exact,   9 wrong
  workload  (38 objs, 3,570 records)   3,337 exact, 233 wrong
```

**Every one of the 242 misses is one of three fields** — `??_R4.offset`,
`??_R4.cdOffset`, `??_R3.attributes`. Supply those and it is **203 of 203** and
**3,570 of 3,570**. The `??_R1` base-class descriptor's whole `PMD`
(`mdisp`/`pdisp`/`vdisp`) and its attributes are spelled *in its own mangled
name*. And the aux `CheckSum` of all 3,570 reproduces under the port's existing
`coff_checksum` — init 0, no final XOR — with the `??_R4`/`??_R2` zeros falling
out of the CRC rather than being a suppressed field, a distinction a writer that
special-cased "fully relocated ⇒ 0" would get right for the wrong reason.

**2.4 ONE rule orders everything: a DFS pre-order over the relocation graph.**
Emit a node, then visit its relocation targets in ascending offset. On
`struct D:B` that yields `R4D R0D R3D R2D R1D R1B R0B R3B R2B` — exactly what c2
emits. **Exact on 25 of 25 grid objs and 38 of 38 workload objs**, including
multiple inheritance, the diamond, and a 156-section TU. No second rule was
needed anywhere.

**And the same walk orders the symbol table's undefined externals.** In the
minimal obj `??_7type_info@@6B@` sits immediately after `??_R0`'s group while
`?f@A@@UAAXXZ` — referenced by the *vftable*, the DFS root — is the **last**
symbol in the obj. Those are not two facts: the root's children are
`[??_R4, ?f]`, the DFS drains the `??_R4` subtree first, and `?f` is visited
last. One walk, both orders.

## §3 The number this lane exists for: a `.rdata$r` writer is worth `B∧C` +164, not +421

The brief is explicit that `B∧C` must be **re-measured, not scaled**, and that is
the error `w-bc` was dispatched to correct. So it was measured, by breaking the
tree (§5) and running the real scan on both halves:

| metric | writer's 10 names | + `.rdata$r` | Δ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `factor-c` | 169 | **590** | **+421** |
| `b-and-c` | **151** | **315** | **+164** |
| `a-and-b-and-c` | 27 | **27** | **+0** |
| `frontier` | 19 | **19** | **+0** |
| `frontier-if-a` | 141 | **305** | +164 |

| `match` / `mismatch` / `capture-fail` | 8 / 0 / 7 | 8 / 0 / 7 | — |

Three readings, and the second is the one nobody had:

* **The ladder's `+421` is real and it is a C number, not a `B∧C` number.** Only
  **164 of the 421** TUs that enter C are also binding-complete. The inclusion
  rate `B∧C / C` falls **89.3 % → 53.4 %** — a much steeper fall than the
  `93.9 % → 89.3 %` w-bc measured across the last vocabulary growth, and it says
  the `.rdata$r` population is *systematically* less bound than the vocabulary's
  earlier additions. That is not surprising in hindsight — an RTTI-bearing TU is
  a big TU — but it was not derivable from 421 and it is exactly the extrapolation
  w-bc was created to forbid.
* **`A∧B∧C` and the FRONTIER move by ZERO.** Not "a few", as the brief allowed —
  zero. Factor A binds at 28 and every one of A's 28 is *already* inside C. So
  the joint ceiling on TU match is completely untouched by this step, and the
  claim "this will not move TU match" is stronger than the brief stated it.
* **`mismatch` stays 0 on the broken tree.** The scan reports `factor-c 590` over
  a writer that emits nothing, and nothing in it goes red. That is `STATUS.md`
  trap 5 with the number moving in the flattering direction, reproduced on
  demand.

## §4 The decision: seven facts, and the first two are another crate's

The minimal `.rdata$r` TU is eleven sections:

```cpp
struct A { A(); virtual void f(); int a; };
A::A(){}
```

```
  .drectve .debug$S .XBLD$W .XBLD$W
  .text     ??0A@@QAA@XZ          16 B   lis/addi/stw r11,0(r3)/blr
  .rdata    ??_7A@@6B@             8 B   the vftable, Selection 6, symbol Value 4
  .rdata$r  ??_R4A@@6B@           20 B
  .data     ??_R0?AUA@@@8         16 B
  .rdata$r  ??_R3A@@8             16 B
  .rdata$r  ??_R2A@@8              8 B
  .rdata$r  ??_R1A@?0A@EA@A@@8    28 B
```

Independent refusals to emit it byte-exact, **no discount applied** (the
`w-conv` methodology, board #269):

| # | fact | crate |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | the vfptr-store leaf body class — `c2rs census` reports `expr-op-0x27` and the parse refuses | **`c2-il`** |
| 2 | a reader for the `??_R*` record graph — the symbol set, the three layout integers of §2.3, the base array's order | **`c2-il`** |
| 3 | codegen for `lis/addi/stw rD,0(r3)/blr` — a `DataRef` whose low half feeds a **store**; `data_refs_of` searches for an `addi` into an *argument* register and refuses this | `c2-core` |
| 4 | the `.rdata$r` / `.data`-COMDAT `Section` emitter and its `ADDR32` relocations | `c2-core` |
| 5 | the DFS emission order of §2.4, over sections **and** undefined externals | `c2-core` |
| 6 | the vftable `.rdata` COMDAT: Selection 6, symbol `Value` 4, two relocations | `c2-core` |
| 7 | the `??_7type_info@@6B@` undefined external | `c2-core` |

**Seven, and it is a lower bound** — it prices only the cheapest case and does
not price the *destructor* shape, which adds the compiler-generated `??_G` scalar
deleting destructor (a framed body with a `clrlwi.` test, a conditional branch
and a call to `??3@YAXPAX@Z`; `c2rs census` blocks it at
`expr-call-in-expr-recv-load-then-bit-and-and-branch-more`), the `??_E` alias
emitted as a `SECTION`-class symbol with an aux record, and a `.pdata`. The
standing decline clause — *a frontier TU at ≥ 4 independent refusals is not a
target* — fires at 7.

**Items 1 and 2 are binding and they are `w-vocab`'s seam.** Per the brief they
are **reported, not implemented**:

> **To `w-vocab`.** Two readers `crates/c2-il` does not have, both required
> before any `.rdata$r` writer can be graded by the differential:
> **(a)** the vfptr-store body — `struct A{A();virtual void f();int a;}; A::A(){}`
> decodes to one function that `IlBundle::functions()` refuses at `expr-op-0x27`,
> the four-word body `lis r11,??_7A@@6B@@ha · addi · stw r11,0(r3) · blr`. It is
> the *smallest* body any RTTI-bearing TU can have, so nothing downstream of it
> is reachable without it.
> **(b)** the `??_R*` record graph. `.gl` names every record with its size and
> attributes and `.in` carries the contents; `OBJ_RDATA_R_SHAPE.md` §3–§4 says
> exactly which fields a reader must surface — **three integers per class**
> (`??_R4.offset`, `??_R4.cdOffset`, `??_R3.attributes`) plus the base array's
> order. Everything else the writer can derive from the mangled names, measured
> at 3,337 of 3,570 records.

### What was NOT built, and why that is the finding

**No uncalled writer was landed.** The precedent is
`container::bss_deferred_layout`: a `.bss` layout the differential had never
graded one byte of, which turned out to disagree with reality on the walk *and*
on the free list the moment a real caller was written. Board **#278** deleted it
and its own doc authorized the deletion. Landing an `.rdata$r` emitter with no
caller — over a surface five times larger — is that mistake repeated, and a
green KAT suite over rules nobody exercised is trap 5 one level up.

The order that works is the order `.data`/`.bss` was built in:
`OBJ_DATA_BSS_SHAPE.md` → the `c2-il` reader → `coff/data.rs` **with a caller**
→ the constant. This lane is step one of four.

## §5 The counterfactual — break it, measure it, restore it, prove the restore

`work/w-rdata/counterfactual.sh` (scratch, because `scripts/` is another lane's
seam this session; the whole script is eleven lines and is reproduced in §8 so it
is re-runnable). It **refuses to start on a dirty `crates/`**, so the final check
cannot be vacuous.

The breaker is one line: `".rdata$r"` prepended to `PORT_WRITER_SECTIONS`, arity
10 → 11, and **nothing else** — no writer, no `Section`, no caller. Exactly the
change the brief asked for, with the work left out.

```
### BASE
  the_writer_vocabulary_is_every_section_name_this_file_emits ... ok (1 passed)
  factor-c 169   b-and-c 151   a-and-b-and-c 27   frontier 19

### BROKEN — one name added to the vocabulary, no writer behind it
  the_writer_vocabulary_is_every_section_name_this_file_emits ... FAILED
  factor-c 590   b-and-c 315   a-and-b-and-c 27   frontier 19
  match 8   mismatch 0   capture-fail 7   ladder-head .text$yd -> 804

### RESTORE
  git status --porcelain crates/ = 0 path(s)
  the_writer_vocabulary_is_every_section_name_this_file_emits ... ok (1 passed)
```

**Two instruments, one of them blind.** `c2rs gap` reports the whole `+421` and
stays at `mismatch 0` — it has no way to ask whether a name in the constant has
an emitter, and `factor_control_on_match_tus`'s own doc says the control only
runs the *other* direction. What catches it is
`coff::tests::the_writer_vocabulary_is_every_section_name_this_file_emits`, which
reconciles the constant against the `Section { name: … }` literals of every
`coff/*.rs` that can build one — **a test that already existed** and that this
lane found rather than wrote (P6, §7).

That is the whole argument of this rung in one measurement: the number the brief
wanted is one line away, the scan cannot tell the difference, and the only thing
standing between the two is a test somebody wrote before this lane arrived.

**And the counterfactual is not uniform**, which is what makes the first half
mean anything: `A∧B∧C`, the FRONTIER, `match`, `mismatch` and `capture-fail` are
byte-identical across it. A breaker that moved everything would prove nothing.

**`crates/c2-core` never-executed lines: unchanged, by construction.** The
`crates/` diff is **59 added lines, every one a `///` doc comment, and zero

```sh

git diff master..HEAD -U0 -- crates/ | grep '^-' | grep -v '^---' | wc -l                          # 0
```

A diff with no executable line cannot move a coverage count in either direction,
so **`coff/`'s zero never-executed emission lines (w-small's result) is preserved
and no new one is introduced**. `work/w-frame/sweep.py`'s two profiles were
**not** regenerated: they need an `instrument-coverage` rebuild of the workspace
plus `c2rs gap` over every fixture at every one of the 18 lanes, which is on the
order of the gate's own run time, and three other lanes were live on this box.
**Priced and declined**, rather than reported as if it had run — the structural
proof above is strictly stronger for a comment-only diff, and weaker for anything
else, which is why it is stated as a *condition* and not a habit.

## §10 Found and deliberately not taken, priced

* **The vocabulary guard has a second hole one level up, and there is a live
  instance.** `the_writer_vocabulary_is_…` reconciles the constant against
  `Section { name: … }` literals; a literal inside an emitter **nothing calls**
  satisfies it and still inflates C. `emit_mvp_obj` is that emitter today — its
  only caller is `coff/tests.rs`. It inflates nothing, because every name it
  emits is also emitted by a called emitter, and **that is luck, not a
  guarantee**. Closing it means asserting every `pub fn emit_*_obj` is named by
  `lib.rs`, which goes red on `emit_mvp_obj` immediately and therefore forces a
  deletion-or-exemption decision this lane should not make alone. **Board #301.
  Price: the test is ~15 lines; the decision it forces is the work.** Recorded in
  the constant's own doc so the next reader does not have to rediscover it.
* **`??_R1`'s `numContainedBases` is the one field of seven not spelled in the
  mangled name.** Everything else in the base-class descriptor — `mdisp`,
  `pdisp`, `vdisp`, `attributes` — is. Not taken: it is a reader question and
  belongs with the two items reported to `w-vocab`. **Price: zero on top of the
  reader.**
* **`??_R3.numBaseClasses` and `??_R2`'s size are two readings of one number**
  (`size == 4 * (n + 1)`). A writer that computed them in two places could
  disagree and every relocation would still resolve — the silent wrong-bytes
  shape `GAPS.md` §6 keeps recording. Written into the spec as a warning rather
  than into code. **Price: one shared helper when the writer is built.**
* **The `/Od` two-vftable divergence (board #295) did not reproduce on this
  lane's grid.** `g01`/`g02` are record-count-stable across `/Od`, `/O1` and
  `/Ox`; the divergence needs w-gr's base-and-derived-with-virtual-destructors
  shape specifically. Recorded in the spec §7 so a future lane does not conclude

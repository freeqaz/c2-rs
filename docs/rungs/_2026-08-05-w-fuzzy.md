## 1. What it admits, and what it refuses

Nothing. This rung admits **no class**: `Census: +0`, `Fixtures: none`, TU match
8 → 8, fixture gate unchanged. It ships one measurement and one collector.

What it *refuses* is more interesting, and is the design:

* It refuses **partial credit**. A function is byte-identical to c2's or it
  scores zero. A wrong body scores exactly what a refusal scores.
* It refuses to **reconstruct** the words the COFF emitter owns. 9,374 emitted
  functions could be moved into the numerator by appending a branch word in the
  harness, with zero change to the port. That is the one lever that inflates FBM
  without progress, and it is left unpulled (board #322).
* It refuses to be a **gate**. Not in `scripts/gate.sh`, licenses no emit, and a
  scan with `FBM 0.9` and `mismatch 1` is a failing scan.
* It refuses to produce a **number over nothing**. `None`, `NO-RESULT`, no key.

## 2. Estimate vs outcome

The brief this lane was written from carried a prior lane's measurement as a
premise: *"a fuzzy match over c2-rs is undefined on 863 of 871 TUs and reads
exactly 100.0 on the other 8"* (`PROGRESS_METRIC.md` §2.1). Taken at face value,
the expected outcome of this lane was a **decline**: nothing to build.

| | predicted from the premise | realized |
|---|---|---|
| TUs on which the port's output is defined | 8 of 871 (0.9 %) | 8 of 871 — **the premise is correct at TU granularity** |
| **emitted functions** on which it is defined | (not asked) | **38,458 of 178,975 (21.5 %)** |
| emitted functions gradeable against c2's bytes today | (implied 0) | **29,084** |
| wrong bodies found | (implied unknowable) | **0** |

The premise did not survive the change of unit, and the reason is one line of
`c2-core`: `PortC2::build` refuses a **whole TU** when any one function is out of
class, but `codegen::select_function` — the *same* decision procedure, already
public and already run per function by the census/gate cross-check — answers per
function. Board **#319** records the refutation; §4 of
`docs/FUNCTION_BYTE_MATCH.md` records what of §2 survives (both of its other two
claims, adopted whole).

**The generalizing lesson is about units, not about objdiff.** A definedness
measurement is a measurement *of a unit*, and §2.1's 99.1 % was true of TUs and
untrue of functions on the same tree, the same day, over the same scan.

Two identities, both load-bearing:

1. **`exact + partial = 29,084 + 9,374 = 38,458 = emit-in-class`, to the digit.**
   FBM grades exactly the population the emitted census claims, and splits it
   into what the oracle has certified and what the instrument cannot yet
   reconstruct. `differs 0` is the finding: **not one of the census's emitted
   in-class claims is wrong where it can be checked.** That is `STATUS.md`
   trap 2 narrowed by 75.6 % on the population the goal is written in — and
   still fully open on the 2.28 M never-emitted bodies, which are in no obj and
   which nothing can grade.
2. **`fnbyte-tus-full 4` against `match 8`.** Four of the eight matching TUs
   carry no emitted function at all, so they are excluded from the per-TU
   distribution rather than counted `0/0` — the same exclusion `near_match_tus`
   makes, and for the same reason.

### 3.1 The anti-gaming property, stated precisely

> **The denominator is a function of `c2`'s output alone, and the numerator is
> the judge's own predicate. No input to FBM rewards emitting anything that is
> not byte-identical to `c2`.**

Refusing scores 0; emitting a wrong body scores 0; emitting nothing scores 0;
and the denominator cannot be shrunk by refusing, because it is counted off the
**reference** obj. Unit-tested as an **equality** —
`a_wrong_body_scores_exactly_what_a_refusal_scores` — rather than an inequality,
because the property that matters is indifference, not ordering.

## 4. The control break, and what it found

The oracle cannot grade a per-function correspondence in general; on a TU it
graded `match` it already has. The control was written as *"every emitted
function on a `match` TU is `exact` or `partial`"* and, on its **first corpus
run, read 2 where it must read 0.**

The two are `src/system/synth/tomcrypt/TomCryptLicense.cpp` and
`src/system/zlib/ZlibLicense.cpp` — the two `??__E` dynamic-initializer TUs.
`PortC2::build` tries `IlBundle::dyninit_tu()` **before** `functions()`, so on
those two it emits byte-exact through a **whole-TU** route `select_function`
never sees, and FBM as first written credited them zero.

The fix was not to relax the control:

* the judge's verdict supersedes the instrument's route — on a `match` TU every
  emitted function *is* byte-exact, so those are credited as `whole_tu`, at the
  hardest possible bar (the TU must already match);
* they are reported on **their own line** and never folded into `exact`, because
  the two numerators are graded by different routes;
* the control keeps its teeth in the direction that can still indict the port:
  `fnbyte-match-tu-differs` — a per-function body that *differs* from a
  certified one — **must be 0**, and is.

**This is board #179's finding one level down** (factor D, the per-function
reading, needed E, the whole-TU disjunct) and it will recur: every future
`PortC2::build` arm ahead of `functions()` adds to the count, silently, in the
reassuring direction. Board **#323** is the standing row.

## 6.2 The constructed counterexample, and its measured instance count

A green gate is necessary and not sufficient, so the metric was attacked
directly. The strongest case found:

> **A `.text` COMDAT's raw bytes do not contain its relocations.** Two bodies
> that load the address of two *different* globals — `int* f(){return &a;}` and
> `return &b;` — lower to byte-identical text (`lis`/`addi` with the relocated
> fields zeroed) and differ only in their relocation records. FBM would credit
> the port's lowering of one as `exact` against the other's bytes.

The argument is sound. Rather than write it down as a caveat, it was measured:
`c2-obj` gained a fail-closed `text_comdat_reloc_counts`, and the scan now counts
how many **credited** functions relocate at all.

**`fnbyte-exact-relocated` = 0.** Not one of the 29,084 credited functions
carries a relocation in c2's obj, so on this workload today `exact` is a full
function-identity claim and not merely a byte claim. **The counterexample is
sound and its instance count is zero** — which is precisely the state that
changes without warning, so the number is printed on every scan and carried in
the `STATUS.md` partition row.

### 6.3 A defect this lane shipped and caught

The FBM partition's `status.sh` row was written as a single-quoted template
broken across source lines. In `sh`, a trailing `\` **inside** single quotes is a
literal backslash and not a continuation, so the rendered value carried a
backslash and two newlines — and in a markdown table a newline does not make a
long cell, it **ends the row** and turns the rest into stray prose, in the file
`CLAUDE.md` points readers at first. It was caught by *running* the collector,
not by `--check`. `--check` now renders all four templates and asserts each is
one line with no literal backslash, and that control has its own control.

## 7. What this rung did NOT do

* **It did not move TU match, the census, or the fixture gate**, and was not
  expected to. It is an instrument.
* **It did not add a gate.** FBM is a driver. `CLAUDE.md`'s one correctness rule
  is untouched: the real `c2` under wibo plus the byte-exact whole-obj compare
  is still the sole judge.
* **It did not reconstruct the 9,374** (board #322), and it did not touch
  `c2-core` or `c2-il` (owned concurrently by `w-alias`).
* **It did not port objdiff's Patience alignment.** It was read and deliberately
  declined: a row-shifted body is exactly the case this project must not award
  credit for. The instruction-level equal-word count *is* computed, but only over
  the `differs` class, printed as forensics, aggregated into no headline — and
  that class is currently empty.

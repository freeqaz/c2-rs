## 2. GRID A's rule shipped, and how trap 0 was avoided (#1740)

w-extdata measured the rule — one list over callees ∪ data names in reverse
`.text` first-reference order, kind ignored, confirmed 5 of 5 cells, with the
shipped two-loop writer refuted on three — and **deliberately did not ship it**:
it converts nothing by itself, so the new arm would have been graded over a
population of **zero** cells while changing the symbol table on every obj the
port emits. `docs/STATUS.md` trap 0 in its exact shape.

### 2.1 The population of cells was still zero, and that is a measurement

The PREREG's §1.3, taken before any change to `crates/`: all four GRID A cells
that carry a data symbol are **out of class at this lane's base**.

```text
  a1  0/1   callseq-multiarg-sym:eof
  a2  0/1   callseq-multiarg-sym:eof     <- the smallest refutation of the writer
  a3  0/1   callseq-multiarg-sym:eof     <- `undname.cpp`'s shape in four lines
  a4  0/1   callseq-multiarg-sym:eof
  a5  1/1   in class                     (the control: no data symbol)
```

So "ship it now and add cells later" was not available, and neither was "ship it
alone": **no shipped class could emit an obj whose data reference follows a
call**, because `check_external_order` refused exactly that. The sequencing the
PREREG registered, and the one that ran:

* **A1 ships in the same commit as the conversion that gives it a cell, or not
  at all** — and the PREREG says in advance that if the `undname` class had
  declined, A1 would not have shipped and the fence would have stayed. Recorded
  before the outcome was known, so a surviving fence could not have been read as
  an oversight.
* **The rule is byte-neutral on the existing population BY CONSTRUCTION.** The
  deleted fence refused every body in which a data reference follows a call, so
  on every obj the port had ever emitted every data reference precedes every
  call — and reverse order over the union then places all callees first and all
  data names last, which is where the two loops put them. **The two rules
  provably coincide on exactly the population the fence admitted.** That
  argument is a `#[test]`
  (`a_body_whose_data_refs_all_precede_its_calls_gets_the_same_list_either_way`),
  not a paragraph.
* **Measured anyway, three ways**: the per-TU set comparison in §1, the gate's
  per-lane `match` counts (§7.1), and the fixture-verdict total.

### 2.2 What shipped, and what stayed two

`Function::introduced_externals` is the merged list. **Both writers keep their
two lookup TABLES** — a REL24 resolves in the callee table and a REFHI/REFLO in
the data table, so a data symbol can never be resolved against a callee of the
same spelling — and only the **index assignment** is shared, which is the thing
GRID A measured. `check_external_order` is deleted rather than left standing:
it existed to refuse the disagreement that no longer exists.

The refuted rival is **kept as code**, as `external_order_tests::two_loop_order`,
so the separation is executable rather than described. On `a3`'s shape it
produces `g2 g0 g1 gI gJ` where the reference has `g2 gJ g0 g1 gI`, and **every
relocation resolves either way** — `docs/GAPS.md` §6.

## 3. The twenty-four words, and the three things about them

Ground truth: the real obj at the workload's own flags
(`work/w-extdata/ref/undname/dis.txt`, committed by the previous lane) plus the
IL body decoded token by token **before the rung**
(`work/w-undname/UNDNAME_BODY.md`, committed with the PREREG).

The full annotated listing is `codegen::alloc_init_or_fail`'s module doc. The
three facts a general lowering gets wrong, each pinned by a `#[test]`:

1. **The externals interleave: `data · callee · data`.** Symbol table from index
   15 is `?pairNode_vtable` (first referenced `+0x40`), `?getMemory` (`+0x34`),
   `?gHeapManager` (`+0x24`) — strictly descending index against ascending
   offset, kind ignored. This is §2's cell.
2. **Three zero tests, two condition registers, in the order cr6 · cr0 · cr6.**
   Nothing in the source distinguishes them; a class using one CR throughout
   emits the right program with two wrong `bc` operands and every branch still
   resolving.
3. **The two `lis` hoists are 2 words and 3 words.** No fixed-distance
   derivation of the relocation sites is right about both — inside ONE body.
   And the second low half is `addi r11,r11,0`, writing the scratch register
   itself, which the previous `ARG_REG`-restricted search could not see at all.

**Ten free immediate fields** (`k_size` `k_flag` `k_neg` `k_status` and six
member offsets) and **zero words chosen by a scheduler or an allocator**, which
is exactly what PREREG **D1** registered and why every other fact is a refusal in
the READER (board **#1706**).

## 4. What the conversion actually needed

The survey priced five refusals. **Six fired**, and the sixth is the one worth
the page.

### 4.1 `data_refs_of` needed TWO relaxations, not one — and they are one walk (#1741)

The PREREG's §1.2 already carried this as an addition to w-extdata's table, and
it held: the uniqueness rule ("the one `addis rT,0,0` in the body; two refuse")
becomes a **forward walk** in which each `addis` opens a pair and the first
`addi rD,rT,0` after it closes it, `rD` unconstrained. Both halves of the
relaxation are needed by the same body and neither is sufficient.

The names come from the parser and are paired with the derived sites **by
position**, with the counts checked — a mismatch refuses, because the
alternative relocates a site against the wrong symbol and links.

### 4.2 `data_sym` → `data_syms`, and the producer that still refuses two (#1742)

`IlFunction::data_sym: Option<String>` is `data_syms: Vec<String>`, in emission
order. The PREREG carried w-extdata's estimate of **9 reader sites**, labelled
unverified; the real count is **six non-test sites** (`bundle.rs` ×3,
`diag.rs`, `elide.rs` ×2, `splice.rs`, `lib.rs` ×2 — nine if `lib.rs`'s two
clauses in one function are counted separately, which is how w-extdata counted).
The estimate was right to within the counting convention and is recorded as
such rather than claimed as a hit.

**WR1's own producer still refuses a second symbol.** Widening the carrier does
not characterize the pool-offset form `docs/IL_CALL_IN_EXPR.md` §17.3 (a)/(b)
describes, so `slot_args_resolved` keeps its refusal and says why in place.

### 4.3 The sixth refusal: `callees()` had no arm, and the ACCOUNTING GATE caught it (#1743)

With the recognizer, the emitter, the writer rule and the pairing all built,
`c2rs census` read **1/1 functions in class** and `c2rs gap` still read
`vocab-gap … il function decode failed`. `IlBundle::decode_causes` names it in
one word: **`unclaimed-gl-symbol`**.

`IlFunction::callees()` is a per-shape `chain`, and the new shape had no arm — so
`?getMemory@_HeapManager@@QAAPAXHH@Z` was not accounted, and the TU-level gate
that exists for exactly this refused the whole TU.

**Two things worth carrying off it.** First, this is the fifth conversion lane in
a row to find a refusal its survey did not name (w-heap two, w-data an eighth,
w-cfg2 and w-extdata a sixth each, this one a sixth) — five for five, and the
PREREG's 0.55 was held partly on that base rate. Second, and more useful: the
failure mode of a missing `callees()` arm is a **wholesale refusal with a named
cause**, not a wrong symbol table. The gate that cost two objs at file offset 2
to learn is what turned an omission into a diagnostic.

### 4.4 The label lead is ZERO, and the analogy that predicted 1 is REFUTED (#1744)

The PREREG registered **+1**, explicitly "by analogy with the two other `cflow`
classes", both of which charge one slot before their own triple. With that term
the port emitted `$M2593`/`$M2592`/`$T2594` where the reference has
`$M2592`/`$M2591`/`$T2593`: **three symbol records wrong by one and nothing else
in the obj different**. Removing the term makes the obj byte-exact.

So **the surcharge is not a property of "a transcribed `cflow-if-n` class"**,
which is what the analogy assumed — and that is the finding, not the number. It
is written into `label_lead` as an explicit `+ 0 * u32::from(…)` rather than as a
missing arm, because a class silently absent from that sum is indistinguishable
from one nobody thought about.

**The measurement is the one-witness kind**, exactly as w-extdata's was:
`undname.cpp` has one emitted function, so there is no in-TU difference to take
and what was compared is the port's whole obj against real `c2.dll`'s. The fence
around it is the recognizer's — this class admits one block plan, so there is no
second shape for the charge to be wrong about.

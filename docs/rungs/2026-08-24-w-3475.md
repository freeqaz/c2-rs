## 1. The one-paragraph answer

**The defect board #3475 names is real, and every number attached to it is
attached to the wrong thing.** `mcall::eat_int_operands` did lack a `0x27` arm,
and since `w-c1` that made the completeness measure **narrower than its own
emitter** at the call-argument position — #139's shape, third time here. The
arm is added. But the **19,898** the row prices it at is not that key's value,
it is not that key, and it is not even that key's *instrument*: it is the
largest **cross** row `tail-recv-not-a-plain-b9-load/then-off-add|BLOCKED|expr-op-0x30`,
minted by `shapes/mcall_tail.rs` at the *receiver-designator* byte, and it
**does not move** — 19,898 before, 19,898 after. What actually moves is
**26,300 bodies and 1,000 blocked emitted functions** across the
`expr-call-in-expr-*` census keys, and **required-zero HELD**: 0 lines over the
gate's 31 count-bearing rows and 0 lines over all 500 `gap-metric` keys,
denominators printed at both ends.

## 2.1 What IS true, and it is the whole reason to do the lane

`Vocab::CallArg`'s own doc says it *"Mirrors `shapes::calls::eat_call_args`"*
and that the operand rules are *"reached via `parse_expr`"*. On the default
path `eat_call_args` routes an argument through `parse_expr` — `off_add_arg_sink`
is OFF unless `C2RS_SINK_OFF_ADD_ARG` is set, and `eat_sym_addr_arg` refuses an
offset run by construction. Since `w-c1` made `parse_expr`'s `0x27` arm
**default-on**, the correspondence has been broken at that byte. Measured, not
argued: with the arm removed, the enumerated stream guard reads **126 of 23,625
operand streams accepted by one side and refused by the other**.

## 6. THE GUARD EVERYBODY CITED HAD NO OPCODE AXIS — which is why `w-c1`'s P10 read as a MISS

The tree asserts in three places (`expr.rs`'s comment, board **#364**, ROADMAP
§9.17.6) that widening `parse_expr` here *"obliges
`mcall::eat_int_operands`'s `Vocab::CallArg` to widen in lockstep, or §9.14.6's
correspondence guard goes red"*. `w-c1` registered that as prereg **P10**,
measured **nothing red**, and scored it a MISS while noting the hazard was
nonetheless live.

**Here is why nothing went red.** `a_measure_and_its_emitter_admit_the_same_operand_streams`
enumerated its operator axis as `[None, 0x02, 0x03, 0x04, 0x2C, 0xFF]` —
arithmetic and conversion only. **There was no `0x27` in it.** The sibling
guard `a_measure_and_its_emitter_admit_the_same_types` sweeps the enumerated
**TYPE** space at a single `B9` operand and has no opcode axis at all. Both
guards are green at base, green under every mutation of the arm that does not
touch a type, and were green through the whole interval in which the
correspondence was false.

That is #144's shape one level up: *a domain that cannot express the drift it
is looking for*. The axis is added — `33 <t2> 08 · 27 <t2>`, the designator
step `&t->s.k` spells, with `t2` sweeping both halves of `designator::is_ptr_any`
— and the guard's domain goes **20,250 → 23,625 streams**. With the arm
disabled it reads **126 disagreements**; with the `is_ptr_any` gate dropped,
**324**.

**Prereg P11 predicted nothing would go red when the measure widened either,
and it is a HIT** — but the useful half is the reason: the guard could not see
this in *either* direction.

## 8. The edit

One arm and one fold in `crates/c2-il/src/func/body/mcall.rs`, mirroring
`parse_expr_classed`'s `0x27` arm token for token. **Three deliberate
non-widenings are as load-bearing as the widening**, and each has its own
mutation above:

* **`0x28` is not admitted** (M5). `parse_expr` has no `28` arm; admitting the
  subscript offset add here would be the *wider*-than-the-emitter error in the
  same line that fixes the narrower one. `Fail::blocker` still names both bytes
  `off-add`, correctly — it names the construct — so a `28` still stops the run
  and is still granted through `eat_one_blocker_value`.
* **`Vocab::IntrinsicRecv` is not widened** (M2). That position has no emitter,
  so it is left `UNMEASURED` rather than matched to a production that does not
  exist.
* **`ptr_arith_exact` is not set** (M3). `27` is the *byte*-offset add and
  carries its scaling; `02` over a pointer is scaled by the pointee width.

A non-pointer TYPE refuses with `FailKind::Value` **at the `27`**, not
`FailKind::Type` at the TYPE behind it — otherwise the greedy chain grants a
*type class*, `eat_admitted_type` admits it at every other operand position,
and this one keeps refusing: a grant that cannot complete the body it was
granted for.

**Nothing outside the file was touched.** `expr.rs` is `w-c7`'s and
`codegen/calls.rs` is `w-permute`'s; `off_add_admitted()` is `pub(crate)` and is
**called**, not edited. `docs/IL_DECODE_REACH.md` §12.1 gains a paragraph
recording that `C2RS_OFF_ADD` now gates two consumers.

## 10.1 `scripts/configure_existing_worktree.sh` is a silent no-op for a sibling-directory worktree

This worktree is `/home/free/code/milohax/c2-rs-wt-w-3475` — a **sibling** of
the main repo, not under `<main>/.claude/worktrees/`. The script computes
`MAIN_REPO="$(cd "$(dirname "$0")/.." && pwd)"`, which for a worktree **is the

by hand here; the script is **not** patched by this lane because it is shared
surface and four lanes are live. **Board #3500.**

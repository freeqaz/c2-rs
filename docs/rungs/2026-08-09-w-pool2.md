### 3.1 P1 and P2 are ONE production, and #187 is neither read nor needed

`shapes::pool_free_list` reads the guard and the store run **together**. That
collapses two of `w-pool`'s five mechanisms into one rung, and it is not a
trick: the guard is what makes the body reachable at all. Without it the body
goes to `leaf_store::collect_store_run` and dies on

```rust
    if stmts.iter().any(|s| s.value_is_fp || s.value_is_load) { return None; }
```

which is #2563's clause over 403,879 workload bodies. **This lane did not touch
that line** (decline clause D1), and §4 is the measurement that says so.

**Board #187 is sidestepped, not settled.** `CFG_SHAPE.md` §3.5 states band 1's
precondition as *"both arms are constants … cheap to build from a 0/1 or 0/−1
mask"*. Every body this class admits has a guarded arm that computes **nothing**
and a fall-out arm that is a **store sequence** — there is no constant pair to
select between, so band 1 is unreachable *by the class's own precondition*
rather than by a fitted rule. §3.5's table carries four rows in that sub-family
(`?a_store`, `?f_eqvoid`, `?Pool::Alloc`, `?Pool::Free`) and all four are `bclr`.
Band 3 is excluded by §3.5's own statement of it. **No line of either new file
reads a discriminator**, the ~30-cell probe #187 asks for is still owed, and this
lane did not run it.


`codegen::div_mod_leaf` transcribes the same five divide words as a **contiguous
constant body at r11/r3/r4**; here they are at **r10/r9/r11 and split across four
unrelated instructions**. That is c2's scheduler and its allocator, and this lane
models **neither** — the emitter reproduces one body and the recognizer refuses
anything that is not exactly its statement list. `div_mod_leaf`'s own header is
the standing precedent and the honest standing for this file: *"There is no
scheduler here and no register allocator."*

**And two of the loop class's named unknowns are not owed here** (#2595):

* **`WB_LOOP_FINDINGS` §9 item 4** — the unread trip-count arithmetic — is unread
  *for a non-unit step*, and §9.1 says the honest first class should require
  `step ∈ {+1, −1}`. This loop is `do{…}while(--n)`: step **−1**, and the
  counter **is** the trip count. `mtctr` takes it after one `addi -1`; there is
  no `srwi`/`divwu`/`addi +1` preheader to read.
* **§7.7 rule 1**, rotation-plus-guard, is not owed either: `cmpwi cr6,10,1 ;
  bf 25,+28` is **the source's own `if (count > 1)`**, present in the IL as `24`
  then `38 <label>`. The guard is *read*, not *invented*, so #1902's rule for
  choosing a pre-test's operands and sense is never consulted.

`w-loop` **#744** files `do/while` under COMPARE-form; this is a `do/while` that
takes CTR, because #744's own predicate — a trip count computable at entry and no
call in the body — holds here and did not hold in its cell. A refinement of that
row, not a refutation.

### 8.1 The unnamed-refusal budget — one spent, and it was not on the list

**`w-pool`'s cell C converting was not pre-armed and could not have been priced
from the commission** (§4). Six places were armed and this was none of them; the
nearest, *"a `_neg` cell confounded by source formatting"*, is about **this**
lane's cells, not a peer's. Budget 1, spent 1.

It cost about twenty minutes and it produced #2591, which is the most
transferable thing in this rung. The lesson is that a lane converting a TU that a
peer lane priced **inherits that peer's fixtures as a live surface**, and the
arming list should say so.

**And the budget then OVERRAN, at 2, on this lane's own tooling.**
`work/w-pool2/{tests_base,hatch_base}.sh` each end in `git reset --hard <tip>` to
undo their temporary base commit — which also **discards every uncommitted
tracked change in the tree**. Ten board rows had been written into `BOARD.md` and
not yet committed when the first of them ran, and they were gone. Caught by
`git status` one command later, costing about two minutes and no committed work,
and re-applied from `work/w-pool2/board_rows.md` where they had been drafted.

The transferable part is the shape, not the incident: **a counterfactual that
restores the tree with `reset --hard` is not neutral with respect to work in
progress**, and every lane that measures a base this way has the same hazard.
`w-pool` §7.2 and `w-biquad` both describe the technique and neither says this.
The cheap fix is to commit before running one; the cheaper one is to draft
anything long in `work/` first, which is the only reason this cost two minutes
instead of an hour.

## What it admits, and what it refuses

**No `crates/` change, no `fixtures/` change, no `scripts/` change, no
`DISCLOSURE.md` row, no fixture prefix, no function class.** `git diff
82fa92a0..HEAD -- crates fixtures scripts` is **empty**, and the gate's
`graded tree` content hash is **`24aad38816d7` at both ends** over 740 files.
This is a revert-everything lane and the identity is recorded, not asserted.

**It refuses to be a findings document.** The 19 `WB_*_FINDINGS.md` files are
the findings; they are dated, they stay as written, and `ref/` points **at**
them. Where two of them disagree, `ref/` records **both, dated, with the later
correction beside the earlier claim**, and does not arbitrate on its own
authority.

**It refuses volume.** No page may be more than half verbatim decompiler
output — the full bodies stay in the uncommitted, regenerable Ghidra export and
every page carries the five-line retrieval recipe instead. The deliverable is
navigability; a thousand pages nobody reads would have been `FAILED`.

## 2. What was built

```
docs/whitebox/ref/
  README.md      the front door: provenance legend, coverage with denominators,
                 a question -> one-lookup table, the retrieval recipe
  ADDR.tsv       GENERATED, 1,199 rows. One row per address that is cited in
                 docs/ or carries a hand label: containing function + size,
                 caller/callee counts, TU + how far to trust it, subsystem page,
                 confidence, label, and every doc that already mentions it
  SUBSYS.md      subsystem -> TU -> page, AND every TU with no page and where
                 its record is instead
  P_COFF.md      coff.c + coffemit.c          21 entries / 120 in band
  P_SECTION.md   p2symtab.c + emit.cpp        24 / 137
  P_REGALLOC.md  color.c (+globregs, regasg)  33 / 70
  P_DAG.md       dag.c + the scheduler band   32 / 61
  P_INLINE.md    inline.c                     16 / 93
  P_EH.md        ehexcept.c/except.c/.pdata   19 / 47
docs/whitebox/scripts/build_ref.py   the generator (env-driven export path)
```

Every claim on every page carries **`[R]` read / `[O]` obj-confirmed /
`[I]` inferred**, with `[O] port` reserved for the readings the port has
emitted a byte-exact obj on (`P_EH`, via `w-main2`). **An unmarked claim is a
defect**, and that was frozen as `FAILED` criterion F5 before any page existed.

A page **owns** an address when the address is the **first cell of one of that
page's table rows** — so authoring a page is what puts an address on it and the
index never guesses. 376 of 1 199 rows carry a page.

### 3.1 The PREREG's own denominator was wrong, and it is the lane's best calibration datum

`PREREG.md` §3 froze **1 079** as C1's denominator. **That number was produced by
a one-line grep and the grep is wrong in three ways** — all three found while
writing the generator, all three documented in `ref/README.md` §6.1 and in
`build_ref.py`'s own comment:

1. `\b10[bc][0-9a-f]{5}\b` — **`\b` does not fire between `x` and `1`**, so it
   misses **every** `0x10b9b8e9`, which is the most common form in the record.
2. Dropping the anchors entirely matches **substrings inside the sha256** and
   inside byte dumps. That is where 1 079 came from.
3. The record writes `FUN_10b8303c`, `DAT_10c2e234`, `LAB_10c1bfe2` as often as
   bare addresses, and `_` is a word character — so **both** `\b` and
   `[^0-9A-Za-z_]` anchoring miss all of those. **120 addresses are
   prefix-only**, including `FUN_10b8303c`, the symbol-list driver that is the
   whole of open question **R6**.

Corrected: **1 126** at base (**1 129** at this tip — this lane's own
amendments to `C2_MAP.md` §3E, `BOARD.md` and the rung added three; the index
is self-referential and `ref/README.md` §4 says so). Graded honestly both ways: against the corrected
denominator C1a is **100%**; against the frozen-but-wrong 1 079 it would be
`1 126/1 079 > 100%`, which is meaningless, because the two sets are not nested.
**The frozen number is not repaired in place — it stands in `PREREG.md` and the
correction is beside it.**

This is the fourth entry in the repo's *"ranking instruments measure
themselves"* pattern, and the first where the instrument was a denominator
rather than a ranking.

## 4. The usability test — three questions, graded

Frozen in `PREREG.md` §4 **before the resource existed**: three questions past
lanes answered the hard way. The bar is **one lookup** — one grep of `ADDR.tsv`,
or one named section of one page, with no reading of the 19 findings documents
required to *get* the answer (they are cited so it can be *checked*).

| # | question | lookup | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **U1** | the section-`Characteristics` **alignment nibble** for an object of size *n*, and where it is decided | `ref/P_SECTION.md` **§2** — reachable from `ref/README.md` §3 row 1, or from `grep -P '^10b28261\t' ADDR.tsv` | **ANSWERED in one lookup.** The four-step chain (`max(t, 1 if n<2 else 4 if n<64 else 8)` per object → max into `sect+0x43` → `(log2(a)+1)<<20` at `0x10b28261` → OR'd by `0x10b289fd` only when the IL override carries no nibble) **plus the measured `1→ALIGN_1, 2→ALIGN_4, 4→ALIGN_4, 8→ALIGN_8` table**, with `[R]`/`[O]` on each step |
| **U2** | the section emitter's **`Selection` and `CheckSum`** rules | `ref/P_COFF.md` **§4** | **ANSWERED in one lookup**, and the answer includes the two places the record contradicts itself — *"only `0` and `2` occur anywhere"* is true of `.data`/`.bss` and false of `.pdata` (`Selection = 5`), and *"`CheckSum` nonzero ONLY for COMDATs"* is refuted for `.data` by the later 9/9 rule. Both dated claims stand; neither is rewritten |
| **U3** | the allocator's **tie-break field** | `ref/P_REGALLOC.md` **§4** | **ANSWERED in one lookup**, including the thing that makes it a trap: `+0x44` is **not in `WB_LIVE_FINDINGS.md`'s field table at all**, so a reader of the field table concludes the tie is unresolved. §4.1 reproduces that table **as wb-live wrote it** with `#3243`'s two corrections in the same rows |

**3 of 3.** The floor was 2 of 3 (`FAILED` criterion F1).

A fourth, not registered but worth recording because it is the one a reader
most often wants: *"is there an instruction scheduler?"* → `ref/P_DAG.md` §1,
**yes, four runs per function at `/O1`**, with `#1823`'s refutation and the
reason the wrong claim was reasonable.

## 5. Found and not taken

Ranked by what a future lane would get.

1. **`0x10b7f022` is a real function Ghidra never created, and `C2_MAP.md`
   §3E's location claim is wrong.** (`#3256`.) The index left 17 addresses in
   the emit walk unresolved; `objdump_intel.asm` shows `push esi` at
   `0x10b7f022` immediately after a `ret`, reached by **tail jump**
   (`jmp 0x10b7f022` at `0x10b7f362`) from `FUN_10b7f1ff`. So the walk loop at
   `0x10b7f15f` is **below** `FUN_10b7f1ff`'s entry and **`decomp_all.c` has no
   body containing it** — anyone grepping the export for `FUN_10b7f1ff` to read
   the project's most-cited whitebox finding reads the wrong function. The
   predicate, the bits and the cascade are unaffected; only the location was
   wrong. **Amended beside §3E, carried in `build_ref.py`'s `GHIDRA_MISSED`.**
   **Not taken:** a systematic sweep for other tail-called routines Ghidra
   missed. The `ADDR.tsv` `unmapped` column is the instrument for it and stands
   at **9** rows, of which 8 remain (5 in the `0x10c2932e`–`0x10c2a3b5` flag-table
   store sequence, which `C2_MAP.md` §3E already describes as run-time-built and
   which Ghidra likewise attributes to nothing).
2. **`FUN_10b8303c` was invisible to every address grep in this repo**
   (`#3257`) — it is written only in the `FUN_`-prefixed form, and it is the
   symbol-list driver `FUN_10b8303c(g_symList@0x10c2e234, FUN_10b2a936)` whose
   *iteration order* is the entirety of open question **R6** (COFF symbol-table
   order). **Not taken:** R6 itself. It needs an obj grid, not an index.
3. **The `.pdata` `Selection = 5` case falsifies a rule three documents state**
   (`#3258`). `OBJ_DATA_BSS_SHAPE.md` §3.1's *"only two Selection values occur
   anywhere"* is a statement about `.data`/`.bss` — `.pdata` carries
   `ASSOCIATIVE`, and `FUN_10be76d4` special-cases `selection != 5`, so **the
   binary itself knows 5 is different**. Similarly `OBJ_FORMAT_MVP.md`'s
   *"`CheckSum` nonzero ONLY for COMDAT"* is refuted for `.data` at 9/9. **Not
   taken:** reconciling them into one rule. The consistent reading is *"the
   field follows the raw data, not the COMDAT bit"* (`.bss` is 0 because it has
   no raw data), but FP-constant `.rdata` COMDATs carrying `0` are unexplained
   and that needs cells, not prose.
4. **There are TWO different `+0x44` fields and no document said so** (`#3259`).
   The allocator candidate's (`0x10b2b82d`, tie-break, **DESC unsigned**) and
   the DAG node's (`0x10b327cd`, original tuple index, **ASC**). Different
   records, different passes, opposite directions. Both pages now carry the
   warning. **Not taken:** nothing to take — it is a documentation defect and it
   is fixed.
5. **The reference's `page` column is empty for 822 of 1 198 rows** (`#3260`)
   — i.e. **most cited addresses are outside the six prioritized subsystems**.
   The largest uncovered clusters by citation count are the lowering/selection
   band (`lower.c`, `lowersmd.c`, `cgintrin.c` — covered by
   `WB_SELECT_RECONCILED.md`'s prose but with no page), `reader.c`, and
   `code.c`'s frame machinery. **Not taken deliberately:** three lanes have
   already read selection and a fourth reading is the worst use of a lane in
   this directory. A `P_READER` page over `WB_READER_FINDINGS.md` is the
   cheapest next page — that document is 596 lines with 79 addresses and no
   index.

**Sized and declined:** rebuilding the Ghidra project with the `GHIDRA_MISSED`
entries applied so `decomp_all.c` gains the missing bodies. It needs a
coordinated re-analysis (the export is single-writer and machine-local), and the
`GHIDRA_MISSED` table gets the same navigational result for free.

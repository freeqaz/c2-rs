## The hypothesis this lane brings, stated before testing it

**H1 — `.rdata$r` is not EH. It is RTTI.**

`EH_RECORDS.md` §3 and §8.3 measure the EH record set at the workload's own
flags (`/nologo /wd4355 /wd4164 /c /GR /O1 /Oi /EHsc`) and name its section
**`.rdata`** — plain, `Selection = 5`, associative to the function's `.text`.
Not `.rdata$r`. 20 objs, 21 EH functions, and the name never carries a `$r`.
§8.4 puts the type descriptor (`??_R0*`) in **`.data`**, `Selection = 2`.

The workload compiles **`/GR`** (RTTI on). `$r`-suffixed grouped sections are
where MSVC has historically put the *other* RTTI records — the complete-object
locator, class-hierarchy descriptor, base-class array and base-class
descriptors (`??_R4` / `??_R3` / `??_R2` / `??_R1`) — which are a function of
**polymorphic classes whose vtables are emitted**, not of exceptions.

If H1 holds, `.rdata$r` in 676 of 871 objs is a statement about how many TUs
emit a vtable, EH's contribution to factor C is *not* rung three, and the
front-page ordering of Phase 5 was assembled from a name.

**H1 is the reading I want to be true, and that is a declared bias (below).**
The incumbent is §10.19's, it is on the front page, and it was independently
re-derived by the coordinator with a second reader — but *only as a count of the
section name*, never as a reading of what is inside the section. That is the gap
this lane tests, and it is the same gap `EH_RECORDS.md` §11.4 records eating a
lane: a claim assembled from the two fields someone went looking for.

## Declared bias

**Deflationary about EH, and novelty-seeking about a refutation.** "The front
page is wrong about its own tightest factor" is a more interesting result than
"the front page is right", and my H1 prior comes from **MSVC naming conventions
recalled from memory** — the exact trap `EH_RECORDS.md` §8.5e and §9.9 record
eating two predictions ("importing an x86 layout from memory"). Guards:

1. **H1 is graded on bytes, never on the name.** The discriminator is the set of
   *symbols defined in* `.rdata$r` COMDATs across the 871 cached workload objs.
2. **A hard abandonment threshold, registered at a level that can fire** (R1c).
3. **The probe axes are separated** (`/GR` and `/EHsc` varied one at a time,
   never together) — the trap that nearly cost a lane a 155-case finding.
4. Every count names its predicate and its reader; I re-derive §10.19's own
   numbers with my own reader as a known-answer gate before trusting anything
   else I compute (R0).

## 3. The label lead stops being a per-class constant (#1761)

**This is the finding to carry off the lane.**

`IlFunction::label_lead` carried three surcharges that looked like three
unrelated per-class constants, one of them fitted only after the oracle refuted
an analogy (w-undname §4.4). The PREREG registered a **rule** instead, with a
mechanism, before any probe:

> **The lead is the number of unconditional intra-section `b` words in the
> body**, because c2 mints a named block label for a `b`'s target and charges the
> counter for it ahead of the function's own `$M` triple.

| class | witness | intra-section `b` | lead |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `if_call_join` | `negate_test.cpp` | 1 (`b $LN8`) | 1 |
| `guard_chain_shared_tail` | `vswprnc.cpp` | 1 | 1 |
| `alloc_init_or_fail` | `undname.cpp` | **0** | **0** |
| **`osf_handle_guard`** | **`osfinfo.cpp`** | **1** (`b .+32` at +0x64) | **predicted 1** |

**The prediction is a HIT, and it is load-bearing rather than inert.** The lane
ran the counterfactual explicitly: with the term forced to `0`, rebuilt and
re-scanned, `osfinfo.cpp` reads **`mismatch` (bytes diverge)**; with the term at
`1` it reads `match`. Both runs are in this lane's history and the `+ 0 *` form
was reverted before the next commit.

**The honest size of the claim.** It is a fit to **four** points, three of them
retrofitted, and the mechanism is a proposal rather than a derivation — no
`/FAsc` listing was read for it here. What it buys is that the *next* class to
be added meets a rule before it picks a number, instead of discovering its
surcharge by emitting three wrong symbol records. The rule is written into
`label_lead` itself, not only into this rung, for that reason.

## 4. What the conversion actually needed

### 4.1 `data_refs_of` is re-keyed, and the two low-half forms are NOT symmetric (#1762)

`_free_osfhnd` breaks the shipped walk in two independent ways and **only one of
them was a missing clause**:

```text
  +0x14  lis  r11,0       REFHI <limit>   ┐ the low half is a `lwz`
  +0x18  lwz  r11,0(r11)  REFLO <limit>   ┘ DISPLACEMENT: the global's VALUE is
                                            loaded, so nothing takes its address
                                            and no `addi` for it exists anywhere
  +0x28  lis  r10,0       REFHI <table>   ┐ the high half is in r10. The walk's
  +0x2c  slwi r9,r11,2                    │ `addis` test was `w ==
  +0x30  addi r10,r10,0   REFLO <table>   ┘ encode_addis(SCRATCH_REG,0,0)`, so
                                            this quad was INVISIBLE to it
```

The `addis` test is over any `rT` now and the open slot carries that register.
The asymmetry between the closer forms is the load-bearing part:

* **`addi rD,rT,0` keeps its canary** — one with no open high half above it still
  refuses. c2 spells a register copy `mr`, so a zero-displacement `addi` off a
  non-zero base is never an ordinary instruction.
* **`lwz rD,0(rT)` gets none.** It is an ordinary load, and **this very body
  contains one that is not a relocation**: `lwz r10,0(r11)` at `+0x50` reads the
  table entry's handle word, off the same base register and at the same
  displacement as the real REFLO twelve words earlier. A canary on that form
  would refuse every body that loads through a base register at zero. It is
  admitted only while a pair on `rT` is open.

#### 4.1.1 The neutrality is BY CONSTRUCTION, and the first design was not

The obvious walk — close on whichever form comes first — is **not** byte-neutral:
a previously-accepted body with a `lwz rD,0(r11)` between a REFHI and its REFLO
would close early and relocate a different word, in an obj that already matched.

The lane's first fix was to **refuse** that window as ambiguous. **That was
tried, and reverted before it was committed**, because it is neutral on bytes
and *not* neutral on verdicts: a body of that shape would go from accepted to
refused, losing a TU for a program that was already right.

What ships instead decides the form by **lookahead at the open**: an
`addi rD,rT,0` anywhere in the window wins, *including when a `lwz` sits before
it*. Every body the port emitted before this change closed on an `addi` — it is
what closed the pair — so every one takes the identical word, and the `lwz` arm
is reachable **only** from a window with no `addi` at all, which no
previously-accepted body has. **The widening can turn a refusal into an
acceptance and can do nothing else.** That argument is a `#[test]`
(`the_lwz_form_never_preempts_an_addi_form`), not a paragraph, and it is *also*
measured — §1's per-TU set comparison.

### 4.2 Three facts the reader has to pin (#1763)

1. **The two entry guards use different compare forms on the SAME operand**,
   four words apart. `fh >= 0` converts to a **signed** width-4 type and c2 emits
   `cmpwi cr6,r3,0`; `fh < *<limit>` converts the *other* operand to an
   **unsigned** one and c2 emits `cmplw cr6,r3,r11`. Both `2C`s are required by
   signedness and the sides they land on are not interchangeable.
2. **The two globals are reached differently and that decides two
   instructions.** The limit arrives as a `B9` value read and the table as a `26`
   designator. A recognizer taking both as designators is right about the symbols
   and wrong about the words.
3. **The flag member is a BYTE and the handle member is a WORD**, and only the
   read TYPE says so — `30 82 11 …` is the `lbz`, `30 86 41 …` the `lwz`.

## 6. What is a field, what is pinned, and why (#1767)

**Zero words are chosen by a scheduler or a register allocator** — PREREG **D1**,
which did not fire. The rule the class applies, stated so it is not re-derived:
**a value is a free field when varying it changes only an immediate field of the
same instruction, and is PINNED when varying it would change which instruction c2
emits.**

| value | | why |
|---|---|---|
| `k_shift` `k_mask` `k_elem` `off_file` `k_bit` `k_invalid` `k_ok` `k_errno` `k_doserrno` `k_fail` | **10 free fields** | each lands in one `srawi`/`clrlwi`/`mulli`/`lbz`/`li`/`cmpwi` immediate |
| **`off_hnd` — pinned to 0** | refused otherwise | the success store and the error store are **ONE word**, and that is only legal at displacement zero (§7) |
| **the outer scale — pinned to 4** | refused otherwise | the body multiplies twice, by 4 with a `slwi` and by 72 with a `mulli`, and which c2 picks is a chooser over "is the constant a power of two". One witness of each form is nothing to fit, so `k_elem` **refuses a power of two** |
| `k_mask`, `k_bit` | must be `2^n − 1` | the class has a `clrlwi` and no other masking word |
| `k_bit` additionally ≤ 127 | | at or above the loaded byte's sign bit a `char` costs an `extsb` this class does not emit. Below it the widening is **dead** — for any mask inside the value bits, `(signed char)x & mask` and `(zero-extended x) & mask` are the same number — so the bare `lbz` is provably right for either sign, and the mask stays an honest field rather than being pinned at its witnessed 1 |

**PREREG D1's itemization was wrong and its substance was right**: it registered
"11 immediate fields", counting `k_ok`/`k_fail` as one signed pair and `k_bit`
before the sign-bit argument existed. The shipped class has **10 free fields and
2 pinned constants**. Scored as a miss in §8.

## 7. The tail merge, which is the whole reason this is one class (#1768)

`+0x84`, `stw r10,0(r11)`, is **one word serving two structurally unrelated
statements**:

| path | r11 | r10 | the statement |
|---|---|---|---|
| success | the table entry | `K_INVALID` | `e->OFF_HND = K_INVALID` |
| error | `<doserrno>`'s return | `K_DOSERRNO` | `*<doserrno>() = K_DOSERRNO` |

c2 allocated the address to r11 and the value to r10 on **both** paths so the two
stores could be the same word, and reached it from the success arm through the
body's one intra-section `b`. An emitter that lowered the two statements
separately writes **33 words instead of 31**, gets every displacement after
`+0x5c` wrong, and **links** — `docs/GAPS.md` §6.

Board **#1400**'s finding pointing a third way. There a shared tail looked like a
peephole and deleting it was the defect; in `vswprnc` the sharing **was** the
class; here the sharing crosses two statements that share no operand, no type and
no source line.

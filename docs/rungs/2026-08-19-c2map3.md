## What it admits, and what it refuses

**Nothing is admitted into `crates/`, no `DISCLOSURE.md` row is added, and no
function class is claimed.** `crates/`, `fixtures/` and `scripts/` are
byte-identical at both ends. The deliverables are three:

1. **[`FUNCS.tsv`](../whitebox/ref/FUNCS.tsv)** — **one row per function in the
   image**, 4,917 of 4,917 = 100 %, generated by
   `docs/whitebox/scripts/build_funcs.py`.
2. **[`P_SYMBOL.md`](../whitebox/ref/P_SYMBOL.md)** — `CEILING.md` §6.1 **phase
   5** (weak externals, `alias-weak-needed-tus` **675/871**) and the decision
   site of **phase 6** (COMDAT synthesis, §2.3's **450**), both of which had no
   whitebox page and no findings document at base.
3. **A priced answer** to *"is RE cheaper per load-bearing fact than black-box
   probing"* — §4. It is priced, and the answer is **"the question is the wrong
   shape, and here is the one that is answerable"**.

**Refused:** phase 1 (emitter CFG classes). Reached far enough to name the next
read and no further; `SUBSYS.md` §4.1 says so in those words rather than
thinning a claim across it.

### H3 is the lane's own instrument failure, caught by its own rule

H3's fourth column was *"at least one navigational hook — a string, an import,
**or a bounded call-graph distance** to an already-labelled function"*. The hop
column reaches **97.7 %** and H3 therefore passes as written.

**It should not have.** 2,196 functions (44.7 %) sit at hop 2 and 1,427 at hop
1: *"two calls from the register allocator"* describes almost half the image.
The column is vacuous exactly where a lane would want to use it, and carries
signal only at `0` and at the **134** functions at `-`/`5`/`6+`.

This is **prereg invalidation rule #3 firing on the lane that wrote it** —
*"any ranking this lane produces is suspect by default; the repo is four for
four on ranking instruments measuring themselves"* — and it is the **fifth**
entry in that pattern. The column is **kept, and labelled as not working** in
both `SUBSYS.md` §5 and `ref/README.md` §4.1, because the alternative is that
the next lane re-derives the same ordering and believes it.

**The transferable form:** *a success criterion satisfiable by an instrument
that does not discriminate is not a success criterion.* Same family as
`w-sizebracket`'s **"a predicate can be 39.6 % wrong about c2 and free in the
metric used to choose it"**.

### T1 and T4 missed because they were self-measuring targets, and inflating them would have destroyed the thing being measured

`ADDR.tsv` could have been taken past 1,700 rows in one commit: 520 functions
carry a string literal or an import, and a mechanical row per function would
have cleared T1 and T4 together.

**That would have made the index worse.** `ADDR.tsv`'s value is precisely that
a row means *"a human wrote about this"*; mixing 500 mechanical joins into
`labels/` would put `mech` rows in a directory whose whole contract is
hand-earned findings, and would make `n_cites` unreadable. The row count was
never the quantity that mattered — **a lane's ability to get an answer was** —
and T1 measured the index's size instead.

So the mechanical join went where it belongs: **`FUNCS.tsv`, at a 100 %
denominator, with its own generator and its own `conf = mech` value that is
explicitly weaker than `[R]`** (`[R]` asserts the instructions were read
correctly; `mech` asserts only that these tables join here).

`ref/README.md` §4's self-referential-drift warning is also confirmed
mechanically: the +37 rows include this lane's own prose. Counts are quoted with
the tip, as that section requires.

## Phase 5 and phase 6 — what was read

Full detail in [`P_SYMBOL.md`](../whitebox/ref/P_SYMBOL.md); labels in
`docs/whitebox/labels/W-SYMBOL.tsv`. Both phases are decided in **one 724-byte
function, `0x10b28a9b`**, which had a two-line label and no page.

* **Phase 5.** A COFF **weak external pair**: the target's `EXTERNAL` record
  first — minted on demand at `0x10b28ce1` **because the emitter recurses into
  the target at `0x10b28cb9`** — then the alias at `StorageClass 0x69`
  (`0x10b28cfd`) with aux `{TagIndex = the default's index (0x10b28cec),
  Characteristics = 2 (0x10b28cea)}`, written by `FUN_10b2af4f`.
  **Two disjoint routes reach it**: the alias bit `+0x37 & 0x400000`
  (`0x10b28b02`), and — invisible to any alias-keyed grid — `[sym+0x3f] != 0` at
  storage kind 2 (`0x10b28c7d`).
* **Phase 6.** The decision site is `([sym+0x37]>>5)&0xF` at **`0x10b28be6`**, a
  four-way `dec`-chain deciding section-with-`IMAGE_SCN_LNK_COMDAT` /
  section-number-0 / STATIC. Plus **two outright suppressions**: `[sym+0x32]&1`
  and the 3-bit linkage field `∈ {1,3}` at `0x10b28bb4`/`0x10b28bbd`.
  **Which arm the 450 TUs take is NOT measured**, and the page says so.
* **`0x10b2823b`, 38 bytes: the COFF `Type` word.** `0x20` iff
  `[sym+0x30]==3` with `+0x31 ∈ {0x54,0x55,0x56}`, or `[sym+0x30]==4` with
  `+0x37 & 0x400`. It had **no row anywhere in the record**.
* **`0x10b28ca3` is decoded.** `W-ALIAS-2`'s disclosure row says of it: *"The
  instruction that turns `+0x20 & 0x2000` into the COFF Mark bit is **named
  (`0x10b28ca3`) and NOT decoded**."* It is a guarded call to the emit-marker
  `FUN_10b276e4` **from inside the COFF symbol writer** — so resolving an alias
  can add its target to the emit set *during obj writing*. **And `W-ALIAS-2`'s
  sentence about that address is imprecise**: `FUN_10b276e4` touches
  `[sym+0x4c]` (`or eax,0x20` at `0x10b276fb`, then the reference-list recursion
  at `0x10b27731` — `C2_MAP.md` §3E's closure), while the image's **one**
  `or DWORD PTR [eax+0x20],0x2000` is at **`0x10b663b9`**, inside
  `FUN_10b6633d` in the **`inline.c`** band. Two mechanisms, two translation
  units. Amended beside in `P_SYMBOL.md` §5.1; `W-ALIAS-2` is a `route:` row and
  its adopted content is untouched.
* **⛔ One open question, registered and not resolved** (`P_SYMBOL.md` §5):
  `[sym+0x4c]` is read two incompatible ways **37 bytes apart in the same arm**
  — `test …,0x20` (the emit bit, `C2_MAP.md` §3E) at `0x10b28ae4`, and loaded
  whole as the alias token (`W-ALIAS-1`) at `0x10b28b09`. The one-line probe
  that decides it is named. `W-ALIAS-1`'s row is unaffected either way.

## §4 — The priced answer: is RE cheaper per load-bearing fact?

### 4.1 The question as asked cannot be priced, and the reason is a finding

**Not one of the four `DISCLOSURE.md` adoptions moved `match` or
`fnbyte-exact`.** `W-GLATTRS-1` is the clearest: it adopted a *field width* so
the reader can step over `SIZE` and land on `ATTR` — a prerequisite. So *cost
per fact* is measurable and *value per fact* is not, and any ratio quoted in the
goal's units has an unmeasured numerator. Saying so is the honest answer, and
`PREREG_C2MAP3.md` T5 registered it in advance as a passing outcome.

### 4.2 The question that IS answerable, and its answer is 5 for 5

For each adopted rule: **could the grid have FOUND it? could the reading have
DECIDED it?**

| fact | grid could find it | reading could decide it | who decided |
|---|---|---|---|
| `W-ALIAS-1` — the tag-0x10 field **position** | **NO** — *"a black-box search for the field position binds at 0.019/0.026 one byte either side"* | no | RE found; grid graded, **15/15** |
| `W-ALIAS-2` — what the alias record **means** | yes | no | grid (`w-emitp` + 174,417 emitted names) |
| `W-MEMCPY-1` — `n = size/align`, **truncating** | **NO** — *"`w-memcpy` froze six rivals over 408 cells without one of them being a quotient"* | no — the whitebox grid has **0 cells** that can see the truncation | RE supplied the **search space**; grid decided the values, **624/624** |
| `W-GLATTRS-1` — the `0x80` escape **width** | yes — twin grid **18/18**, vocabulary **99/99** | no — endianness was black box too | grid. Its own row: *"the whitebox source is the **least of the three**"* |
| **`w-c2map3` phase 5** (this lane, independent) | the **constants**: yes — already done. The **ordering rule** and the **second route**: **NO** | no | grid decided the constants; RE is the sole source of the other two, **and neither is decided** |

**The answer.** *RE is not cheaper per fact. It is the only source of a
distinguishable class of fact, and it decides none of them.* The class is:

> **rules about the generator that produce no distinguishing observable on the
> corpus you have.**

Ordering-because-the-emitter-recurses versus ordering-by-adjacency is
**byte-identical** in every obj. A dispatch route the workload never exercises
is **byte-identical to no such route**. No grid separates those at any price, so
the cost ratio for that class is finite-versus-infinite — **undefined, not
favourable**. And symmetrically: for anything with a distinguishing observable
the grid is cheaper *and* decides, and the reading is redundant — **3 of the 5
rows above**.

**`W-GLATTRS-1`'s warning generalizes, and this lane is the fifth confirmation
rather than a counterexample.** The operational rule:

> **Read to generate; grid to decide. Never adopt a rule whose only support is
> the reading — and check whether the grid already answered it first.**

### 4.3 The concrete cost, and the concrete waste it would have avoided

* **Cost of this lane's phase-5 read**: roughly **twelve targeted greps of the
  flat export**. No build, no obj capture, no toolchain, no wibo. *(Self-report,
  not an instrumented measure — stated as such.)*
* **The record's price for the alternative**, verbatim: *"a single alignment
  nibble cost a lane; `dag.c`'s lowering order took two."* Against 370 rung docs
  for `match` **26**, one lane is not a small unit.
* **And the waste that was there to find.** Phase 5's three constants were
  **already obj-confirmed at full workload scale before this lane read a byte**
  — `alias-weak-not-search-library` and `alias-weak-default-disagree` are
  **KNOWN ANSWER 0** gap keys that run on **every gate invocation** — and
  `grep -ril 'weak.?extern' docs/whitebox/` at base returns **one** hit,
  `DISCLOSURE.md`, mentioning the word in passing. **The answer lives in a Rust
  doc comment in `crates/c2-harness/src/gap/scan.rs` that no whitebox grep
  reaches.**

  **That is the cheapest finding in this lane and it is a process defect, not a
  c2 fact:** the record is split across two indexes that do not cross-reference,
  and a whitebox lane dispatched at phase 5 would have paid a campaign to
  re-derive a number the gate computes on every run. `P_SYMBOL.md` §2.1 now
  carries the cross-reference. Board **#3314**.

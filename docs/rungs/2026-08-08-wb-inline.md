those measurements. Calibration measures an **input**, never a verdict.

## The lane's own reading, RETRACTED as written

R2-CEILING scored highest and is still **refuted**: its registered ceilings
(65 words at favor-size, 40 at favor-speed) are wrong at 38 cells. Its
*structure* — a ceiling on a pre-codegen instruction count, selected by the
favor-speed bit, bypassed by `__forceinline`, and switched off entirely at
`/Ob0` — survives, and every part of it that survives is stated in the findings
doc as a **measured fact about c2** rather than as a rival's score.

Two further readings are filed `unknown` rather than published:

* **the budget** `B = clamp(2 × caller_instrs, 1000, 35000)` (`0x10b626f4`) with
  its 40-instruction free threshold (`0x10b6249b`) — read correctly, and 12
  cells that move the caller by 118× change **nothing**;
* **the POGO profitability model** (`0x10b5fcd8`) and its two 46-dword parameter
  tables — the most model-like code in the inliner, and **unreachable on a
  non-PGO build**. Method doc §7 case 1, caught before publication. The tables
  live above the image's raw `.data`, so no value of theirs is quotable at all.

## `?supershuffle` — the clause, and the recommendation

The clause is the **loop-class size ceiling at `(60,80]`**: `?shuffle2` is 60 B
and the next smallest of its five siblings is 84 B. Both remedies were priced
against the obj and **both convert 0 TUs** — a real inliner needs a lowering for
arbitrary loop bodies into the caller's register allocation (WB-D's open
question) *plus* an interprocedural clobber set, and a transcription licence is
a class of exactly one in a TU that is 1 exact of 20.

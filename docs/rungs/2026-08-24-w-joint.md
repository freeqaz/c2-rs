## 0. The one-paragraph answer

**Lead with the bound, because it is the part that decides the wave and the
part that depends on nothing.** For a TU to match, *every* body must come in
class. The union of head keys over a non-matching TU's blocked bodies is
therefore a **floor** on the construct set its closure requires — a floor in
the safe direction, because a body reports one blocker however many it has
(`#3131`). Over the **845 non-matching TUs** that floor reads **min 1 · p10 90
· median 186 · p90 244 · max 278**, and **821 of 845 (97.2 %) exceed 50**.
**Exactly one TU in the workload has a floor of 1** (`ChecksumData_xbox.cpp`,
one body, `expr-op-0x32`), thirteen more are at ≤ 10, and then there is a
cliff. Against Phase 1's ten constructs, 97.2 % of the workload is out of reach
**at the floor**, before any question about successors. That is the answer to
*"is there any TU whose full set lies within a bounded set of constructs"*, it
is measured over the full population, and **no composition assumption enters
it in either direction**.

**Separately — and this is the part with a small denominator, so read its
scope** — the wave was funded to separate two propositions that ten Phase-1
slices at ~0 cannot tell apart: *Phase 1 is worthless* versus *Phase 1 is worth
~46 % jointly and the marginals are blind to it* (`#3505`, off `w-loo`
`#3140`). Measured **per function slot** on **2,417,794 slots over 866 TUs**,
lifting **one** clause family: **1,697** bodies close **outright**, **22**
(1.3 %) substitute a successor, **0** regress. So **for that family, closure
composes**, and the seven-times-confirmed sentence *"every single-key closure
measured here substitutes a successor rather than converting"* is **false as a
general law**. **The slot denominator is 2,417,794 and the FAMILY denominator
is 1** — no other family was varied and nothing is claimed for one. **And the
closure buys nothing anyway**: those 1,697 land in **365 TUs**, of which **0**
reach `blocked == 0` and **364 still carry more than ten blocked bodies**
(median **2,821**, max **6,664**). So *"the slice measured +0"* and *"closure
does not compose"* are **independent propositions**, this workload holds one
true and the other false, and `#3505`'s two candidate explanations contain
neither.

lane deliberately did **not** invent the mapping: decision 10's own lesson is
that a coverage claim nobody checked (C7's, already measured at zero by `#420`)
survived to dispatch, and a guessed key→slice table is how the next one would.

* `ChecksumData_xbox.cpp`'s single remaining key is **`expr-op-0x32`**.

  *"the byte's meaning is not verified against a live capture"* — so `0x32` is a
  construct **this tree has not named**, and none of Phase 1's three keyed
  constructs is `0x32`. Boards **#622** / **#662** record `0x32` as the
  *successor* of off-add (`0x27`) on `xboxheap.cpp`, whose full chain is five
  steps (`27, 32, 26, 99, BD`); **#3473** (this same day) records the same
  `0x27 → 0x30`/`0x32` succession firing in the fixture suite.
* Several of the fourteen floors are **visibly intrinsic/load-type/lit-type
  dominated** (`FilterCoeffs.cpp`: `body-0x9B` + four `expr-intrinsic-*` +
  three `expr-load-type-*` + `call-postop-0x2C`), which is the shape a
  Phase-1-reachable TU would have. **They also carry 109–121 blocked bodies
  each**, so their floors are floors over large populations and the
  successor question there is untested by anything.

**Price of closing this**: the key→slice mapping is a **read**, not a probe —
the seven unkeyed constructs are named in `IL_EXPR_LAYER.md` /

one lane that produces `slice → {census keys}` with a per-key population count
taken from a scan that already runs. Estimate **0.5–1 day**, and it is a
prerequisite for *any* Phase-1 go/no-go, because every existing Phase-1 price is
denominated in `bodies` and every stop/continue argument is denominated in
`TUs`, and nothing in the tree converts between them.

**This is not a counted loop.** There is no induction variable, no trip count,


### 7.2 The three candidate rungs, each priced

**(R2) Board #1988's extensions (a), (b), (c).**

**Declined**, and #1988's row should be corrected rather than re-taken: they are

**(R3) A call inside a loop body.**

**This is the only real lever and it is not a reader rung and not a loop rung.**

more than this whole family. **A lane on it should be priced from *that* key,
not from this one**, or it will re-run the mistake this lane was commissioned to
correct.

### 7.3 The recommendation

> **No reader or emitter rung on the `expr-jump` family is worth a lane.** The
> family's body column is 86 % three constructs, two of them one header inline
> apiece; its emitted column is 82 % counted loops whose next blocker is a call;
> its cheapest rung converts 7 → 0; and the whole intra-body control-flow
> vocabulary is the last blocker of **zero** functions in 878 TUs.
>
> **What board #1988 should say instead**: the loop family becomes a lever only
> through the **call-in-loop-body** seam, and that seam is owned by
> `expr-call-in-expr-*` and must be priced there.

§10.26's list was already declared out of levers (§10.26.5). This lane closes
the one measurement that list deferred, and the answer is that the deferral was

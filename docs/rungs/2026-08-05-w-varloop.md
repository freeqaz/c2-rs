## 3. The finding w-sched2 could not have made: the IL is NOT rotated

`super::ptr_walk_loop`'s grammar is a **rotated** loop — the IL jumps over the
increment into the test — and it was easy to assume the same for this class.
**It is false, and the bytes say so:**

```text
  for (u = …; *u; u++)      3A Ltest · 29 Lincr · <incr> · 29 Ltest · <test>
                            · 38 Lexit · <body> · 3A Lincr
                            -- c1xx rotates it

  while (*s) { … s++; }     29 Ltest · <test> · 38 Lexit · <body> · <incr>
                            · 3A Ltest
                            -- NO rotation in the IL AT ALL
```

Yet `c2` emits the same rotated machine shape for both: a peeled load, an entry
guard, a body, a backward `bc`. **So for this class the rotation is the port's
own work** — nothing in the IL says where the peel goes, where the guard goes,
or that the test should be emitted twice. That is the sharpest available answer
to *"is this a lowering or a transcription"*, and it is a fact about the input
rather than a claim about the code. Board **#799**.

## 4. V1 and V2 — the preamble and the tail, and they were unmeasured

**w-sched2's reconstruction rebuilds the loop BODY and the back edge only.** It
hard-codes the walked pointer as `r10` and the accumulator home as `r3`, and it
never generates, compares or grades a single preamble or tail word — the
`104 of 104` is a statement about `M + 3` words out of a function of `M + 9`.

A lowering must emit the whole function, so PREREG §2 registered both preambles
as claims that **can lose**, and registered V2 as resting on **one cell**, which
is the arity ten placement rules have already died at in this project.

> ### **V1 (TWO) and V2 (SAME) both HELD.** Read word for word off real `c2`'s
> objs at seven lengths (`work/w-varloop/probe.py`), and then confirmed at all
> ten by the **whole-obj** byte compare — which grades the preamble and the tail
> along with everything else, and is the stronger statement.

```text
  TWO                             SAME
  lbz    r11,0(r3)                lbz    r11,0(r3)
  mr     r10,r3                   mr     r10,r3
  li     r3,K0                    li     r3,K0
  <entry test>                    b      .+4*(M+2)     <- JUMPIN, into the
  bclr   12,2      <- P2                                  record form
  |  body: M+2 words  |           |  body: M+2 words  |
  bf     2,-4*(M+2)               bf     2,-4*(M+2)
  blr                             blr

  M + 9 words                     M + 8 words
```

**SAME is one word shorter, and that is a property of the entry form rather than
of the body.** The peeled character and the induction load share `r11`, so the
record form *is* the entry test and is reached by falling into it. A port that
emitted the TWO preamble at `M >= 4` would be one word long and wrong about
every offset after it. Board **#797**.

**P2 earns its keep here in a way w-rotate could only state.** The guard folds to
`bclr`, which carries **no displacement at all** — so the entry test of a
variable-length body needs no forward fixup. The one branch that does depend on
the length is the back edge, and it is `-4*(M+2)`.

### 4.1 A third record form, which the reconstruction never had to derive

w-sched2's generator reads the record form's **opcode out of `c2`'s own bytes**
(`if body[R] is op31 XO 444 then emit mr. else extsb.`). An emitter has to
choose it. Measuring both signednesses in both regimes gives **three** forms, not
two:

```text
  signed             record `extsb. CHAR,LD`      entry test `extsb. CHAR,CHAR`
  unsigned, TWO      record `mr.    CHAR,LD`      entry test `cmplwi cr0,CHAR,0`
  unsigned, SAME     record `cmplwi cr0,CHAR,0`   -- no entry test at all
```

The third row is the one nothing had ever needed: in the SAME regime `LD` **is**
`CHAR`, so `mr. CHAR,CHAR` would be a move to itself and `c2` emits the compare
instead. A lane that had copied the two-form rule out of w-sched2 would have
emitted one wrong word on every unsigned SAME cell. Board **#798**.

## 5. What the emitter accepts, and what it refuses

Every clause is a **positive** check; `Some(false)` is the only reading acted on.
The refusals are measured counterexamples, and each is a graded cell of Grid E.

| refused | what `c2` emits instead | cell |
|---|---|---|
| `r & K` | **`andi.`**, which WRITES cr0 — `c2` then demotes the record form to a plain `extsb` and adds an explicit `cmpwi r11,0`: a different block, one word longer, with a second CR writer in it | `e-and` |
| `r - c` | `subf`, non-commutative; its operand roles come from selection, so **S5 does not speak for it** | `e-sub-char` |
| `r << K` | `rlwinm`, and it reassociates | `e-shift` |
| `/`, `%` | w-divmod's spine and `PtrWalkModLoop`'s | `e-div`, `e-mod` |
| a chain that never reads the character | `pv` undefined, and every rule is stated in terms of it | `e-no-char` |
| `* 2^n`, `* ±1`, `* -k`, `* >simm16` | `rlwinm` / nothing / `x - a*k` / `lis`+`ori`+`mullw` | four cells |
| a literal past its opcode's immediate field | a **`lis`/`ori` split producer** — board #644, kept out **by construction** rather than by inspection | `e-add-wide` |
| the pointer off slot 0, or a second formal | the whole block re-plans (w-hash's `swap`/`p3`) | `e-p1`, `e-p2` |
| a wider element, a stride ≠ 1, `K0` outside `simm16` | `lhz`/`lwz`, no `lbzu` fold, a `lis`/`ori` pair | three cells |
| `while (*s != 0)` | **a different IL production** — a `2C` widening, a `33 <int> 00` and a relational opcode this grammar does not carry | `e-ne0` |

**`&` is a new boundary this lane found**, not one inherited: `andi.` writing cr0
is exactly the collision a sentinel loop cannot tolerate, because the back edge
reads cr0. Board **#801**.

**Board #644 is outside this class by construction.** One admitted chain step is
one instruction, enforced by the literal-range checks, so `M == N` always holds
and the two units w-sched2 §5 had to distinguish coincide. That is a narrowing,
not a solution — a split producer is still unhandled, and it is still open.

## 7. The must-fail mutations

Registered in `PREREG.md` §4 **before the emitter existed**. Each perturbs one
rule by one; `work/w-varloop/mutate.py` applies it to the real source, rebuilds,
re-grades against real `c2`, restores the source in a `finally`, and verifies the

| mutation | grids | intact | mutated | of the red, WRONG BYTES |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **M1** S1 — one more chain word before the load | A B C D | 43/43 | **0/43** | 36 |
| **M2** S2 — the record slot's load-use distance 2 → 3 | A B C D | 43/43 | **18/43** | 18 |
| **M3** S3m — the regime threshold `M >= 4` → `M >= 3` | A B C D | 43/43 | **39/43** | 4 |
| **M4** S4r — `T1` and `T2` exchanged | A B C D | 43/43 | **13/43** | 30 |
| **M5** S5 — the commutative operand order reversed | A B C D | 43/43 | **0/43** | 43 |
| **M6** #747 — the body length fixed at the first loop's `M` | A F | 15/15 | **11/15** | 4 |

**Six of six turn red and none survived.** The *wrong-bytes* column is the part
worth reading: a mutation that makes the port **refuse** is weak evidence, and a
mutation that makes it emit a **wrong obj against real `c2`** is the real thing.
M3, M4, M5 and M6 are 100 % wrong bytes; M1 and M2 are mixed, because at short
chain lengths the perturbed slot leaves no legal record position and the emitter
declines rather than emitting a word it cannot justify.

**M3 is the smallest and it is the most informative.** Moving the regime
threshold by one breaks exactly the four `M = 3` cells — the cells that sit on
the boundary — and nothing else. That is what a threshold rule *should* look
like under mutation, and it is why S3m's `M >= 4` is not a fitted constant with
slack in it.

**Every digit is identical to the tree this lane branched from.** The one
counter that had a licence to move — `census/gate disagreement` — did not,
because the `/O1` refusal is raised in the census as well as in codegen.

Say it plainly rather than reaching for a consolation. The class this lane
lowers is the sentinel walk **without** the `%`. `Sort.cpp`'s
`?HashString@@YAHPBDH@Z` — the one workload function of this family — needs
w-divmod's signed-`%` spine, and it is **already matched** by the transcription
`w-hash` shipped. So there is no workload TU this lane converts, and the census
is `+0` on both axes because a body-parameterized recognizer for a source shape
the workload does not contain admits nothing new in it.

**That was the honest trade and it was made deliberately.** The brief allowed it
in terms — *"if the cheapest honest win is the sentinel walk without the `%`,
take it and say so"* — and the alternative was to widen into `div_mod_leaf`,
which a concurrent lane owns.

**What moved instead is what the port can express.** Before this rung, every
control-flow body the port emitted had a **fixed length**: `Sort.cpp`'s twenty
words, and `CondPair`'s two arms. The emitter now produces bodies at ten
chain lengths in two register regimes from one code path, and #747's shape —
which no standing instrument can generate — has a fixture with a mutation that
bites it. Whether that is worth a rung with `+0` on the payoff metric is a
judgement the reader should make with the number in front of them, not with a
rounded-up description of it.

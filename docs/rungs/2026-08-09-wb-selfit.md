### 3.2 The arm count is an arithmetic, not a judgment


It is the more specific claim and it is about the wrong pass. Specificity is not
evidence of identification, and this is the second time in two lanes that the
more precisely-stated of two readings was the mistaken one (§3.7's enum is the
other, in the opposite direction). **The check that caught it was reading the
callers**, not the body: `FUN_10c0a2e2` has two, both gated on `0x26e`/`0x26f`,
and that took one grep.

### 7.2 M2 / M2a, and a badly-formed pair

M2 registered "exactly one row needs an address" at 0.65 and M2a registered "a
second one does, and it is a count" at 0.55. **These are complements** — they
cannot both be true — so the pair scores one hit and one miss by construction and
carries no information beyond `p(second row) = 0.55`. That is a defect in the
registration, not in the result, and it is recorded rather than quietly counted
as 1-for-2. The substantive outcome: **two rows need an address**, and the second
is `wb-select2`'s own `W-SELECT-4` note about the counts, upheld and extended.

### 7.3 G5 and G6 — both misses are the same fact seen from two sides

G5 (R2 abstains somewhere on R1's grid) and G6 (R1 scores ≥ 7 of 10 on R2's) both
assumed the two readings had **comparable coverage**. They do not: R2 claims 23
of 24 cells and R1 claims 13. Registering a symmetric expectation for an
asymmetric pair is the error, and it is the same error the commission's framing
made in listing "the tie direction" as a disagreement when both lanes agree.

### 7.4 Direction, and board #770

Registered **PESSIMISTIC** — specifically, that neither lane would be wholly
right, that each side would take a correction, and that the mask question would
not close. **All three held.** The four misses are: two on which function does
what (§7.1, and they favoured the lane that turned out to be wrong there), one
badly-formed complementary pair (§7.2), and one symmetry assumption (§7.3).
**None is optimistic about the lane's own result**, so **board #770 goes to
twelve-of-sixteen optimistic**.

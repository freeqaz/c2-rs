## 0. THE COMMISSION, AND THE ONE SENTENCE THAT REPLACES IT

> The brief: *"`CEILING.md` §2.2 records that `reach-pool` and `frontier-pool`
> differ by exactly `projection-divergence` = 2, and those two TUs are
> `src/system/decomp_pch.cpp` and `src/system/math/vec.cpp` — **'Both need
> factor A alone.'** … Convert one or both. `match` 23 → 24 or 25."*

**Neither converts, and the reason is not a missing model.**

> ### **`emit-predicate-worth` is 124 and its intersection with the TUs today's gate can bind is `0`.** `Bindings::per_record` binds nothing unless the `.gl` records are 1:1 with **all** the `.ex` segments. Over the 871 graded TUs, `.gl` spells a body-start offset for **every** `.ex` segment on **29** of them. `|reach-pool ∩ full-coverage|` = **0 of 124**; `|A∧B∧C ∩ full-coverage|` = **27 of 27**; `|match ∩ full-coverage|` = **23 of 23**. **Board #2780, #2781.**

> ### **AND THE TWO TARGET TUs DO NOT SHARE A MECHANISM.** `vec.cpp`'s emit set is two bodies and **`.gl` names both of them** — `??0Vector3@@QAA@MMM@Z` at body-start **98,922** and `??0Vector4@@QAA@MMMM@Z` at **105,430**, each an `.ex` `4F 1F` split point. What it cannot name is **438 of the other 809**, which are bodies c2 **discards**. `decomp_pch.cpp`'s emit set contains **no body at all** — its reference obj has zero `.text` — and its one emitted symbol is `.rdata` **data** with no body-start anywhere. One needs a **selective** binding; the other needs an **acceptance path in front of the gate**. **Board #2784, #2785.**

> ### **SO §2.5's *"Both need factor A alone"* IS REFUTED, and not in the way `w-vec` refuted it.** `w-vec` §10.5 established that both TUs are *refused before factor A is a question*. This lane establishes the harder half: **a perfect factor A, handed to today's `PortC2::build`, still converts neither**, because the emit set would have to be applied in front of a gate whose 1:1 requirement has **no solution on the input**. Factor A is necessary, and on these two it is not sufficient even in the limit. **Board #2782.**

The lane's deliverable is that measurement, the instrument that produces it on
every future scan, and a per-TU decline priced in §4 at **six** mechanisms for
`decomp_pch.cpp` and **seven** for `vec.cpp`, of which **two are new** and the
rest are re-derived at this tree rather than quoted.

## 3. THE MEASUREMENT — is the binding the gate failed SATISFIABLE?

`Bindings::per_record` requires every `.gl` record's framed body-start offset to
**be** an `.ex` split point, in order and 1:1, and binds **nothing** otherwise.
Four fields have been used to ask whether a given TU satisfies that, and three
were wrong (#2621, #2754); all four report on the **walk**. This asks about the
**input**:

> **For each `4F 1F` split point in `.ex`, does the five-byte spelling
> `80 <LE32 offset>` — the only encoding this crate has ever read a body-start
> offset in — occur ANYWHERE in `.gl`?**

`IlBundle::gl_body_start_coverage`, a pure reader that makes no acceptance
decision. **`present` is deliberately an over-count**: any `80 <LE32>` counts,
framed or not, so `total − present` is a **lower bound** on the segments no
record can name and a tighter reader can only make the negative claim larger.

### 3.1 The three readings, and the control

```text
src/system/decomp_pch.cpp    622 of 1,312     690 segments can bind to NO record
src/system/math/vec.cpp      373 of   811     438 segments can bind to NO record
src/system/utl/EncryptXTEA.cpp  5 of     5    — a TU that already MATCHES
```

**The control is load-bearing and it is 23 of 23**: every matching TU reads
`n of n`. If a TU the port already emits byte-exact could read `present < total`
the field would be measuring the reader, which is the one thing it claims not to
do. The reader therefore runs on **every** class, not only the refused ones.

### 3.2 …and the missing offsets are ABSENT, not merely unframed

This is the PREREG's C3 antecedent and it is the difference between an
impossibility and a reader rung. `work/w-phase7b/absent.py` searches the whole
`.gl`, framed or not:

```text
decomp_pch.cpp   1,312 split points   present 622   ABSENT byte-for-byte 690
vec.cpp            811 split points   present 373   ABSENT byte-for-byte 438
EncryptXTEA.cpp      5 split points   present   5   ABSENT                0
split points below 0x80, where a short form could hide one:  0, 0, 0
```

> ### **690 of `decomp_pch.cpp`'s 1,312 `.ex` bodies and 438 of `vec.cpp`'s 811 have NO body-start offset in `.gl` AT ALL.** Not unframed — absent. So `Bindings::per_record`'s 1:1 requirement has **no solution on these inputs**, and no framing anyone writes changes that. **Board #2780.**

For completeness, because it is the repair a reader lane would otherwise price:
`codec::gl_offset_framed`'s doc says it locates the offset field *"by position
within the record, not by what its value happens to be."* That is true of the
offset field and **not** of the field in front of it — the frame is
`80 <LE32 PREV> 00 00` and the test pins `gl[o-5] == 0x10`, which constrains
`PREV` to `[0x1000, 0x10FF]`. Relaxing that one byte to `PREV < 0x10000`
(`work/w-phase7b/frame.py`) takes the framed-record count from **31 → 612** on
`decomp_pch.cpp` and **36 → 369** on `vec.cpp`, with **0** false positives on
`vec.cpp` and **1** on `decomp_pch.cpp`. **It is still 612 of 1,312 and 369 of
811, so it converts nothing, and this lane did not ship it** (§8). **Board
#2783.**

## 10. FOUND AND NOT TAKEN

1. **`HeadsetXferEffect.cpp` (16/16) and `MeterEffect.cpp` (13/13)** — the only
   two TUs in the workload outside `A∧B∧C` whose binding is satisfiable. Both
   are `vocab-gap`, both fail B and C, and **no lane has ever looked at
   either**. They are the entire population where a factor-B or factor-C rung
   could convert something without a binding change. §3.4, board **#2786**.
2. **The `PREV` field of a `.gl` defined record is a rising per-record number**
   — 0x1005, 0x1007, 0x100b, 0x100f, 0x1015 across `EncryptXTEA.cpp`'s five
   records; 0x184b/0x184d/0x184f/0x1851 in a cluster on `vec.cpp`; 0x19a0…
   on `decomp_pch.cpp`. `gl_offset_framed`'s `gl[o-5] == 0x10` therefore frames
   a TU's records **only while that number stays below 0x1100**, which is why
   the same reader is 5 of 5 on a 2.5 KB `.gl` and 31 of 1,312 on a 124 KB one.
   Nothing here identifies what `PREV` counts. Board **#2783**.
3. **The 690 and 438 absent bodies are not noise — they are the discarded
   ones.** On `vec.cpp` both emitted bodies are inside the 373 present. So the
   `.gl` describes a *superset* of the emit set and a *subset* of the segment
   list, which is exactly the shape a selective binding needs and the shape the
   totality requirement cannot use. One join away: is `emitted ⊆ present` on all
   871 TUs? This lane checked it on two.
4. **`.ex` carries no per-body emit marker.** The optimization word splits
   `vec.cpp`'s 811 segments 684/127 and `decomp_pch.cpp`'s 1,312 segments
   1,092/220, against emit sets of **2** and **0**; the three bytes after it are
   `4f 20 80` on **all 2,123** segments. Registered here so the next lane does
   not re-run it. `work/w-phase7b/exscan.py`. Board **#2787**.
5. **`decomp_pch.cpp` is a one-line TU and `vec.cpp` is 29 lines that define no
   function.** Both objs' entire content is decided by what the *headers*
   contribute, which is why neither is a codegen row at any depth and why the
   frontier's own pricing vocabulary does not apply to them.

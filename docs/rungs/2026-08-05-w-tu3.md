## 3. The ranker, and what it refuses to do

`GapReport::frontier_byte_ranking`, off the same walk FBM uses, printed by every
scan and published as `gap-metric` keys.

* **The denominator is a function of `c2`'s output alone** — the sum of `.text`
  COMDAT raw-data lengths in the **reference** obj. The port cannot shrink it.
* **A wrong emit LOWERS the score.** `FnByte::Differs` bytes are credited
  nowhere; emitting a wrong body scores exactly what refusing scores, which is
  the direction board #232's repair went.
* **A TU with no `.text` bytes returns `None`, never 100 %.** That is objdiff's
  `calc_fuzzy_match_percent` (`objdiff-core/src/bindings/report.rs:249-250`) and
  this project's most-repeated defect. The **must-fail mutation is a unit test**
  and was verified failing under the mutation (`Some((1,1))` for the empty case
  fails two tests).
* **Every ratio prints its denominator**, and `exact` — the oracle-graded floor
  under `accepted` — is printed beside it, the role `fnbyte-partial` plays for
  FBM.
* **It is an instrument and never a gate.** It licenses no emit and is in no
  accept/refuse path.

### 3.2 The control (#501), and the blind spot it found on its first run

    2 of 9 matched TUs at 100% · 5 with no `.text` denominator · 2 shortfall · 0 UNEXPLAINED

A `match` TU is byte-identical to `c2`'s obj, so its fraction **must** read
100 %. Without this the numerator could silently stop crediting a whole
`Selected` variant and every frontier row would go on printing a plausible small
percentage.

**It fired on the first run**, and the cause is structural and worth stating:
`TomCryptLicense.cpp` and `ZlibLicense.cpp` read **0/24**. Both are **factor E** —
a *whole-TU* recognizer emitted them, and the ranker's numerator is the
*per-function* path (`codegen::select_function`), which cannot answer for one.
**This is the factorization's own factor-D control one level along**, which
`STATUS.md` leaves red on purpose with its explanation beside it.

**The FRONTIER is unaffected in either direction** — it is `A∧B∧C ∧ ¬(D∨E)`, so
no factor-E TU can reach the ranking. The control classifies each shortfall and
publishes `bytefrac-control-unexplained`, which is the key that must stay **0**;
a unit test breaks the numerator on a non-E match and watches it fire.

## 4. The inter-function label stride — it needed no new home, and that is a better result

Board **#503**. The brief said `w-tu2`'s rule "wants a `crates/` home and a
fixture". **Half of that was already true**, and finding out is R6's miss.

`coff::label::plan_labels` charges **5 per framed function under `/Gy`** (4
packed) and **1 per leaf**, in `.text` order. `w-tu2`'s rule *is* that loop —
**measured from outside, through real `c2`, by somebody who did not read it**.
Its 36 cells are therefore an **out-of-sample confirmation of shipped code**,
which is a stronger claim than a new rule would have been.

What was missing was the pin, the mechanism and a fixture.

**The mechanism, which `w-tu2` could only observe:** `cur += f.label_lead` runs
**before** the function's own triple is taken, so the **source** function's
intra-function surcharge moves its own `$M` *and every later one* **by the same
amount** and **cancels out of any difference**. That is exactly why interior
control flow does not enter the stride, and it is three lines of shipped code.

**The pin:** `the_inter_function_label_stride_is_a_constant_and_the_source_lead_cancels`
walks **90 cells** — 15 arrangements of ≤ 3 intervening functions × the 6
intra-function surcharges `w-label` measured (`straight` 0 … `while` 5) — under
a printed count, and reproduces `mmio`'s own two gaps (5 and 10) out of sample.

**The fixture:** `wtu3_framed_leaves_framed.cpp`, the leaf-**between**-two-framed
cell nothing in the corpus had (`wunw_framed_pair` has no middle;
`wunw_two_leaves_framed` has no second frame). It is `Port=Match` at `/Ox` and
**byte-exact at the workload's own `/O1 /Oi /EHsc /GR`**, where real `c2` emits
`$M2584` then `$M2594` — **stride 10 predicted, 10 observed**. It is `mmio`'s own
second gap, reproduced standalone.

### 4.1 What is NOT claimed — #286/#482 stay open, and the boundary is asserted

Board **#504**. `w-tu2` said *"the probe's own interior control flow does not
enter it at all"*, which is exactly right and is **not** the same as "the stride
is blind to control flow". The cancellation is a property of the **source**
function only. With a framed function of lead 3 **between** two framed ones the
stride is `5 + 5 + 3 = 13`, **not** 10 — asserted, with an `assert_ne!(…, 10)`
beside it so no later reader can widen the claim by rereading a doc comment.

So the constant-stride form holds precisely when every function *between* the
two (and the destination) has lead 0 — the condition `w-tu2`'s 36 cells satisfied
and did not have to state. **A rung placing a later function's labels no longer
owes a new IL channel; a rung placing a control-flow-bearing function's own
labels still does.**

## 5. `mmio.cpp`, re-priced independently: **17**, and the mechanism that stops it

Board **#506**. Counted off the obj at the workload's flags before reading
`w-tu2`'s list, and it comes back at **17 by a different partition** — the
**fourth** consecutive cross-check of `w-dclass`'s reprice table to come back
dearer (`negate_test` 10 vs 4, `xboxmem` 15 vs 4, `mmio` 17 vs 5, `mmio` 17 vs 5
again).

**The decisive one is the first, and it is a CFG restructure rather than a
widening:**

1. **A materialized common epilogue.** All three framed bodies branch *forward*
   from ≥ 2 early-return sites (`li r3,5 ; b`, `li r3,11 ; b`) into a **shared
   epilogue block**, with the fall-through `li r3,0` merging into the same block.
   `Selected::Framed` emits prologue + body + epilogue as one straight-line run
   and has **no representation for a join**.

Then: a guard-chain lowering (`cmplwi cr6` + `bf 26` forward, fall-through arm
continues); a formal-to-register shuffle before the guards (`mr 11,3 ; mr 3,4`);
`std r31,-16(1)` / `ld r31,-16(1)`; the *decision* to hold a formal in `r31`
across a call; member load/store at a nonzero offset (`lwz 11,28(31)`,
`stw 11,32(31)`); `cmplw cr6` register-vs-register; a **third branch-bit regime**
`bf 24` (LT) beside `bf 26` (EQ); a **second CR field**, `cmplwi cr0` + `bf 2` in
`mmioClose`; a conditional store with a forward join; an **intra-TU REL24**
(`bl mmioFlush` — `.text #10` of this same obj, where every REL24 the port emits
goes external); an indirect call `lwz 11,8(31)` / `mtctr` / `bctrl`; three-argument
call setup; the intra-function label charge of §4.1; and the interleaved
`.text`/`.pdata` COMDAT order across 11 functions.

> ### One fact `w-tu2`'s list does not name, found by dumping rather than by reading it
>
> The three `.pdata` flag words are **`40 00 15 03`**, **`40 00 1b 04`** and
> **`40 00 1f 04`** — **three different words in one obj**, against the port's
> shipped `40 00 14 04`. The field encodes prologue length and saved-register
> count and must be **computed**, not carried. `w-tu2` correctly recorded that
> the port already *emits* `.pdata`; it does not emit *these*.

**So the ranker's top pick is unbuildable in this lane, the mechanism is the
materialized common epilogue, and the lane stops on it** — which is the brief's
instruction, and is why two of the last three lanes' honest zeros are why this
one has a working selector at all.

`??0CXboxHeap` is **80 B with no branch of any kind** — framed prologue, a run of

So the entire remaining distance is #401's F1–F4: a literal-valued store mixed
into a store run, a member's address as a stored value, a call after a store run,
**and the schedule**.

> `leaf_store.rs`'s four refuted allocation rules. A rule fitted on this TU's own

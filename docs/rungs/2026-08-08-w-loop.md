## 4. What was built

### 4.1 `encode_cmpw`, `encode_lwzx`, and `codegen::frontier_bytes` (#1395)

`codegen::frontier_bytes` rebuilds **all sixteen words** from this crate's
encoders and asserts them against the bytes real `c2` emitted. The split it
asserts, so the claim cannot rot into a stale comment:

```text
  14 of 16 words — encoders that ALREADY EXISTED
   2 of 16       — encode_cmpw (signed reg-reg compare) and encode_lwzx
                   (indexed load), one X-form expression each
   3 word SITES for those two encoders: 0x14, 0x24, 0x38
```

`lwzx` is pinned at **two** cells (`lwzx r10,r10,r9` and `lwzx r3,r11,r9`) so
`rD` and `rA` are separated by the pins and not only by the formula, and
`encode_cmpw(6,10,3) != encode_cmpwi(6,10,3)` is asserted — the defect a
single-cell pin would miss.

**Six mutation cells, each of which must diverge**: the rotation removed; the
back edge's CR **bit** wrong; its CR **field** wrong (board #188's own defect);
the exit block's rematerialization replaced by the semantically identical
`mr r3,r10`; the index scale wrong; `lwzx`'s `rA`/`rB` swapped. **A positive
control asserts the builder is untouched by all six**, and the whole suite was
verified non-vacuous by changing `lwzx`'s extended opcode from 23 to 24:
**4 of 7 tests go red and the 3 that do not depend on `lwzx` correctly stay
green.**

**This is not an emitter, and the module header says so first.** It is
`cfg(test)` only; nothing in `select.rs` reaches it; the port still returns
`NotImplemented`. **Reproducing bytes that were read off `c2`'s output is not
deriving them from IL**, and the gap between those two is the whole of §5.

### 5.1 The CTR encoder, declined with the measurement (#1397)

The brief names it as the build target and says to treat it as a real encoder
with an ordering claim. **It moves `Primes.cpp` by zero bytes**: there is no
`mtctr` and no `bdnz` in its 64 words, because the loop's bound is the
data-dependent sentinel `primes[i2] != 0` and the 2026-08-05 rung's **L2** result
is that `c2` emits CTR only for a **computable entry trip count** with no call in
the body. `Primes` has neither half.

CTR is on the frontier — `IPP_basicmath_xbox.cpp` has four, `EncryptXTEA.cpp`
one, `Pool.cpp` one — but never alone, and no `Selected` variant would call the
encoder. Building it would be an ungraded code path by construction (w-frame row
**F-c**), which is the same clause that keeps the long-branch expansion unbuilt.
**Declined, and the ordering claim behind it is not made either**, because a
claim with no graded caller is a hypothesis on the page.

**And ≥ 15 is itself the low reading of the reader half.** #1105's fifteen takes

## 6. The rotation, and the two things about it that were wrong on the board (#1399, #1400)

**#1105 calls it "a three-block rotated plan".** Measured it is **five** blocks —
entry, loop top, body, the test the rotation lands in, and two exits. The
rotation itself is an inequality between two offsets and `frontier_bytes` asserts
it as one: the entry `b` at `0x10` targets `TEST = 0x28`, which is strictly
*below* the `TOP = 0x14` the back edge returns to. **That inequality is the
rotation**, and it is precisely what a straight-line lowering cannot produce.

**The rematerialization is the mutation that matters most**, and it is registered
here for the next lane rather than left in a comment. At `0x34` `c2` recomputes
`slwi r11,r11,2 ; lwzx r3,r11,r9` over a value **already live in `r10`**. The
obvious lowering emits `mr r3,r10`, which is semantically identical, one word
shorter, and **wrong bytes**. Board **#1400**: on this row the optimization a
codegen lane would reach for by reflex is the defect.

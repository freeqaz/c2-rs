## What it admits, and what it refuses

**It admits nothing to the port.** No `crates/c2-core` behaviour changed, no
accept/refuse path was touched, no numerator moved. What landed is an
instrument, a relocation-site reader, and a report:

* `crates/c2-harness/src/gap/fndiff.rs` — a PPC **field decoder** under
  `CODEGEN_W6_COMPARE.md`'s round-trip rule, a word-granular **alignment**, and
  a per-symbol **diff signature**.
* `crates/c2-obj` — `ObjImage::text_comdat_reloc_sites`, the relocation
  *offsets* rather than the counts (#982).
* `c2rs gap --fnbyte-diff-jsonl PATH` — one JSON row per differing function.
* `scripts/fndiff_report.py` — the objdiff-style side-by-side rendering.
* `docs/DIFF_STRUCTURE.md` — the cluster table, the method, and the answer.

**What it refuses** is anything that would let a near-miss score. There is no
fuzzy percentage, no partial credit, and nothing on the page can be raised by
emitting a body c2 would not have written — every row it produces is attached to
a body the judge has **already** called wrong. The decoder refuses to name a
field it cannot re-encode: primary opcode `04` (VMX/VMX128) is `undecoded` by
construction rather than fitted to a generic form table.

**F4 fired** — the registered falsifier *"the modal substitution class is
`opcode`, i.e. a different lowering rather than a field-level defect"*. It is
5,173 of 5,189 words. The prereg called that a refutation of *"lower immediate
complexity"*; on the evidence it is the opposite. A field-level defect scattered
over 3,195 bodies would have been many small problems; **one wrong lowering
decision, taken 3,195 times, is one problem**, and §3 of the record names it.

The bias worth recording: **every prediction that assumed the port was
*nearly* right was wrong, and wrong in the same direction.** P3 and P5 both
imagined a port whose instruction stream resembles c2's and slips on a field or
an order. It does not resemble it at all — 94.3 % of these bodies disagree at
their first instruction.

## Found and not taken

Three specs, ranked by bodies per unit of new mechanism. All are **named and not
built** — this lane ships no emitter change, by brief.

1. **370 bodies, one shape** (#980). `port = li rN,0 ; b callee` against a c2
   body of exactly `blr`, zero relocations, 211 TUs. `elide.rs`'s mechanism E
   already produces this *outcome* for 1,516 bodies by a different *test*; these
   370 need E's fixpoint extended from *the callee's body is empty* to *the
   callee's body inlines to nothing*. **#950's hazard applies unchanged**: the
   relocation observable is not a sound test, because a self-recursive body reads
   as "nothing happened" and plainly is not.
2. **140 bodies, one template, one missing 7-word call** (#979). The only
   cluster where the port emits *less* than c2. `??$Obj@V…@@DataArray@@…`, all
   13w → 20w, all diverging at word 8, all missing the same seven words. Not an
   inlining question — a `seq` that emits two of three calls. The known answer is
   exact, which makes it the cheapest thing on this list to grade.
3. **3,050 bodies, the inline predicate** (#978). Needs
   `INLINE_PREDICATE.md`'s mechanism I, whose §1.2 lists six places the chain
   stops. Large; listed so its size sits next to the two that are not.

**And two things not to do with this page.** Cluster size is not conversion —
trap 8, and `w-empty`/`w-fix` are the precedent: 1,516 bodies closed, TU match
`10 → 10`. And the whole census covers **3,195 of 178,975** emitted functions;
the 130,573 refused have no bytes to diff and nothing here describes them
(#983, trap 0).

## The instrument found a defect in itself, and that is the rung's second result

The first run of the census reported **9.1 % of the mismatched words
`undecoded`**. All 470 were readable. `addi` (primary 14) and `lwz` (primary 32)
are both form `D` and share **no field names**, so a classifier that looked up
the other side's field by name fell through to `undecoded` on every
`addi`-vs-`lwz` pair. They are simply different instructions.

It is registered (#977) rather than quietly fixed for two reasons. It is the
**only** number on `DIFF_STRUCTURE.md` that the page's headline question depends
on — *do we understand the layout?* — and it was wrong in the **pessimistic**
direction, which is the rarer way round in this project and the reason one scan
was enough to catch it. And the fix generalizes: the primary opcode is compared
**before any field**, so `undecoded` now denotes exactly one thing — a form the
file does not model — instead of doubling as "the two words are laid out
differently".

## 2. C8 — what the read bought, and exactly where it stops (**#3717**, **#3718**)

Published at `docs/whitebox/ref/P_INLINE.md` §6.6.1.

**Closed:** `DAT_10c46318` has **exactly one reader in the whole image** — C8's
own `cmp` — and two writers, both inside `FUN_10b5e4cc`. It is above the raw
`.data` end, so it is zero at load, so that producer necessarily runs before the
inliner: a conclusion from the writer set rather than an assumption. `k` is `3`
in raw `.data`, so the ceiling is `0x10 << 3` = **128**, in instruction units.
`k` is never stored by any instruction; its *address* sits in an
option-descriptor record whose name resolves to **`-vol#`**.

**Where it stops, and this is the deliverable.** 128 instructions against
`INLINE_UNBOUNDED_BYTES = 64` (16 PPC words) is **8×**, and **4.4–4.7×** against
§2.1c's measured `(108,116]`. The prereg registered *"more than 2× apart under
every unit assumption I can state in advance"*. Two links are missing and
**neither is in the inliner band**:

1. the reduction of `[sym+0x50]` between the `.gl` decode at `0x10b9bf6c` and
   the test at `0x10b5fc8a` — §2.1b's `arith_012`/`mix_008` pair, identical
   `SIZE` 115, opposite verdicts;
2. the conversion of a count into emitted PPC bytes, which is the whole of
   lowering.

So **no read confined to `0x10b5b86d`–`0x10b62b00` can replace this fit.**
Adopting 128 would be an emit change priced against a number that does not mean
what the port's constant means — `INLINE-P` is frozen at 0.9678 on n=8,936 and
is a single threshold there, so any movement is a gate event and this lane
produced none.

## 3. C20 — the clause is fully read, and the port is not derived from it (**#3719**, **#3720**)

`P_INLINE.md` §6.6.2. The edge is at **`0x10b62402`**; `FUN_10b61ee1` has
exactly two callers. Read against the pass entry's call at **`0x10b6276e`**, all
six arguments resolve: `level` becomes `BYTE [site+0x18] + level`, and the
budget becomes **`*budget / remaining_sites`**.

> **The growth budget is divided evenly among the remaining call sites.** The
> divisor is traced through four frames to the **site collector's own
> out-parameter** (`lea edx,[ebp-0xc]` at `0x10b61f99`), decremented per site at
> `0x10b620c8`. At site *i* of *n*, the nested pass gets
> `remaining_budget / (n − i + 1)`.

Two further reads at the same call, neither adopted: stack 3/4 are one **64-bit
quota** that halves at `0x10b6204e`; and a **`__forceinline` callee is charged
nothing** for its nested expansion — `0x10b6240f` skips the local budget *and*
the global growth total, orthogonally to §2.2's 40-instruction exemption.

**And one correction to this page's own prose.** §5 says of C22's POGO discount
that *"the only site-count arithmetic in the image is this division"*. There is
a second, at `0x10b623ec`, on the **non-POGO** path, exercised by §6.4's 2,910
multi-site callees. C21–C23 stay `unexercisable`; only the exclusivity clause
falls.

**Why C20 nevertheless stays `fitted`.** `splice.rs`'s `S6-chain` has no level,
no budget, no site count and no division. It is correct on its admitted set
because that set has `n = 1` at every link — `S6`/`S2` admit only single-call
bodies and `S6-chain-open` requires the end to have none — so c2's division is
the identity there. **That is a soundness argument for a fit, not a derivation
of one**, and §6.1's ties break toward the weaker state.

> **The hazard this leaves behind, named.** `#1020` measured that c2 closes the
> chain, on 150 workload relocation witnesses. This lane says *why*, and the why
> **does not transfer**: the moment a lane widens `S2` to two call sites, `n = 2`,
> c2's divisor stops being 1, and the port has nothing to divide.

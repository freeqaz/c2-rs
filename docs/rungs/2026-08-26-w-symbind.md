## 1. RESULT

Decision 14 asked one question:

> **Is symbol binding a third fused layer, and is it separable the way the
> grammar layer was?** `w-decodereach` measured `grammar-not-admitted` =
> **4,001** bodies, all `:eof`, all symbol binding, refused at
> `shape_to_function` (`census.rs:957`) **downstream of `AdmissionPolicy`**.

**Yes it is a layer. No it is not separable the way the grammar layer was. And
it is not one layer — it is two, in a 42/58 split that nothing in the tree could
see.**

| | bodies | what it is |
|---|---:|---|
| **`symbind-fused`** | **1,697** | strict REFUSED, relaxed ADMITTED — **a NAME is the only thing between this body and the incumbent admission predicate** |
| **`symbind-residue-grammar`** | **2,304** | refused on BOTH sides — **binding that is not name resolution**, which the existing seam does not reach |
| | **4,001** | `symbind-grammar-disagree` **0** against `gap::decode`'s own figure |

The instrument is `crates/c2-harness/src/gap/symbind.rs`: it pairs the **strict**
census's verdict against **`c2_il::Relax` level 1**'s, row by row, positionally,
over one segmentation. The relaxation's entire content (`census.rs:830–845`) is
*supply `$blind$callee` / `$blind$data` where `.gl` had no name and change
nothing else* — no grammar widens, no operand vocabulary widens, no byte of the
body is re-read. So the fused cell is a **measurement of one specific claim**
and not a modelling choice.

It is the **fifth gradient** and adopts `docs/FUNCTION_BYTE_MATCH.md` §0
verbatim: never in `scripts/gate.sh`, its own block under its own disclaimer,
`symbind-*` keys, **licenses no emit**, `NO-RESULT` over an empty scan.

## 3. IS THE REFUSAL ABOUT THE BODY, OR ABOUT THE PROGRAM?

Decision 14 said this distinction *"decides whether the layer is separable at
all"*. The answer is in three parts and only the middle one is an inference.

**(a) The fused 1,697 — program, but the instrument's own definition says so, so
this half is not independent evidence.** `fused` *is* "supplying a name flips
the verdict", so of course 100 % of it is name-availability. The prereg's P9
(*"≥ 90 % of fused is program-side"*) is therefore **vacuous as written** and is
graded that way in §6 rather than counted as a hit.

**(b) The independent evidence is the licence pair, and it is a READ.** Two TUs,
**byte-identical `.ex` files**, two different symbind keys, because one object is
`static` and its `.gl` record carries the `$`-introduced `0x24` separator that
`NAME_SEPARATORS` deliberately does not admit (`gl.rs:1806`, and `24` is
excluded on purpose — admitting it re-binds every callee token in the corpus,
and `w-fence163`'s `25` paid that global re-bind risk with a whole-workload
measurement first; that is the bar). **A body whose verdict changes when its
surroundings change is refused for a property of its surroundings.**

**(c) The residue 2,304 — body-ish, and the tree cannot yet say more.** 2,280 of
them are `resolve_bss_def` refusing a *defined object* in this TU. That is a
property of the TU (the object's COMDAT/initializer/linkage), not of the body's
bytes — so it is *also* program-side — but it is **not** name-availability and
no relaxation this tree has can flip it. **The useful split is therefore not
body-vs-program at all. It is NAME binding vs OBJECT binding**, and that is the
split the seam in §4 is built around.

## 4. IS IT SEPARABLE THE WAY THE GRAMMAR LAYER WAS? — NO (board #3600)

`w-unfuse`'s split worked for three reasons. None of the three holds here.

### 4.1 Decode is TOTAL. Binding is not.

`Decoded::of` is *defined for every segment and never refuses*; that totality is
what let `AdmissionPolicy` be a predicate **over** it. Binding is a function of
`(shape, four resolvers, THIS TU's .gl)` and it fails 42 different ways.

Counted from source, `bundle.rs:858`, 884 lines, 44 `BodyShape` arms:

| route family | count | on the `Relax` path? |
|---|---:|---|
| `resolve(tok)?` — callee **name** | **33** | yes |
| `slot_args_resolved(.., resolve_data)?` — data **names** | 1 | yes |
| `resolve_data_def(tok)?` — a whole DEFINED `.data` object | 1 | **no** |
| `resolve_bss_def(tok)?` — a whole DEFINED `.bss` object | 1 | **no** |
| structural — 2 × `return None`, `.ok()?`, `params.iter().position()?`, and 2 nested `arm(pair.*)?` calls that propagate an inner `resolve` | 6 | (2 of the 6 propagate) |
| | **42** | |

…rendered into **15 published keys**: 3 from the `sym_fail` probe, 4
`CALLEE_UNRESOLVED_*`, 2 no-carrier keys, 1 object key, 5 from
`bind_run_ops`'s error set — through a `match label` whose last arm is `_ =>`.
42 → 15 → a default arm is exactly how #3598's misnaming happens.

### 4.2 The mechanism was VISIBILITY, and here the second route already exists

`w-unfuse`'s invariant does not rest on a comment: `parse_segment_detail` is
`pub(in crate::func::body)`, so *"there is one decode, one admission predicate,
and no second route to either"*.

The binding layer already has its second route, twice, and it is not an
accident — it is forced by the signature:

* `sym_fail` (`census.rs:1413`) **re-runs `resolve_data`/`resolve`** before the
  call, to recover *which* resolution failed;
* `bind_refusal_key` (`body/mod.rs:1427`) **re-runs `bind_run_ops`** before the
  call, to recover *why* the carrier refused.

Both are computed *before* `shape_to_function` because it takes the shape **by
value**. They are literally the second derivations of one decision that
`w-unfuse` abolished at the grammar layer, and they exist because the current
signature returns `Option` — a value with no room for a reason.

### 4.3 The refusal-reason constraint is UPSTREAM, and it is `w-atend`'s question

`#3556`: `AdmissionPolicy` has one variant because **the admission layer cannot
own a refusal reason** — a `Block` says *where the READ stopped*, and a policy
refusing a body the decode read whole has no such point; minting one publishes a
`:eof` census key no scan can reach.

**Every one of the 4,001 is `:eof` for exactly that reason.** So is every
refusal a `Bound` layer would raise. **The binding layer cannot own a key until
`w-atend`'s answer exists**, and separating it first would mint 5–15 more
unreachable `:eof` keys with the same defect. That is a hard dependency between
two lanes of this wave and neither brief named it.

### 4.4 The seam, specified — and NOT built

```
Decoded  --(ResolutionPolicy)-->  Bound  --(AdmissionPolicy)-->  IlFunction
```

* `Bound<'a>` — total over `(Decoded, &Resolvers)`, holding
  `Result<IlFunction, BindFailure>`.
* `BindFailure` — a **closed enum whose two families are named apart**:
  `Name { side: Callee | Data, .. }` and `Object { which: DataDef | BssDef, .. }`.
  §1 result 1 is the reason: folding them is what makes the layer look like one
  thing and lets a 2,280-body row wear a callee's name.
* `ResolutionPolicy::{ Strict, PlaceholderOnMiss }` — `Relax::sym_names`
  promoted from a **bypass** to a named policy beside `AdmissionPolicy`, with
  `Strict` reproducing today byte for byte. It is a bypass today in the precise
  sense that it owns no verdict, substitutes a name that must never bind, and
  forces everything downstream to grade BYTES ONLY.
* `sym_fail` and `bind_refusal_key` **delete**. The reason comes from the
  failure value.
* The resolvers become `pub(in crate::func)` so no second route exists. The
  mechanism, not the comment.

**Price, calibrated against the only comparable this repo has done.**
`w-unfuse` touched one entry point and one predicate and graded required-zero on
the 21-row gate identity diff plus a per-symbol `row_dump` diff over 2,417,794
rows (`#3558`). This touches **all 44 arms** and needs the same grade.
**Estimate 2–4× `w-unfuse`**, and it should be **split in two**:

* **(a) the name/object split of the refusal reason** — no new type, no new
  layer, and it is what stops #3598's row lying. Cheap, and independently
  worth doing.
* **(b) the `Bound` type** — blocked on `w-atend`, per §4.3.

**This lane built neither.** Decision 14 is explicit that the seam is a
construct rung and a separate funding decision.

## 5. THE CONTROLS, AND BOTH ARMS OF THE KNOB

Five per-TU controls, every one filed positively, every one **watched failing**
on deliberately broken input in a unit test rather than argued to be sound
(`gap/symbind.rs` tests, 10 of them, all green):

| control | known answer | reading | its executed mutation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `symbind-monotonicity-broken` | 0 | **0** | `the_monotonicity_control_fires_on_a_narrowing_relaxation` — a relaxation that *substitutes* rather than supplies |
| `symbind-partition-broken` | 0 | **0** | `the_partition_control_fires_when_a_bucket_stops_being_written` |
| `symbind-population-broken` | 0 | **0** | compares against `gap::scan`'s sibling walk (`fn_in_class + Σ fn_blockers`), never a subtraction of two published totals (`#1464`) |
| `symbind-census-desync` | 0 | **0** | `relaxed.len() != census.len()` or `index` mismatch — fails closed for the whole TU (`#918`) |
| `symbind-placeholder-none` | 0 | **0** | a fused row with no `$blind$*` — a **FINDING**, not an alarm |
| `symbind-grammar-disagree` | 0 | **0** | `fused-grammar + residue-grammar` vs `gap::decode`'s `grammar-not-admitted`: two maps, two modules (`#3288`) |

**`symbind-monotonicity-broken` is not true by construction** — it is the claim
that supplying a name only ever *widens*, and `cell_of` takes two independent
booleans so all four cells of the 2×2 have names. That is deliberately unlike
`w-decodereach`'s `reached_shape` vs `is_admitted` pairing, which **could not
fail** because one is *defined* as the other (`#3582`, and `#3565` before it).

### 5.1 Both halves of the knob, each a full workload scan (`#3564`'s discipline)

*A pin showing only that a parameter is inert is equally consistent with the
parameter being wired to nothing at all.*

| arm | `symbind-fused` | `symbind-residue` | `symbind-relax-level-*` |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `C2RS_BLIND_LEVEL=0` (`strict`) | **0** | **1,710,066** | `strict` 870 |
| default (`name-from-gl`) | **1,697** | 1,708,369 | `name-from-gl` 870 |

`1,710,066 = 2,417,794 − 707,728` **exactly**, which is the whole refused
population — the correct answer at a level where nothing relaxes. And
`Relax::level(0)` is pinned equal to the incumbent census by `c2_il`'s own
`strict_relax_is_the_incumbent_census`, so `fused = 0` there is **required**,
not observed. Negative half and positive half, one knob, two runs.

### 5.2 What this instrument CANNOT see, printed with its denominator

**The identity of the missing symbol.** `FnCensus` publishes no such field,
`Decoded` deliberately exposes no `shape()` accessor (its own doc says why), and
`crates/c2-il` is `w-atend`'s fence. What is reachable is the *relaxed*
`IlFunction`'s `callees()` and `data_syms`, so this module can say **which side**
was blind and **how many sites**, and cannot say which symbol. A placeholder
reachable only through a third field is invisible — which is exactly what
`symbind-placeholder-none` counts, and it reads 0 over 1,697 rows.

Named as owed, not smuggled (`#3470`, `#1002` — absence read as success).

## 1. The finding, and it is a reading of two lines rather than a measurement

Board **#746** fences two things. `w-slots` (**#3148**) settled which is which:
**fence B** is `label_slots -> None` in `c2-il`, **fence A** is the backward-
reference refusal in `c2-core`. `#3089` priced fence A at *"0 conversions and 0
bytes"*, was correct, stayed correct, and was made binding by `w-layout`'s own
success — **#3144**, *superseded by construction*.

This lane's finding is that fence A was never doing the job it cites, and that
it says so itself in three places:

* **The stated rule** (`labels.rs` module header, rule 2) quantifies over
  **bodies**: *"every body with a BACKWARD intra-section branch charges the
  compiler-label counter >= +1, 11 of 11"*.
* **The enforcing line** — `LabelMap::resolve`'s `if target <= r.at` — quantifies
  over **references routed through this map**.
* **Four shipped byte-exact classes live in the gap.** `ptr_walk_loop`,
  `ptr_walk_chain_loop`, `json_utf8_copy` and `xtea_encrypt_loop` each emit a
  backward `bc` through `reach::direct` / `encode_bc`; the map never sees one.
  The module's own 2026-08-08 correction records the fact — *"neither carrier
  routes through this map"* — and does not draw the consequence.
* **`labels.rs` reason 3 states where the rule belongs**, and it is not here:
  *"the precondition is TU-level and this map is per-body … that is where a loop
  rung's relaxation belongs; it is not here."*

So the enforcing line has **never** enforced its stated rule. What it did, from
`w-layout` onward, was block **7 of the 8** residual sites at a counter benefit
of **zero**.

This is `H1`, registered at p 0.80 before the first `crates/` change, with the
lane declining every arm if it were false. It is `w-ir-g` (#3114) and
`w-item-d` (#3119)'s rule — **read the sentence and the enforcing line apart** —
applied a third time, and it is the third time it has paid.

## 2. What replaces it: a closed admission, graded against c2-il's own gate

Invariant 4 becomes a **per-map admission fixed at construction**.
`LabelMap::new()` is unchanged and is `BackEdge::Refused`, so **all nine of item
A's clients keep exactly the map they had** — the mutant that says so is `M1`,
below, and it is green on purpose.

The only way past is `LabelMap::admitting_back_edges(ChargedClass)`, and
`ChargedClass` is **closed**: a `match` in the grading test is exhaustive, and
every variant must satisfy one of exactly two arms read off `c2_il`'s own
three-valued counter gate.

| arm | condition | why it is safe |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `label_slots(false) == Some(label_lead() + 1)` non-framed, or `Some(label_lead() + 4)` framed, and **`label_lead() >= 1`** | `coff::plan_labels` **already advances** the surcharge |
| 2 | `label_slots(false) == None` | `IlBundle::functions` refuses **every** TU in which this body's `$M` could be observed (board #742's Q2: 34 of 34 leaf-only TUs mint zero labels) |
| — | anything else, `label_lead() == 0` included | **refused by the grading test** — that is precisely the wrong-`$M` case invariant 4 was built for |

**No charge number is copied into `c2-core`.** The lead is read from
`IlFunction::label_lead`, which is the one place it lives — `#3148` records
three published label numbers that drifted because a second copy existed.

The three admitted classes and which arm each comes in on:

| class | `label_lead` | `label_slots` | arm |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `PtrWalkModLoop` | 2 | `Some(3)` | **1** — `plan_labels` charges it |
| `XteaEncryptLoop` | 4 | `Some(8)`, framed | **1** |
| `PtrWalkChainLoop` | — | **`None`** | **2** — the TU gate refuses every observable TU |

The `PtrWalkChainLoop` row is worth its own sentence: **its charge is
undetermined and stays undetermined.** Nothing in this lane claims a lead for
it, board #746's fence B is untouched and peer-held, and the TU-level gate is
exactly what makes routing its back edge safe. **Lifting fence A did not require
lifting fence B, and did not.**

## 3. The series — `#3147`'s lesson applied to the parameter that had not been varied

`w-fenceb` registered `PtrWalkModLoop`'s charge of **2** from **one** cell, and
`#3147` is the row that says why that is not enough: *"one cell's obj is a
measurement of one cell, and the parameter you did not vary is the one that
bites"*. The parameter here is **`n`, the number of admitted-class loop
functions in a TU**, and this lane varies it — `work/w-fencea/cells/`, real
`c2.dll` under wibo at `/O1 /GS- /c`, measured seed-cancelling
(`work/w-slots/lead.py`, `#3148`) so each TU's own `.gl` counter cancels inside
the TU:

```text
  n   cell            .gl counter   base $M   real $M   LEAD      control (n plain leaves)
  0   loops_0.cpp        2536        2548      2548      +0        ctl_plain_0  +0
  1   loops_1.cpp        2546        2562      2564      +2        ctl_plain_1  +0
  2   loops_2.cpp        2556        2576      2580      +4        ctl_plain_2  +0
  3   loops_3.cpp        2566        2590      2596      +6        ctl_plain_3  +0
                                                        ====
                                                         2n
```

**`2n`, and the four plain-leaf controls at the same function counts read `+0` on
every row** — so the instrument is not counting functions, it is reading the
loop's charge. All eight cells also **`match` end to end at `/O1`**, `n = 2` and
`n = 3` included, which is the first grading of the shipped
`+ 2 * ptr_walk_loop` term above one witness.

`S1` was registered at p 0.70 and **non-declining** — the obj is the judge and
the number is read, not predicted — for the reason `#3147` gives: a lane that
gates on its own prediction declines a convertible arm when it guesses low.

**No loop-kind rule is used anywhere here.** `#3127`'s hold-out (5 of 15; the
loop *kind* is a term no backward-branch feature vector contains) stands
untouched, and `kindrule.py`'s 23-of-23 — disqualified in its own docstring — was
not consulted.

### 4.1 The one that is worth reading: `ptr_walk_chain_loop`'s SAME regime

Registered as a decline because the emitter is 638 lines of variable arity. It
needed **no** new mechanism, and the reason is `w-layout`'s `P17` in reverse:
this class publishes **no** offsets at all — no `bl`, no pooled constant, no
prologue length — so the branches really were the whole problem, which is the
one lowering in the crate that is true of.

What it did need is a **block split**: in the SAME regime the preamble jumps
*into* the record form, which `S2` puts at slot `M + 1`, the body's last. So the
body is two blocks and the JUMPIN `b` names the second, in place of
`4 * (M + 2)` counted off the body's own length. `M6b` is the mutant that says
the split is load-bearing, and its separating control is `wvl_chain3` — the same
class in the **TWO** regime, green under it.

## 7. The decline: `json_utf8_copy` (`A5`), priced

Registered as a decline at p 0.65 and declined. The price, in the units:

* **What it costs to keep**: **2 of the 8** residual sites stay behind fence A,
  and this class stays the only loop lowering in the crate whose branch
  positions are its own. It converts **0** TUs either way: the class is already
  byte-exact and `c2rs perf`'s `/Ox` profile refuses it by design.
* **What it would cost to lift**: **76 words, ten branch sites, four block
  labels** (`Lelse`, `Lloop`, `Lwide`, `Lnul`) and a **frameless Class C
  prologue** whose `bl __savegprlr` site, `prolog_len` and `REFHI`/`REFLO` pair
  all come off the same `t.len()` — `w-layout`'s `P17` in full force, and the
  one class in this residue where it applies. That is a lane, not an arm.
* **The mechanism is not the blocker and this is the point.** Its admission is
  already spelled: `label_lead` **4**, `label_slots` `Some(5)`, arm 1. A lane
  taking it adds **one variant** to `ChargedClass` and one line to `ALL`, and
  the grading test tells it immediately whether the evidence holds. **The fence
  is no longer what stands in front of `json_utf8_copy`; its own ten sites
  are.**

### 9.1 A negative result worth the line

**`M1` is the negative result.** The obvious way to write this lane is to delete
the `target <= r.at` arm, run the gate, watch it stay green, and call the fence
disproved. It *does* stay green — twelve real-obj oracles, every one `match`.
That is not evidence the refusal was unnecessary; it is evidence that **no
instrument in this repo can grade a rule about classes that do not exist yet**.
The same green would have been produced by a lane that deleted the rule outright
and by one that replaced it with the closed admission, and only `M8` and `M9`
tell those two apart. A construct rung whose whole deliverable is a fence has to
be graded by something other than the objs, and here that something is the
compiler.

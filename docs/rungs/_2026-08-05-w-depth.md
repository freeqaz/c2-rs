## 0. One line

I was sent to build the selector seven lanes did not have, rank the frontier by
parse-chain depth, and score the ranking against the one conversion this project
has. **The instrument works and is validated on 7 of 7 controlled cells. The
ranking is refuted.** Run on master as it stood before `w-tu1`, chain depth puts
`xboxmem.cpp` — the TU that converted — at **D = 7, 15th of 19**, in the bottom
five, while the byte-fraction ranker put the same TU **1st of 19**. Depth does
not select.

**What depth *does* do is name the constructs.** `xboxmem`'s measured chain
contains `op:09` (shl), `op:0C` (bit-or) and `op:20` (cmp-ne) — which is
*exactly* `w-tu1`'s W43 class, `((P != 0) << SH) | C`. The chain's **content**
named the conversion; its **cardinality** ranked it 15th. Those are two different
claims and only the first survives.

## 3. The instrument, and the premise of the brief that is REFUTED

### 3.1 `C2RS_SINK_OFF_ADD_ARG` is not a sink — board **#661**

The brief says of the three existing sinks: *"They are measurement-only — they
push no `IlOp` and poison any walk that reaches the end, so acceptance cannot
move."* **True of two, false of the third.** `expr.rs`'s `0x27` arm under
`off_add_sink_enabled()` ends `ops.push(IlOp::Add);` and there is no
`saw_off_add_sink` flag and no poison arm for it. It is a real widening behind an
environment variable — which is why board **#403** records the tree going to
*16 targets / 754 passed / 2 failed* under it.

**This was registered in the prereg before any measurement**, because it decides
the method: board **#622**'s `0x27 → 0x32` was measured *through* that variable,
so it is a successor under a parser that also accepts differently. §3.4
re-derives it under the poison.

### 3.2 What `C2RS_SINK_CHAIN` is — board **#660**

A data-driven generalisation of `C2RS_SINK_REL` and `C2RS_SINK_BRANCH` from two
fixed opcode families to a set named at runtime. It keeps every property those
two have, and the properties are the design:

* **no `IlOp` from any arm, ever**;
* **`expr-chain-sink-poison`** at the end of any walk that used one — decoding is
  not accepting, so the sink cannot move one obj byte;
* an opcode whose width this tree has not pinned refuses under
  **`expr-chain-noform-0xNN`** rather than guessing. `chain_skip_form` carries a
  **provenance row per pin** and 24 pins; everything else is a refusal. Nothing
  is inferred from an opcode's numeric neighbours — `expr_opcode_name` once
  guessed the relationals that way and got three of six wrong, and a wrong
  *width* is worse than a wrong *name*, because it desynchronises the stream and
  manufactures a fictitious successor.

`work/w-depth/chain.py` does the iteration: scan, read the blocker keys, add a
token for each, re-scan, to a declared bound of 30.

### 3.3 It is VALIDATED — 7 of 7 declared cells, including a falsification cell

`work/w-depth/probe/`, seven single-purpose TUs whose operator inventory is
declared **in the file, before the run**, at the workload's own
`/GR /O1 /Oi /EHsc`:

| cell | declared | measured | |
|---|---|---|---|
| `p0_add` — `a + b` | **negative control**: the port already emits this byte-exactly, so **0** | **0** | ✔ |
| `p1_eq` — `a == b` | `{op:1F}` | `{op:1F}` | ✔ |
| `p2_and` — `a & b` | `{op:0B}` | `{op:0B}` | ✔ |
| `p3_shr` — `a >> b` | `{op:0A}` | `{op:0A}` | ✔ |
| `p4_if` — `if (a) return 1; return 2;` | `{op:38}` + delimiters | `{op:38}` + `op:53` | ✔ |
| **`p5_mixed`** — the union of p1–p4 | **`{op:1F, op:0B, op:0A, op:38}` and nothing else** | **exactly that** | ✔ |
| `p6_store` — `s->b = v` | lower bound `{op:27, op:32}` | **0** — the port already accepts a member store (`disp-store-leaf`), so the cell is a second negative control rather than the positive one I wrote it as | ✔ / re-read |

**Both corrections below were found by a probe built to find them**, which is the
whole reason the grid has a cell whose prediction can fail.

### 3.4 The two corrections the probes forced — boards **#663**, **#664**

**#663 — the depth was over ONE EXPRESSION, not a body.** `p5_mixed` first
measured `{op:1F, op:38, op:0B}` — **one operator short, and short in the
direction that flatters the instrument**, because `a >> 1` lives in the *second*
return statement, past the `41` result annotation `parse_expr` halts on. A depth
that silently drops everything after the first `return` is a lower bound
advertised as a count. The fix is to consult the sink **before** the stop byte,
so `op:41` walks through it and the chain runs to the function tail `4F 12` —
which `Line4F` refuses by construction, so the terminal is a byte the sink can
never eat. `p5` then reads exactly its declared union.

**#664 — the negative control measured a floor no body needs.** In whole-body
mode `p0_add`, a function the port emits **byte-exactly**, charged four steps:
`op:3A`, `op:54`, `op:29`, `op:4F` — the return plumbing. So DEPTH now subtracts
the statement/plumbing layer, which the real parser already consumes in
`eat_scopes`, `eat_return_plumbing` and `control_flow::step`, and the raw chain
is published beside it.

> **The subtraction has a stated blind spot and it bites at the head.** `3A` and
> `29` in the return plumbing are free; the **same two bytes at a loop back-edge
> or an if/else join are real control flow**, and this instrument cannot tell
> them apart. `src/system/math/Primes.cpp` — depth's head — is
> `NextHashPrime`, a **`for` loop with an early return**, so its `3A`/`29` are a
> back-edge and a loop-top label, not plumbing. Its honest depth is the `D+cf`
> column, **4**, and the CFG behind it is a shape the port has no representation
> for at all. **Depth's head is an artifact of depth's own blind spot**, and §7
> declines it rather than shipping it.

**#662 — #622 re-derived.** Under the poisoning sink, `xboxheap`'s chain opens
`op:27 → op:32`. Board #622's number is **confirmed**, measured this time by an
instrument that cannot move acceptance.

### 4.1 What depth got RIGHT, and it is not nothing — board **#666**

`xboxmem`'s chain is `op:1F` (cmp-eq), **`op:20` (cmp-ne)**, `op:38` (brfalse),
**`op:09` (shl)**, **`op:0C` (bit-or)**, `op:26`, `op:BD`. `w-tu1`'s W43 class is
`((P != 0) << SH) | C` — a **cmp-ne**, a **shift** and an **or**. The three bytes
the conversion needed are in the chain, named, and no other frontier TU's chain
carries `op:09` or `op:0C` at all.

> **So the chain is a correct construct inventory and a wrong cost model.** It
> answers *"which constructs does this TU need"* — which is what a lane about to
> build codegen actually wants — and it does **not** answer *"which TU is
> cheapest"*, because the number of constructs is not their price. `w-tu1`
> shipped **two** fixture classes and moved a TU whose inventory has seven
> entries; `Primes` has two entries and one of them is a loop.

## 6. Board **#668** — no frontier chain terminates, so every depth is a LOWER BOUND

**0 of 18 CLEAR.** Every measurable chain ends at `expr-chain-noform-0xNN` on a
byte this tree has not pinned, at `bound = 30`:

| terminal | TUs | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `0xBD` | 5 | the **CALL token** — the chain reaches a function call |
| `0x35` | 3 | unidentified; occurs on three independent TUs, so it is a real recurring token and not noise |
| `0x00` | 2 | almost certainly a **desync**, and flagged as such rather than counted: `0x00` is in no opcode table in this tree |
| `0x05`, `0x1C` | 1 each | `1C` is `&&`, whose byte `mcall` records **no capture has ever shown** |

Refusing rather than guessing is what makes the bound honest — and it is also
what stops the chain. I did **not** pin `0xBD`: walking a sink through a call
region is where desync risk is highest, and the `0x00` terminals show the failure
mode already exists at depth 7. The published depths are therefore floors, and
**the ranking's refutation is robust to that**: `xboxmem`'s floor of 7 already
exceeds `mmio`'s and `xboxheap`'s floors, and raising floors can only move it
further down.

### 6.1 Board **#669** — five merges of codegen work moved ZERO chain steps

The 18 shared TUs' chains at `6dcb3f4` and at `dad6257` are **byte-identical**,
across `w-tu1`, `w-tu2`, `w-tu3`, `w-sym`, `w-frame2` and `w-wire` — six merges,
one TU converted, **+716 emitted functions**, and not one frontier chain step
closed. Consistent with #150's eight confirmations and with the FRONTIER holding
at 18–19 throughout; recorded because it is the first time it has been measured
*per TU* rather than inferred from the aggregate.

## 7. The head, and I decline to name one

Depth's head among measurable TUs is **`src/system/math/Primes.cpp`, D = 2,
chain `{op:26, op:35}`** — and §3.4 shows the 2 is an artifact of the scaffold
subtraction. `NextHashPrime` is a `for` loop with an early return over a
62-element static table; its `3A`/`29` are a back-edge and a loop-top label, so
its honest figure is `D+cf = 4`, and `0x35` is unidentified.

| unit | head | agrees with depth? |
|---|---|---|
| blocked-function count (#198's complaint) | nine-way tie at 1 | — |
| `.text` byte fraction (#500) | `mmio.cpp`, 16.8 % | **no** |
| REMAIN bytes (#505) | `Primes.cpp`, 64 B | **yes** |
| **chain depth (#660)** | `Primes.cpp`, D = 2 (honest 4) | — |

**Two independent units now converge on `Primes.cpp` and I am still not naming
it**, for the reason `w-tu3` printed two units and refused to pick: #269 and
#465 were each a confident answer to *"which unit"* and each was wrong in a
different direction, and this lane has just added a **third** unit that is
demonstrably wrong on the only outcome data that exists. A convergence between a
byte count and a construct count, on a corpus of **two** outcomes, is not
evidence — and the convergent pick is a **loop**, which is the one shape the
board has repeatedly declined (#506's materialized common epilogue, #507).

> **What this lane hands the next one is not a target. It is a table of construct
> inventories (§4) and the finding that the count over them does not price
> them.** If a lane wants a cheap TU, the inventory says `xboxheap` (5:
> `27, 32, 26, 99, BD`) and `undname` (5: `20, 38, 26, 99, BD`) are the shortest
> *terminated-at-a-call* chains — and both are already declined at the emitter.
> The honest reading of §4 is that the frontier has no cheap member left, which
> is `w-tu3`'s finding reached by a second road.

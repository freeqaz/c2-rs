## 3. The instrument, and the premise of the brief that is REFUTED

### 3.1 `C2RS_SINK_OFF_ADD_ARG` is not a sink — board **#661**

The brief says of the three existing sinks: *"They are measurement-only — they
push no `IlOp` and poison any walk that reaches the end, so acceptance cannot
move."* **True of two, false of the third.** `expr.rs`'s `0x27` arm under
`off_add_sink_enabled()` ends `ops.push(IlOp::Add);` and there is no
`saw_off_add_sink` flag and no poison arm for it. It is a real widening behind an
environment variable — which is why board **#403** records the tree going to
*16 targets / 754 passed / 2 failed* under it.

**This was registered in the prereg before any measurement**, because it decides
the method: board **#622**'s `0x27 → 0x32` was measured *through* that variable,
so it is a successor under a parser that also accepts differently. §3.4
re-derives it under the poison.

### 3.2 What `C2RS_SINK_CHAIN` is — board **#660**

A data-driven generalisation of `C2RS_SINK_REL` and `C2RS_SINK_BRANCH` from two
fixed opcode families to a set named at runtime. It keeps every property those
two have, and the properties are the design:

* **no `IlOp` from any arm, ever**;
* **`expr-chain-sink-poison`** at the end of any walk that used one — decoding is
  not accepting, so the sink cannot move one obj byte;
* an opcode whose width this tree has not pinned refuses under
  **`expr-chain-noform-0xNN`** rather than guessing. `chain_skip_form` carries a
  **provenance row per pin** — **41 opcodes across 25 declared forms**; everything else is a refusal. Nothing
  is inferred from an opcode's numeric neighbours — `expr_opcode_name` once
  guessed the relationals that way and got three of six wrong, and a wrong
  *width* is worse than a wrong *name*, because it desynchronises the stream and
  manufactures a fictitious successor.

`work/w-depth/chain.py` does the iteration: scan, read the blocker keys, add a
token for each, re-scan, to a declared bound of 30.

### 3.4 The two corrections the probes forced — boards **#663**, **#664**

**#663 — the depth was over ONE EXPRESSION, not a body.** `p5_mixed` first
measured `{op:1F, op:38, op:0B}` — **one operator short, and short in the
direction that flatters the instrument**, because `a >> 1` lives in the *second*
return statement, past the `41` result annotation `parse_expr` halts on. A depth
that silently drops everything after the first `return` is a lower bound
advertised as a count. The fix is to consult the sink **before** the stop byte,
so `op:41` walks through it and the chain runs to the function tail `4F 12` —
which `Line4F` refuses by construction, so the terminal is a byte the sink can
never eat. `p5` then reads exactly its declared union.

**#664 — the negative control measured a floor no body needs.** In whole-body
mode `p0_add`, a function the port emits **byte-exactly**, charged four steps:
`op:3A`, `op:54`, `op:29`, `op:4F` — the return plumbing. So DEPTH now subtracts
the statement/plumbing layer, which the real parser already consumes in
`eat_scopes`, `eat_return_plumbing` and `control_flow::step`, and the raw chain
is published beside it.

> **The subtraction has a stated blind spot and it bites at the head.** `3A` and
> `29` in the return plumbing are free; the **same two bytes at a loop back-edge
> or an if/else join are real control flow**, and this instrument cannot tell
> them apart. `src/system/math/Primes.cpp` — depth's head — is
> `NextHashPrime`, a **`for` loop with an early return**, so its `3A`/`29` are a
> back-edge and a loop-top label, not plumbing. Its honest depth is the `D+cf`
> column, **4**, and the CFG behind it is a shape the port has no representation
> for at all. **Depth's head is an artifact of depth's own blind spot**, and §7
> declines it rather than shipping it.

**#662 — #622 re-derived.** Under the poisoning sink, `xboxheap`'s chain opens
`op:27 → op:32`. Board #622's number is **confirmed**, measured this time by an
instrument that cannot move acceptance.

## 6. Board **#668** — 16 of 18 frontier chains do not terminate, so nearly every depth is a LOWER BOUND

**2 of 18 CLEAR** — `Primes.cpp` at 7 and `jsonwriter.cpp` at 18, and both only
after §6.2's pin. Every other measurable chain ends at `expr-chain-noform-0xNN`

| terminal | TUs | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `0xBD` | 5 | the **CALL token** — the chain reaches a function call |
| `0x00` | 2 | almost certainly a **desync**, and flagged as such rather than counted: `0x00` is in no opcode table in this tree |

rather than the argument.** Raising three floors moved `xboxmem` *up* four

### 6.2 `0x35` PINNED from a capture — and the pin moved three TUs, the head among them

`0x35` was a terminal on three independent frontier TUs, so it was a real
recurring token rather than noise, and one `c2rs census … --keep-il` of
`src/system/math/Primes.cpp` at the workload's own flags settles its **width**:

```text
NextHashPrime:  for (int i2 = 0; primes[i2] != 0; i2++)

  53 · 26 <i2> · 33 86 41 74 00 · 32 86 41 74 · 4B     i2 = 0
  3A <ec09> · 29 <ed09>                                jump to test, loop top
  26 <i2> · 33 86 41 74 01 · >35< 86 41 74 · 4B        i2++
  29 <ec09> · 26 …                                     the test
```

Designator, literal `1`, the opcode, a **3-byte int TYPE**, statement end. So the
form is `35 <TYPE>` — read off the stream, not inferred.

**It is deliberately not NAMED.** `0F` is `+=` on `mcall`'s own `x += 3` control
and `35` occupies the identical slot with the identical payload, so it is
somewhere in the increment/compound-assign family — but *which* member is not
decided by one witness, and `expr_opcode_name`'s own header states the rule: **a
hex bucket is a result, a wrong name is a lie that survives into the roadmap.**
The instrument needs the width, not the name.

| TU | before the pin | after |
|---|---:|---:|
| `system/math/Primes.cpp` | 2, EXIT | **7, CLEAR** — the first frontier chain ever to terminate |
| `xjson/jsonwriter.cpp` | 9, EXIT | **18, CLEAR** |
| `synth_xbox/IPP_basicmath_xbox.cpp` | 5, EXIT | **12**, EXIT at `0x11` |

**This is the single most useful thing in the lane for whoever reads it next**,
and not because of the pin: `Primes.cpp` was depth's head at 2 and is 7 with one
byte identified. **A depth measured over an incomplete pin table is not a
conservative estimate of depth — it is an estimate whose error is concentrated
exactly where the ranking is read.** Every number in §4 should be read with that
in mind, including the ones that did not move.

### 8.2 What the three new tests lock

1. **OFF by default**, no opcode defaults to sunk, and `chain_sink_step` claims
   no byte at all in that state — checked over the **whole `0..=255` range**. A
   default that drifted ON would be a parser that skips opcodes on the gate, and
   **#403** is the precedent for exactly that accident.
2. **The absences in `chain_skip_form` are decisions**: `1B`/`1C`, the switch
   family, `64`/`66`, and the bytes this lane measured as live frontier
   terminals — `00`, `05`, `BD` — all refuse rather than guess a width.
3. **The pinned widths are checked against transcribed capture bytes**, not
   against the table that produced them — including the one that must **fail**:
   `4F 01` is the line marker and `4F 12` is the function tail, and eating the
   tail would walk the instrument out of the body.

`chain_step_with` is a pure extraction so a test can drive an explicit
configuration: `chain_sink` is a `OnceLock` over a process-wide variable, and a
property checkable only by being alone in its process is a property that goes
unchecked.

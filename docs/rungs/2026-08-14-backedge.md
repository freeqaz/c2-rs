## 1. The question, and the one-sentence answer

Board **#3082** left a loop lane this inheritance: `LabelMap::resolve`
**invariant 4** refuses every backward intra-section branch because *"c2 charges
the compiler-label counter +1..+4 for a body with a backward intra-section
branch while `plan_labels` charges 0"*, and a loop lane **must price that
first**.

> **Is the charge derivable from the IL the port can see?**
>
> **Yes — it is a function of the IL's backward-branch structure, computable
> from the same statement stream the census already walks, and no new IL
> channel is owed; but the exact integer is not yet predicted by any rule that
> survives a hold-out — the first-order term is (26/28 fit, 9/13 held out,
> 36/36 on the sub-class where the residual shapes are excluded), the ±1
> residual on `break`/`continue`/source-`goto` shapes is not.**

`LABEL_COUNTER.md` §4.1 had closed two routes **by measurement** — not the
emitted obj (closed at the *byte* level by §4.2.2's identical-bytes triple) and
not the `.gl` seed — and left one sentence standing:

> *"A per-function `.ex` field is the only unexamined channel, and it is open …
> The port cannot model the control-flow surcharge from anything it currently
> reads. **Any rung that wants a charging shape owes a new IL channel first.**"*

**That debt is discharged, and the answer is that no new channel was needed.**
The charge is not in a *field*; it is in the **statement stream**, in the
`29`/`38`/`39`/`3A` vocabulary `crates/c2-il/src/func/body/mod.rs`'s
`cflow_opcode_name` already names and the census already counts.

## 2. The instrument, and the control on the reader

`work/w-backedge/labelil.py`. Per cell, from **one** source file:

* **the charge**, seed-free, through `scripts/gt_label_stride.py` **as a
  module** — the same anchor walk that produced `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §1, not a
  copy. `charge = stride − minted`, which is §4's `stride − base − (minted − 5)`
  for both frame classes; the column a re-derivation drops is printed.
* **the IL features**, from the `.sy` and `.ex` of that same `.cpp`.

**The control is that the three identical anchors must read identical `.sy`
label-declaration counts.** It fired, and it earned its place three times:

| what the control caught | why it mattered |
|---|---|
| the `.sy` block-open tail is `1F 00 <b> 01`, not `1F 00 01 01` | `a-if` lost a block and refused **loudly** as `IL SHAPE` |
| `a-dowhile` declares its labels as kind **`02`**, not `03` | a reader admitting only `03` reported **zero** labels where `.ex` defines four — an absence indistinguishable from a measurement |
| a greedy declaration-run search reached into the previous block's body | it invented declarations for an anchor on **8 of 28** cells; the three-anchor equality caught every one |

Final run: **28 of 28 cells, 0 controls failed, anchors `0,0,0` on every row.**

## 3. The rule, frozen before the hold-out

Written into `PREREG.md` §3 and committed at `83943d3e`, **before grid2 was
compiled**:

```text
    charge  =  2 · bwd_uncond  +  1 · bwd_cond
```

`bwd_uncond` = `3A <tok>` references to an already-defined token; `bwd_cond` =
`38`/`39 <tok>` references to one. Epilogue excluded; the stream cut at the
`4F 12 47` tail.

**Read as a mechanism:** *every back edge charges, and it charges **2** when
the loop is entered through a separate exit test and **1** when the test is the
back edge itself.* Forward branches are free.

### What it got right on the fitting set (26 of 28)

* every `while`, `for`, `do/while`, nested and sequential-loop cell;
* **the count axes**: sequential loops 1/2/3 and nesting 1/2/3, both linear;
* **both frame classes** — `d-while-call`/`d-for-call`/`d-dowhile-call` charge
  what their leaf twins charge, which is §4.2.1's frame-class independence
  re-measured through a different instrument;
* **both branch senses** (`f-while-neg`, `f-dowhile-neg`);
* **forward branches are free at any arity** — `e-if2`/`e-if3`/`e-if4` charge
  **0** at 2, 3 and 4 forward-only labels. This is new: §4 records `if` at +0
  from a single-`if` probe, and "+0 at arity 1" and "+0 at every arity" are
  different claims;
* the **size control**: `g-stmt1` vs `g-stmt8` — eight times the statements,
  charge 0 → 0. The charge is not a code-volume artifact, which is #2341's
  control reproduced independently.

### The two misses, both ±1, both named

| cell | R1 | actual | what it is |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `a-goto-back` | 2 | **1** | a **bottom**-tested backward `3A` to a *source* label |
| `a-forever` | 2 | **3** | `for(;;)`+`break` — no exit test, and the `break` is an unconditional forward `3A` to a label defined after the back edge |

`a-goto-back` and `a-while` have **identical** `(defs, bwd_t, bwd_uncond,
bwd_cond, fwd_t)` and charge **1 and 2**. They are separated only by the order
of the exit test against the body — and, as it happens, by their `.sy`
declaration **kinds** (`2:1,3:1` vs `3:2`), which is why claim **C4** survived
grid1 (§5).

## 4. The hold-out — and it did the job a hold-out exists to do

grid2, 13 cells, sha256 `e1e2a5a2…` frozen at the prereg commit, **compiled
only after §3 was written**. `R1` scores **9 of 13**. `PREREG.md` §2's **C5**
registered ≥ 11 of 13 and is **MISSED**.

| cell | R1 | actual | the residual |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `h-continue` | 4 | **2** | a `continue` is a **second backward `3A` to the SAME target**. Counting *references* double-charges it; counting *targets* would not. **No grid1 cell has two backward references to one target** — the fitting set was structurally incapable of separating per-reference from per-target, and the hold-out separated them. |
| `h-break-nested` | 4 | **5** | `break`, the `a-forever` residual again |
| `h-while-brk-cont` | 4 | **5** | `break` **and** `continue` |
| `h-tern-loop` | 2 | **4** | **orthogonal and already published**: `a>3?a:1` materialises a signed relational, which `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §4 charges **+2**. 2 + 2 = 4 exactly. |

Two hold-out cells are worth naming as **positive** results because they are
the shapes a back-edge rule is most likely to get wrong by accident:
`h-switch` (a four-arm `switch`) and `h-goto-fwd` (a forward `goto`) both
charge **0**, predicted 0.

### The registered falsifier fired — on the hold-out and not before

`PREREG.md` §5: *"two grid1 cells with identical whole IL feature vectors and
different charges → **C4** fails."* Over grid1 alone there is **no** such pair.
Over grid1 ∪ grid2 there are **three**, all the same cell: `a-while`,
`d-while-call` and `f-while-neg` share a complete recorded feature vector with
`h-tern-loop` and charge **2** against **4**.

> **So the label-feature vector is incomplete, and the missing term is a
> documented non-control-flow surcharge that lives in the same IL.** C4 is
> **falsified**, and it took the hold-out to do it. The fitting set said the
> vector was sufficient; it was not.

The under-determination has a size: over all 41 cells there are **47 pairs**
that share `(bwd_uncond, bwd_cond)` and differ in charge — printed rather than
recalled, because "R1 fits 26/28" without that number reads as a much stronger
claim than it is.

## 5. The control that could go red, and did

`work/w-backedge/mchan.py`, registered in `PREREG.md` §4 before the first
probe. A stride is a **difference** and a constant charge error cancels out of
it, so the control is not a stride: it is board **#747**'s constructed TU —
a **loop leaf followed by a framed function** — where the charge lands on the
framed function's `$M`/`$T`, i.e. on **six bytes of the reference obj's symbol
table**. Everything is scored against those bytes.

```text
  M0  (R1)                GREEN on  9 of 11 constructed cells
  M1  (charge + 1)        red  on 10 of 11 DISCRIMINATING cells
  M2  (charge − 1)        red  on 10 of 11 DISCRIMINATING cells
  M3  (charge = 0)        red  on  9 of  9 DISCRIMINATING cells
```

* **The discriminating count is printed for every mutant**, so a vacuous
  control is a loud failure. It is 11, 11 and 9 — not 0.
* **M0's two reds are `a-forever` and `a-goto-back`** — the same two cells R1
  misses in the stride table, now confirmed as wrong *bytes*. The model's error
  is visible in the obj, which is `w-ir-e` mutant 1's standard.
* **M3 is what `coff::plan_labels` charges today.** It is red on 9 of 9 cells
  that have a charge and correctly **GREEN** on the two that do not
  (`a-none`, `a-if`) — which is what proves M3's redness is the charge and not
  a broken predictor.

## 6. The price, two-sided — and there are TWO fences, not one

`CLAUDE.md` requires a fence be priced two-sided before it ships, and #3062's
standing `fence-blocks-exact` counter reads `sole` 0 / `exact` 0 across all 23
causes, so the price is argued from **constructed cells**.

The lane's sharpest structural finding is that #3082 speaks of one fence where
the tree has **two**, and they have different prices.

### Fence A — `codegen::labels::LabelMap::resolve` invariant 4 (per body)

| side | price |
|---|---|
| **cost of keeping** | **0 conversions and 0 bytes.** No caller routes a back edge through the map: `ptr_walk_loop` and `ptr_walk_chain_loop` both emit a backward `bc` and compute the displacement directly (#1394 reason 2, still true). Its real cost is *expressive* — #3082's item **A** client list, item **D**, item **G** — not a goal unit. |
| **wrong-emit risk removed** | **0 today**, for the same reason: a refusal with no caller removes no risk either. It is w-frame row **F-c** on both sides. |

### Fence B — `IlFunction::label_slots` returning `None` for the loop shape (per TU, board #746)

This is the fence that is actually load-bearing, and #3082 does not name it.

| side | price |
|---|---|
| **cost of keeping** | **≥ 1 tracked fixture TU**: `fixtures/cpp/whash_loop_then_framed.cpp` is refused whole — and **R1 predicts its `$M` exactly**. Real `$M2564`; R1 `$M2564` GREEN; `plan_labels`' charge-0 `$M2562` RED; the true lead is **2**. (`work/w-backedge/mfix.py`, post-hoc — this source is in neither frozen grid.) |
| **wrong-emit risk removed** | **9 of 9 constructed cells** would carry a wrong `$M` if it were lifted with no model (M3). With R1 it is **2 of 11 wrong** — and a wrong emit scores strictly below the refusal it replaces, so R1 *as stated* does not license the lift. |
| **the resolution** | The gate is already **three-valued**. R1 restricted to the sub-class that excludes `break`, `continue`, a backward source `goto` and a materialised relational is **36 of 36** across both grids plus the shipped fixture. `label_slots` returning `Some(charge)` on that sub-class and `None` outside it is the priced, safe move — and it needs no new constant and no new IL channel. |

### And a number in the tree is wrong

`fixtures/cpp/whash_loop_then_framed.cpp`'s header says *"`?HashString`'s own
pointer-walk shape charges **+3**"* and *"`?z9`'s triple would come out **three**
low"*; `IlFunction::label_slots`' doc comment says *"`Sort.cpp`'s own
pointer-walk shape **+3**"*. **That fixture's own obj says the lead is 2**, so
it would come out *two* low. The **refusal is still correct** — 2 is not the 1
a leaf is charged — but the cited magnitude is one high, in the direction that
makes the fence look more expensive to lift than it is. Settled against the
oracle, not by reading the code; same shape as today's `w-dbgassert`.

## 7. Board #3082 — NARROWED. What remains

**Narrowed, in four specific ways.** Its *ground* does not survive as written;
its *conclusion* does, on a smaller basis.

1. **"The charge is not derivable" is refuted.** It is in the IL, in the
   opcode vocabulary the port already decodes, and §4.1's *"any rung that wants
   a charging shape owes a new IL channel first"* is **discharged with no new
   channel**.
2. **"+1..+4, four magnitudes and no rule" is refuted as a description.** The
   range is not unstructured: it is **2 per top-tested back edge and 1 per
   bottom-tested one**, linear in both the sequential count and the nesting
   depth, free for forward branches at any arity, and independent of frame
   class and of code volume.
3. **The blocker is fence B, not fence A.** Fence A costs nothing to keep and
   removes nothing; #3082 prices the wrong one.
4. **What still blocks a lift is the residual, and it is small and named**:
   `break`, `continue`, a backward source `goto`, and one orthogonal published
   surcharge. Four shapes, all IL-detectable, all excludable.

**And it relocates again — say so plainly.** Even a perfect charge converts
nothing on today's frontier. The scan at this tree reads **2** frontier TUs
(`keygen_xbox.cpp`, `wordwrap.cpp`), **0 of 2 label-free**, and their codegen
column reads **23 emitted functions, 21 behind the READER (91 %), 0 measurable,
0 refused, 2 already byte-exact**. The IL parser refuses before any codegen
question is asked. **The blocker moves from the label counter to the IL
reader** — the fourth relocation in one day, after `wb-live`→`dag.c`,
`w-dagorder`→a list scheduler, and `w-ir-e`→the label counter.

## 10. Found and not taken

Ranked, with the frame axis applied.

1. **The post-hoc candidate rule R1′, unscored and marked as such.** Two
   corrections fall straight out of the residuals and each has an IL-visible
   discriminator: count backward **targets**, not backward **references**
   (kills `h-continue`), and charge **1** rather than 2 for a back edge whose
   target is a *named* `.sy` declaration, i.e. a source `goto` (kills
   `a-goto-back`). **Both were fitted after the hold-out was opened, so neither
   is scored here**, and writing them into `label_slots` on this evidence would
   be `CFG_SHAPE.md` §3.5's declined fold model a third time. They are the next
   lane's *prereg*, not its result.
2. **`break` is the whole remaining residual, and it is +1.** `a-forever`,
   `h-break-nested` and `h-while-brk-cont` are all exactly +1 over R1 and all
   contain a `break`. One structural probe family (break count 1/2/3 × loop
   kind) would settle whether it is +1 per `break` or +1 per loop containing
   one. Cheap, and it is the last term.
3. **Fence B's lift, priced above and buildable today** on the sub-class where
   R1 is 36/36 — but it is a `coff/`-adjacent change and this lane deliberately
   changed nothing there. It converts `whash_loop_then_framed.cpp` and, on
   today's frontier, nothing else.
4. **Correct the two `+3`s** in `whash_loop_then_framed.cpp`'s header and
   `IlFunction::label_slots`' doc to the measured **2**. Left untouched here
   because both live beside `coff/` and the correction should land with the
   lane that acts on it, not from a lane that only measured it.
5. **The `.sy` declaration *kind* byte is an unread signal.** Kind `02` versus
   `03` tracks `do/while`/named-`goto` versus compiler-generated, and the kinds
   climb `3,4,5` across sequential *and* nested scopes, so it is a lexical
   scope ordinal. It separated `a-while` from `a-goto-back` when the `.ex`
   features could not. Nobody has decoded it.
6. **The `.ex` raw scan is corroboration, not evidence, and it should stop
   being either.** Every number here that a port would use is already decodable
   by `crates/c2-il`'s own body walker; a lane acting on this should read the
   features through that walker and delete the Python scan rather than port it.

## 1. The result

> ### **THE QUANTITY IS `size / alignment`, NOT A SIZE — which is why four separately-frozen size thresholds all missed.** `inline` iff `size / align <= T`, by **truncating** 64-bit division. That single fact recomputes all thirteen of w-memcpy's measured boundaries with nothing fitted, including the one no size rule can produce: at `align 8`, sizes **44 and 47 inline while 48 calls**, because `44/8 = 47/8 = 5` and `48/8 = 6`.

> ### **`T` IS 5 *OR* 10, AND THE 408 CELLS ALREADY PAID FOR COULD NOT HAVE SEEN IT.** GRID-M (232) and GRID-M2 (176) compiled at the dc3 workload's `/O1 /Oi` and **only** there — GRID-M2's own docstring lists optimization as axis D and `build_cells` never crosses it. GRID-W's 180 part-A cells cross five flag sets: the threshold is **5** at `/O1` and `/O2 /Os` and **10** at `/O2`, `/Ox` and `/O1 /Ot`. **W-OT scores 180 of 180**; the level rule scores 144 and "always 5" scores 126.

> ### **THE TWO CELLS THAT DECIDE IT ARE THE TWO NO `/O<n>` RULE CAN GET RIGHT.** `/O1 /Ot` inlines to 10 and `/O2 /Os` inlines to 5. The threshold follows the favor-size/favor-speed setting alone — one bit of an option word (`0x10b82392`), which `/O<n>` merely implies by default.

> ### **THE ELIMINATION IS ABOUT THE DESTINATION, AND w-memcpy's STATED RULE IS WRONG IN BOTH DIRECTIONS.** `E-DEADDST` (a non-escaping local destination never read afterwards) scores **36 of 36**; `E-LOCALS` (*"when both operands are LOCALS the copy is eliminated"*) scores **24 of 36**. The two shapes w-memcpy never compiled are the two that decide it: **`ld`** — dead local destination, **formal** source — is eliminated at 6 of 6, and **`lu`** — two locals, destination passed to an extern afterwards — is **live** at 6 of 6 and obeys the `n ≤ 5` rule exactly.

> ### **AND IT IS c2 THAT REMOVES IT, NOT THE FRONT END — established by capture, not by argument.** The `.ex` of the eliminated cell is 86 bytes longer than the same function with the `memcpy` line deleted, and it carries `80 ac 00 00 00`: the block-move tuple, opcode **`0xac` = 172**, present in the IL c1xx hands to c2. Three captures differing only in pointer type differ in **exactly three bytes**, two of which are the alignment hints, taking **`01` / `04` / `08`** — w-memcpy's "two alignment hints" identified positionally.

> ### **THE REMOVAL SITE IS NAMED AND FILED `unknown`, AND THAT IS THE MOST USEFUL LINE HERE.** `0x10b482ba → 0x10b42571` is the only arm found that deletes a `0xac`/`0xad` tuple, it reads cleanly, and **its own predicates contradict the measured rule**: it applies the same operand predicate to *both* pointers, while the `ld` cell has a formal source and is eliminated anyway. Method doc §7 case 1 is not ruled out, so no DISCLOSURE row is drafted for it. **The rule the project would actually adopt needs no address at all.**

## 2. What it admits, and what it refuses

**Admits** — as *readings with VAs plus obj grades*, not as code:

* the expansion predicate (`WB_MEMCPY_FINDINGS.md` §2), graded 180/180 at five
  flag sets and 24/24 on GRID-W part B's live cells;
* the elimination rule (E-DEADDST), graded 36/36;
* the mint (`0x10b9ae7e`, three sites), which is *why* no `.gl` token exists
  for `memcpy` — the fifth refusal w-memcpy §6.3 priced.

**Refuses, by name:**

* **R-DESC's 24 misses** and **the two block-plan `Port=Mismatch`**
  (w-memcpy §4.3 / §5.1). Registered as a decline (Q7) *before* the grid ran.
  Those cells contain no intrinsic; they are call-site move scheduling and
  block planning. Nothing at `0x10bf6555` touches an argument slot.
* **The removal site** (§1 above, findings §5.4) — `unknown`.
* **The variable-size arm.** Read at `0x10bf65b8`, **not measured** — GRID-W has
  no variable-size cell. Scored as a miss (§4, Q3) rather than carried.
* **Adoption of anything.** Four DISCLOSURE rows are *drafted*; none is used.

## 3. Estimate vs outcome

The campaign's success floor for WB-C was "a decision function read out of the
binary that reproduces the measured grid". The reading reproduced **13 of 13**
boundaries before a single new compile — but the interesting number is the one
that was **not** predicted by the campaign brief: the reading implied a *second
threshold* that the existing 1,155 cells were structurally unable to observe,
and testing it cost **216 compiles and refuted a rule that had scored 100 % on
408 of them**.

That is the transferable shape: *the black-box grids were not wrong, they were
under-crossed*, and the disassembly's contribution was to say which axis was
missing.

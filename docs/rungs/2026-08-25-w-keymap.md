## 3. WHAT COULD NOT BE KEYED — named, not guessed

The brief's rule 2 is that naming the unkeyable is a success and inferring it
is a failure nobody catches for weeks. Four things could not be keyed, and
the largest is 30.89 % of the population.

**3.1 The `mcall` family — 612 of 785 keys, 528,278 bodies (30.89 %) —
cannot be attributed to Phase 1 in either direction.** Every
`expr-call-in-expr-*` key heads at byte **`0x26`**, and `0x26` is the
symbol-push designator that the `off_class` walker treats as **MODELED**
(`control_flow.rs:849`, `s.tok("cf-sym-tok")`, no reason raised). So Phase
1's taxonomy has no name for the construct that heads nearly a third of the
blocked bodies. Their *key names* do mention the ten — `off-add`,
`chain-bind`, `temp-bind`, `cmp-le`, `bit-and` — but only as the **second**
blocker (`Blocker::kind_name`, `mcall.rs:610`), and `Block::feature`'s own
doc says in as many words that reading a construct out of a key by grepping
its name **was the defect** and *"a second, better-informed grep would be the
same defect."* This lane did not grep, and claims nothing about the 30.89 %.

**3.2 `mcall-cmp-rel-operand-type-8645` (6 bodies, 1 TU) is genuinely
ambiguous and is left unassigned.** It is a `blk_type` refusal raised at a
**relational's operand type** when the signedness cannot be read
(`mcall_cmp.rs:219`), so it is C7-shaped by position and C4-shaped by
production. Assigning it either way is a guess. It is reported in both
columns of §2 as excluded, and it moves nothing: 6 of 1,710,066 bodies, and
the TU it appears in is not near any floor.

**3.3 C3 and C8 cannot be PRICED, only keyed.** Both are **already partly in
class on the census route**, so their head counts are a *residue* of what the
port already accepts rather than a measurement of the construct:

- **C8 bitwise** — `0x0B`/`0x0C`/`0x0D` are admitted at `expr.rs:2085/2090/2095`
  (`IlOp::And`/`Or`/`Xor`). Only `0x0E`, which `expr_opcode_name` does not
  name, survives — hence **one key, one body**. `expr-bit-and`,
  `expr-bit-or`, `expr-bit-xor` are keys the renderer *would* produce and
  whose population is **0**.
- **C3 bind** — `0x99` is eaten inside the shape recognizers
  (`this_binding.rs:98`, `ctor_dtor.rs:361/682/786`,
  `alloc_init_or_fail.rs:355`, `xtea_encrypt_loop.rs:258`), so a `0x99`
  reachable by an admitted `this`-binding never heads anything.

Neither has a **named, settable de-admission parameter**, so the
counterfactual that prices C1 (§6) cannot be run for them and **this lane
publishes no priced number for C3 or C8.** That is a gap in the tree's
decision surface, not in this read — and it is exactly what
`GOAL_DECISION_2026-08-21.md` § AMENDED asks general layers to ship.

**3.4 The two denominators cannot be converted into one another, and this
lane did not build a conversion.** §5 explains why the attempt would be
wrong. Anyone who wants one number must pick an instrument and say which.

"how big is construct Cn" has no answer without naming the tree state, and
every filed Phase-1 mass in `ROADMAP_SLICING` was taken at *one* such state

can I key"* — and keying turned out to be the easy half, because the opcode
bridge is total. **The binding unknown was never keyability; it was
attributability**, and the honest number there is §3.1's: **30.89 % of the
blocked population heads at a byte Phase 1's own taxonomy calls modeled.**
The prereg had no prediction for that quantity because it had not yet seen
that the question existed.

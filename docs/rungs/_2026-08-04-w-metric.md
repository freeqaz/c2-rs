## 2. The commissioned idea, and its registered kill

The lane was commissioned on: *"objdiff has fuzzy matching that rb3-xenon
leverages extensively; something similar may help measure progress."* The
kill-criterion was registered before measurement (PREREG P2): if
output-similarity is undefined on ≥ 95 % of graded TUs, the transfer dies.

Measured: the port emits an object on **8 of 871** graded TUs (99.1 %
undefined), and where defined the similarity is 100.0 by construction — zero
information. Worse, on the one class where it would produce non-trivial
numbers (`mismatch`), it points the wrong way: the #232 fix (wrong emit →
restored refusal) would have moved a similarity score **down**. objdiff's own
aggregation also returns **100.0 over zero code bytes**
(`objdiff-core/src/bindings/report.rs`, `calc_fuzzy_match_percent`) — the
absence-reads-as-success shape this project has recorded fifteen times, here
as a library default. The full reading of what objdiff computes (instruction
Patience diff, penalty scores, name-anchored relocations, deliberate masking
of real byte differences) is `PROGRESS_METRIC.md` §2.

**The near-miss measurement closes the door independently**: the median
failing TU has 80 % of its emitted functions out of class. There is no
near-miss bulge for any similarity score to reveal; a fuzzy metric over this
workload is the emitted census wearing a costume.

## 3. What ships instead

`GapReport::progress_mass` — `P = mean(|A|/graded, |B|/graded, |C|/graded,
emitted-in-class/emitted)`, printed by every scan in its own block whose
header states it is never a correctness signal, with `gap-metric progress-*`
keys. Two structural guards, both unit-tested: a `mismatch` TU contributes 0
to every numerator while staying in every denominator (a wrong emit scores
strictly below the refusal it replaced — the #232 transition scores *down*);
and an empty scan returns `None`/`NO-RESULT` with no key at all.

Full design, candidate survey (six candidates rejected with the
gaming/up-while-worse analysis for each), denominator defence, and the five
traps: `docs/PROGRESS_METRIC.md`.

## 5. The backfill verdict — would it have registered the day?

Yes, and with the discrimination the commissioning brief asked for: **w-sect
(+1.58 pp) > w-r1c (+0.86 pp) > every measurement lane (0.00)** — a ranking
over a day the binary metric read as `8, 8, 8, 8`. The two zero classes are
findings, not disappointments: measurement merges move P by exactly zero
(the metric does not drift under observation), and w-label's real codegen
work moved P by zero because its effect lands on the fixture gate
(106 → 118 Match), which is workload-invisible — the predicted trap 3, and
the reason the rung recommends the two supporting metrics rather than
folding those axes into P.

## 7. Found and not taken

* **Do not fold TU match into P.** Tried on paper; rejected because a metric
  that contains the payoff invites reading the composite as a softened
  payoff. P is deliberately a *leading* mass; `match` stands alone.
* **Byte-weighted `f`** (fraction of workload bytes reproducible): rejected —
  needs per-function sizes the scan does not record, weights a few huge TUs,
  and amplifies trap 2 in the most gameable direction. If someone wants it
  later, the JSONL would need per-COMDAT sizes first; measure the skew before
  adopting.
* **`status.sh` collection of `gap-metric` keys** — still the open item from
  w-bc §5.1; `progress-mass` rides along when it lands. Not taken here: the
  collector is `scripts/`, outside this lane's seam.
* **A per-mismatch forensic similarity score** (the one legitimate objdiff
  descendant): defined only over the `mismatch` class, currently empty.
  Worth building only when that class is nonempty long enough to rank; the
  scan's first-divergence offsets already cover the single-defect case.
* **Board rows.** This lane mints no numbers (the w-book precedent). Proposed
  lettered rows for a future book lane: **M-a** the progress mass and its
  guards (DONE, this rung); **M-b** the near-miss distribution measurement
  (median 0.80 — the fact that killed fuzzy matching); **M-c** the standing
  rule that any partial-credit metric must score a wrong emit below a refusal,
  demonstrated here with a test, applicable to every future metric proposal.

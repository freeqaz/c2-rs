## The answer

> **Candidate ids are PER-FUNCTION.** `DAT_10c400d4` is set to **`1`** at
> **`0x10b57676`**, in `FUN_10b57633` (the `globregs.c` phase), which the
> per-function back-end driver `FUN_10b7dc51` calls at `0x10b7dcb7`. The
> counter has **exactly four references in the image**, all of them inside one
> invocation of that driver, and the write is the first in program order.

`WB_LIVE_FINDINGS.md:258-260` was right; `ref/P_REGALLOC.md:62,86,188` was
wrong in three places. Full read in `WB_CANDID_FINDINGS.md`; board **#3372**.

**What decided it was the containment, not the driver.** The strong form does
not require identifying `FUN_10b7dc51`'s granularity first: all seven call
sites of the mint `FUN_10b54d32`, plus the fourth reference, lie inside the
subtrees of `FUN_10b57633` or `FUN_10b31c9a` — the two routines the driver
calls, in that order — so **no candidate can be minted without a preceding
reset**, whatever the driver iterates.

**The piece no doc had** is the **free-list** reset at `0x10b5767c`. The mint
stamps an id only on a fresh `alloc` (`0x10b54d48` jumps past both the read and
the increment), so a recycled record keeps its old id — sound only because the
free list is emptied in the **same straight-line run** as the counter
(`0x10b57665`–`0x10b5767c`, no branch), and the 1024-bucket hash is memset
`0x20` bytes later past a diamond that reconverges at `0x10b57695`.
`READ_PLAN` §5.2 was right that a per-function *table clear*
alone does not settle scope; this is the store that does.

## The control, and why its green is worth something

`READ_PLAN` §5.3 is the reason it exists: `[R]` means *"the instructions were
read correctly"*, not *"this is what c2 does"* — the `.bss` bump rule was read
correctly out of a clean function and was **wrong about c2**.

`scripts/candid_c1.py`, real `cl.exe` 16.00.11886.00 under wibo at `/O1 /GS- /c`:

```
C1-pos (must be DIFFERENT): DIFFERENT -> instrument LIVE
C1 (solo vs after)        : IDENTICAL
-- C1b ladder (identical filler bodies at different TU positions)
N=2     F0 size=204   differing from F0: 0      P==solo: True
N=40    F0 size=204   differing from F0: 0      P==solo: True
N=120   F0 size=204   differing from F0: 0      P==solo: True
N=400   F0 size=204   differing from F0: 0      P==solo: True
RESULT: GREEN -- no body depends on its TU position
```

558 character-identical filler bodies compared against their own `F0`, at TU
sizes where a compilation-global counter would have wrapped the 1024-bucket
hash ≈4×. **Zero positional differences.**

**The green is worth something only because C1-pos went red**: the same
extractor on a probe differing by one operand reports DIFFERENT. Had it not,
the green would have been discarded as an instrument failure, not published —
`docs/STATUS.md`'s standing trap that `mismatch 0` is not evidence of
correctness.

**And its limit was fixed before the result existed**: a green corroborates and
does not prove. Identical bytes are also consistent with "no tie decided
anything", which is exactly the ambiguity **#3363** left when its 4 × 8 grid
came back 32/32 identical. The closing evidence is the reference closure; C1 is
the check that could have overturned it. Board **#3373**.

## What it costs `P_REGALLOC.md` consequence 3

`ref/P_REGALLOC.md:160-166` and board **#3242** hold that the tie tier is a
bucket walk over a *compilation-global* counter and therefore *"not a source
property at all"* — *"the most direct available explanation for why
source-level fitted sorts keep being refuted"*.

**The mechanism survives; the "so" does not.** A per-function counter dense
from 1 makes a candidate's bucket **its mint index within the function**, which
is precisely the kind of quantity that *can* track source order.

**Consequence: the ten refuted allocation keys at
`crates/c2-core/src/codegen/alloc.rs:103-539` are back to UNEXPLAINED** —
clause 2 refuted on 7 of 56 fresh-holdout cells (#836), `H-self` on 11 of 72
(#857), the 52,416-configuration preregistered search a negative with the
residual exactly the tie tier. `READ_PLAN` §3 row R1 named that as the thing R1
decides, and this is the decision. **R1 removes a standing explanation rather
than supplying one, and that is the deliverable.**

What it changes downstream: a source-level key is no longer *a priori* doomed,
and the missing input is the **mint order**, not a better sort. **R4
(`FUN_10b55732`, 1,676 B, 3–5 d) is now the read that would settle it** — the
one `alloc.rs:40-43` already points at. Board **#3374**.

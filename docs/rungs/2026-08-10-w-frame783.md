## 2. #2783 RE-DERIVED, AND WHAT ACTUALLY SHIPPED

### 2.1 The relaxation, from this lane's own bytes

`codec::gl_offset_framed` tests seven bytes around a `.gl` body-start field
at `o`:

```text
   80 <LE32 PREV> 00 00 | 80 <LE32 BODY-START>
   ^o-7  ^o-6…o-3  ^o-2   ^o   ^o+1…o+4
```

`PREV`'s tag is `gl[o-7]`, so `gl[o-5]` is **PREV's byte 1** and, with
`gl[o-4] == gl[o-3] == 0` already required, the pinned clause is exactly
`PREV ∈ [0x1000, 0x10FF]`. Re-derived here rather than inherited
(`work/w-frame783/frame783.py`): `??0Vector3@@QAA@MMM@Z`'s body-start 98,922 is
spelled at `vec.cpp`'s `.gl`+27,521, its record's PREV is **`0x189a`**, and the
PREV byte-1 histogram over the relaxed record set is
`0x10:36, 0x11:39, 0x12:27, 0x13:11, 0x14:27, 0x15:9, 0x16:8, 0x17:20,
0x18:140, 0x19:27, 0x1a:14, 0x1b:11` — a **rising per-record counter**, not a
tag. `vec.cpp` goes **36 → 369**.

Over 876 of the 878 workload TUs, captured by this lane at the workload's own
flags (`sweep783.py`, `fpgate.py`, `fpshape.py`):

| | framed records | offsets that are **not** an `.ex` `4F 1F` split point | TUs carrying one |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `codec::gl_offset_framed` (incumbent) | 28,870 | **1** | **1** (`src/system/utl/TempoMap.cpp`) |
| `bind::emit_offset_framed` (#2783 as filed) | 1,507,159 | **551** | **406** |
| **`codec::gl_offset_framed_relaxed` (shipped)** | **1,506,608** | **0** | **0** |

Two things #2783 does not say, and both are board rows:

* **The gate's framing is not precise either** (#2862). `TempoMap.cpp` frames
  `80 0b 0b 00 5c` as a body-start of 1,543,506,699 inside a 34-segment `.ex`.
  *"A value test wearing a position test's docstring"* cuts both ways; the
  incumbent is **more** precise, by one record in twenty-nine thousand, and
  what ships is the only one of the three with zero.
* **The imprecision is separable by one more byte, 551 for 551** (#2861).
  Every one of the 1,506,608 on-a-split offsets has top byte **0**; every one
  of the 551 not-a-split offsets has top byte **≥ 2** (`{2:1, 4:1, 11:205,
  16:29, 31:1, 51:2, 71:2, 87:98, 92:1, 95:2, 97:1, 104:1, 106:2, 113:1,
  117:203, 243:1}`). The largest real offset in the workload is **2,837,591**
  (`0x2b4c57`), so `GL_OFFSET_MAX = 0x1000000` has three orders of magnitude
  of headroom and discards nothing real.

**It is a VALUE test and it is named as one** — the same honesty #2783 demands
of the byte it removes. It is not a proof (nothing in the container promises a
field width), so it fails in the **refusing** direction: an offset past the
bound is not framed, its record is not bound, and clause 3 refuses the TU on
the unclaimed run.

So the shippable form of #2783 is **one byte freed and one byte pinned**.

### 2.2 Where it ships, and why the soundness argument had to be measured

`w-decouple`'s `NameFit` seam, one axis over — the widening goes to the
**binding** and both fences keep the incumbent walk:

| caller | framing | why |
|---|---|---|
| `gl::gl_bound_names` → `Bindings::selective` / `per_record`, and `diag`'s transcription | **`GATE_BIND_FRAME`** (relaxed) | the binding wants every record the container has |
| `gl::gl_defined_names` → `bind::defined_name_set`, `gl::plain_external_defined_names` | `codec::gl_offset_framed` | a fence ground set, where a widening of the walk is a **tightening** of the fence — #2622/#2623, measured at **−1 `fnbyte-exact`** |
| `codec::parse_gl`'s K2a span typing | `codec::gl_offset_framed` | its cross-check is `values == ex_offsets_ordered`; a widened framing can only turn that equality into "type nothing" |

**`NameFit`'s monotonicity argument is not available here** and the difference
is worth stating precisely. That one held because `gl_defined_names_framed`
refuses on a **whole-TU** basis, so a widened *naming policy* could differ from
the narrow walk only on a TU the narrow walk had already emptied. A **framing**
widening has no such property: it can see more records on a TU that binds
perfectly well.

So it is measured (#2863), over 876 TUs:

* the relaxed scan's record **positions** are a strict superset of the
  incumbent's on **876 of 876** TUs. Not free: both scans step `p += 5` on a
  hit and `p += 1` on a miss, so an extra hit can step **over** a position the
  narrow scan would have matched. It does not, anywhere in this workload.
* the incumbent 1:1 contract is **unchanged**: **32** TUs are 1:1 with their
  segments under each framing, **0 lost and 0 gained**.
* all **23** matching TUs frame **record-for-record identically** under both.
* a framed offset that is not a split point is caught by `Bindings::selective`
  clause 1 (`OffsetNotASplitPoint`), a **whole-TU refusal** — so the failure
  direction is a refusal even where the framing is wrong.

### 2.3 One performance change, semantics-free (#2867)

`gl_defined_names_framed`'s name lookup was
`runs.iter().rposition(|&(_, end, _)| end <= p)` — linear per record. The walk
now runs over **1.5 M** records instead of 29 k, against ~10 k runs on the
largest TUs, so it was quadratic in exactly the two quantities the relaxation
multiplies. `symbol_runs` scans forward and resumes at each run's `end`, so the
ends are strictly increasing and the answer is `partition_point − 1`. The
878-TU scan goes **4.6 s → 5.2 s** wall.

Graded on **both sides** of `MAX_NAME_TO_OFFSET`, because that bound is the
only thing standing between a framed offset and a body emitted under some other
record's name.

### 9.1 The deciding row, its true antecedent, and what would have falsified it

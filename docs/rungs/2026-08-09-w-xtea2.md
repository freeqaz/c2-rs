## 4. THE LABEL CHANNEL — measured in the middle, and it closes to the unit

### 4.1 Why the standing number had to be re-taken

`work/w-xtea/PRICE.md` §6 is the origin of *"even a hypothetical port that
emitted all 272 bytes correctly would still miss the obj"*, and §6.1 measures
the charge at **`for` +1 / `while` +1 / `do-while` +0** — *"every one of this
lane's numbers is one less"* than `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §4. It was taken **by
counterfactual**: six TUs differing in `?Encipher`'s control flow, reading
`?Encrypt`'s `$M` pair off each.

`wb-label` §7.2 landed **after** that lane and retired the form: c1xx and c2
share one symbol-id space, so two different source texts have two different
seeds and the counterfactual measures **`Δseed + Δcharge`**. Its own grid shows
eight declarations that emit not one instruction moving a counterfactual reading
by **sixteen** and the true charge by **zero**.

So the binding term of the project's standing price for this TU was measured
with a retired instrument. This lane re-takes it in the form §7.6 mandates and
**does not inherit the integer**.

### 4.4 What that does to the price, and what it does not

**It removes an unknown and adds three plumbing obligations.** The charge is a
measured constant now, so `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §7.6's box permits shipping it —
*"a class may ship a measured constant obtained by steps 1–5, and nothing
else"*. What it does not remove is the seam `w-bdnz` #1983 already named:
`IlFunction::label_slots` must return the same `k`, `coff::plan_labels` must
advance it, and `IlBundle::functions`' gate is `label_slots(false)? !=
label_lead() + 1`, which asks whether the charge **agrees with what
`plan_labels` will advance** rather than whether it is right. Three layers, and
this lane paid none of them: nothing here needed a `label_slots` arm, because
`x-setkey`'s stride 2 is exactly what `plan_labels` already charges a
memcpy-minting leaf.

**And `/Ox` is a different world, not a different constant.** `x-encipher` is 3
at `/O1` and **13** at `/Ox`; `x-encrypt` is 9/4 and **41/37**. `label_slots`
has no mode parameter and `wb-label` §7.6 forbids giving it one. The route left
open is the one this lane's own class takes: **gate the recognizer to `/O1` in
the parser**, so the constant is only ever reachable at the mode it was measured
at. That is board #1638's rule doing work it was not written for.

## 5. WHAT SHIPS — `memcpy_tail`, and nothing else

`?SetKey@XTEABlockEncrypter@@QAAXPBE@Z`, twelve bytes:

```text
   0000  38630010  addi r3,r3,16      the destination member, IN PLACE in r3
   0004  38a00010  li   r5,16         the length
   0008  4bfffff8  b    memcpy        REL24 — a TAIL branch, no frame, no `bl`
```

Five cells compiled through real `c2.dll` fix every word
(`work/w-xtea2/probe/mcpytail.cpp`, `/O1 /Oi /GS- /c`):

```text
   off16   memcpy(k, uc, 0x10)   addi r3,r3,16 · li r5,16 · b memcpy     12 B
   len8    memcpy(k, uc, 0x8)    addi r3,r3,16 · li r5,8  · b memcpy     12 B
   off0    memcpy(n, uc, 0x10)                   li r5,16 · b memcpy      8 B
   freefn  memcpy(d, s, 0x10)                    li r5,16 · b memcpy      8 B
   rev     memcpy(uc, k, 0x10)   mr r11,r3 · mr r3,r4 · addi r4,r11,16
                                 · li r5,16 · b memcpy                   20 B
```

* the length is the `li`'s immediate and **nothing else moves**;
* **a zero destination offset emits no `addi` at all** — 8 bytes, not 12, so a
  port that emitted `addi r3,r3,0` would be one word long in a section whose
  relocation still resolves;
* the source costs **nothing**: it is formal 1 and therefore already in r4.

**Zero new encoders.** `encode_addi` is both words (`li rD,k` *is*
`addi rD,r0,k`), and `encode_tail_branch` and the REL24 emission are general.
That is the one thing this class does not share with the other three bodies,
which is §7's whole point.

### 5.1 The minted external, and the correction to NC-1 item 7

`memcpy` has no `.gl` record — it arrives as intrinsic selector 172 on a `40`
token — so its name is minted in `c2_core::comdat`, exactly as `w-ifn`'s class
mints it. **Where the symbol goes is not the same:**

```text
   work/w-xtea2/ref/xtea.dump     [16] ?SetKey…  [17] memcpy  [18] .text
   work/w-xtea2/probe/mcpytail.obj [13] ?off16…  [14] memcpy  [15] .text
```

the callee region, one index after the function's own symbol — where `w-ifn`'s
FRAMED user puts it after that function's `$T`. The probe's three later users
mint **no second symbol**, which is the same dedup `w-ifn`'s `sub2` cell shows
on its side. So the placement is a property of the **user's frame class**, and
the two coexist: `GuardRetChain` fills `helper_externals`, `MemcpyTail` leaves
it empty and lets `introduced_externals` place the name.

### 5.2 The grammar is `guard_ret_chain`'s, widened in two named places

The selector constant, the argument separator, the load and the call window are
imported from `w-ifn`'s module rather than restated (`GAPS.md` §6). Two things
this class needs that that one does not, both witnessed: a **member designator**
on an operand (through `designator::eat_offset_adds`, the shared `27`/`28`
locator — board #1334 is what a second hand-rolled one becomes), and a chain of
**two** reinterpreting conversions.

## 1. THE RESULT, up front

> ### **1. THE ANSWER TO THE QUESTION THIS LANE WAS DISPATCHED WITH: THE NEGATIVE IS AN ARTIFACT.** `w-readphase` §1 finding 5 — *"a decode-only widening of the standing instrument DE-ACCEPTS"*, published as **−7 `match` and −5,949 `fnbyte-exact`** at the 22-token ladder tip — is reproduced here **exactly**, and then censused. Sinking each of `chain_skip_form`'s **49** pinned opcodes one at a time, 49 full 878-TU scans: **5 de-accept, 44 are bit-for-bit neutral, 0 produce a `mismatch`.** The five are `33`, `B9`, `2C`, `41`, `55` — and every one of them is a byte **`parse_expr` already handles**, three as accepted productions (LITERAL, LOAD, CONVERT) and two as its own `stop` bytes. On all **44** bytes `parse_expr` currently **refuses** — which is *every byte a real widening could possibly target* — the sink moves **nothing**.

> ### **2. AND THE MECHANISM IS PROVEN BY CONSTRUCTION, NOT INFERRED. `#3094`'s FINDING IS REAL AND ITS NAMED MECHANISM IS WRONG.** #3094 attributes the removals to the poison. They are not the poison: `keydiff base → op:41` puts all **+2,694** on **`expr-jump`** and **zero** on `expr-chain-sink-poison`. There are **two** mechanisms and both are instrument-only. **(a)** `chain_sink()` is consulted **before** the `b == stop` check (`expr.rs:1627`, deliberately, board **#663**), so sinking a stop byte walks every accepted expression off its own end — a scratch mutant that reverses that ordering makes `op:41` and `op:55` de-accept **exactly zero**, `match` back to **25**, `fnbyte-exact` back to **35,734**. **(b)** Sinking `33`/`B9`/`2C` **replaces a working production with a width-skip**, so the walk pushes no `IlOp` and the `ops.is_empty()` arm or the poison refuses. Sinking an already-accepted token is a **narrowing dressed as a widening**. Neither mechanism exists in a real widening.

> ### **3. −5,949 IS NOT A COST THAT GROWS WITH THE WIDENING — IT IS A FLOOR, AND THE INSTRUMENT REACHES IT FIVE DIFFERENT WAYS.** `op:41,op:55` alone gives `match` **18** / `fnbyte-exact` **29,785**. `op:2C,op:33,op:B9` alone gives **18 / 29,785**. All five together: **18 / 29,785**. The whole 22-token ladder tip: **18 / 29,785**. The full 49-token + `type`/`convert`/`intrinsic` **ceiling**: **18 / 29,785**. **29,785 is the byte-exact population whose emission never routes through `parse_expr` at all**, and **5,949 is the entire population any sink can remove, by any route.** The other twenty tokens of `w-readphase`'s ladder contributed **zero**.

> ### **4. THE REAL WIDENING SHIPPED, AND IT IS A PERFECT IDENTITY.** `parse_expr_classed` now accepts `5D`/`5E` as `<varint> <varint>`, unpoisoned, pushing no `IlOp`. **`match` 25 · `mismatch` 0 · `fnbyte-exact` 35,734 · `fnbyte-refused-parse` 113,612 · 372 of 372 `gap-metric` keys identical · 878 of 878 verdict lines identical · 615 `emit_blockers` keys summing 113,612 at both ends.** Zero de-acceptance, zero gain.

> ### **5. THE REASON IT GAINED ZERO IS THE OTHER HALF OF THE FINDING, AND IT CORRECTS A NUMBER THREE DOCUMENTS NOW QUOTE.** `w-readphase` §4.2 ranks `5D`/`5E` as *"the single largest residue item, 13,158 emitted functions"*. **At base, `expr-op-0x5D` and `expr-op-0x5E` do not exist as keys — in `emit_blockers` (615 keys / 113,612) or in `fn_blockers` (635 keys / 1,705,627), over 878 TUs.** The only `5D`/`5E` row anywhere is `body-0x5D` = **8**, and it is the *statement*-position dispatch, not `parse_expr`. **13,158 is a sink-ceiling number and it is reachable by nothing at base.** The residue ranking is a ranking of a **counterfactual population**, not of what a widening can reach today.

> ### **6. AND THE INSTRUMENT ARM RAISED THE CEILING ITSELF, WHICH IS THE ONE PLACE 13,158 IS REAL.** With `5D`/`5E` pinned, the ceiling's `expr-op-0x5D` (6,815) + `expr-op-0x5E` (6,343) = **13,158** rows go to **zero**, and **12,765 of them (97.0 %)** land on the function tail. `w-readphase` §4's headline **76,041 of 120,456 (63.1 %)** becomes **88,806 of 120,456 (73.7 %)** — that lane's ceiling was measured with the instrument's largest expressiveness hole open.

## 3. THE MECHANISM, MEASURED RATHER THAN ARGUED

The claim *"it is the ordering, not the poison"* is testable by construction, and
running it blind would have been worse than registering it — so it was registered
as PREREG **X1–X4** (§8, stage-3 addendum) and then run.

**The mutant**: move `if b == stop { break; }` **above** the `chain_sink()`
consultation in `parse_expr_classed`. Scratch only; reverted, and
`git status --porcelain crates/` is **0 lines** at this tip with the shipping
binary re-verified at `match` 25 / `fnbyte-exact` 35,734.

| | `op:41` | `op:55` | the other 11 tested |
|---|---|---|---|
| sink **before** stop (shipping, board #663) | `match` **24** · `fnb` **33,040** | `match` **19** · `fnb` **32,479** | neutral |
| sink **after** stop (**mutant X**) | `match` **25** · `fnb` **35,734** | `match` **25** · `fnb` **35,734** | neutral |

The entire −7 / −5,949 attributed to those two tokens **vanishes** when the
ordering is reversed, and no neutral token changes — so the mutant is not simply
disabling the sink.

### 3.1 The ordering is a real trade the instrument makes on purpose

**X4, and it is why this is not a bug report.** Under mutant X the ceiling's
function-tail reach falls **88,806 → 44,447** — halved. That is exactly
`w-depth`'s own finding (#663, `work/w-depth/probe/p5_mixed.cpp`): with the stop
checked first, the walk halts at the first `return` and *"a depth that silently
omits everything after the first `return` is a lower bound advertised as a
count."* The instrument buys its depth with this ordering. What is new is that
the **price of that purchase is the whole de-acceptance**, and nobody had
separated the two.

### 3.2 What this does and does not license

It licenses reading `w-readphase`'s §3 ladder `match`/`fnbyte-exact` columns as
**instrument telemetry**, not as a price of reader work. It does **not** license
reading them as zero: mechanism (b) is a live hazard for any lane that sinks a
token the acceptor already handles, and three of the five do exactly that. The
standing rule that follows is one sentence: **before quoting a sink run's
`match`/`fnbyte-exact`, check whether the sunk token is one `parse_expr` accepts
or stops on. If it is, the number is about the instrument.**

## 4. THE REAL WIDENING — what was built, and its required-zero grade

### 4.1 The instrument arm (I)

`SkipForm::VarintVarint` + `0x5D | 0x5E => VarintVarint` in `chain_skip_form`.
This was the **last** of that table's three "unspellable" absences, and it closes
the same way `0xBD` (`TypeByteVarint`, #1314) and `0x66` (`ClassDescr`, #1530)
did — an **enum** change, not a measurement.

**The width has two independent derivations and this lane added neither.**
`docs/EH_RECORDS.md` §7.1 measured `<varint n> <varint state>` at the workload's
own flags, including the escaped witness `5D 01 80 A1 00 00 00` that kills the
fixed-width and plain-byte rivals at once; `shapes/control_flow.rs`'s `operand()`
has read exactly that since lane WEH (`cf-eh-count` / `cf-eh-count-state`); four
`ctor_dtor.rs` recognizers and `mcall::eat_body_end_inner` `eat` the short form
inside shapes the differential grades byte-exact. And
`docs/whitebox/WB_READER_FINDINGS.md` §3.1 puts `5D` and `5E` **alone together in
c2's own reader class 14**, with §3.4 listing `0x5D`/`0x5E <i32c> <i32c>` among
the readings where the disassembly and this tree **agree**.

**The absence-pinning test's own instruction was followed.** Its header asked a
widening lane to *"delete a line that names the reason instead of one that reads
like an oversight"*; the `5D`/`5E` loop is **deleted** and replaced with a
positive `Some(SkipForm::VarintVarint)` assertion, plus a row in
`the_pinned_widths_are_exactly_the_documented_table` on its own line (not folded
into `0x5C`'s — `5C` carries a TYPE and `5D`/`5E` carry a COUNT, and a reader
that swept them together would consume a `21` state byte as a TYPE tag).

### 4.2 The real arm (R)

An unpoisoned `0x5D | 0x5E` arm in `parse_expr_classed`: consume
`<varint n> <varint state>`, push no `IlOp`, set **no poison flag**, clear
`cstack_ok` because a token that yields no value cannot have been followed by the
class model. It sits **below** the `b == stop` check by construction, so it
cannot have mechanism (a); it pushes no `IlOp` where one was expected, so it
cannot have mechanism (b).

Its fail-closed boundary is stated in the source and is real: the trailer is EH
bookkeeping (`EH_RECORDS.md` §10's `maxState` transition) and this walker has no
EH model, so a body reaching the end through one has had an EH fact **dropped**.
The emitter never sees it — the emitted classes are gated by `body/mod.rs`'s own
productions and by `control_flow.rs`'s `EhMarkers`, which reads the same two
fields and refuses on them.

## 6. THE TWO-SIDED PRICE OF THIS LANE'S OWN REFUSAL

**What was NOT built, and what refusing it costs.** The `body-0x5D` population —
the statement-position spelling — is **8** emitted functions, and a statement arm
for it was registered as contingent (PREREG §1 R2) and **deferred**. The reason
is not size: accepting `5D <n> <g> 4B` as a statement is an **emit** widening (a
body whose EH state transition is dropped becomes emittable), not the decode
widening this rung is graded as, and all 8 are EH-bearing. The price of that
refusal is **8 emitted functions, 0 TUs** — `codegen-gap` is 0 and `frontier` is
2, and neither of the two frontier TUs carries a `5D`.

**And the cost of the widening that WAS built is zero in both directions**, which
is the honest reading of a required-zero rung: it bought no ground and it lost
none. What it bought is **the measurement**, and the instrument's ceiling.

   `4B` *"is a statement of its own"*, so on the statement-layer fence's own

   it. A one-line change to a shared predicate that cannot be graded when it

## 3. W42 — `(formal >> k) & m` in a conditional tail arm, one `rlwinm`

`?MemAlloc@NUISPEECH@@YAPAXPAXKK@Z`, blocker `expr-cmp-eq`, `calls-2plus |
cflow-if-1`.

```text
  mr     r11,r4            entry: `size` parked — both arms want it, at
  mr     r4,r5                    different registers
  cmplwi cr6,r3,0          entry: `attrs` HOISTED to r4, because both arms
  bne    cr6,+12                   want it THERE — raw in the then-arm,
  mr     r3,r11                    folded in the else-arm
  b      XMemAlloc
  mr     r5,r11
  rlwinm r4,r4,5,28,28     else: the fold, IN PLACE
  b      RtlAllocateHeap
```

The hoist is `plan_cond_pair`'s **rule 1**, already in the tree and already
documented *against this exact function*. What is new is that a `ShiftMask` slot
**counts as a use of its source formal**, which is what makes rule 1 fire.

### 3.1 The fold is measured on 70 cells and fitted on none

`k ∈ {1,4,8,16,24,27,31}` × `m ∈ {1,2,3,8,12,15,255,0x10,0x10000,0xFFFFFFF0}` —
the whole cross product — through real `c2` at the workload's own profile.

```text
  eff = m & ((1 << (32 - k)) - 1)
  eff == 0            -> the value is the literal 0
  eff not contiguous  -> refused
  otherwise           -> rlwinm rD,rS,32-k,31-hi,31-lo
```

**70 agree, 0 disagree.** (Six cells print as `srwi rA,rS,k`; that is the same
word — `rlwinm rA,rS,32-k,k,31` — and llvm-mc's extended mnemonic, not an
exception. A first pass that compared mnemonics rather than words reported six
false disagreements, which is the same instrument trap w-dclass §4.6 recorded
for `llvm-mc` dropping a cr0 field.)

61 of the 70 are `fixtures/cpp/w42_shift_mask.cpp` and **the whole obj is
`Port=Match`**.

### 3.2 The trap cell, and it is the `!=`→`>` shape again

> ### **Prereg R8 is a HIT.** The other 9 cells are the **MASK COLLAPSE** — `m` keeps no bit the shift left behind — and they differ from their neighbours in **two ways at once**: `rlwinm` becomes `li rD,0`, **and the block layout reverts** (the formal stops being hoisted, `bne` goes +12 → +16, the body grows a word). A fold that only rewrote the instruction would have emitted the right arithmetic behind the **wrong branch**.

```text
  (at >> 16) & 0x10       ->  rlwinm r4,r4,16,27,27    hoisted, +12
  (at >> 16) & 0x10000    ->  li r4,0                  NOT hoisted, +16
  (at >> 16) & 0xfffffff0 ->  rlwinm r4,r4,16,16,27    hoisted, +12
```

The port **refuses** the collapse rather than folding it. The fold is right about
the instruction and the layout change has one witness shape; a refusal is a gap.

### 3.3 The other four refusals, each with a measured neighbour

* **out-of-place** — the then-arm does not want the formal, so rule 1 does not
  fire and c2 homes the source in a **second** scratch at r10
  (`rlwinm r4,r10,5,28,28`). `plan_cond_pair` already declines a second park
  because that register model is uncharacterized; **rule 1b** (`dst == src` or
  refuse) is the same decision one level in.
* **unmasked** (`srwi`), **non-contiguous mask**, **signed source** (`srawi`).
* **the INLINE spelling** — `h3(hp, (at>>27)&8, sz)` emits *byte-identical*
  `.text` and is refused anyway: c1xx puts the arithmetic in the argument's own
  operand stream and `IlOp` has no `Shr`/`BitAnd`. Widening `IlOp` would widen
  every shape that consumes an operand list on one witness. Recorded with its
  bytes in `w42_shift_mask_neg.cpp` §4 rather than closed by guessing.

## 4. W43 — `((P != 0) << SH) | C`, and the selection I refused to claim

`?GetXAllocAttributes@NUISPEECH@@YAKH@Z`, blocker `expr-cmp-ne`, `calls-0 |
cflow-straight`. 24 B, no relocations.

```text
  addic  r11,r3,-1              the `!= 0` fold, unchanged from W6
  lis    r10,0x249b             the constant, materialized INTO the gap
  subfe  r11,r11,r3               between the carry producer and its consumer
  rlwimi r10,r11,30,0,1         the shift and the OR, ONE word
  mr     r3,r10
  blr
```

**Prereg R3 is a MISS in its mechanism and a HIT in its conclusion.** I
registered that `expr-cmp-ne` would be a fall-through key here as well, i.e. that
the shl-only rung would refuse at `09`. It does refuse there — but the reason is
not #150's: `il_bool_materialization.cpp` already emits `addic`+`subfe`
byte-exact, so `expr-cmp-ne` is *genuinely* the last modelled token and the key
names a real construct boundary. The ladder's fixed-point argument does not apply
to a key whose own construct is already emitted.

### 4.1 The `/O1` register is the INCUMBENT rule, and that is the whole reason this is shippable

`subfe` writes **r11 at `/O1`** and **r9 at `/Ox`**. Everything else — opcodes,
order, immediates, the `lis`'s position between the producer and the consumer —
is identical. That is `docs/CODEGEN_W6_O1.md`'s standing rule, verbatim: *a temp
whose defining instruction makes the last use of the value in r11 is written to
r11 instead of taking a fresh descending number*, already graded across that
doc's 108-cell matrix. The `subfe` is the last use of the `addic` result, so it
takes r11; at `/Ox` the descending counter has spent r11 on the `addic` and r10
on the `lis` and hands out r9.

**Nothing new is fitted here.** Had this been a new two-regime rule on three
cells it would have been the moment §3 of the prereg names, and I would have
asked for the one-shot gate instead of shipping.

### 4.2 The selection is a real optimizer decision and I do **not** claim its boundary

c2 has three lowerings and picks between them on `C`'s **bit pattern**:

```text
  slwi rT,rS,SH ; oris r3,rT,C>>16      C's low half 0 and SH <= msb(C)
  slwi rT,rS,SH ; ori  r3,rT,C          C's high half 0
  lis rT,C>>16 ; rlwimi rT,rS,SH,0,31-SH ; mr r3,rT
```

The third is the **longest** of the three, so an instruction count does not
choose it.

**288 cells** — nine constants × `SH ∈ 0..=31` — through real `c2` at the
workload's profile (`work/w-tu1/p/grid_kc.cpp`). The `rlwimi` region does **not**
reduce to any clean rule this lane could state. Two rows break every candidate:

| | |
|---|---|
| `C = 0x80000000` | `rlwimi` at `SH` 1..30 and **something else at 31** |
| `C = 0x00030000` | `msb = 17`, so a `SH > msb(C)` rule predicts 18 — c2 crosses at **16** |

> ### So `shift_or_rlwimi` **does not claim the boundary**. It claims a region strictly inside it — `C_low16 == 0 && SH > msb(C)` — on which **all 288 cells agree and none disagrees**, and it refuses everything else, the two anomalous rows included. `0x80000000` has `msb = 31`, so the region there is *empty*: the row is excluded rather than explained.

This is the difference between a rule fitted 6/6 and shipped, and a **claim
bounded by its own evidence**. The region is smaller than the truth — that is a
gap, and `w43_cmp_shift_or_neg.cpp` §3 carries both rows by name with their
measured bytes so the next lane starts from the anomaly rather than rediscovering
it.

**Prereg R9 is a HIT, in the seam it was registered against and in one more.**
Registered: some `k` in `(x!=0) << k` does not emit `rlwimi`. Measured on a
32-column grid at `C = 0x249b0000`: `k ∈ 0..=28` emit `slwi`+`oris`, only
`k ∈ {29,30,31}` emit `rlwimi`. A rule read off the workload's single cell
(`k = 30`) would have been wrong on **29 of 32 columns of its own row**.

### 4.3 A word-exact check, and the axis it removed from the class

62 cells of the admitted region were compared **word for word** against real c2,
not by mnemonic: **57 matched exactly.** The 5 that did not are the
**parameter-position** axis — with the source formal in any slot but r3, c2 puts
the constant in **r3** and drops the `mr` entirely, five words instead of six.
So the class requires the compared formal to be the function's **only** formal,
which is the gate the plain comparison leaf has always had. The five cells are in
the negative fixture.

### 4.4 The label stride, checked rather than assumed

A W43 body charges the compiler-label counter through `label_slots`'s default
arm. `fixtures/cpp/w43_cmp_shift_or.cpp` ends with `int led(int a) { return
gg(a) + 1; }` — a **framed** function sharing the TU — so a wrong stride would
give `led` the wrong `$M` numbers and the obj would not match. It matches.

## 7. What this says about board #269, stated plainly

I was authorized to exceed **#269**'s decline clause and I did. The honest
accounting:

* **The clause's threshold was right about the cost.** My own re-price said 15
  and the work was nine mechanisms deep. Nobody would have picked this TU off a
  ranking that scores it at 4.
* **The clause's conclusion was wrong about the outcome.** The cost was
  affordable, it converted a TU, and the metric moved for the first time from
  codegen.
* **What made it affordable is not in the price at all**: 2 of the 4 functions
  were *already byte-exact* because a previous rung had been built from this very
  TU and stopped two functions short. A price counted over *blocked* functions
  cannot see that, and it is the single largest term in whether a frontier TU is
  reachable.

> ### The repair I would propose is not to lower #269's threshold. It is to price a frontier TU by **how much of it is already emitted**, not by how much is not. `xboxmem` at 15 refusals and 2-of-4 already byte-exact was reachable in a lane; a TU at 6 refusals with 0-of-1 emitted and a frame, a `.pdata` record and a `$M` number is not, and w-conv's ranking cannot tell them apart.

`mmio.cpp` is the standing test of that proposal: **8 of its 11 emitted functions
are already in class**, its 3 blockers are all one key, and the reprice table
puts it at 5. It is the next TU I would take.

   production `IlOp` cannot represent. `Shr`/`BitAnd` in `IlOp` would close it and

   invisible to the fixture gate. **A `/O1` fixture lane would have caught it
   without a shell script.**

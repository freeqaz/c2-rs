## 1. The result

> ### **THE PRICE CAME TRUE.** w-callprice §5.2 measured the value-tail clause inside `eat_member_stmt_call` at **447 emitted over 13 constructs** and recommended it as **R2**. Built and run over 878 TUs, it converts **444 emitted over 10 constructs — 99.3 % of the priced population**. One lane earlier, #2025's R1 was priced at 2,188 emitted and converted **0**. Same family, same instrument, three orders of magnitude apart in realization; what separated them was that R2's key carried the census's own **`-whole`** bit and R1's did not. **Board #2080.**

> ### **AND FUNCTION BYTE MATCH MOVED BY ZERO.** `fnbyte-exact` is **36,228 before and after**; `fnbyte-differs` goes **2,111 → 2,555 = +444**. Every single function this rung converted is graded by the oracle's own bytes and **not one is byte-exact**. The emitted census went up by 444 and the byte judge went up by nothing. **Board #2081.**

> ### **THE REASON IS ONE WORD: c2 INLINES THEM.** `?SplitMs@Timer@@QAAMXZ` is **434 of the 444, and it is the ONLY name on the new census key.** In the fixture its callees are declared and not defined, so c2 emits `bl · bl` and the port is byte-exact — a whole-TU `match` at `/O1` **and** `/Ox`. In the workload `Timer::Split()` and `Timer::Ms()` are inline in the same header, so c2 emits **31 words where the port emits 13**, with `Ms()`'s `lfd`/`fcfid` among the words c2 has and the port does not. The class is byte-exact exactly where the callees are opaque, and on this workload they never are. **Board #2082.**

> ### **THE `_fltused` OBLIGATION NEEDED NO NEW INSERTION POINT, AND THE FENCE THAT CARRIES IT IS A MISSING FIELD.** `SeqTail::CallValueFp` is the **fifth** producer of `touches_floating_point` and the first that emits **no FP instruction at all** — c2's listing shows the float body and the `int` body are the same instruction stream, and the only difference in the obj is one undefined external. The variant has **no `add_k` field**, so the `addi r3,r3,k` that would be nonsense on a value in `f1` is unrepresentable rather than guarded. **Board #2083.**

> ### **THE SAME-WIDTH RULE IS ONE REQUIREMENT AND THE IL DRAWS IT ITSELF.** A converted real result carries an explicit `2C <TYPE> 00` between the `4C` and the `41`, in **both** directions — and only one direction costs an instruction (`float`←`double` is `frsp fr1,fr1`; `double`←`float` is **nothing**). Requiring the `41` to stand immediately after the `4C` refuses both. **A free conversion is given up because the fence that refuses the costly one cannot tell them apart**, and that is stated as decline **D6** rather than smuggled. **Board #2084.**

> ### **w-mcall's `_neg` CELL N6 WAS FULLY PAID AND ITS OWN FIXTURE COULD NOT SEE IT.** `wmcall_seq_neg.cpp` graded `Port=NotImplemented` before and after this rung, because a `_neg` fixture's graded property is a **whole-TU refusal** — one of its six cells silently becoming a positive changes nothing it measures. Found by censusing the file, not by the gate. Re-taken, not deleted. **Board #2085.**

> ### **THREE OF THE FOUR REFUSALS THIS RUNG ADDS ARE INERT, MEASURED WITH AN INSTRUMENT THAT COULD SEE THEM.** `callseq-fp-tail-postop`, `callseq-fp-tail-result-type` and `callseq-fp-tail-result-not-real` fire on **0 of 2,463,443 bodies**. ## 6. THE FINDING: 444 converted, 0 byte-exact, and the reason is the inliner

`fnbyte-exact` **36,228 → 36,228**. `fnbyte-differs` **2,111 → 2,555**. Every
one of the 444 is graded by the judge's own per-function byte test and every one
**differs**. `work/w-fltret/fbm.py` over the tip's `--fnbyte-diff-jsonl`:


The reference `?SplitMs` is **31 words** where the port emits 13, and the words
c2 has and the port does not are `Split()`'s body and `Ms()`'s —
`e9630008` (`lfd`), `3d400000`/`3d200000` (`lis` pairs), `fc00069c` (`fcfid`).
**c2 inlined both callees.** They are `inline` members of `Timer` in the same
header; in the fixture the callees are declared and not defined, c2 cannot
inline them, and the port is byte-exact. The nine `vector::at` rows are the same
mechanism with the sign reversed — c2's body is *shorter* (12–13 words against
the port's 17).

**Read precisely, this is not a wrong emit and it is not a retraction.**
`mismatch` is 0, `IlBundle::functions()` is untouched, and all 434 TUs are
`vocab-gap` — no obj has ever carried one of these bodies. What is wrong is the
**emitted census's claim**, which is STATUS.md trap 2 in its standing form, and
the population it wrongly claims is now 444 larger. It is visible on a standing
instrument, by name, which is what STATUS.md asks of exactly this hazard.

**What it says about the metric:** w-callprice priced R2 on the emitted column
because *"the body ranking and the emitted ranking disagree by 13×"*. That was
right, and the emitted column moved by 444 — and the byte judge moved by **0**.
This is a **ninth** instance of the ranking-instruments lesson and a **fourth
mechanism**: a column that ranks correctly, a clause that converts almost
exactly what it was priced at, and no bytes. Board **#2081**.

The remaining distance for these 444 is `crates/c2-core/src/splice.rs`'s
territory — the inliner — and this rung deliberately **refuses** to let
`CallValueFp` through S3 (`S3-seq-tail-callvaluefp`), because whether a caller
still owes `_fltused` once its only FP-returning call has been spliced away has
never been captured.

## 1. THE RESULT, up front

> ### **1. THE SELECTOR, AND IT IS BOARD #3107'S OWN RULE APPLIED TO THE OTHER FIVE.** `w-deaccept` proved that `w-readphase` §4.2's residue ranking is a ranking of a **counterfactual population** — `expr-op-0x5D`/`0x5E`, published as *"the single largest residue item, 13,158"*, do not exist as keys at base. **Nobody had applied that rule to the remaining five.** At base: **`0x9A` = 0** · **`0x00` = 0** · compound-assign = **45**, not 5,269 (**117×**) · `0x64` = **422**, not 8,000 (**19×**) · and the brief's seventh item `op:BD` = **31**, not 40,530. **The statement layer reads 7,911 against a published ceiling of 7,903 — the only agreement in the table.**

> ### **2. AND THE REASON IT IS THE ONLY AGREEMENT IS STRUCTURAL, WHICH MAKES IT A RULE RATHER THAN A COINCIDENCE. EVERY SINK INSTRUMENT IN THIS TREE IS CONSULTED FROM INSIDE `parse_expr`.** `chain_sink` (#660), `branch_sink` (#440), `rel_sink_enabled` (#420) and `off_add_sink_enabled` (#143) are all reached from `parse_expr_classed`, so **a census key raised outside `parse_expr` is invariant under every ceiling any of them can measure.** Measured rather than argued: the five statement-layer keys read **7,911 at base and 7,911 at the full 49-token + `type`/`convert`/`intrinsic` ceiling** — `+0` on every one of the five — while the other 610 keys of the 615 moved. The published ceiling was measured with **25.0 % of its own residue held fixed**.

> ### **3. THE PUBLISHED CEILING IS CORRECTED, AND THE ADDITION IS EXACT RATHER THAN ESTIMATED: 88,806 → 93,990 OF 120,456 (73.7 % → 78.0 %).** The `base → stmt-sink` key diff and the `chain-ceiling → both-sinks` key diff are **identical, key for key and count for count**. So the expression layer and the statement layer are **orthogonal** on this workload — they share not one function — and the two ceilings compose by addition. `stmt-chain-fntail` **3,684** + `rsc-chain-fntail` **1,500** = **5,184**.

> ### **4. THE FINDING THE LANE EXISTS FOR: THE GAIN IS A STEP FUNCTION, NOT A RAMP, AND A MASS RANKING STRUCTURALLY CANNOT FIND ITS STEP.** A greedy ladder over the statement-layer population, granting the current head token each round exactly as every published ladder in this repo does, runs **19 rungs** — `29`, `9B`, `53`, `B9`, `26`, `BD`, `33`, `2C`, `55`, `4C`, `32`, `30`, `67`, `20`, `44`, `27`, `43`, `4B`, `4F` — and the reach is **0 at every single one**. The residue never drops below 7,911. The three tokens that produce any gain at all are `54`, `3A` and `29` **together**: 19 tokens + any one or two of them is still **0**, and all three is **860**. A terminator is **never a first blocker**, so no first-blocker ranking will ever name one.

> ### **5. AND THE LEAVE-ONE-OUT SAYS THE SAME THING FROM THE OTHER END, CATEGORICALLY. THE MARGINAL WORTHS SUM TO 98,039 AGAINST A TOTAL OF 5,184 — 18.9×.** Three tokens — `op:54`, `op:3A`, `op:29` — are **each individually worth the ENTIRE reach**: remove any one from the 49-token spec and the reach goes **5,184 → 0**. **`op:29` therefore reads `0` as a rung-1 grant and `5,184` as a leave-one-out margin — the same token, two standard instruments, an infinite spread.** Meanwhile **`op:67` is worth exactly ZERO at the margin**, and `body-0x67` is 1,044 emitted functions, the fourth-largest statement-layer key.

> ### **6. SO ARM R IS DECLINED, PRICED, AND THE PRICE IS MEASURED RATHER THAN ARGUED.** No incremental reader widening in the statement layer can move `fnbyte-refused-parse`: the cost of refusing to ship one is **0 emitted functions**, measured at 20 granted tokens, and the width-walk reach of 5,184 is an upper bound on an *accept* that would additionally need the whole class model. The cost of shipping one is `IL_STMT_GRAMMAR.md` §14.2 step 5's fail-closed boundary unpaid — a decoded label is not a lowered CFG — plus `w-readphase` §7's live pre-emption hazard. **The statement layer is a PHASE, not a rung**, and this lane's number is why.

## 3. THE INSTRUMENT

`C2RS_SINK_STMT`, in `expr.rs` beside the four it joins. It takes **the same
spec grammar** `C2RS_SINK_CHAIN` takes, parsed by the same `ChainSink::parse`,
so the two compose and one spec measures the whole body.

**Two call sites, and they deliberately do not share a bucket.**
`parse_body`'s dispatcher `_` arm reports under `stmt-chain-*` and
`eat_return_head`'s scope-close run under `rsc-chain-*` — exactly as
`body-cflow-label` and `return-scope-close-cflow-label` are two rows today on
purpose (`Block::feature`'s own comment: *"the production they interrupted is
different work"*). Both are a `0x29`; one bucket would have been the merge
`GAPS.md` §6 calls the one failure a census instrument cannot survive, and §5
shows their successors genuinely differ.

**The poison is structural, not a flag.** `stmt_sink_walk` returns
`Option<Block>` — it has **no representation for accepting** — and is consulted
only on a path that has already decided to return `Err`. It replaces the key on
a refusal, never the verdict. Board **#3094**'s lesson was that a poisoned
instrument can still *de-accept*; this one cannot, and the measurement confirms
it: at the full 49-token spec, `match` **25** and `fnbyte-exact` **35,734**,
both unmoved.

**The terminal is resolved in the shipping code, not in a scratch tree.**
`w-readphase` §4.1's whole 76,041 turned on whether `expr-chain-noform-0x4F`
meant *"fully walked"* or *"an undecoded mid-body marker"*, and it had to split
the arm in a scratch tree to find out. `FN_TAIL` is checked **before** the step
and reports `<site>-fntail`, so the successor question can never be asked of an
ambiguous key again. §6's mutant B is that guard removed.

   rung.

## 2. The instrument

`crates/c2-harness/src/gap/fnbytes.rs`, on the `differs` path only — the same
place `differ_witness` already pays for evidence. Per differing function, keyed
per `(TU, symbol)` on **`FnCensus::emit_name`** (**#918**):

| key | what it carries |
|---|---|
| `fnbyte-differs-why\|shape\|n\|dispositions\|refblr\|sym` | the port's own callee set, and each callee's **disposition** in this TU |
| `fnbyte-splice\|…` / `-splice-fn\|…` / `-splice-why\|…` | **SPLICE-P** and the first word it gets wrong |
| `fnbyte-splice0\|…` / `-splice0-fn\|…` | **SPLICE-0** and the first word it gets wrong |
| `fnbyte-spliceN\|…` | the multi-call concatenation |
| `fnbyte-contains\|…` | is the callee's code inside c2's body **at all** |
| `fnbyte-callee-resolved-{emit,mangled}` | **#918's control**, both bindings, positive counts |

**A callee's disposition is graded, not asserted.** `body:exact` means the
*port's own* `/Gy` body for that callee is byte-identical to c2's COMDAT for it
in the same obj — so "can the port lower the callee" is the judge's answer and
not the census's claim. The refusal disposition carries `FnVerdict::key()`, the
blocking-feature key a widening rung is priced in; the gate's own `&'static str`
is the coarse `blocked` and printing it named nothing (it read
`refused:blocked` 1,774 times before this was fixed).

### 2.1 The two splice hypotheses, and why there are two

> **SPLICE-P** — `splice(F) = port_body(F)[.. len-4] ++ ref_body(G)`.
> The port keeps its argument setup and substitutes c2's own callee body for the
> branch word.
>
> **SPLICE-0** — `splice0(F) = ref_body(G)`. The setup goes too.

Both are graded by **real c2 on the whole workload**, not on hand cells, because
the reference obj carries *both* COMDATs: `w-empty` §2 and `INLINE_PREDICATE.md`
§2 both record that c2 emits a callee whether or not it inlined it, and that is
what makes the right-hand side readable. `ref_body(G)` is resolved **by name
through the COMDAT table** (#644), never by position.

**SPLICE-0 was not in the prereg.** It was written after SPLICE-P's `pw > 1`
stratum came back at 0 of 953 and the failure witnesses showed the port's *setup
word* at index 0 every time. It is scored as an unregistered hypothesis in §7 and
its result is the lane's finding, which is exactly why P1's registered form is
recorded as a loss rather than restated.

## 6. THE SPEC — what the emitter change would be, and what it may not be

**Nothing is shipped.** This section exists so the next brief does not
re-derive it.

> **SPLICE-0-PORT (proposed, NOT shipped).** For a function `select_function`
> classifies `Selected::{Tail, Seq}` whose call set names **exactly one** callee
> `G`, with `G` defined in this bundle and the port's own `/Gy` body for `G`
> available: emit **`G`'s body** as this function's `/Gy` COMDAT, with no branch,
> no REL24 and no external symbol on the caller's account.

Its measured worth **on today's port**, with no parser widening at all, is the
population where SPLICE-0 is exact **and** the callee's disposition is
`body:exact`:

| | pairs |
|---|---:|
| SPLICE-0 exact | 1,967 |
| — and the port already lowers the callee **byte-exactly** | **726** |
| — of which `seq` | 634 |
| — of which `tail` | 92 |

**726 is the number to size a rung at, and 1,967 is not.** The other 1,241 need a
parser widening first, because the port has no bytes for the callee.

### 6.1 The five things this spec is NOT allowed to assume

1. **It is not INLINE-P.** SPLICE-0 says what the bytes are *when c2 inlined*; it
   says nothing about *whether* c2 inlines. `INLINE_PREDICATE.md` §7 leaves that
   at 2.84 % residual and **NOT MODELLED**, and 2.84 % of a guess is a wrong
   emit (`w-empty` §2.2 declined a match on exactly this ground). A shipped rule
   needs a decision procedure, and this lane did not build one.
2. **It must not fire when the port emits a setup.** SPLICE-P is 0 of 953 there,
   and the three fields §4.2 names are a *rewriting* of the callee's body, not a
   concatenation.
3. **It must not fire on `framed`.** 0 of 123, every one a destination-register
   rename.
4. **It must not fire on a multi-call body.** SPLICE-N is 0 of 548.
5. **It must not be keyed on `mangled_name`.** §8's control: the callee
   resolution rate is **3,702 of 3,928 under `emit_name` and 546 under
   `mangled_name`** — a 6.8× difference on this population, which is #918 with a
   number attached to the exact lookup this spec needs.

## 10. Found and not taken

1. **SPLICE-0-PORT is worth 726 today and 1,967 behind a parser widening** (§6).
   Not taken: this lane measured it, and shipping an emitter change in the lane
   that measured it is the retreat `w-fnbyte` §8.1 forbids. It also needs a
   decision procedure for *whether* c2 inlined, which is `INLINE_PREDICATE.md`'s
   open residual and not this lane's.
2. **The three fields are a REWRITING and they look cheap** (§4.2). A destination
   rename (123), a source rename (286) and a displacement fold (~92) are 501 more
   functions, and each is one field of one instruction. **Do not take that as a
   pricing** — the rename that produced `?back@?$vector` picked `r11` because a
   later instruction needed `r3`, which is a register allocator's decision and
   not a field edit; the measurement says *what changed*, not *what decides it*.
3. **`expr-op-0x27` is the largest single refusal production behind a differ
   (573) and it converts nothing on its own** (§5.1). It is also `w-rtti`'s
   blocker on the `.rdata$r` ladder. Two rungs, one dependency, and neither can
   quote the other's worth as its own.
4. **`fnbyte-contains` is published and under-reads by exactly the rename**
   (`tail` 1,151 yes / 380 no; `seq` 855 / 1,242; **`framed` 0 / 123**). The
   containment test is byte-exact on a word run, so a body whose only change is a
   register field reads `no`. That is why it is printed beside SPLICE-0 and never
   instead of it.
5. **`s12` reproduces #882 with a compiled obj** (§4.3): two bodies that are both
   `48000000` with different relocation targets, and FBM calls them `exact`.
   `fnbyte-exact-relocated` is 4,664 and this is its four-line reproducer.
6. **The 177 ungraded multi-call `seq` differs** name a callee with no `.text`
   COMDAT in the reference obj at all — c2 emitted the caller and not the callee.
   That is a *different* fact from either mechanism and it is **NOT MODELLED**;
   it is 5.5 % of the population and it is counted, not absorbed.

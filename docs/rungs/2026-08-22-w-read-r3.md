## 2. The three findings, shortest form

**#3387 — closure buys the sites, not the charge.** `READ_PLAN` row R3 said
*"closed by construction (one increment instruction)"*. One increment: true,
and stronger than stated. The call sites are the whole population: true, and
the lane supplies the argument the row did not — a direct `call` encodes a
*relative* displacement, so a function whose absolute VA never appears as data
cannot be reached indirectly, and `FUN_10b97dd0`'s VA occurs **zero** times as
data anywhere in the image. Therefore the charge is a per-construct constant:
**false** — **42 of the 163 sites sit on loop back edges**, the decisive one a
nested loop over a 1,024-bucket symbol table that numbers every symbol c2
minted. `charge(TU) = |objects c2 constructs itself|`, a sum, not a table.

**#3388 — `LABEL_SEED_GAP = 9` is not a constant.** `7 + 2·[/Og] + 1·[/GF ∧ a
string literal pooled in the data phase]`, over 22 cells. `/Od` reads **7** and
is one of the 18 graded lanes. **Latent, not live**: all 21 `/Od` matches are
data-only TUs that emit no `$M`, and the `/O1`+string shape returns
`Port=NotImplemented`. What is live is the *licence*.

**#3389 — naming never charges.** The formatter's 201-function subtree has
zero calls to the allocator, and the `$L*` numbers come from a *second*
counter over a *different* population. That is the mechanism behind a seam
`LABEL_COUNTER.md` §7.3 had closed by measurement alone.

**#3390 — the lane's own instrument fault**, caught before publication. §4.

## 4. The instrument fault, and what the controls could not catch

**The first version of `gt_label_seedgap.py`'s grid reported the gap moving
9 → 10 → 11 → 12 → 13 → 14, and every one of those numbers was
`9 + the functions sitting ahead of the subject`.** A string-returning leaf
ahead of `f0` reads 10; three of them 12; an FP leaf 11 (`9 + 1 + _fltused`);
an FP leaf with a pooled constant 13; a static+framed pair 14 (`9 + 1 + 4`).
Linear, plausible, and entirely an artifact. Caught before a verdict was
published; the guard is now in the script (`before == 0` asserted from the
obj's own symbol order, else the row prints **VOID**) and the episode is a
comment in its source.

> **The transferable rule.** An *absolute*, seed-relative instrument has a
> confound the in-TU instrument does not — everything between the seed and the
> subject — and it must **assert** nothing is in between rather than assume
> it. `LABEL_COUNTER.md` §7.6 step 5 is the same sentence from the other side.

**The registered self-test is what made the second reading trustworthy.** The
prereg gated the lane on reproducing the banner's own cells: `s_loc2`
counterfactual **+2**, `s_loc8` **+8**, true in-TU charge **0** on both. GREEN,
to the digit. A miss there would have made the outcome word `FAILED` whatever
else the lane produced.

**What the controls could NOT catch**, stated in the prereg and unchanged:

* **any site no probe executes** — the PGO-gated `$zz`/`$zy` sites, the
  `.cil$` IL-embedding sites and the intermodule-thunk sites were read and
  never executed. `[R]`, and they stay `[R]`;
* **two sites that always fire together** — nothing here separates "one
  constructor takes two numbers" from "two take one each"; the `+2` rows are
  reported as pairs;
* **order** — no probe here places a label;
* **`/Ox`'s loop charge** — four magnitudes, no rule; the lane names `lur.c`
  (six label constructors, 15,115 lines, unread) as the mechanism and stops;
* and the sharpest one: **the standing `/Od` lane is, for the seed gap,
  exactly the structurally-incapable control the prereg was written against.**
  It is green because every one of its 21 matching TUs is label-free. That is
  why the probe set was fixed in advance to require a stride ≥ 3 subject, two
  functions after it, a dedup cell and an instrument self-test.

### 4.3 `expr-op-0x27` is a FALL-THROUGH on this body, and the ladder proves it byte for byte

The census reports `expr-op-0x27` for `xboxheap.cpp`, for **402,148** functions
workload-wide (23.0 %, the #1 blocking feature), and for **every refusing cell
of the ladder** — `x3`, `x4`, `x5`, `x6`, `x0`. The blocked byte in `x3`, `x4`,
`x6` and `x0` is **identical, in the same 40-byte context**:

```text
53 4f 01 0e  b9 ff 09  a6 43 81 20  33 86 41 74  10 >27<  a6 43 f5 08 …
                                                  ^^ offset 16 = `mSize`
```

`x6` has **no member address anywhere** and `x3` has **no call anywhere**, and
they block at the same byte — a `this + 16` off-add that is the base address of
`mSize = size`, **a store `x1` also contains and matches on**. The key names
where the *generic expression walk* stopped after the whole-body `store-run`
production rejected, not what made the body leave that production.

Two consequences, and the second is the one that moves a number:

* it is boards **#420**/**#440**/**#441** again (*"the head of the blocker
  ladder is a fixed point of the sink operation, not a name for work"*) and
  board **#1091** (*"the census key is the first blocking feature, not a
  description of the body"*) — now with a **byte-identical context across two
  disjoint constructs** as the witness, which is the sharpest form the claim
  has had;
* **the chain depth over-counts any body whose acceptance runs through a
  whole-body production.** `xboxheap`'s `D = 5` (`op:27, op:32, op:26, op:99,
  op:BD`) walks a path the accepting reader never takes. Its by-construction
  count is **2**. Where the two disagree, **the ladder wins** — and this is the
  first measured instance of `D` being an *over*-estimate, where every prior
  caveat on it was about it being an under-estimate.

Board **#622**'s *"`0x27 → 0x32`, two operators deep, converts nothing"* stands
as measured and is **not** the price of this TU.

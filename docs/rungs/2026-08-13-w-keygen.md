### 2.2 Two refusals this lane charged and then withdrew

Stated first, because they are the useful half. An ad-hoc relocation table off
by one convention reads keygen's `.pdata` record as **ADDR64** and its data
references as **TOCREL16/TOCREL14 paired against `@comp.id`**. Both would have
been refusals — the port's writer emits neither. Cross-checked against the
port's own constants in `crates/c2-core/src/coff/reloc.rs` (`ADDR32 0x0002`,
`REL24 0x0006`, `REFHI 0x0010`, `REFLO 0x0011`, `PAIR 0x0012`), the record is
`ADDR32` and the data references are the **absolute REFHI/PAIR + REFLO/PAIR
quad the writer already emits** — including against an *undefined* external,
which is `coff/writer.rs`'s WR1 in as many words.

**So `.pdata` and extern-data addressing are already paid**, and this is
`w-wordwrap2`'s finding repeating: five of that lane's six `.bss` layout rules
were already in the tree. A price is not derived until the writer has been
asked what it already does.

### 3.2 (c) Is the 14-word `?shuffle2` gap an inline decision?

**Yes — and both published statements about it need amending.**

**First: it is an inline decision, and `INLINE-P` gets it right.** Graded by the
project's own instrument on `keygen_xbox.cpp`'s 17 in-TU callees:

| leaf reading | accuracy | |
|---|---:|---|
| from the `/O1` obj | **11/17** | 6 false inlines — this is where *"predicts SIX and gets ONE"* comes from |
| **dropped — the frozen configuration** | **17/17** | precision 1.000, recall 1.000 |
| source-leaf | 16/17 | one false inline, `?asciiDigitToHex` |

> **Board #1843 — *"`INLINE-P` predicts SIX and gets ONE"* — is an artifact of
> reading the leaf bit off the `/O1` obj**, which `INLINE_PREDICATE.md` §5 had
> already measured as the *worst* of the three readings and warned about in
> exactly these terms: *"`/O1` over-reports leaf: a callee whose calls were
> inlined away reads LEAF."* The six shuffles are that case precisely — they
> call `swap`/`roll` in **source**, c2 inlined those away, and the `/O1` obj
> then reads all six as leaves and subtracts 48 from each.
>
> In the configuration the 0.9716 was measured in, `index = s` for these
> (one parameter, `SELECT_NODUPLICATES`, so no `inline` term), the sizes are
> **104 / 60 / 84 / 84 / 88 / 88**, and the `≤ 64` arm selects **`?shuffle2`
> alone**. #1843's own numbers, read with #1843's own rule in its published
> configuration, give #1843's own answer. **No separate "loop-class ceiling at
> (60,84]" is needed to explain this TU** — that clause may well exist in the
> image, but the anchor is not evidence for it.
>
> Stated against over-claiming: **17/17 on n = 17 is not evidence the rule beats
> 0.9681**, and the first version of this paragraph got the reason backwards.
> It read *"this TU has no boundary case — every callee ≥ 12 bytes clear of the
> step"*. **It has two.** The callees run index 16…108, and the pair straddling
> the 64/68 step is `?shuffle2` at **60** — four below 64 — and
> `?asciiDigitToHex` at **76** — eight above 68. §7 puts the rule's whole 2.84 %
> residual *"within ±8 bytes of the step"*, so **both of this TU's boundary
> cells are inside the residual band and the rule got both right**.
>
> That is a better result than the claim it replaces and still not a rate: n is
> 17, and the two cells are one observation each. It is also exactly where the
> *source-leaf* variant's single miss lands — `?asciiDigitToHex`, `76 − 48 =
> 28`, predicted INLINE — so the three readings are separated by the boundary
> cells and by nothing else. Trust the direction of this result, not its rate.

**Second: the inlined bytes are `SPLICE-0`, not a re-allocation.**
`WB_INLINE_FINDINGS.md` §6.3 and board **#1844** both say the copy is *"14
frameless re-allocated words, not `?shuffle2`'s own 15-word COMDAT"*, and #1844
prices the remedy as *"a lowering for arbitrary loop bodies **into the caller's
register allocation**"*. Measured with `work/w-keygen/anchor.py` — take
`?shuffle2`'s own `.text` COMDAT, drop its trailing `blr`, search
`?supershuffle`'s words for that run:

```
?shuffle2@@YAXPAD@Z: 15 words, SPLICE-0 run = 14 words
  ?supershuffle@@YAXPAD@Z   26 w  bl:5  run@[4]  SPLICE-0 EXACT
```

**0 of 14 words differ.** No rename, no displacement fold, no reorder. The
callee's parameter is `r3` and so is the caller's, and c2 keeps it there — which
is #1573's interprocedural finding seen from the other side. This is exactly
`w-seq`'s **SPLICE-0** (#968) and exactly what `crates/c2-core/src/splice.rs`
already ships; what it is *not* is a whole-body site.

**This lowers the anchor's mechanism price and moves none of its conversion
price.** Refusal 19 is a widening of `splice.rs` from "the caller's whole body
is one call" to "a call in a sequence", not a register allocator — but the port
still cannot lower `?shuffle2` (refusals 7 and 12), and eighteen other bodies
are still blocked. **#1844's decline stands; its reasoning does not.**

### 3.3 (b) Would shipping mechanism I *fenced* move anything?

**No — measured three ways, and the third is the one that settles it.**

**1. The fence's whole workload reach is one function.** Over all 878 TUs,
`callee-defined-in-tu:eof` is the first blocker of **1 function on 1 TU**, and
that function is `?supershuffle@@YAXPAD@Z`. This reproduces `w-inlfence` /
board **#2222** from an independent scan.

**2. No decline-side fence is safe near the step.** In the frozen configuration,
**79 of 2,523** predicted-DECLINED callees are observed **INLINED-ALL** — the
fatal direction: the port would emit a `bl` where c2 emitted a body. Their index
distribution, and the threshold a fence would need:

| fence threshold `index ≥ T` | callees still called DECLINED | of which **WRONG** |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 64 (the published step) | 2,523 | **79** |
| 80 | 1,935 | 5 |
| 112 | 1,295 | 1 |
| 192 | 550 | 1 |
| **256** | **401** | **0** |

77 of the 79 sit in `index ∈ [64, 96)` — hard against the step — with outliers
at 100 and **204**. A fence reaches zero false declines only at `T = 256`,
**four times the published boundary**, where it can speak for 401 of 8,916
callees (4.5 %). And that zero is a sample bound with one datum of margin, not a
proof.

**3. Even a perfect fence emits nothing here.** Deciding *"c2 inlined
`?shuffle2`"* does not produce the 14 words; producing them means lowering
`?shuffle2`, which is refused at `expr-jump` and has no CFG class. The fence's
precondition and the port's gap are the same object.

**Verdict: do not ship it.** `INLINE_PREDICATE.md` §6 item 4 already says *"do
not narrow `functions()` on I"*, and the 79 are what that sentence is worth in
this workload.

## 6. Found and not taken

1. **`expr-op-0x30` is the dereference, and it blocks 20,099 functions across
   828 of 878 TUs** — second only to `expr-op-0x27` (off-add, 400,819 / 841).
   It is `?swap`'s only first blocker and `?swap` is five words of
   `lbz/lbz/stb/stb/blr` in `cflow-straight`, a class the emitter **has**. That
   makes it the single largest reader widening visible from this TU and the one
   a lane should size next. **Not taken here**: 828 TUs is a blast radius that
   needs its own grid over pointee type, arity and operand position, and board
   **#1465** says an opcode grant is as likely to *rename* the refusal as to
   lift it. Do not take it as a side effect of anything.
2. **The COUNT axis breaks `SPLICE-0` and nothing had varied it.**
   `wkg_splice_pos.cpp`'s `kg_two_spliced` inlines one callee twice; c2 hoists
   the address computation out of the repeated copy and re-derives each one's
   pointers from the hoisted registers, so the two runs differ from the COMDAT
   by 5 and 3 words and the caller acquires an `r31` save. `w-seq`'s `SPLICE-N`
   (0 of 548) is *different* callees concatenated and has never seen this. Any
   interior-splice widening must refuse count > 1 until it is gridded.
3. **`?asciiDigitToHex` is the source-leaf reading's only miss on this TU**
   (`index = 76 − 48 = 28`, predicted INLINE, c2 declined) and it is the only
   callee here with a *branchy* body. §5's *"leaf's true input is NOT
   MODELLED"* has a candidate discriminator sitting in it that no cell tests.
4. **A hold-out frozen by TU name is not frozen by content.** `sample_b.txt`
   lost 10.8 % of its callee population without a line changing. Whatever
   re-freezes it should record content hashes.

The two remaining rows are not "expensive versions of the same thing". They are

converts a TU with, plus **four refusals whose entire workload population is
this one TU**. Codegen breadth buys `fnbyte-exact` on the first kind and buys
one TU on the second, and only both together buy a conversion.

The honest next question is therefore not *which frontier TU* but *whether the
`fnbyte-exact` driver is worth spending on when the target has no reachable
row left* — `CEILING.md` §6.2's call, which this page does not make.

## 2. The declared bias was `ChunkIDs.cpp`, and the pre-committed mitigation killed it

Prereg §1 named the bias — 15 `const ChunkID kX("LIST")` objects looked like the
proven shape ×15 and the only route from TU match 8 to 9 — and pre-committed to
**dumping the real obj before writing any emitter line**. That is what happened,
and it is the reason no emitter was written against a wrong model.

`ChunkIDs.cpp`'s `??__E` thunk is **not** the proven tail-call shape. The
constructor is *inlined*:

```
3d 60 00 00   lis  r11, ??_C@_04PCOPKCKE@LIST?$AA@
3d 40 00 00   lis  r10, ?kListChunkID@@3VChunkID@@B
81 6b 00 00   lwz  r11, …(r11)
91 6a 00 00   stw  r11, …(r10)
4e 80 00 20   blr
```

**20 bytes, 8 relocations, no REL24, and it returns rather than tail-branching.**
`dyninit_thunk_text` emits 24 bytes / 9 relocations ending in `b ctor`. The TU
needs **four** new capabilities, not one:

1. the inlined-constructor thunk body above;
2. ≥2 objects (the assigned target);
3. an ordinary `.text` COMDAT — `??0ChunkID@@QAA@PBD@Z`, 36 bytes of unrolled
   `lbz`/`stb`, co-resident with dyninit groups;
4. a 4-byte non-dyninit `.rdata` **before** `.text` holding
   `?npos@?$basic_string@…@2IB`, an stlport static data member. That is a fourth
   section-order slot Rule S1 does not describe.

**Prediction A1 (final TU match 8) stands; its named alternative (9) is
refuted**, and the refutation is specific rather than "it turned out to be
harder".

## 4. An instrument fault in my own measurements, found and controlled

**`c2rs capture` hardcodes `/Ox /GS- /c` and ignores flags** — `cmd_capture`
calls `Toolchain::capture_il`, whose flag list is a literal; only
`capture_il_with` takes flags, and no CLI path reaches it. So every `.gl` above
was captured at `/Ox` while every obj was compiled at `/O1`: **a cross-flag
correlation I should not have had.** `/Ox` does not imply `/GF`, which is the
exact trap `gl_string_comdat_names` exists to catch, so this is not a hypothetical.

Controlled rather than argued away. Recompiling all three probes at `/Ox` and
comparing `.bss` against the `/O1` objs:

| probe | `.bss` at `/Ox` | `.bss` at `/O1` |
|---|---|---|
| `p6` (6 × 1 B) | `s2@5 s1@4 s5@3 s3@2 s4@1 s6@0` | **identical** |
| `psize` (1/8/4 B) | `sB@8 sA@4 sC@0` | **identical** |
| `pmix` (mixed linkage) | `sA@3 gB@2 gD@1 sC@0` | **identical** |

So the correlations in §3 now rest on a **same-flags** instrument (`/Ox` `.gl`
against the `/Ox` obj), and the deferred `.bss` walk is additionally shown to be
**invariant across `/Ox` ↔ `/O1`** — it does not key on `/GF`. The skew was real
and harmless, in that order.

The fix belongs in `crates/c2-harness` (`cmd_capture` should take
`--flags-file`), which is lane w-fifth's seam, so it is **proposed, not made**.

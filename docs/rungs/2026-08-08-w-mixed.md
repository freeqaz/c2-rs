## 3. H-MIX, and the mechanism that died before the grid was built

**The hypothesis.** Board **#892**'s `cu <= ru + 1` is the best-scoring rule on
record — 67 of 77 over w-ilx's two populations, beating the IL-derived `KEY ILX`
on `KEY ILX`'s own frozen holdout — and board **#912** has been asking for a
frozen grid for it since 2026-08-07. H-MIX is that rule plus one term:

```text
  H-MIX   the producer takes POOL_TOP  iff  cu <= ru + 1 + b
            b = 1 when the address-valued stores go through a bound reference
                distinct from the literal stores' base (w-spell's `2base`)
          DOMAIN: two producers, one an interior address that is a PREFIX of
          every address it is stored into, the other one `li`.
```

It is **41 of 41** over every in-domain cell of `w-ilx` GRID S/X/V and `w-spell`
GRID H, 15 of them on frozen holdouts, and it repairs every miss those tables
record inside its domain. **That is exactly the standing `RULE W2` had at 388 of
388 and `RULE BIND` had at 33 of 33, which is none** (#912), and the prereg says
so: the `b` term was read off three of those cells, **so not one of the 41
counts as evidence**. The lane therefore **fits nothing** — H-MIX is frozen as
stated and graded once.

### 3.1 The mechanism was refuted by a disassembly committed six days earlier

`PREREG.md` §2.1 argued that `b` is **not** a kind bonus: that in the bind
spelling the address register is read one extra time, as the store BASE, so
H-MIX is `cu <= u + 1` with `u` = *register reads* and the six lanes before it
had miscounted. **`work/w-spell/gridS_dis.txt` refutes that**, and
`PREREG_ADDENDUM_1.md` §A1.1 records it **before the generator existed**:

```text
  == S-self-1base-r2k3            == S-self-2base-r2k3
  li   10, 7                      li   10, 7
  addi 11, 3, 96                  addi 11, 3, 96
  stw  10, 32(3)                  stw  10, 32(3)
  stw  11, 96(3)                  stw  10, 36(3)
  stw  10, 36(3)                  stw  10, 40(3)
  stw  10, 40(3)                  stw  11, 96(3)
  stw  11, 100(3)                 stw  11, 100(3)
```

The bind is folded to `r3` plus a displacement in **both**; `r11` is read exactly
`ru` times in both. The two cells differ in their store **SCHEDULE** and in
nothing the register file can see. That is board **#1128** (*a bind IS a second
base symbol*) showing up in `order`, where it was already known to be, and **not**
in `alloc`. Board **#1222**.

So `b` reverted to what the record already knew it was: a **free parameter fitted
on three cells**, with no account of why the allocator would read the scheduler's
symbol structure. The addendum registered the consequence — **P3 becomes the
more likely outcome, P1 more likely still** — and both graded HIT.

## 5. The decision

**DECLINED. The answer is the refusal that already ships**, and the lane's own
prereg floor **F-1** said so before the grade: *if the frozen holdout shows any
wrong emit, the answer is the refusal, and this lane writes the eighth graveyard
entry.* It shows twelve.

`cu <= ru + 1` at 60 of 62 is the closest anything has come, and it is **not
proposed for shipping**, for the reason the port's correctness rule gives: a rule
that is right 97 % of the time loses to a refusal that is right 100 % of the time
on what it refuses. Nor may a successor **narrow around `SELF-2B`** — that is a
gate drawn around the failing cells, it is how all eight refuted keys were
written, and F-1 forbade it in advance.

Two further reasons, both independent of the allocation:

1. **Shipping an allocation rule converts nothing**, because §2's ladder says
   `xboxheap` is behind two more reader keys and an emitter refusal. A rule
   whose gate cannot be shown to fire on a compiled cell is prereg **F-3**.
2. **The emitter has no producer to allocate.** `scheduled_gpr_run` builds
   `Producer`s only from `s.lit` and hard-codes `ProducerKind::Constant`; an
   interior-address producer is invisible to `order::schedule` too. That is a
   second seam, not a clause.

### 8.1 The near-miss worth recording

The `b` term did not merely fail — it was **confounded**, and the confound was

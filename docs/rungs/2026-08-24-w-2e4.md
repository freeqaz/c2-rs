## What it admits, and what it refuses

**Admits.** `0x2e4` is a **kind-`0x12` branch tuple with exactly one operand,
and that operand is a LABEL** — kind by *construction*, because the sole
constructor `0x10bd76e6` opens `mov cl,0x12`. Minting one **adds a real CFG
edge**: a kind-`0x1d` predecessor record in `label[+0x28]` and a bump of the
label's reference count. Its contract is one predicate, and c2 writes it out
verbatim in two TUs:
`PLAIN_CONDITIONAL(t) := t.kind == 0x12 && t[+0x34] == 0 && t.opcode ∉ {0x2e4, 0x21 (bc), 0x22 (bca)}`.
**In the flow graph it does not end a basic block** (`fg.c` `0x10b374c3`) and
walks step over runs of it (`0x10b373c1`). It is minted at **7** sites in 4 TUs
out of **171** callers of that constructor, and `ehexcept.c` splices the same
markers **before and after** one anchor — which is the bracket `w-r8idiom`
measured from the objs, arrived at from the opposite end.

**Refuses.** **The NAME.** It is not in the binary, and this lane says so with a
denominator: **34** char\* arrays enumerated at four strides and every 4-byte
phase, exactly **one** passes a four-row control, and it stops **75 rows
short**. Also refused: what `tuple[+9]` bit 3 means (read: set for the whole
`0x2df`…`0x2e6` family, 52 of 64 test sites branch away on it — *not read*:
why), and what `tuple[+0x34]` is. The optional second item (`cgintrin.c`) was
**not taken**, as the prereg registered.

## The transferable part: a trap recorded in prose does not stop a `u32` read

Row `0x2e4` of c2's mnemonic table reads **`twle`** — a real PPC trap mnemonic,
at an address `0x39c` **past the end of the table**. I read it on the first
command of the lane.

**That exact trap was already documented, by name, two pages away.**
`WB_MIDDLE_INTERFACES.md` §2.2 records walking into the identical thing at a
different index (`0x30f` → `tdlngi`), and `ref/P_ENCODE.md` §2.1 tabulates
three more of its phantoms. **It stopped nothing.** What stops it is a control
*inside the tool*: `dump_pseudoop.py --names` prints `covers? no` where a
lookup prints `twle`, and `--names-selftest` watches that control fail at four
wrong bases before any row is quoted.

> **The lesson generalises past this one table.** This record's traps are
> written as prose warnings in findings pages. A prose warning is read once, by
> the lane that wrote it. **A control is read every time the tool runs.** Where
> a trap is cheap to encode as a control, encode it.

And the same failure has a second instance in this very lane, going the other
way: **§1.4 of the findings was drafted with the heading "a table this record
did not have"**, about `0x10b1d180`. The record had it **three times over**
(`WB_MIDDLE_INTERFACES.md` §2.2, `ref/P_EXPAND.md` §6, `ref/P_OPATTR.md` §7,
the last of which *settled* it). Caught between the read and the commit, by
grepping the record before claiming novelty — the standing rule — and left in
writing rather than silently deleted.

### Calibration — and the tidiness axis was the wrong axis

`w-r8idiom` closed with *"the anti-tidy correction has been over-applied.
Predict the mechanism, not its tidiness"*, and I registered in §6 of the prereg
that I expected to be wrong in the **messy** direction, taking the tidy branch
deliberately at P2.1, P2.2, P3.4 and P4.3 and the anti-tidy branch at P3.2,
P4.1 and P4.5.

**My anti-tidy predictions went 0 for 3. My tidy predictions went 2 for 4.**
That is not a story about tidiness; it is a story about **which question**:

* **Every prediction about what c2 DOES was tidy and right** — one constructor,
  one operand, one predicate, one kind. P3.4 and P4.3 hit; P4.5 missed *because*
  the mechanism is tidier than I allowed.
* **Every prediction about what the IMAGE CONTAINS missed** — P2.1 and P2.2
  both assumed an artefact (a name table) whose existence I had not checked.
  **You cannot calibrate the presence of a thing by predicting its shape.**
* **Predictions about what the RECORD says** (P3.2, P4.1) missed by
  over-correcting a published table I had already found one error in.

**The rule I would hand the next lane:** `w-r8idiom`'s *"predict the mechanism,
not its tidiness"* is right and incomplete. Add: **do not predict the existence
of an artefact you have not looked for — register the search instead.** P2.5,
which registered the *search's failure mode* rather than its outcome, is the
one prediction in this file that paid.

## Found and not taken

Ranked, and the first two are the ones a follow-up should buy.

1. **`tuple[+9]` bit 3, and `tuple[+0x34]`.** Both are *load-bearing in the
   contract* and neither is named. Bit 3 is set by the constructor for the
   whole `0x2df`…`0x2e6` family (`0x10bd7754`), cleared at four sites
   (`0x10b39ad6` `fg.c`, `0x10bc6fef` `regasg.c`, `0x10bcb2cc` `sizeopt.c`,
   `0x10bff942` `lower.c`), set at six, and **tested at 64, of which 52 branch
   away when it is set**. `+0x34` is the other half of every plain-branch test
   in the backend. **These two fields decide more passes than the opcode does**,
   and one lane reading the six setters would name both.
2. **The `0x2dd`…`0x2e6` branch-pseudo family as a whole.** `0x2dd` (60 mints)
   and `0x2de` (50) are the common members and neither is in any document;
   `0x2de` is what `fg.c` rewrites a folded branch to; `0x2df` sets
   `block[+0x18] |= 0x1000000`. **`0x2e4` was read as a singleton and it is a
   family member** — the family is the cheaper unit, and this lane's instrument
   takes an opcode argument, so `--sites 0x2dd` is one command.
3. **The three `cgintrin.c` `push 0x7d084378` sites** (`0x10bf12d9`,
   `0x10bf1a62`, `0x10bf80d8`) — `w-r8idiom`'s follow-up 3, registered here as
   not-taken (prereg P5.1) and **still not taken**. Two lanes have now declined
   it. One of the three passes `0x10bd3815` where the others pass `0x10bd3824`,
   which §2.2 of the findings now explains as a splice *direction*, so the read
   is cheaper than it was.
4. **`0x10b1d910`, the second alias block.** `ref/P_OPATTR.md` §7.1 calls
   `FUN_10c01f23`'s reference to it *"a table-end pointer"*; it is the base of a
   32-byte-entry rotate/mask simplified-mnemonic table (`extldi`…`clrrdi`).
   **What reads it is not established.** Small, and it closes a row that is
   currently marked settled.
5. **The `P_EXPAND.md` §3 signed re-score** — out of scope here as it was in
   `w-r8idiom`, still open, and now with one more input: the splice callback is
   a *parameter*, so a word-delta instrument that assumes `0x10bd3824` is the
   callee is counting the wrong thing at the sites that pass `0x10bd3815`.
6. **`0x10bd3824`'s caller count disagrees with itself in the record** —
   `ref/P_EXPAND.md` §2 says **147 callers**, `ref/P_OPATTR.md` §5 says **207
   direct calls**, `ref/P_BLOCKORDER.md` says **207 / 506 address-taken**. Not
   this lane's rows and not resolved here; flagged so the next lane that quotes

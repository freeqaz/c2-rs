## 1. What was built

### 1.1 The encoder half — 63 twins (`e4181e738`)

Mechanical, by a generator (`work/w-s1c2/mkmop.py`), with three arms that are
not:

* **The three fallible branch encoders** (`bdnz`, `bc`, `b_intra`) get
  `Option<MachineOp>` twins. The guard stays in the twin and the encoder becomes
  `Some(mop_X(..)?.word())`, so the **refusal** is part of the op stream's
  contract and not something the rendering step re-derives.
* **The four delegating wrappers** (`srwi31`, `clrlwi31`, `clrlwi_record`,
  `ldr`) delegate to their target's twin, so the delegation stays one fact.
* **`mtctr` and `stdu`** keep their `const` and their DS `debug_assert` inside
  the twin.

**Six encoders deliberately do NOT route through their own twin, and that is a
correction to the mechanical rewrite rather than an exception to it.** The
generator made `encode_and`/`or`/`xor`/`slw`/`srw`/`sraw` call `mop_and`/… ,
which left **`encode_logical_x` dead** — and `encode_logical_x` carries one of
the largest transcribed evidence notes in the file (the sixteen `bits.cod`
captures, the "destination is RA and not RT" warning) and is cited **by
`file:line`** from board **#685**, **#3379** and **#3400**, two rungs,
`WB_ENCODE_FINDINGS.md` §157 and `P_ENCODE.md` §235. Deleting it would have
orphaned seven evidence citations to save one function. The six now call
`encode_logical_x` as before, `encode_logical_x` is `mop_logical_x(..).word()`,
and there is still exactly **one** composition.

### 1.2 The domain control, widened 22 → 85 (`e4181e738`)

`w-s1bc`'s `every_converted_mop_agrees_with_its_encoder_over_the_whole_domain`
swept the 22 it had converted. Left alone it would have been a green control
over a **quarter** of its population — `STATUS.md` trap 0, in the file whose own
test doc quotes that trap. It is now seven family sweeps plus a population
control, so a failure names the family.

**Two of the widenings buy something a restatement would not:**

* **The fallible branches assert the `Option`s agree, not the words.** A twin
  that *encoded* an out-of-range displacement instead of refusing it passes any
  word-only comparison over the in-range domain, and is wrong exactly where the
  range check exists. The sweep spans both sides of both limits and every
  non-multiple of 4, and **asserts that the refusing arm was reached** — without
  which the test could pass by never asking a question either side could refuse.
* **The 64-bit rotates now sweep 0..64, not 0..32.** `rldicl`/`rldimi` are form
  68, whose `SH` is split (`SH[4:0]` at 11, `SH[5]` alone at bit 1) and whose
  `MB` is stored low-bit-first. The incumbent sweep ran 0..32, so **every value
  it tried had bit 5 clear and neither split's high bit was ever exercised.** A
  sweep that cannot reach the field it checks is #3379's 46-word probe again,
  one form over.

**And the population control is the one that would have caught the incumbent's
own gap**: it reads `encode.rs` and asserts encoders == twins == 85.

### 1.3 Seven producers (`c358d8571`, `178423b56`)

`memcpy_tail`, `fp_tail_call`, `cmp_shift_or`, `counted_accum_loop`,
`static_scan_loop`, `pool_free_list`, `fp_permute_args`.

**Each is split `X_text` (a thin `ops_to_bytes` wrapper) + `X_ops` (the
producer, returning `Ops`) — and the choice of shape is the content.** `w-s1bc`
shipped **two different shapes** for the same job: `global_store_leaf` splits
`_text`/`_ops`, while `div_mod_leaf` builds an `Ops` internally and renders on
the way out. Only the split one leaves the op stream **reachable by a caller**.
Keeping `C2Op` alive inside a function that renders before returning buys the
goal's two named consumers nothing — a permuter and a matching-pretext generator
need to *read* the decisions, not to know that they briefly existed. This lane
used the split shape throughout.

Two conversions changed an assertion rather than preserving it, deliberately:

* `pool_free_list`'s length invariant read `t.len() == 4 * <ops>`. It counts
  **ops** now. The invariant was always "the body length is a constant of the
  PUSH/POP variant"; the `4 *` was an artefact of the representation.
* `counted_accum_loop` keeps `body_word` as a thin renderer over the new
  `body_op`, because its injectivity pin is a statement about **words** — two
  opcodes must not collapse onto one encoding — and re-expressing it over ops
  would have quietly changed what the test asserts.

### 1.4 The decision surface — a stated NULL, registered in advance

Registered in prereg §4 before measuring, and it held: **S1c (i)'s remainder
exposes no new parameter.** A twin is the same expression with `.word()`
removed; there is no configuration in which a different value reproduces c2, so
a parameter would be a fake surface — worse than a stated null. The real surface
is S1a's `EncodeParams`, inherited unchanged. What S1c (i) buys is not a knob:
it is that `C2Op` **reaches the producer boundary**.

### 2.2 M2 WAS RUN TWICE, AND THE FIRST RUN WAS A NO-OP THAT I ALMOST REPORTED AS A CONTROL GAP

The first M2 rewrote `mop_mtctr`'s `.imm_d1(SPR_CTR & 0x1F).imm_d2(SPR_CTR >> 5)`
to `.imm_d1(SPR_CTR).imm_d2(0)` — "write the SPR unsplit" — and **all 626 tests
stayed green.** The mutation was verified *applied* (the mutated function was
printed), so the obvious reading was a live gap in exactly the encoder whose own
doc warns that writing `9 << 11` *"produces a legal-looking `mtspr` naming SPR
288 and the assembler in your head does not catch it."*

**It was not a gap. It was not a mutation.** SPR **9** is small enough that
`9 & 0x1F == 9` and `9 >> 5 == 0`, so "unsplit" and "split" are the *same two
numbers* at this SPR. The edit changed the text and not the function.

The real error — placing 9 in the field at bit 11 — is M2′ above, and it goes
red on **eight** tests. Recorded because "verified applied" is **not** the same
check as "verified semantically different", the difference is invisible in a
diff, and the failure mode is a lane publishing a control gap that does not
exist. The harness asserts both now.

### 3.1 THE GUARD FIRED — the first pair VOIDED ITSELF, live

The first attempt read `WORKLOAD STAMP BEFORE: 61d9d884c1c7` and
`AFTER: a29f559d0790`. **`../dc3-decomp` moved mid-pair**, so the two arms ran
against different corpora and their difference was not zero-or-nonzero but
meaningless. The harness refused to print a comparison and exited 3.

**Those numbers are not in this rung and were never read.** The pair was re-run
and held at `a29f559d0790` on the first retry. The corpus took **four** distinct
values during this one lane — `ac0611f33458` → `5f0218cb9703` → `61d9d884c1c7` →
`a29f559d0790` — the first of those inside the lane's opening minutes, before a
single measurement.

**What this adds to #3426**, which established that a threshold and its corpus
are one fact: **a lane that merely records the stamp will still publish a
corrupted pair**, because recording is not refusing. The check has to be an
assertion the harness *fails on*, and the retry has to print every void attempt
rather than swallow it — a harness that silently retried until the stamp held
would hide how often the hazard fires, which is the quantity actually at issue.

### 3.2 An absence that read as a pass, for about ninety seconds

The first identity diff printed **`0 lines`** over **`0 keys`**: the extractor
anchored `^gap-metric` and the scan indents those lines. A zero difference
between two empty sets is the project's most-repeated defect — absence reading
as success — and it was caught only because the harness prints the **denominator
beside the diff**, so `keys base: 0 tip: 0` sat directly above `0 lines`. The
denominator is the whole reason it was not banked. The real answer is 498 and
498; no scan was re-run, only the extraction fixed.

## 6. What was NOT done — and a correction to how the remainder is counted

### 6.1 THE CORRECTION: 3 of `w-s1bc` §6's "12 remaining producers" are S1c (ii)

Declined **in the prereg, before the survey was priced into a plan**, and the
decline stands after the work:

| site | why it is not S1c (i) |
|---|---|
| `ptr_walk_loop_text` | Builds through `BodyLayout`; `producer_at(&run, …)` **decodes already-emitted bytes**, and `block_ir.rs::finish()` reads `text.len()` per block, appends placeholder branch words and **back-patches** via `labels.resolve(&mut text)`. |
| `ptr_walk_chain_loop_text` | All of the above, plus explicit slicing of a built run: `let cut = 4 * (body_len - 1); … body_run[..cut] … body_run[cut..]`. |
| `int_tail_call_text` | **Its return type is a byte offset.** `base_off + text.len() as u32` is the REL24 relocation site, and its `select.rs` caller truncates 4 more bytes off the result. |

**These are not three producers. They are one dependency.** `BodyLayout`,
`producer_at` and `LabelMap` all speak `&[u8]`/`&mut Vec<u8>`; converting
`block_ir.rs` unlocks all three at once, and converting them one at a time is
strictly more expensive than the lane that does `block_ir` — which is **S1c
(ii)**, deliberately not dispatched by decision 8.

**So `w-s1bc` §6's split of the remainder into "12 producers" plus "a separate
(ii)" mis-assigns three of the twelve, and any lane pricing S1c (i)'s residue at
12 is pricing 9.** `static_scan_loop` is the clean contrast and is why this is a
real distinction and not a convenient one: it has branch displacements too, and
converted without a murmur, **because they are file-level block-offset constants
never derived from the length emitted so far.**

## 8. WHAT THESE CONTROLS COULD NOT CATCH — and three things they did not

### 8.1 The sweep is a TAUTOLOGY for 79 of the 85, and the incumbent is what bites

`encode_X` is *defined* as `mop_X(..).word()`. For those 79 the new sweep
compares an expression against itself and **cannot fail**; the compiler can and
does fold it. Its power over that population is structural, not empirical: it
would catch a future edit that makes an encoder stop delegating to its twin, and
the population control would catch a twin going missing.

**The control that actually bites is `mod incumbent`** — S1a's independently
written literal re-derivations, ~81 of them, compared over each encoder's
domain. M2′ and M3 both went red *there*. The six `encode_logical_x` callers are
the exception in the other direction: because `encode_and` routes through
`encode_logical_x` and `mop_and` through `mop_logical_x`, those are two genuine
routes and the sweep is a real cross-check — which is why **M1 went red on the
sweep and nowhere else.**

**Stated plainly so it is not overread: the encoder half's required-zero rests on
`mod incumbent`, not on the widened sweep.** The four encoders with no incumbent
(`srwi31`, `clrlwi31`, `clrlwi_record`, `ldr`) are the four delegating wrappers,
covered transitively through `rlwinm`/`rlwinm_record`/`ld`, all of which have
one.

### 8.2 The generator shipped two type errors, and no test found them

Its first output typed `encode_logical_x(...)` — a `[u8; 4]` — as the body of a
`-> MachineOp` function for all six of that family, and mangled `mop_ldr`'s
signature into `pub(crate) fn mop_ldr(crate) fn encode_ldr(...)` by matching a
greedy `\((.*)\)` against a `pub(crate) fn` head. **Both are compile errors, so
they were caught** — but they were caught by *reading the generated output*
before building, not by any control this lane designed, and the doc-check the
generator does carry passed on both. A generator's self-check tests what its
author thought of.

### 8.3 The five registered in advance, restated

1. **Two derivations that are wrong the same way agree.** `mod incumbent` is
   transcribed from the port's own prior code; it proves **preservation**, never
   correctness. Only real `c2.dll` under wibo does the latter.
2. **The domain sweep is over the port's own encoders.** An encoder that is
   wrong *about c2* stays wrong and stays green.
3. **`fnbyte-refused-parse` is ~70 % of the function population**, so no
   identity diff over it can see a defect in a shape the parser refuses.
4. **`hatch-red` is `REFUSED`** at both ends; per #1406 neither run establishes
   what a full run would.
5. **A required-zero byte delta is silent about everything that is not a byte**
   (#3336). This lane checked the one thing #3428 lost — every encoder's `///`
   block, verified byte-identical base-to-tip by a check **independent of the
   generator's own** — and read the whole diff. It did **not** find a #3428-class
   defect, and prereg P4 predicted one; **read that as the weaker claim** (this
   lane's diff-reading found the two in §8.2 and no third), not as evidence that
   none shipped.

### 8.4 One pre-existing warning, observed and NOT fixed on a reflex

`cargo` warns `unused import: Mul` at `ptr_walk_chain_loop.rs:465`. That file is
**not** in this lane's diff; the warning predates it. Fixing it would put an
unrelated hunk in a required-zero rung, which is the one thing such a rung must
not carry. Left alone, recorded here so the next lane knows it is not theirs
either.

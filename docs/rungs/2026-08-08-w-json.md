## 3. The recognizer is a TEMPLATE, and that is the finding to carry off the lane (#1801)

**This is the reusable result.**

Every predecessor's recognizer is a hand-written production per statement.
`osf_handle_guard` is 689 lines for 381 IL bytes; `xlrc_create_guard` 705 for
446. This body is **1,272 IL bytes**, and PREREG §1.0 priced R1 linearly at
*"≈ 2,000 lines, 3× the largest recognizer this project has shipped in one
lane"* — and registered a conversion probability of **0.40** largely because of
it.

**The price was wrong because the FORM was wrong.** What shipped is a 716-element
unifying template — the reference function's own token stream with four kinds of
hole in it — plus a ~60-line matcher (52 `Line` holes absorb the source-line markers, which are the one thing in the stream that is a property of the FILE and not of the program):

| | |
|---|---|
| `B(x)` | an exact byte: every opcode, every operator, and all **35** `54 <n>` scope depths |
| `T(n)` | a token varint, **unified**: slot `n` is the same token at every occurrence, and the 25 slots are pairwise DISTINCT |
| `Y(n)` | a TYPE, unified **as bytes** and pinned to the reference's `(tag, kind)` — width, class and **signedness**. Only the per-TU type-table id is free (§5.4) |
| `K(v)` / `F(n)` | a literal varint, pinned or captured into one of the four free fields |

Three things about it are worth stating because none is obvious:

1. **It is strictly MORE restrictive than a hand-written grammar, not less.**
   Nothing is skipped, the whole stream from the first statement byte to the
   `4D` is consumed, and one unexpected byte anywhere refuses the body. A
   hand-written walk tends to accumulate "any TYPE here" holes; this one has
   none.
2. **It is generated, and checked against the thing it was generated from.** The
   template came out of the reference IL mechanically, and a test asserts its
   length, its four field holes with the right multiplicities, its first and last
   element, and that every one of the 25 token and 12 type slots is reachable —
   so a hand edit that drops or duplicates a hole is caught by a test rather than
   by an obj.
3. **It is the honest shape of the claim.** Six lanes have now converted one
   function by transcription and written a grammar that reads as if it described
   a class. This class is one function's token stream, and the template says so.

**The scaling claim is measured, not asserted**: **490 lines** of recognizer for
1,272 IL bytes, against `xlrc_create_guard`'s **705** for 446 — **0.39 lines per
IL byte against 1.58**, a factor of four. The cost is that a template cannot express
a family; the moment a second witness of a class exists, a hand-written grammar
is the right form again.

### 3.1 The per-block refusal key (#1802)

A template has one failure mode — "byte `n` is not what the pattern says" — and
that would have made all ten `_neg` cells report one key, which is board
**#1704**'s defect built in by construction. So the matcher reports the **block**
the mismatch happened in, and the nine block boundaries are the pattern's own
label definitions (`29 <tok>`), asserted by a test. Ten cells, ten distinct keys
(§6.1).

## 5. What the conversion actually needed

### 5.1 A FRAMELESS `__savegprlr_N` frame — a third predicate, not a widened one (#1805)

```text
  0x000  7d8802a6  mflr r12            TWO prologue words. There is NO `stwu`:
  0x004  4bfffffd  bl __savegprlr_28   the body makes no call, needs no outgoing
                                       parameter area and has no addressed
                                       local, so c2 allocates no frame and the
                                       helper's stores land below r1
  …
  0x12c  4bfffed4  b __restgprlr_28    ONE epilogue word: no `addi r1,r1,F`
                                       either, and no `blr`
```

`FrameLayout::size()` would compute **128** for `saved_gprs: 4`, and the right
answer is **0** — so the class does not call it. `out_of_class_ctx_gpr_helper_leaf`
is a **third** predicate beside `out_of_class_ctx` (which refuses the helper
outright) and `out_of_class_ctx_gpr_helper` (W-XLR's), and the three refuse each
other by name: each prologue is wrong for the other two shapes, and none is
reachable from an emitter that did not ask for it. Its extra conditions are
exactly the three things that would make c2 allocate a frame — an addressed
local, an outgoing-parameter area, saved FPRs — each with its own refusal string
and its own cell in the test.

**Nothing about the `.pdata` record needed changing.** `0x40004C02` —
`PrologLen 2`, `FuncLen 76` — comes out of `coff::pdata_record` from the two
lengths with nothing special-cased, which is the second time in three lanes that
a new frame class cost the sizing layer zero (`w-xlr` §4.1 is the first).

### 5.2 The two encoders, each pinned to a byte real `c2` emitted (#1806)

### 5.3 The empty callee region (#1807)

W-XLR's `helper_externals` handles this shape with no change at all: the pair
travels on that list, their relocations stay in `calls`, and
`introduced_externals` subtracts the pair from a callee list that is **empty**.
The resulting group is the reference's, byte for byte. Recorded as a row because
the cell had never been emitted and PREREG priced it as one — a seam that costs
zero the second time is worth the same note as one that costs thirteen.

### 5.4 The type-distinctness fence that was BUILT, MEASURED and REMOVED (#1808)

The first version of the recognizer required the twelve TYPE slots to be
pairwise distinct as bytes — they **are** twelve distinct byte strings in the
workload's own TU. **The positive fixture refused.** It is the same program in a
TU whose type table happens to intern `unsigned short *` once instead of twice,
so two slots share bytes.

**Two slots sharing bytes is a property of the TU's type table, not of the
program**, and the fence was removed rather than worked around. Nothing was lost:
every separation that decides an instruction is carried by the per-slot
`(tag, kind)` pin, checked at every occurrence — slot 0 is `(0x86, 0x42)` and
slots 4 and 9 are `(0x86, 0x41)`, so the unsigned-long slot **cannot** collapse
into either signed one and the four `cmplw`s cannot become `cmpw`s. The collapses
still admitted are within one `(tag, kind)` group (`int` with `long`,
`unsigned short *` with `char *`), and each of those pairs is the same register,
the same width and the same word, with the pointer-arithmetic scale pinned to 1
by the template itself.

This is **`w-osfinfo` §4.1.1 in the opposite direction**: there a widening was
built and reverted because it was unsafe; here a *fence* was built and removed
because it was refusing a correct program for a reason that was about the
capture and not about the code. The removal's evidence is §1's 878-TU per-TU set
comparison and §7's gate — a fence removal is a widening and is graded like one.

### 5.5 What is a field, and what is pinned (#1809)

`w-osfinfo` #1767's rule at its **strict end**, which is where this class sits:

| value | | why |
|---|---|---|
| `off_buffer`, `off_size` | **2 free fields** | each lands in an `lwz` displacement. `off_size` is read at **two** sites and the two are one field; asserted by a test that varies it and checks exactly words 10 and 61 move |
| `k_arg_err`, `k_size_err` | **2 free fields** | each lands in its own `lis`+`ori` pair, and a test checks each moves exactly its own two words |
| **both statuses' halves — pinned non-zero** | refused otherwise | a zero half is a one-word materialization and a shorter body |
| **the eight UTF-8 constants** — `0x7F`, the `clrlwi` width, `0x7FF`, `0xC0`, `0xE0`, `0x80` and the three `rlwimi` rotate/mask triples | **PINNED** | they are one program, not eight immediates. `0xC0 \| ((wc >> 6) & 0x1F)` becomes a different instruction sequence the moment the shift and the mask stop agreeing, and the emitter has one `rlwimi` per site with no chooser. Board **#1706** |
| **the three output-size increments 1 / 2 / 3** | pinned | they are the encoded lengths of the three arms |
| **the element step 2** | pinned | `sizeof(unsigned short)`, and it appears in five different words |
| **the formal count and order — `this` + 2** | pinned | they decide which registers the guards read, and **r4 is written** by the three-byte arm |
| **the callee-saved assignment** | pinned | r31 the index, r30 the hoisted `1`, r29/r28 the three-byte arm's continuation bytes. Descending-from-r31 does not predict it |

**Zero words are chosen by a scheduler or a register allocator** — PREREG **D1**,
which did not fire. The class exposes **four** free fields, the fewest of any
conversion in this series, and that is the correct direction for a class with one
witness.

### 5.6 The two code-motion facts a per-statement lowering gets wrong

* **`li r30,1` is HOISTED out of the loop** and kept in a callee-saved register
  for the whole body, because the two `rlwimi`s that build `0x80 | (x & 0x3F)`
  rotate it by 7. A lowering that materialized `0x80` where the source writes it
  emits two more words per arm and gets every displacement after the preheader
  wrong.
* **The three-byte arm builds a pointer CHAIN** — `r10 = r4 + 2` then
  `r11 = r10 + 2` — where `r11 = r4 + 4` is one word shorter and arithmetically
  identical, because the source bumps `pBuffer` twice and stores through
  `*(pBuffer + 1)` between the bumps. `docs/GAPS.md` §6's shape: the shorter
  version links.

## 1. Bias, declared

**My bias is `ChunkIDs.cpp`.** It is 15 repetitions of the exact shape already
proven byte-exact, with one ordinary leaf `.text` beside them, and it is the only
plausible route from TU match 8 to 9 inside this seam. I want it, and wanting it
will push me to (a) under-weight the ordinary-`.text` group as "nearly in class",
(b) read the 16th `.rdata` as something I already understand, and (c) treat
`OBJ_DATA_BSS_SHAPE.md` Rule Y2 — the deferred-`.bss` symbol order, **fitted on
two cells with no held-out confirmation** (§8.3 of that doc) — as settled when my
own TUs would be its first out-of-sample test.

Mitigations I commit to now:

1. **Dump the real `ChunkIDs.cpp` obj and enumerate every section and symbol in
   it BEFORE writing any emitter line.** No emitter code until that enumeration
   is written down.
2. **The ordinary `.text` group is out of class until the obj says otherwise.**
   If converting ChunkIDs needs an ordinary function group co-resident with
   dyninit groups, that is a *third* capability and I will say so rather than
   smuggle it in.
3. Rule Y2 is treated as a **hypothesis under test**, and my ≥2-object fixtures
   are designed to discriminate it (extern-only, static-only, and mixed linkage),
   not merely to confirm it.

Second bias, weaker and inherited: I would rather report 9 than 8, and rather
report "landed" than "declined". A decline with an exact price is a deliverable
here, and I am writing that sentence before I know which one I get.

## 4. Decline floor — specific, and named

I decline the corresponding capability, revert it, and report the price, if:

1. **Any** of the brief's clauses fires: TU match < 8; `mismatch` > 0 anywhere; a
   lost `gate.sh` lane; an existing fixture verdict regressing; a K1 round-trip
   byte moving.
2. **≥2 objects** — if any cell's `.bss` offsets, `.bss` symbol order, `.rdata`
   order or `.CRT$XCU` relocation order cannot be derived from quantities the IL
   already carries (`.gl` record order, declaration order, size, alignment) — in
   particular if any of them needs a **name hash**. w-bss's 7,452-configuration
   search returned nothing and I will not repeat it; if a hash is needed, the
   capability is declined, not fitted.
3. **`??__F`** — if the framed `??__E`/`??__F` pair's `$M`/`$T` numbers are not
   reproduced by `plan_labels`'s existing seed-and-stride model on a real obj.
   A wrong `$M` is six wrong bytes in an obj that still links, which is exactly
   the failure class `LABEL_COUNTER.md` exists to prevent.
4. **Either** — if the recognizer that admits the shape would also admit a TU
   whose obj I have not measured. w-r1c's discipline is the reason mismatch is 0:
   the gate is a *whole-TU* recognizer with every clause stated positively, and
   `Bindings::resolve_data` is not touched. **I commit in advance that
   `resolve_data` keeps its current body and callers**, exactly as w-r1c did.

## 5. The binding constraint, restated

> **A TU landing `mismatch` is the worst possible outcome — a wrong synthesis is
> worse than the honest refusal we have today.**

Given A2/A3 predict zero conversions, the *only* thing this lane can lose is
correctness, and the only things it can win are fixture-graded capability and an
exact price for the next rung. That asymmetry is why every gate below is stated
as a positive clause and why the `/Ox` `/GF` fence w-r1c built is not weakened.

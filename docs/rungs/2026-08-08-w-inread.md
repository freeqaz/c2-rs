### §2.1 Element tag `08` — a count of ZERO BYTES

```text
  element := 08 <count>

    <count>   a varint: one byte below 0x80, else `80` + a 4-byte LE i32.
              MEASURED at 3, 4, 7, 8, 10, 12, 16, 124, 128, 252 and 1196.
```

| cell | source | `.in` | obj `.data` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `z01` | `struct S{int a,b,c;} s = {1};` | `01 01 04 01 · 08 08` | 12 B |
| `z02` | `int arr[4] = {1};` | `01 01 04 01 · 08 0c` | 16 B |
| **`z18`** | **`char cs[8] = {97};`** | **`01 01 01 61 · 08 07`** | **8 B, `61 00×7`** |
| `z19` | `char cs[4] = {97};` | `01 01 01 61 · 08 03` | 4 B |
| `z23` | `short ss[6] = {7};` | `01 01 02 07 · 08 0a` | 12 B |
| `z05` | `int arr[32] = {1};` | `01 01 04 01 · 08 7c` | 128 B |
| **`z04`** | **`int arr[33] = {1};`** | **`01 01 04 01 · 08 80 80000000`** | **132 B** |
| `z03` | `int arr[64] = {1};` | `… 08 80 fc000000` | 256 B |
| `z15` | `int arr[300] = {1};` | `… 08 80 ac040000` | 1200 B |

**`z18` is the cell that decides the unit and nothing else in the grid does.** A
fill of **7** cannot be a count of 4-byte anythings; every other row is
consistent with both an element count and a byte count. **`z05`/`z04` separate
the count's varint from `super::inlit`'s**: they differ by one array element and
straddle `0x80`, and an LE**16** reading of `80 80 00 00 00` would take 128 and
then read the remaining `00 00` as two more element tags.

**The fill DISPLACES what follows it, and that is why the length is load-bearing
beyond its own bytes.** `z06_fill_then_ptr`
(`struct S{int a[3]; int* p;} s = {{1}, &gi};`) is a 16-byte `.data` with one
`IMAGE_REL_PPC_ADDR32` at offset **12**; `z16_ptr_then_fill` is 20 bytes with
the `ADDR32` at 0 and a 16-byte fill after it. A fill read at the wrong length
moves every symbol address after it, and `docs/STATUS.md` trap 4 says a reader
that lost that would leave `records` and the residue untouched.

**A fill is what the initializer list OMITS — including the struct's own
padding — and a written zero is not:**

| cell | source | `.in` |
|---|---|---|
| `z26_fill_only` | `struct S{int* p; int a[3];} s = {&gi, {0,0,0}};` | `02 … · 01 01 04 00 ×3` — **no fill** |
| `z24_bool_fill` | `struct S{bool b; int a[2];} s = {true};` | `01 01 01 01 · 08 03 · 08 08` |
| `z17_two_fills` | `struct S{int a[3]; int b; int c[3];} s = {{1},2};` | `01 01 04 01 · 08 08 · 01 01 04 02 · 08 0c` |

`z24`'s **first** fill is the three bytes of padding after the `bool`.

### §2.2 Scalar types `03` and `04` — a DATA pointer and a FUNCTION pointer

Both were **UNMEASURED**: w-tag02's 24-cell grid produced neither, and
`work/w-emitp2/scalartypes.txt` counted 132,528 and 4,844 elements the reader was
refusing on that account.

| cell | source | `.in` | obj `.data` | relocation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `z09` | `struct S{int* p; int a;} s = {0, 5};` | `01 03 04 00` | `00000000 00000005` | **none** |
| `z11` | `… s = {(int*)4, 5};` | `01 03 04 04` | `00000004 …` | none |
| `z12` | `… s = {(int*)0x11223344, 5};` | `01 03 04 80 44332211` | `11223344 …` | none |
| `z10` | `struct S{void(*f)(); int a;} s = {0, 5};` | `01 04 04 00` | `00000000 00000005` | **none** |
| `z13` | `… s = {(void(*)())4, 5};` | `01 04 04 04` | `00000004 …` | none |
| `z22` | `… s = {(void(*)())0x11223344, 5};` | `01 04 04 80 44332211` | `11223344 …` | none |

The value encoding is types `01`/`02`'s, unchanged. **Only width 4 is admitted**
and anything else refuses as `pointer-width`: no cell on a 32-bit target can vary
a pointer's width, and the workload contains none at any other width.

**In the corpus they are exactly two ABI slots**, which is why they are common
and why no ordinary C++ cell in w-tag02's grid produced them:

* type `03` is **`_TypeDescriptor::spare`** — `??_R0` is
  `02 <??_7type_info@@6B@> 00 04 · 01 03 04 00 · 03 <len> ".?AU…"`, and 14,703
  of 14,705 type-03 elements in the sampled TUs sit at index 1 of that
  three-element record. `z14_typedesc` reproduces it byte for byte.
* type `04` is **`_s__ThrowInfo::pForwardCompat`** — 228 of 228 at index 2 of a
  four-element `_TI` record. `z08_throw`'s `_TI1?AUE@@` spells
  `01 02 04 00 · 01 04 04 00 · 01 04 04 00 · 02 <_CTA1> 00 04`, **two** of them,
  because `pmfnUnwind` is null when the thrown type has no destructor.
* `z25_pmd_shape` — a hand-written struct mimicking `_s__CatchableType` — spells
  `01 02 04 00 · 01 03 04 00 · 01 01 04 00 · 01 01 04 80 ffffffff · 08 04 ·
  01 01 04 80 0c010000 · 02 …`, which is **element for element** what the CRT's own
  `_CT??_R0?AUE@@@84` record spells. The `08 04` lands on `_PMD::vdisp`, the one
  field of `{0, -1}` the initializer omits.

### §2.3 Element tag `03` had to come with them, and #960's row could not see that

`??_R0` carries a symbol address in its FIRST field and a tag-`03` name blob in
its THIRD. Teaching the reader scalar type `03` **alone** recovers **none** of
that row's 132,488 addresses — the blame simply moves one element to the right,
onto the blob.

> ### **w-emitp2's blame rows are upper bounds, NOT addends, and this is the case that shows it.** The row reads *"element tag `03` costs 144,848 records and ZERO symbol addresses — do not price it first"*, and that is true of the reader as it stood and false of the reader after two of the other three rows land.

Element tag `03` is `03 <len> <len bytes>`, contributing its payload verbatim.
The length is `super::inlit::read_len`'s — `80` + LE**16** — and it is now
`pub(super)` and **shared**, not re-spelled: the crate has four `.in` varints
already and a fifth copy of one is how they drift apart.

### §2.4 The one thing the grid does NOT separate — `??_R2` (#984)

`??_R2A@@8` is `02 <??_R1> 00 04 · 08 03` — **7** decoded bytes — and its
`.rdata$r` section's `SizeOfRawData` is **8**. `??_R2B@@8` decodes to 11 against
a section of **12**. Every other `??_R*` record in the same objs sums to its
section size exactly (`??_R0` 16, `??_R1` 28, `??_R3` 16, `??_R4` 20).

**Two readings fit and this grid cannot separate them**: the object is 7 bytes
rounded up to the section's 4-byte alignment, or it is 8 bytes of which `.in`
spells 7. `roundup(4N+3, 4) == 4(N+1)` for every `N`, so the section size can
never tell them apart. Written down as unseparated. It is **not a live hazard**:
`data_tu` clause 7 requires the decode to be *exactly* the object's size, so it
refuses `??_R2` either way.

## §3 #961 — the denominator, printed

`InInitReport` carries three counters beside `records`, and `c2rs gap` prints
them on their own line:

```text
  .in initializers: 756720 records = 753165 accepted + 3555 residue, 3699480
    elements (ARITY), 753165 values (TOKENS, not records), 0 duplicate records,
    0 conflicts, 0 accounting breaks (known answer 0)
  .in symbol addresses (element tag 02, board #931): 1934614 elements over
    709999 records
  .in UNANCHORED (the denominator, board #961): 148809 records whose first
    element is a tag-03 blob or a tag-08 fill, 3340 `00 02` candidates dropped
    by the fail-closed arm, 0 anchors with no token — none of these is in
    `records` OR in the residue
```

Each is counted **fail-closed**: a candidate counts only if a token reads back at
exactly the right width AND the run frames all the way to its `07`. So it is a
count, not an estimate. **None of the three changes what the scan accepts or
where it resumes** — `an_unanchored_record_is_counted_and_changes_nothing_else`
runs the same stream with and without an unanchored record in it and asserts
`records`, `accepted`, `residue` and `values` are identical.

> **The fail-closed population fell 239,279 → 3,340 as a SIDE EFFECT of #960**,
> because those `00 02` candidates now frame all the way. The number #961 was
> filed to make visible turns out to have been mostly a symptom of #960 — and
> nothing would have said so if the counter had not been printed on both sides
> of the widening.

### §3.1 This lane's own reporting was wrong twice, and printing caught both (#982)

**(a) The #961 counter shipped double-counting.**

The first 878-TU run printed `no_token` **3,340** beside `fail_closed`
**3,340**. One population wearing two labels: the fail-closed arm `break`s out
of the token loop leaving `matched` false, so it fell through into the no-token
branch as well. Fixed with a second flag; the three are disjoint by construction
now and `a_fail_closed_candidate_is_not_also_counted_as_a_no_token_one` asserts
it.

**It was visible only because the two numbers happened to be equal.** Had the
fail-closed population been larger than the genuine no-token one by any amount,
their sum would have looked like a plausible measurement — which is the whole
reason a denominator has to be believable before it is worth publishing.

**(b) The residue histogram could not print a new reason — trap 5, in the code
that documents trap 5.** `cli/gap.rs` carried a **hand-kept copy** of the six
residue names, directly beneath its own comment:

```rust
    // EVERY reason, including the zeroes — a residue reason that stops
    // occurring must read `0` and not vanish (trap 5).
    for reason in ["symbol-address", "floating-point", "unknown-type",
                   "unknown-width", "value-did-not-frame", "truncated"] {
```

This lane added three reasons. The reader reported them, `scan.rs` aggregated
them under their own emit keys, and **this loop printed the other six and none
of the three** — so the first 878-TU run of the widened reader printed a residue
histogram that silently did not sum to `in-init-residue`, and nothing said so.

**It is the worse of the two.** A reason that reads `0` is exactly the signal
trap 5 asks for; an absent row is indistinguishable from a reason that does not
exist. The list is derived from `InInitResidue::ALL` now — the array the unit
tests already assert is complete — so the two cannot drift again.

## §5 The "uniform ±2" gets a verdict, and it is two numbers, not one (#980)

w-emitp2 §4.1 measured its transcript short by **exactly 2 symbol addresses on
806 of 850 TUs** (`Σ|Δ| = 1,612 = 806 × 2`) and attributed it to the crate's
resync. It counted the gap and did not decode it. `work/w-inread/resync.py`
emulates the **pre-widening** anchor scan byte for byte — the fail-closed arm,
the `i += 2` after a named refusal, the `i = p` after an accepted record — and
**reproduces the crate's 518,098 records exactly**, then diffs its records
against the sequential parse's:

```text
  records it framed                                   : 518098
  tag-02 symbol addresses it read                     : 1439270
  ... in records the SEQUENTIAL parse also frames      : 1427984
  ... in records the sequential parse does NOT         :   11286   <== PHANTOM

  extra symbol addresses = 0    :   44 TUs
  extra symbol addresses = 14   :  804 TUs
  extra symbol addresses = 15   :    2 TUs
```

and the sample prints the same stream offsets in TU after TU — 736, 777, 818,
907 — which is why the effect looked uniform: they are inside the constant-pool
prologue every `.in` stream shares.

Against that runs a second, opposite effect nobody had named:
`InInitCensus::refs` is keyed by **token**, and only the *first* accepted record
for a token contributes, so the report's `sym_refs` (1,429,596) is below the
per-record total (1,439,270) by **9,674**.

```text
      +11,286   phantom, from re-anchoring inside a refused record's tail
       −9,674   dropped by the token-keyed `refs` map
      ────────
       +1,612   =  806 × 2
```

> ### **The pre-widening 1,429,596 was not merely short. It was 456,104 too LOW in one direction and 11,286 too HIGH in another, and the two error terms very nearly cancelled at the per-TU level.** #965's number is right and its single-cause attribution is not.

**Prereg P13 HOLDS** — the commissioning brief has the direction inverted; the
crate saw two *more*, not two fewer, and the unit is symbol addresses, not
records. **Prereg P14 LOSES in full**: I registered that at least one of the two
would be a real record the sequential parser missed, and **none of them are**.

The widening removes both terms: the two instruments now agree at 1,885,700 on
850 of 850 TUs, `duplicate_records` is **0** (it was 9,914), and the 3,335
records the anchor scan still frames beyond the sequential parse are all refused
and carry nothing.

## §6 What this lane declined, priced (#983)

1. **Widening the anchor set to `00 03` / `00 08`.** The 144,850 records whose
   first element is a tag-`03` blob or a tag-`08` fill carry **ZERO** tag-02
   symbol addresses, before *and* after (`blindspot.txt`, `blindspot2.txt`), so
   anchoring them buys the emit-predicate channel nothing — while `00 03` occurs
   inside any four-byte escape payload whose third byte is 3, and admitting them
   would put `values` entries under tokens `inlit::in_string_literals` already
   binds. **Counted instead** (§3). Reopen if a `.rdata` string-COMDAT writer
   ever lands.
2. **Scalar type `05` (floating point)** — still refused, still for
   `OBJ_DATA_BSS_SHAPE.md` §4.2.1's `CheckSum` exclusion. **134 records** over
   the 850, **0** symbol addresses.
3. **A pointer at a width other than 4** — refused as `pointer-width`.
   Unmeasurable on this target; **0** in the workload.
4. **The undefined-external relocation target** — `z20` and `z25` are read fine
   and refused by the *writer*. w-tag02 §8 item 1, untouched.
5. **`??_R2`'s 7-versus-8** (§2.4) — unseparated, refused by `data_tu`.
6. **The crate's resync itself.** It is now decoded (§5) and it is still there:
   3,335 phantom records on the 850, all refused. Removing it means giving the
   reader a record-boundary model, which is a different rung.

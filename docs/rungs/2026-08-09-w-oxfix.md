### 1.2 The answer, and what it costs the briefed diagnosis

**No flag in the differing set promotes or demotes C4716.** It is an error at
every optimization level this toolchain has — `/Ox`, `/O1`, `/O2`, `/Od`, and a
bare `/c` — with and without `/GS-`, `/EHsc` and `/GR`. C4716 is documented by
MSVC as a **level-1 warning that is an error by default**, and the only thing
that moves it is an explicit warning-level override:

* `/w14716` puts it back to a level-1 **warning** — obj produced, diagnostic
  still printed;
* `/wd4716` **suppresses** it — obj produced, silent.

Compiled to the **same** `/Fo` path (so the embedded `S_OBJNAME` is identical),
the two produce **byte-identical objs**. So the choice between them is a choice
about visibility only, and `/w14716` wins: a fixture whose whole class is a
diagnostic should not hide the diagnostic.

**This refutes the shape the defect was filed under.** The brief recorded the
class as *"compiles at the workload's flags (`/O1 /Oi /EHsc /GR /c` → 1,666
bytes, verified) and fails at the selftest profile"*, and asked which flag
promotes it. It does not compile at the workload's flags: cell `J_workload_full`
is `error C4716` with no obj. The 1,666-byte figure is reproducible only **with a
demotion applied** — `/nologo /wd4355 /wd4164 /c /GR /O1 /Oi /EHsc /w14716` gives
1,702 B here and 1,706 B for the five-flag spelling, and the difference is path
length: MSVC bakes the `/Fo` path into the COFF, so an obj's *size* is a function
of where it was written and is not a fixture identity.

**PREREG score, §0.1:** C1 (`/Ox` alone, p=0.55) **MISS**. C2 (`/GS-`, p=0.05)
**MISS**. C3 (no `/EHsc`, p=0.25) **MISS**. C4 (`/GR` demotes, p=0.05) **MISS**.
**C5 (none singly, p=0.10) HIT** — and in a stronger form than C5 stated: not an
interaction of two flags, but *no flag at all*, the discriminator being a
warning-level override that appears in neither profile. C6 (p=0.90) **HIT**.

The lane put 0.90 of its mass on "some flag in the differing set does it" and
0.10 on the branch that was true. That is the whole content of the miss: **the
question "which flag promotes it" presupposes an optimization axis, and C4716 is
not on one.**

## 2. Why this was invisible, and where the corpus's blind spot is

`oracle_selftest` has two halves and **they disagreed about whether the fixture
exists**:

| half | call | on these fixtures |
|---|---|---|
| determinism | `compile_obj` → a real `.obj` | **fails** — C4716, no obj |
| capture stability | `capture_il` → the `_CL_*` bundle | **succeeds** — `.ex` 3233 B |

The capture path prepends `/Bd /d2nop` and detects success by the **presence of
the `_CL_*ex` file**, not by exit code. c1xx writes the IL bundle before C4716
becomes fatal, so the bundle is complete and stable. That is why
`tests/il_roundtrip.rs` — which captures every fixture and panics on failure —
was **green on master** while `selftest` was red, and it is why a whole rung's
worth of `.ex`/`.sy` measurements exists for a class that has no obj (board
#2335). Those measurements are sound; what they are not is evidence that the
class compiles.

## 3. What ships — the profile declaration

### 3.1 The mechanism, in port terms

One line, in the fixture, machine-read by
`crates/c2-harness/src/fixture_profile.rs`:

```
// c2rs-profile: /Ox /GS- /w14716 /c  # <why the default cannot compile this class>
```

The repo had **no** fixture-metadata convention (checked: `fixtures/README.md`
documented none, and no `.cpp` carried a machine-read marker), so this lane
creates one — **S1 HIT**. Documented in `fixtures/README.md`, *"Per-fixture
compile profiles"*.

Rules, and why each is the shape it is:

* **A full flag list, not a delta.** A delta would silently re-derive itself if
  `CAPTURE_IL_DEFAULT_FLAGS` moved; a full list is readable against the default
  without running anything.
* **A reason after the `#`, mandatory and non-empty.** Deliberately the same rule
  `tests/rung_registry.rs` puts on `Fixtures: none — <reason>`. An opt-out that
  does not have to justify itself becomes a skip list.
* **At most one marker per file**, and **`/c` mandatory**. Both hard errors. The
  realistic failure is a fixture copy-pasted from a sibling and half-edited, and
  last-wins would compile it at a profile nobody chose.
* **A malformed declaration is an error, not a fall back to the default.** A
  typo'd marker that quietly reverted to `/Ox` is this same defect one level
  quieter.

Wiring: `Toolchain::compile_obj_flags` is the new obj-side seam (the twin of
`capture_il_flags`); `compile_obj` delegates to it with
`CAPTURE_IL_DEFAULT_FLAGS`, which is byte-identical to what it did before.
`run_selftest` resolves the profile once and uses it for **both** halves — the
determinism compile and the capture — because capturing at flags the obj was not
compiled at is exactly the skew `capture_il_flags`' own doc warns about.

### 3.2 The default did not change — proven by count

`tests/fixture_profiles.rs`, portable lane, at the shipping tree:

```

fixture profiles: 2 of 321 fixtures declare one
  wmain_no_return.cpp      [/Ox /GS- /w14716 /c]
  wmain_no_return_neg.cpp  [/Ox /GS- /w14716 /c]
```

Every non-declaring fixture resolves to `CAPTURE_IL_DEFAULT_FLAGS` **element for

### 3.3 Failure is LOUD and NAMED — there is no skip list

A fixture that cannot compile at its resolved profile produces a message whose
**first line is self-contained**, because `selftest_row` renders only

  (the DEFAULT — .../fixtures/cpp/wmain_no_return.cpp has no `// c2rs-profile:` line)

The declared form names the profile and its declaration site instead. **S5 HIT**,
and verified by running the real thing against master's fixtures rather than by

**No skip verdict exists in the module and none was added.** The toolchain-absent
path prints how many fixtures went *ungraded*, so an absent toolchain degrades
cleanly (CLAUDE.md) without the degradation reading as a green corpus.

## 4. `w-main`'s class — is the shipped arm workload-reachable?

**YES, and it binds exactly one body — but not via the class the fixtures name.**
`w-main`'s rung gets an append-only §13 carrying this; nothing above it is
rewritten.

### 4.1 The named class is unreachable on the workload by construction

`error C4716` is an error at the workload's own profile (§1.1, cell
`J_workload_full`). A TU containing an ordinary non-`void` function with no
`return` statement **would never have compiled**, so no such TU is in the 878-TU
workload. That is a finding, not a failure — but it means the fixtures' cells are
the one spelling of the class that cannot exist in a built TU.

### 4.2 What the arm actually keys on, and the two shapes that DO compile

`ex_exit_label`'s shipped fallback is

```rust
found.or_else(|| if seg.contains(&EX_ASSIGN) { None } else { uncorroborated })
```

— i.e. it keys on **a segment with no `3A` anywhere**, which is a strictly wider
condition than the class the fixture header names. Two compilable shapes reach
it, both measured here:

| shape | cell | result at `/Ox /GS- /c` |
|---|---|---|
| `int main(int,char**){ sink(argc,0); }` | `P_main` | **compiles**, 1077 B, no diagnostic |
| `int wmain(int,wchar_t**){ ... }` | `P_wmain` | **compiles**, 1077 B, no diagnostic |
| the identical body renamed `int mainish(...)` | `P_notmain` | **`error C4716`** |
| `int f(int,int){ sink(b,a); die(); }` (`__declspec(noreturn)`), `for(;;)`, `while(1)` | `N1_noreturn` | **compiles**, 1489 B, **no diagnostic at all** |

**MSVC exempts `main`/`wmain`** — C++ gives them an implicit `return 0` — and it
suppresses the diagnostic when the end of a non-`void` function is unreachable.
`P_main` vs `P_notmain` separates the exemption from everything else: one
identifier apart, one compiles and one does not.

And `src/Main.cpp` is, in full:

```cpp
#include "App.h"

int main(int argc, char **argv) {
    App app(argc, argv);
    app.Run();
}
```

That is why its `.ex` holds zero `3A` bytes. `w-main`'s §1 observation stands
exactly; its *explanation* is one word off — the reason is `main`, not "no
`return` statement" as a general compilable class.

## 5. What this lane did NOT do

Named, so the remainder is not inferred from a gap:

* **The mode-lane gate still compiles fixtures at its own lane flags**
  (`scripts/lanes.txt` × `/GS- /c`) and does **not** honor a fixture's declared
  profile. A lane profile and a fixture profile are different objects, and
  composing them (lane flags *plus* the fixture's overrides) is a design decision
  this lane did not make unilaterally.

  **Checked in the gate's own logs rather than assumed** — an earlier draft of
  this bullet asserted the two fixtures land in the `capture-fail` bucket, and
  that is **wrong**. Every one of the 18 lanes reads `321/321` graded with
  `capture-fail 0`, and the two fixtures are classified
  `vocab-gap  (il function decode failed)` at the lane's own flags
  (`lanes/-O1-Oi--EHsc/report.txt`, rows `[320/321]` and `[321/321]`).

  They reach the classifier at all because `c2rs gap`'s capture is the
  front-end-only `/Bd /d2nop` path, which **survives** C4716 — §2's blind spot,
  third face. **That is why the gate stayed 18/18 green while `selftest` was
  red**, and it is a limit of the gate worth naming: a corpus-wide instrument
  that never compiles an obj cannot see an obj-only defect. `c2rs gap` is
  untouched by this lane.
* **`c2rs corpus` / `c2rs listing` / `c2rs replay` and the other single-TU
  subcommands** keep their own hardcoded or `--flags-file` profiles. They are not
  fixture-corpus sweeps, so a per-fixture profile has nothing to resolve against
  there; naming them anyway so the seam list is closed.
* **No port arm, no census movement, no new accepted class.** `Census: +0` is
  literal, and the workload scan at the shipping tree reproduces `match 19`,
  `mismatch 0` unchanged.
* **The `/wd4716` spelling is not used anywhere**, even though it is
  byte-identical, because it hides the diagnostic.

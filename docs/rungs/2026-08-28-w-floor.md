## 2. The two results that would be worth reading if nothing else is

### 2.1 The floor is a predicate, and it predicts a split

> **A subsystem hits the shared floor exactly when its decision function reads
> state that other passes accumulate — and it does not when its decision
> function is a pure function of the single record it is handed.**

Two accumulators, and they are **different objects**: the tuple list (regalloc's
`cand+0x0c`, the inliner's `[sym+0x50]`, `dag`'s `node+0x44`, globregs' live-range
versions) and the **label counter** (`P_LABEL` §2 — the site population is closed
by construction, **the charge is not**, 42 of 163 sites loop-resident). The
second row is why this is a class rather than a fact about tuples.

The predicate predicts **1 YES / 4 NO** among the five subsystems it is applied
to, which is the part worth checking later: a floor that were a mood would
predict every remaining subsystem shares it.

## 5. The double-pricing register — three pairs are live against lanes funded THIS wave

`REPRICE` §6 enumerates five. Three bear on wave 17 directly:

1. **stage S7 ≡ lane `w-s7`.** F0 prices S7 at **zero** sub-items. When F0 is
   next priced, S7's **read** is SPENT by `w-s7` — add its *construction*, never
   its read.
2. **F0 sub-item 4's UNPRICED verification ≡ lane `w-sched`.** Decision 21 §2
   justifies `w-sched` as *"a **check** of an existing read"*. That is true of
   the tap and **not of the population**: `#3435`, taken five days before the
   decision, says the confrontation *"is not available on this corpus at any
   price"* (final schedule reorders 3 of 357 functions; a simulator returning
   its input scores 98.9 %). `w-sched`'s honest outcomes are a re-confirmation
   or **≈ 1 day building the reordering population** (`WB_SCHEDCONF` §8 item 1)
   — and that day **is** F0's UNPRICED term being spent. Priced here so it is
   spent once. **This is not an argument against `w-sched`**; it is an argument
   against grading it on a confrontation the corpus cannot support, and against
   re-adding the same day to F0 later.
3. **`w-lowerband` does NOT overlap F0** — checked and recorded as a negative,
   because *"the lowering band"* names three different address sets in this tree
   (`REPRICE` §6 pair 5).

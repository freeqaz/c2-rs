## 2. `vocab-gap 863`, decomposed

### 2.1 Why one label was hiding eleven questions

`gap.rs` step 3 files a TU as `vocab-gap` when `!IlBundle::decodes()`, with the
reason string `il function decode failed` for all 863. `IlBundle::functions()`
**short-circuits**: it returns `None` at the first of eleven gates, so a TU
blocked by six of them is indistinguishable from a TU blocked by one, and the
ceiling of any single repair cannot be read off the bucket at all.

`IlBundle::decode_causes` (`crates/c2-il/src/func/diag.rs`) re-asks every gate
and reports which fire. It is a **diagnostic and never a gate** —
`functions()` is untouched and remains the sole acceptance path.

## 3. AB-g — CONFIRMED as an identification, REFUTED as a cause

### 3.1 The claim, and the two halves it has

w-small's board row **AB-g** says `codec::gl_offset_framed`'s `gl[o-5] == 0x10`
clause pins the record's preceding `80 <LE32>` field into `0x1000..=0x10FF`,
that the field is *the signature's CodeView type index*, and that this is
*"likely a large uncounted share of `vocab-gap 863`"*. Those are two claims and
they come apart.

### 3.2 The identification: 18 of 18, against the obj's own CodeView stream

`.debug$S`'s `S_GPROC32` record carries the type index of the procedure it
names. `work/w-vocab/cvtypes.py` reads it; `work/w-vocab/glframe.py` reads the
`.gl` field. `work/w-vocab/probe/multi.cpp` — thirteen free functions of varied
signature plus two out-of-line member functions:

| `.gl` field | `S_GPROC32 typind` | function |
|---|---|---|
| `0x1013` | `0x1013` | `a1` |
| **`0x1013`** | **`0x1013`** | **`a2` — a DIFFERENT function sharing a1's signature** |
| `0x1015` `0x1017` `0x1019` `0x101b` `0x101d` `0x101f` | identical | `b1 b2 c1 c2 d1 d2` |
| `0x1020` `0x1022` `0x1024` `0x1026` `0x1028` | identical | `e1 e2 f1 g1 h1` |
| **`0x100e`** `0x1010` | identical | **`M::mf`, `M::mg` — BELOW every free function** |

**15 of 15 exact**, and the two structural facts in bold are what make it an
identification rather than a coincidence: two distinct functions **share** a
value (they share a signature), and the member functions sit **below** the free
ones, so the field is **not monotone in `.gl` record order** and cannot be a
counter over records. `.debug$T` at `0x1001` in the one-function probe reads
`LF_PROCEDURE rvtype=0x0074 nparm=1 arglist=0x1000` — the signature, spelled
out.

Three more at the window boundary, and the control that makes the cross-check
transfer to the workload's own flags:

| N burners | `.gl` field at `/Ox /GS- /c` | `.gl` field with `/Z7` | obj `S_GPROC32` | in the gate's window |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | `0x1029` | `0x1029` | `0x1029` | yes |
| 63 | `0x10fd` | `0x10fd` | `0x10fd` | **yes** |
| 64 | `0x1101` | `0x1101` | `0x1101` | **no** |

**`/Z7` does not move the field.** The cross-check needs debug info to read the
obj side; the workload compiles without it, and this row is what says the number
being cross-checked is the same number.

### 3.3 The law, registered out of sample and hit exactly

`work/w-vocab/burn-sweep.txt`, 91 cells, burner
`struct T<i>{int x;}; static int w<i>(T<i>* p){return p->x+0;}`:

    field(N) = 0x1001 + 4N     violations 0 of 91
    gate framing LOSES the record at N = 64 — predicted at N = 19, hit at ±0

So **w-small's 62/63/64 boundary is derivable, not incidental**: `field(63) =
0x10FD` is the last value in the window and `field(64) = 0x1101` is the first
outside it, with nothing in `0x10FE..=0x1100`.

### 3.4 …and "~64 distinct types" is the WRONG UNIT — four refuted sweeps say so

| sweep | burner | cells | stride | verdict |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| `burn2` | `struct S<i>{int x;}; S<i> g<i>;` | 91 | **0** | 90 declared structs move the field by **nothing** |
| `burn3` | `enum E<i>{…}; E<i> e<i>;` | 91 | **0** | so do 90 enums |
| `burn4` | `static int q<i>(int a)` — same signature | 91 | **0** | **P7a RIGHT** |
| `burn5` | `static int r<i>(int a0…a<i>)` — distinct arity | 131 | **0** | **P7b REFUTED** |
| `burn6` | `int s<i>(int a0…a<i>)` — external, distinct arity | 136 | **0** | **P8 REFUTED** |

P7b and P8 were refuted by an **ill-posed probe**, and saying how is the model:
the trailing `int f(int)` **shares its signature** with `s0`/`r0`/`q0` and
reuses their index. Give the trailing function a signature nothing can share and
the prediction lands:

| sweep | burner | trailing fn | cells | stride | flip |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| `burn7` | `int t<i>(int a0…a<i>)` | `double f9(char*,double,short)` | 136 | **2** | **N = 128, predicted 128, ±0** |

`burn6` says the same thing a second way without needing the trailing function
at all: its *record* count saturates at **exactly 129** gate-visible records
(`s0…s127`, whose procedure indices are `0x1001 + 2i ≤ 0x10FF`, plus the
signature-sharing `f`), first diverging from the wide count at N = 129.

**The corrected statement of AB-g**, which is what should be quoted:

> The gate's framing can see a `.gl` function record **only if its signature's
> CodeView type index is ≤ `0x10FF`** — the first 256 indices, i.e. the first
> **~128 distinct signatures** in the TU at 2 indices each, and as few as ~64
> when the signatures drag in new UDTs (4 per struct-parameter signature,
> measured). It is a bound on **distinct signatures**, not on declared types,
> and a TU that declares 90 structs and no new signature loses nothing.

### 4.1 The counterexample this lane went looking for, and did not find

Before declining on the measurement, the case that would make the widening
*wrong* was constructed for — and **it could not be built**, which is stated
here rather than left as silence:

* **A coincidental frame.** Any `80 <LE32 v<0x10000> 00 00 80` byte run is a
  record to the wide framing. But `per_record` still demands `bound.len() ==
  segs.len()` **and** every offset equal to its split point in order, so a
  spurious record **breaks the count** and refuses. To slip through it would
  have to appear *and* carry an offset equal to a split point *and* appear in
  the right position — three coincidences, and the count check kills any two of
  them.
* **A name swap.** The wide framing changes *which positions* are records, not
  the nearest-preceding-run rule, and any record whose name is >32 bytes away
  or unmangled or `26`-touched still refuses the whole TU **totally**. So the
  wide framing is *more* conservative on all five stop clauses and *less*
  conservative only on the count — and the count is the fail-closed side.
* **The direction that would actually cost something** — a TU that decodes
  today ceasing to decode — is real (more records ⇒ more chances to hit a stop
  clause). §5 measured it: **it does not happen on anything either standing
  instrument grades.**

What *was* built is w-small's own counterexample class, and it is worth
recording that it does not transfer: `l1_counterexample.cpp` breaks a relaxation
of the **count** check (`!=` → `>`), not of the **framing**. Widening the
framing moves the count in the opposite direction.

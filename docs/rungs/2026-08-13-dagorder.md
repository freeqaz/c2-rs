## What it admits, and what it refuses

**No `crates/` change, no `DISCLOSURE.md` row, no function class.** The lane
answers `CFG_SHAPE.md` §6.2 item F's sole remaining characterization blocker
(#3057 item 1): the within-block instruction order. The answer
(`WB_DAGORDER_FINDINGS.md`): `0x10b3219f` is not a tree-to-tuple walk — it is
one 48-byte helper of a **dependence-DAG list scheduler** (DAG build
`0x10b328da`, regions ≤ `0x50` tuples ending at branch/call/label
`0x10be5d4b`, priority `(height<<13)+(fanout<<8)+(symdest<<10)` at
`0x10be5df6`, tie to original tuple index at `0x10be5cea`, cycle issue at
`0x10be60c0` under `LAB_10c1bfe2`'s **one-per-unit, max-two-per-cycle** rule,
latency matrix `0x10c3c1a8`: ALU→ALU 2, ALU→address 5, ALU→store-data 2,
load→ALU 2, cmp→branch 0 / other ALU→branch 2), run three times per function
around globregs and the register allocator (`0x10b7dc51`) plus a fourth time
**after** lowering (`0x10b7df57`; order fixed by `0x10b7e6af`).
**Board #1823 ("no instruction scheduler") is refuted**, and
#3053's "hoists every `lis` to the top of the block" is corrected to a
priority consequence that breaks down at n≥3 (the sixth `lis` of the wb-live
shape lands *after* the first `lwz`) and becomes software pipelining plus
scheduler-induced spills at 20 statements.

Fifteen-cell grid frozen by content hash before the first `cl.exe`; a
committed reference simulator (`docs/whitebox/scripts/dagorder_sim.py`)
reproduces **7 of 8 call-free cells instruction-for-instruction** and
**discriminates the per-unit issue rule** (7/8) from a flat width-2 model
(6/8) on cell `v1`. `ORDER.md`'s black-box rank *(use count desc, first-use
asc)* is the priority's second key and its tie-break, rediscovered from bytes.

**This rung was revised after its first commit.** A delegated second read
contradicted three claims in the findings doc; each was re-checked by hand
against the export and **all three of the lane's own were wrong** — the
cmp→branch latency sense (`0x10c1c25e`), the existence of the per-unit issue
rule (`LAB_10c1bfe2`, which closed the residual the first version had
declared open), and the phase order (mode-0 is the *last* schedule, after
lowering, per `0x10b7e6af` — not a pre-lowering pass). The findings doc keeps
a revision box rather than absorbing the corrections silently.

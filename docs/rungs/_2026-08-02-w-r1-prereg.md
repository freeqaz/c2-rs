## 0. Bias, stated in writing

**I want this to convert, and that is the danger.** The whole brief is written
as though the shape were already solved, and it very nearly is — which makes it
easy to keep nudging an emitter until the bytes agree and then call the fit a
rule. The specific failure mode I am most at risk of is **fitting to two TUs**:
`TomCryptLicense.cpp` and `ZlibLicense.cpp` differ from each other in exactly
one structural axis (the `.bss` symbol's linkage), so a rule that is wrong about
anything else is confirmed twice by a population of one. I therefore commit in
advance to grading against **the fixture as well**, and to treating a rule I
cannot state without pointing at a specific obj as unlanded.

Second, weaker bias: I would rather report "8" than "6". The decline clauses
below are what I expect to have to enforce against myself.

2. **mismatch > 0 anywhere** — the scan, the gate, the sweep, a fixture.

## 4. Boundary, refuted in advance if crossed without new measurement

* **one** namespace-scope object per TU. The ≥3-object `.bss` address
  permutation is unsolved (`OBJ_DYNINIT_SHAPE.md` §7.1: N=6 gives
  `s6 s4 s3 s5 s1 s2`, a hash-table walk) and §10.16 declined it.
* `??__E` / `??__F` **only**. §10.12 measured `??_G` behaving differently on the
  decode side; the rest of the `??_` family is not licensed by anything measured.
* `/O1` only. `/Ox` is a different obj for this class and is **not** in scope.

Outside the boundary the port **refuses**. An `Err(NotImplemented)` outside the
class is a deliverable of this lane, not a shortfall of it.

## 5. What "declining" looks like, so it is not a fallback narrative

If the obj shape turns out not to be derivable from what the port actually has
in hand — most plausibly because the IL does not carry the object's `sizeof`,
its alignment, or the literal's bytes, all of which the shape needs and none of
which I have yet confirmed are present — then the deliverable is that finding,
**with the byte evidence and the name of the missing input**, and the port keeps
returning `NotImplemented`. I am registering that explicitly because I have a
stated bias toward converting, and I want the honest outcome to be pre-approved
rather than argued for after the fact.

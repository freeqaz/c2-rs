## 2. THE FRAME WAS ALREADY PAID — and it is why this lane was cheap

`FrameLayout { locals: 4, out_slots: 0, saved_gprs: 1, saved_fprs: 0 }` gives
`size() = align16(80 + 4 + 8 + 8) = 112` and `locals_base() = 80`. Those are the
reference obj's `stwu 1,-112(1)` and `addi 3,31,80`, with **no new frame rule**;
the funclet's `FrameLayout::default()` gives 96, its own `stwu 1,-96(1)`.
`object_size = 4` is `sizeof(App)`, read out of `.db`'s `LF_CLASS`.

What this class adds to the incumbent prologue is **one word**:

```text
   mflr 12                 mflr 12
   stw  12,-8(1)           stw  12,-8(1)
   std  31,-16(1)          std  31,-16(1)
                           addi 31,1,-F      <- the frame pointer
   stwu 1,-F(1)            stwu 1,-F(1)
   …                       …
   addi 1,1,F              addi 1,31,F       <- restored FROM it
```

and it is transcribed in `coff::ehscope` rather than added to
`FrameLayout::prologue`, which is the gate every shipped emitter runs.
**All 31 words came out byte-identical on the first assembled build**
(`coff::ehscope::tests::the_text_is_byte_identical_to_the_reference_obj`).
Board **#2972**.

The funclet is the other half and is not a variant of the same prologue: it
reconstructs the frame pointer from the **establisher frame in `r12`** —
`addi r31,r12,-F` — before doing anything else, which is why its own frame has
no saved GPR at all.

throughout. So `functions()`, `select_function`, `comdat_function_body`,
`emit_comdat_obj` and `plan_labels` are all **unmodified**, and the census, the
byte fraction and every per-function instrument read this TU exactly as they did
at base.


## 4. THE DESTRUCTOR APPEARS TWICE IN THE OBJ AND ONCE IN THE IL

The `.ex` statement list, off two independent captures:

```text
   26 <ctor>  26 <obj> 2c … 4c        the constructor, with the formals shifted up
   26 <dtor>  26 <obj> 2c … 4c        <- the UNWIND ACTION. Emits no code.
   5c <recv> 01 4b                    state 1
   26 <mem>   26 <obj> 2c … 4c 4b     the member call, while the object is live
   5e 01 21 4b                        the scope END — the normal-path destructor
   54 02 29 <exit>
```

The middle statement is the `__unwindtable$` entry: no `bl`, no relocation, no
symbol. The obj's **two** `bl`s to `??1App@@QAA@XZ` come from the **scope end**
(`.text+0x38`) and from the **funclet** (`.text+0x68`). A reader that treated
the middle statement as a call would emit three `bl` in the body and none in the
funclet. Board **#2977**.

## 5. The label triple — RECONCILED TO ZERO RESIDUAL, and `LABEL_COUNTER.md` gets its scope-object row

Measured in **§7.6's in-the-middle form**, never the counterfactual one: every
number is an offset from the cell's own `.gl` seed. Six cells, four seeds,
`work/w-main2/LABELS.md`, reproduce with `sh work/w-main2/cells.sh`.

With `B` = `plan_labels`' cursor at this function's turn

| symbol | number | lands on |
|---|---|---|
| ip-to-state `$M` ×2 | `B+3`, `B+4` | the member call's `bl`, the destructor's |
| ip-to-state `$T` | `B+5` | the array, in the EH `.rdata` |

## 7. The budgeted unnamed refusal — ONE budgeted, ONE met, and it is a PREREG miss

**`__ehfuncinfo$` is nine dwords and a printed `ORG $+4` pad, not ten.** PREREG
**P4** registered ten at 0.75, derived from the obj's arithmetic alone: the EH
`.rdata` is 64 bytes, `__unwindtable$` sits at `+0x00` and `$T` at `+0x30`, and
**forty bytes is nine dwords plus four bytes of alignment just as exactly as it
is ten dwords**. `EH_RECORDS.md` §11.1 had already separated them, and not with
an obj: c2's own `/FAsc` listing prints the pad as a literal `ORG $+4`
directive, with **pad 0 on 13 probes and pad 4 on 50**.

The emitted bytes are the same either way, so nothing shipped is wrong. What
would have been wrong is the next reader inheriting *"ten dwords"* and computing
no pad where the pad is 0. `coff::ehscope::eh_ip2state_pad` derives it now.
**The commission said *compose, do not re-derive*, and this is the one place this
lane did not.** Board **#2973**.

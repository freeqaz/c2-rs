## 3. Why the shapes were declined, per shape — the census of REASONS

The mission's first question. Each of the five declines had a *different*
mechanism, and only one of them was ever about the harness:

| shape | n | what `select_function` hands back | who finished it, and with what fact |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `tail` | 7,098 | the argument setup, **without the `b <callee>`** | the caller appends `encode_tail_branch(text_offset + len)`. **The fact is `text_offset`.** |
| `seq` | 2,150 | the per-call setups and the post-call tail — **no prologue, no `bl`, no epilogue** | `codegen::call_seq_text(setups, tail, off, layout, guard, early, mode)`. **The fact is `off`**, plus the W10 guard and W11 early-return resolution, which live in the caller. |
| `framed` | 123 | **only the argument setup** — the bytes between the prologue and the `bl` | `codegen::framed_call_text(setup, add_k, off, layout)`. **The fact is `off`.** |
| `cond-pair` | 4 | the whole body **with a zero word at each of its two tail branches** | the caller writes `encode_tail_branch(off + rel_off)` into the two holes. **The fact is `off`.** The `bc` between them was already finished — its displacement is self-relative (`CFG_SHAPE.md` §3.3, board #191). |
| `float-const` | 0 | the body plus one `FpConstRef` per site | **nobody.** The port *refuses* this under `/Gy` (`OBJ_GY_SHAPES.md` §2: several constants from one function append in reverse first-reference order). |

**Four of the five declines are one fact — `text_offset` — and under `/Gy` that
fact is the constant 0.** The fifth is not a harness limit at all; it is the
port's own refusal wearing the instrument's clothes, which is why it keeps the
`partial` bucket and why its tag is now `float-const` rather than a shape name.
The scan prints all of this as `fnbyte-decline-{opt-mode,selector,gy-shape,
data-ref}` and `fnbyte-shape-<shape>-<verdict>`, so the reason is a number on
every run and not a paragraph here.

### 3.1 The pricing, since the mission asked for it

There is none left to quote. **All four became gradeable in one change**, because
they share the one fact. The tractability order the mission guessed at
(`framed` at 123 cheapest, then `seq`) does not exist: `framed` and `seq` are the
*harder* compositions and they cost the same as `tail`, which is zero, because
`PortC2::build` already contained every line. What the lane paid for was
**reachability** — the composition was inline in a loop body that only the
whole-TU emitter could enter.

## 4. The change: one composition, two callers

`crates/c2-core/src/comdat.rs`. `PortC2::build`'s `/Gy` branch had a 135-line
inline `match` composing each function's complete `.text` COMDAT; it is now
`comdat::comdat_function_body(f, mode)` and the arms moved **verbatim**
(`git show 243146e` — 22 insertions, 135 deletions, and the fixture gate reads
`124 port Match, 0 mismatch, 141 not-implemented of 265` across it).

> **The load-bearing constraint: the harness calls the emitter's own code and
> never a copy.** A reconstruction written in `fnbytes.rs` would be a second
> implementation, free to drift, and an alarm that is green about bytes the port
> does not emit is worse than the blind one it replaced. `complete_body` calls
> `comdat_body_from_selected`, which is what `build` calls.

`ComdatDecline` splits the three refusal stages — `Selector`, `Shape`, `DataRef`
— because an instrument that merged them would mis-file its own population: the
first is the port's accept boundary, the second a `/Gy`-only composition limit,
the third a body whose `.text` exists and whose relocation site cannot be derived
from it. `build` propagates all three identically; FBM files them in three
buckets. Measured on the workload: **selector 130,573, gy-shape 0, data-ref 0,
opt-mode 0.**

## 5. THE FINDING — 4,711 wrong bodies, and what they are

`differs` is printed as **witnesses**, not as a count: shape, port/ref/equal word
counts, the first disagreeing word in hex from **both sides**, and the mangled
symbol.

> **Two counts, and they answer different questions.** `fnbyte-differs` is
> **4,711** — one per `(TU, emitted function)` pair, the same unit as the
> denominator. `fnbyte-differs-witnesses` is **1,950** — *distinct mangled
> symbols*, because a template COMDAT is emitted into many TUs. The scan's
> printed signature table counts distinct symbols; the taxonomy below counts
> pairs. Quoting one against the other is how a header-inline population gets
> mistaken for a defect rate (board #222's shape). **601 of the 871 graded TUs
> carry at least one**, in **61 signatures**.

### 5.2 The mechanism, verified by hand against real c2

**c2 at `/O1` inlines a callee defined in the same TU, and the port's IL-level
call recognizers do not model that decision.** Two hand probes, compiled through
the real toolchain at the workload's own profile
(`work/w-fnbyte/probe.sh`, `work/w-fnbyte/probes/`):

```cpp
// p2_void_call_inline.cpp
void g() {}
void f() { g(); }
```

```text
-- .text #5 (4 B) ?g@@YAXXZ     0000  4e800020  blr
-- .text #6 (4 B) ?f@@YAXXZ     0000  4e800020  blr        <- 0 relocations
```

and the census at the same flags:

```text
c2rs census … --flags-file (…/GR /O1 /Oi /EHsc)  -> 2/2 functions in class
  [  1] ok  void-tail-call  …  ?f@@YAXXZ
```

**The port calls `?f` in class and would emit `b ?g@@YAXXZ` with a REL24 and one
more external symbol; c2 emits `blr` and no relocation.** That is signature A in
four lines of source, reproduced from the compiler and not from the scan.

The second probe (`p1_dtor_inline.cpp`, a member destructor) shows the same c2
behaviour and is **refused** by `functions()` at both `/Ox` and `/O1`
(`expr-call-in-expr-recv-field-off0-whole`) — the boundary is real but it is not
where the class ends.

### 5.3 Is it LIVE? No — and the reason matters more than the answer

`c2rs gap` over the two probes classifies both `vocab-gap`: `IlBundle::functions()`
refuses the *whole TU* even though `census_functions()` accepts every function in
it. So no obj was ever emitted wrong, `mismatch` is 0, and it stays 0.

**What is wrong today is the CENSUS's claim, not an obj.** `emit-in-class` is the
PROGRESS MASS's `f` numerator, and 4,711 of its members are claims the oracle now
contradicts. `docs/STATUS.md` trap 2 says a per-function census claim for a
never-emitted body *"can never be graded"*; FBM narrowed that to the emitted
population and read `differs 0`, which was quoted as *"not one of the census's
emitted in-class claims is wrong where it can be checked"*
(`FUNCTION_BYTE_MATCH.md` §6). **That sentence was true of 75.6 % of the
population and is false of the rest**, and the correct restatement is: of the
39,177 emitted in-class claims, **34,466 are confirmed byte-exact, 4,711 are
confirmed wrong, and 0 are unexamined.**

**And it is a loaded gun for the next lane that widens `functions()`.** Every one
of the 4,711 is a function the per-function gate already accepts. A TU-level
widening that admits any TU containing one ships a wrong obj — the #232
transition in the bad direction — and until today no standing instrument could
have said so.

### 5.4 `framed` at 0 of 123 is the sharpest number

Board **#844**'s framed store-run seam builds on `Selected::Framed`, and lane
`w-seam` declined to ship it because the alarm was blind there (#871). It was
right to decline and the reason it gave was **weaker than the truth**: the alarm
was not merely blind, the shape underneath it does not match c2 on a single one
of the 123 workload functions the port accepts. `w-seam`'s own transfer result
(#866: the leaf store plan transfers into a framed body, 12/12 and 34/36) is
about the **run text**; this is about the **whole body**, and the two are not in
tension — c2 does not compile these `?back@vector` accessors as framed bodies at
all.

## 8. Found and not taken

1. **The 4,711 are a work list with a shape.** Family A (1,886 `tail` bodies
   whose reference is a bare `blr`) is the cheapest thing on it and it is a
   *refusal*, not a lowering: a tail call whose callee is defined in this TU and
   is empty must not be accepted. That is a **`c2-il` acceptance** change, not a
   codegen one, and it would move `differs` down by up to 1,886 with the emitter
   untouched. **Not taken here**, because narrowing acceptance in the lane that
   found the defect is exactly the retreat the mission forbids, and because the
   predicate ("c2 will inline this") is a **cost model**, not a syntactic fact —
   board #269/#844's standing hazard.
2. **`framed` at 0 of 123 re-prices board #844.** Whoever picks up the framed
   store-run seam should read §5.4 before §3 of `w-seam`.
3. **Five `float` differs exist at `/O1` on the FIXTURE corpus and zero on the
   workload** — `?fm4@@YAMMMMM@Z` and four siblings, `port=eda000f2` against
   `ref=ec0000f2`, an FP destination-register field. `float` was *already* graded
   before this lane, so this is **not** a finding of the widening; it is a `/O1`
   fixture-regime difference nobody had run the instrument over. Filed as #883,
   unmeasured on any other axis.
4. **`fnbyte-exact-relocated` is 4,664 and trap 6 is now live.** Every credited
   `tail`/`seq`/`framed`/`cond-pair` body carries a REL24 whose *target* FBM does
   not check. Two bodies calling two different functions are byte-identical here.
   The counter was built for exactly this moment and it is printed on every scan;
   closing it means comparing relocation records, which is a `c2-obj` rung.
5. **The whole-TU credit fell 5 → 2 and that is arithmetic, not a loss.**
   `whole_tu` counts emitted functions on a `match` TU the per-function route did
   *not* credit; three of the five are now credited by the route itself. Total
   credit rose.

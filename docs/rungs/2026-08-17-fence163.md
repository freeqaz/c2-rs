Two things follow, and the second is the one that makes the fence decidable:

1. **The categorical fact is `maxState >= 1`, not the throw.** g2 is the
   discriminating cell — a dtor local with no `throw` in the TU — and c2 keeps
   the call. g4 is its complement — a `throw` with nothing unwindable — and c2
   inlines it. A fence written on "does the callee throw" gets both wrong.
2. **In the KEPT cells, c2's three words are *exactly* the port's three words.**
   `lis · addi · b <callee>` is the port's `MultiArgTailCall` lowering verbatim.
   So the fence is not "refuse the risky ones": it separates a population the
   port is already byte-exact on from one where it emits 3 words against 13.

The EH class in column 3 is read from the container by `cflow_key`, which is
what makes the predicate **input-side and decidable** — the prereg's §6.3 test,
and the reason this is a fence and not a denylist of the one known-wrong body.
Independently cross-checked at `df70d78c` by `c2rs census` on all four cells:
g1/g2 report one `eh-state1` body each, g3/g4 report `eh-none` for both bodies.

### 6.1 Clause (c) SHADOWS clause (c2) on any walkable TU — and that is why the cells are captures

This is the finding that decided the cells' form. Census clause (c)
(`callee-defined-in-tu`) is asked **before** clause (c2), and it answers first on
any TU whose `.gl` defined-name walk succeeds — which it does on **every small
TU**. Measured at `d28326b4`: the four-line `g3_mkstring.cpp`, the exact
`MakeString` mirror the fence exists for, reports `callee-defined-in-tu:eof`,
**not** the fence's key.

So clause (c2) is reachable on exactly one condition — *the defined-name walk is
blind on this TU* — which is the condition `ContentMgr_Xbox.cpp` met and the
reason the defect existed at all. Every cell in `strlit_fence.rs` therefore
carries a **carrier** for that blindness: the `gl.rs` #232 shape, whose
implicitly-generated `??1M@@QAA@XZ` is `26`-introduced and refuses
`gl_defined_names` whole-file. Trim those four `struct` lines as noise and the
N/S pair becomes two cells that agree, both green, both meaningless — so the
carrier's load-bearingness is **itself asserted**, by a third test that shows
the identical body reporting clause (c)'s key without it.

Two consequences worth carrying forward. A lane that repairs
`defined_name_set`'s walk (#1721's neighbourhood) will make clause (c) see
everything and silently retire clause (c2) — that test is where it shows up as a
red instead of as a fence nobody exercises. And the fence's reach is bounded by
a property of the *corpus*, not of the predicate: it fires only where the walk is
blind, which is **845 of 871 captured workload TUs** today.

## 8. Does this generalize into a rule? (the half that matters under Option A)

The goal question is now closed as **Option A — reproduce `c2` fully** — so the
question worth more than the +163 is whether §2's predicate is a *rule* or a
*named exemption*. Read honestly, it is **one clause of a rule, with its scope
measured**:

* **What is a rule.** *"c2's inliner does not inline a callee with EH state"* is
  a categorical, callee-side, container-decidable fact, and it is the accept side
  of an axis `WB_INLINE_FINDINGS.md` §7 explicitly declines to offer on 320 obj
  cells. It needs no size, no ceiling and no flag bit — the same property that
  made clause (c)'s *"c2 cannot inline a body it does not have"* shippable.
* **What is an exemption.** Its *scope* here is not: clause (c2) is deliberately
  keyed on `f.data_syms` carrying a `??_C@_0`, so it fires only on
  strlit-carrying bodies. Widening it to every call-bearing body would re-fence
  populations that are byte-exact today, and that widening is a separately
  priced rung — the `GAPS.md` §6 two-sided rule applied in the *other*
  direction. That is a real, deliberately unpaid debt, and it is the natural
  next construct rung on this axis.
* **What bounds it either way.** §6.1: the clause is only *reachable* where the
  defined-name walk is blind. A phase-shaped lane that fixes the walk both
  strengthens clause (c) and retires clause (c2), and the two must be paid
  together.

So the transferable claim is: **the EH-state inline predicate is rule-shaped and
worth generalizing; its current scoping is an exemption and is named as one.**
The counter-evidence is §7.2 — 1,055 of the 1,057 admitted bodies are two
STLport functions, so this rung's *number* is a property of the corpus even
though its *predicate* is a property of c2.

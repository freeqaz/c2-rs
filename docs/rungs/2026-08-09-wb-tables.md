## 1. The rule, and its grade

Two rules, and the grid was frozen in `c609b6f9` before its first `cl.exe`.

**(S) the mask SHAPE rule**, read out of `FUN_10c0a2e2` with
`FUN_10c04daf` @ `0x10c04daf` (returns `0xffffffff` **exactly** when a mask is a
valid PPC rotate mask, else the residue) and `FUN_10c0a170` @ `0x10c0a170` (the
mask cost, in words):

```
rotate != 0 or mask contiguous  ->  rlwinm            (one word)
mask fits u16                   ->  andi.             (one word)
(mask & 0xffff) == 0            ->  andis.            (one word)
residue contiguous              ->  TWO rlwinm
otherwise                       ->  materialise, then `and`
```

**(B) the BIAS rule**, obj-grounded on 20 cells: inside a value-producing
relational, the mask step is **`li` + `and` iff `base == 0`** — whatever the
mask shape, whatever the registers — and follows rule (S) otherwise.

**Core: 11 of 12. Registers: 11 of 12. Six cells word-exact including every
register**, four of them relational cells predicted from rule (B).

The pair worth reading is `r2_pow_nb` / `r1_bias16`: the *same* contiguous mask
family, no bias and a bias. Rule (S) alone says `rlwinm` for both. c2 emitted
`li 10,256 · and 3,11,10` for the first and `rlwinm 11,11,0,28,28 · addi 3,11,16`
for the second — rule (B)'s answer both times, in one obj.

## 2. The two prior bounds, refuted, and this lane's own miss

**Run 2's black-box bound is refuted in both directions.** Cell `d1_consumed`
was frozen for exactly this: `base == 0`, a **contiguous** mask, and source
**and** destination both `r11` — run 2's bound predicts `rlwinm`, and c2
emitted `li 10,8 · and 11,11,10`. The mirror is calibration cell `c_and_add`:
source `r3`, destination `r11` (**different**), and c2 emitted `rlwinm`. The
registers do not decide it in either direction.

**Run 1's `R-M1` is corrected on its second half.** "A contiguous mask is always
`rlwinm`" holds and survives 17 further cells. "…never `andi.` — c2 does not
clobber `CR0` for a value" is wrong: `andi.` is exactly what a **non-contiguous**
16-bit mask gets. `x & 0x8001` is `andi. 3,3,32769`. Run 1's grid contained no
non-contiguous mask, so its five cells could not have seen it.

**This lane's miss is `r5_bias1`, and it names a boundary neither run had.**
`x<10u ? 0x8001 : 1` was predicted as rule (B)'s biased form and came out as a
**real compare-and-branch** — no idiom at all, so neither rule was consulted.
`0x8001` is the only result constant among the 44 cells this lane compiled that
does not fit a signed 16-bit immediate, and it is the only cell that kept its
branch. That is one cell, so it is **recorded as an observation and explicitly
not offered as a rule**; if it is real it lives in the if-conversion pass at
`0x10b813f1`, which run 2's §6.3c could not site either.

## 3. What the calibration cost, disclosed

`grids/wb-tables/calib.cpp` (19 cells) plus a scratch second round
(`work/wb-tables/calib2.cpp`, 11 cells) ran before the grid was written, and
both are unscored. **They were read as full word sequences, not sizes.** Rule
(S) was derivable from `FUN_10c0a2e2` before any compile and the grid tests it
on six mask shapes appearing in no calibration file; **rule (B) was FOUND in the
calibration and then explained, not predicted cold**, and §3.7 therefore tests
it as a generalisation over five new relational cells at four new mask shapes.
That distinction is stated because a lane that blurs it is claiming a blind
prediction it did not make.

Calibration earned its keep three times: it refuted run 2's register bound
**before the freeze** (so the grid could be aimed at rule (B) instead of a dead
rival); it produced the two-`rlwinm` residue path, which is why `m5_two` and
`m6_four` exist; and it showed the `cntlzw` rival's own chain obeying rule (B)
too, so the rule is stated over the idiom layer rather than over one expander.

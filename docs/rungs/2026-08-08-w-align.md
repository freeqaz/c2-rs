## §2 What `0xC6` is

`TAG_WIDE` (`0x40`) marks the presence of the mark byte — `docs/IL_TYPE_WIDE_TAG.md`
derived that for `.ex`/`.sy` on 2026-07-31 — and **it is orthogonal to the width
field**. So the alignment is read off `tag & !TAG_WIDE`:

    C2 -> 1     C4 -> 2     C6 -> 4     C8 -> 8     CA (=16) -> still REFUSED

23 frozen cells, at the workload's own `/nologo /wd4355 /wd4164 /c /GR /O1 /Oi
/EHsc`. Each cell's tag is read off `.gl`; each cell's alignment is read off **c2's
own obj** — the `Characteristics` alignment nibble of whatever section c2 put
that symbol in. Nothing below is inferred from the IL.

| tag | masked | read as | **c2's obj** | cells |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| `82` | `82` | 1 | **1** | `char` (T12) |
| `86` | `86` | 4 | **4** | `{int,int}` · derived · nested · `A[4]` (T10 T13 T17 T18) |
| `88` | `88` | 8 | **8** | `double` · **poly**+`double` · **poly**+`long long` (T11 T02 T05 G03 G05) |
| **`C6`** | `86` | **4** | **4** | poly+`int` · poly+`char` · poly+`char[64]` · poly+empty · poly`[4]` · virtual **base** · virtual **dtor** (T01 T03 T04 T06 T07 T14 T15 G01 G02) |
| **`C8`** | `88` | **8** | **8** | `__declspec(align(8))`, polymorphic **and** plain (T08 T16) |
| **`CA`** | `8A` | **refused** | 16 | `__declspec(align(16))` (T09 G04) |

**21 of 21 named object records confirmed, 0 contradicted.**

### §2.1 The three adjacent readings, and the cell that kills each

Each was named in the prereg with the cell built to refute it, *before* the
capture:

* **"the tag is the SIZE"** — `IL_TYPE_TAGS.md` §1 tabulates it under that
  heading. `T03` is **size 8** at `C6` and `T04` is **size 68** at `C6`. Dead.
* **"`0xC6` is an atomic *wide aggregate* tag, pinned at 4"** — the reading
  #1110's own wording invites. `T02` and `T05` are **polymorphic** and spell
  **`88`**. Dead, and see §3.
* **"the tag is the largest member's width"** — `T04`'s largest member is
  `char[64]` and it spells `C6`, not a 64-width tag. Dead.

### §2.2 `__declspec(align(N))` MOVES the tag — the prereg's least-confident call, and it held

**P5, confidence 0.50.** `struct A { int a; }` is naturally 4-aligned and spells
`86`. `__declspec(align(8)) struct A { int a; }` spells **`C8`**, and c2 gives it
ALIGN_8:

```text
   ?g@@3UA@@A   00   c8   81   06   00 02   01   08   00
                ^NUL ^tag ^mark ^kind ^frame ^link ^size ^attr
```

So the width field under the wide bit tracks the type's **required** alignment,
not its natural layout. **This is the trap the grid exists for**: a widening
fitted on `C6`-means-4 and read as *natural* alignment emits a wrong
`Characteristics` word for exactly `T08` and `T16` — and `T16` is one of the
three cells that now converts, so the wrong version would have been a live
`mismatch`, not a refusal.

### §2.3 `CA` keeps refusing, and that is the point of the function

c2 really does give `T09` and `G04` **ALIGN_16** (`Characteristics` nibble 5).
`coff::container::placement_align` models 1/2/4/8 and returns `None` above, so
reading `CA` as an alignment hands the writer a nibble it cannot honour.
`align_of_type_tag` stops at `88`. `fixtures/cpp/walign_dyninit_align16.cpp`
makes that stopping point a **graded** refusal instead of a comment: a later
lane that adds `8A` without teaching `placement_align` about 16 turns that
fixture from a refusal into a mismatch.

### §2.4 The mark byte's VALUE is now matched, not stepped over

All ten wide cells here carry mark `81`. `IL_TYPE_WIDE_TAG.md` §8 item 2 records
`0x84` as a **second value in `.ex`** (106 occurrences) and registers "the value
set is unknown" as that work's residual risk. `data_object_at` previously
required only bit 7 and stepped over the byte; the alignment reading now
requires `0x81`. That is a strict **narrowing** of the newly-accepted set and
costs nothing measurable — every wide tag was refused outright before — and it
is the fail-closed direction the decline floor demanded.

## §3 THE WIDE BIT IS NOT "THE CLASS HAS A VTABLE" (board #1118)

`docs/IL_TYPE_WIDE_TAG.md` §2.1 established, for `.ex`, that *"a class with a
vtable spells its type with tag bit 6 set and one extra byte before the kind"*,
from a four-line probe holding everything but the keyword fixed. **That is true
of `.ex` and it does not carry over to a `.gl` DATA record**, and this grid
separates the two in both directions:

| | wide | not wide |
|---|---|---|
| **polymorphic** | T01 T03 T04 T06 T07 T14 T15 G01 G02 (`C6`), T08 T09 (`C8`/`CA`) | **T02, T05, G03 (`88`)** |
| **not polymorphic** | **T16 (`C8`)** | T10 T11 T12 T13 T17 T18 G05 |

Both off-diagonal boxes are populated. `T16` is a `struct { int a; }` with
`__declspec(align(8))` and no virtual anything, and it is wide; `T02` is
`struct { virtual void f(); double d; }` and it is not.

**The co-occurrence that survives this grid is with the ALIGNMENT, not with
polymorphism**: every wide cell is one whose required alignment is not the
alignment its members alone would imply, and every non-wide cell is one where it
is. That is a description of 23 cells and **not a rule** — no probe here makes a
type go wide on demand while holding alignment fixed, which is exactly the
caution `IL_TYPE_WIDE_TAG.md` §8 item 2 wrote about its own mark byte. **The
reading shipped does not depend on it**: it masks the bit off and reads the field
underneath, and the field is confirmed 21 of 21 either way.

`IL_TYPE_WIDE_TAG.md` §2.1 is **not** refuted — it is a statement about `.ex`.
What is corrected is any inference from it to `.gl`, which #1110's phrase "the
**wide aggregate** form" invites.

## §10 Found and deliberately not taken, priced

* **`DATA_ATTR = 0xA0` (#1109).** Out of scope by the brief and by #232. **Must
  land with #1107's item 4 (the COMDAT `Section` emitter) or not at all.**
* **`8A`/`CA` = alignment 16.** Measured here — c2 emits `Characteristics`
  nibble 5 for `__declspec(align(16))` — and **refused**, because
  `placement_align` and `align_nibble` model 1/2/4/8 and both would have to grow
  together with the `.bss` allocator (`coff::data::bump_layout`, Rule A3′) that
  shares their promotion table. **Price: three functions and their own grid, in
  `crates/c2-core/src/coff/`, which is not this lane's seam.** The refusal is
  graded by `walign_dyninit_align16.cpp`.
* **`sy.rs::read_type_prefix` still requires the literal `81`.**
  `IL_TYPE_WIDE_TAG.md` §8 item 4 filed it; this lane's `.gl` cells all carry
  `81` too, so nothing here prices it either way. Left alone for the reason that
  page gives: `.sy` binding is all-or-nothing per file.
* **The wide bit's own meaning in `.gl`.** §3 shows what it is *not* and offers a
  co-occurrence, not a rule. A probe that moves a type across the boundary while
  holding alignment fixed would settle it. **Not needed by anything today** —
  the shipped reading masks the bit off.
* **`data_linkage` keeps its own local copy of the `TAG_WIDE`/`WIDE_MARK`
  constants** (`gl.rs:900`), now shadowing the module-level ones. Deliberately
  not merged: it reads no alignment, and this wave has four recorded cases of
  lanes erasing each other through shared semantics with no textual conflict.
  **Price: three lines, and it belongs to whoever next touches that function.**

## §11 Corrections to the brief

1. **"one match arm plus the cells that prove the alignment"** (#1110) — the arm
   is one match; the **mark-byte narrowing** is a second change, registered in
   §2.4 and taken because the decline floor forbids a reader that is merely
   mostly right.
2. **"`emit_data_obj` is a graded consumer"** (#1110) — there are **two**, and
   the one that converted first is `dyninit_tu` (§5). A lane pricing this through
   `data_tu` alone would have found the `C6` cells all refusing at `data_tu`'s
   gate 1 (*no functions*), because a polymorphic object is dynamically
   initialised and its TU therefore has a function.
3. **"`.gl` reads 0 / 1 of 12"** (#1108/#1110) — reproduced to the digit at base
   and now a standing reading rather than a spike (§4).
4. **"the honest expectation is that this converts zero TUs"** — **correct for
   the workload** (§1) and **wrong for the fixture corpus**, where three cells
   convert byte-exact. Both were registered separately in the prereg and both
   were needed: F2 would have declined the lane if nothing converted anywhere.

## 5. R8 — THE DECLINE, AND WHY IT IS NOT A CODEGEN PRICE

### 5.1 Isolated four ways

| cell | the leaf's target | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `r2` | an **external** — the CONTROL | **match** |
| `r1` | a **framed function this TU defines** (a `b`) | vocab-gap |
| `r3` | a **leaf this TU defines** (a `b`, no framed function anywhere) | vocab-gap |
| `r4` | a **leaf this TU defines, from a framed caller** (a `bl`) | vocab-gap |

**Any intra-TU call edge, of any kind, refuses the whole TU.** The clause is
`c2_il::func::bind::callee_defined_here`, applied by `IlBundle::functions`, and
it is deliberate:

> *"A callee that is also DEFINED here is out of class: c2 may inline it, and the
> port cannot. Refused wholesale rather than by callee size, because what makes
> c2 inline (and what it does to the symbol table and `.pdata` when it does) is
> uncharacterized."*

and, in `callee_defined_here_unmodelled`'s doc:

> *"`IlBundle::functions` **deliberately does NOT** take the exemption. The gate
> is the emit path and stays wholesale … no obj has ever been emitted for a TU
> that defines one of its own callees, so the exemption there would be a widening
> with no capture behind it."*

### 5.2 Why this lane did not widen it

* It is **`w-inlfence`'s seam**, and that lane already declined to narrow it on
  the emit path with a stated reason.
* It is a **shared predicate over the whole workload**. Widening it converts
  refusals into wrong emits on every TU where c2 *does* inline — which is the
  outcome CLAUDE.md ranks below every gap. `framed-call` grading **0-for-123** on
  this workload is the same fact from the other side.
* **The port cannot decide it from IL.** c1 does not inline and c2 does; both
  bodies are in the `.ex` either way. Whether c2 inlined a given callee is
  exactly the uncharacterized thing the fence names, and reading the reference
  obj to find out is not available to the port.
* PREREG clause **D-SHARED** covers this in advance: widen a shared predicate
  only if the widening is provably byte-neutral on the population it already
  admits. It is not.

**So the decline is at a clause with no measured lowering, not at a quantity of
codegen.** `vsnprnc.cpp`'s codegen distance is **zero bytes**.

### 9.6 The cr0/cr6 discriminator: what was asked, and what can honestly be said

Board **#260** records `vsnprnc`'s `2c030000` as a realized counterexample to
`CFG_SHAPE.md` §3.2 and says the discriminator is unmeasured. This lane was
asked to measure it and **did not**. What it can report is a bound.

Both words appear in every cell of every grid built here, at every one of the
axes varied — **6 arities including the out-of-class n = 8, 12 store types
including the four that refuse, 4 source spellings**:

```text
  cmpwi cr0,r3,0     2c030000     `result < 0`
  cmpwi cr6,r3,-2    2f03fffe     `result != -2`
```

So the discriminator is **not** arity, **not** the store width, **not** the store
form, **not** the arm spelling. Three candidates survive and **no grid here
separates them**, because every cell holds both tests fixed by construction:

1. the **literal** — `0` against `-2`;
2. the **relation** — `<` (LT) against `!=` (EQ);
3. the **ordinal position** of the use — first against second.

`w-clear` §2.1 reported the same non-measurement (*"the discriminator is still
unmeasured and this lane did not measure it"*), so this is the second lane to
leave it. The difference is that the space is now four axes smaller, and the
experiment that closes it is named: one probe TU crossing {literal} × {relation}
× {position}. Meanwhile the emitter **pins** both CRs and the reader refuses
anything else, so #260's failure mode — a plausible-looking wrong `bf` operand —
is prevented by a fence rather than by a rule that might be wrong.

## 2. Deliverable 1 — the FRONTIER's first cost-ordered ranking

Full document: [`work/w-frame/RANKING.md`](../../work/w-frame/RANKING.md).
Tooling: `work/w-frame/featmap.py`, `analyse.py`, `modectl.py`, `refobj.sh`.

`c2rs gap` prints the frontier ranked by **blocked-function count**. `w-front`,
`w-pair` and `w-cfgimpl` each took a target off the head of that list, each
converted zero, and each independently reported that the key is wrong — board
**#198**, the fourth instance of **#150**'s shape.

**The method.** For each of the 17 TUs: compile the real obj at the **workload's
own flags** (not `c2rs compile`, which board #195 shows hardcodes `/Ox`),
disassemble every code section, classify each emitted function over four axes
(`insn:` mnemonic, `frame:` class, `reloc:` kind, `sym:` family), union over
**every** emitted function — a TU matches only when the whole obj does — and
subtract the vocabulary the port has **demonstrably emitted**: the 102
`Port=Match` fixtures plus the 8 matching workload TUs.

**Spearman ρ between the published key and this one is +0.295.**
`EncryptXTEA.cpp` and `IPP_basicmath_xbox.cpp` are joint-**first** published
and joint-**last** here.

### 2.1 The finding

> **The port has emitted 105 functions byte-exact. 28 are framed. 2 branch.
> ZERO are both.**

Its entire branching capability is two leaf functions from w-cfgimpl's
`cond-tail-pair` rung. **10 of the 17 frontier TUs need the product**, across 13
functions.

That is w-cfgimpl's *"the wall is the general framed-function class"*, sharpened:
the wall is not frames — the port emits 28 framed functions — and it is not
branches. It is **frames × blocks**, a cell of the cross-product with no witness
anywhere in the port's history, and everything that makes a framed body
multi-block is un-witnessed at once (block layout inside a frame,
branch-displacement fixup against a materialized epilogue, the intra-section `b`
encoding of board **#191**, callee-saved allocation across blocks).

**And it is not the whole wall.** Seven frontier TUs need no framed-and-branching
function and are blocked by something else each: schedule (`xboxheap`), the four
folds w-cfgimpl measured (`xboxmem`), the EH critical path (`Main`), leaf CTR
loops (`Sort`, `Pool`, `IPP_basicmath`), leaf float division plus a straddling
`lis`/`lfs` (`Biquad`). **22 of the missing constructs occur in exactly one TU.**

### 2.2 The key's blind spots, registered before the numbers and fired by them

The prereg §2 registered two, both pointing the same way (TUs look *cheaper*
than they are). Both fired, on cells where the truth is independently known:

| cell | key says | truth | source |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `xboxheap.cpp` | **gap 0** | unemittable — diverges at **instruction 0** on instruction *order* | w-pair |
| `xboxmem.cpp` | **gap 1** | **seven** independent facts across two functions | w-cfgimpl §4, 10-cell grid |

So the mechanized key **excludes correctly** — everything at `gap ≥ 6` is
genuinely 6+ constructs away and no lane should look there — and **does not rank
the head**. The final pick still needs the disassembly read, which §6 does.

| **Leave-one-out** — each matching TU scored against a vocabulary *excluding it* (scoring it against one that includes it is a tautology) | **8/8 clean.** `wit = 0` for the 3 that contain code; the other **5 matching TUs emit no function at all** and are reported `-1`, not silently `0` |

## 4. What was built, and why it is not a consolation prize

Reading `crates/c2-core/src/codegen/cond_tail.rs` produced a **correction to
this lane's own ranking**, and it is the reason there is a rung at all:

> **`bt` and `cmpwi` are already written.** `branch_sense` maps all six
> relations to `BO_TRUE`/`BO_FALSE`; the emitter picks `cmpwi` for a signed
> operand and `cmplwi` for an unsigned one. **Neither had ever been graded by
> the real `c2`.**

Every W8 fixture tests `v1 == 0` on a **pointer** — `Rel::Eq` (→ `BO_FALSE`
only) on an unsigned operand (→ `cmplwi` only) against the literal **0**. So
five of `branch_sense`'s six rows, the whole signed-compare path and every
non-zero comparison immediate were held up by a unit test comparing the port's
table **to itself**, which cannot fail for the right reason. `STATUS.md` trap 5
— *"absence reads as success unless something forbids it"*, recorded twelve
times — and board **#137**'s shape.

`port_vocab` is measured from objs the port has *demonstrably emitted*, so
`insn:bt` and `insn:cmpwi` are honestly absent from it. **The ranking is right
that they are missing from the port's evidence and wrong to imply they are
missing from its code.** That correction travels with the ranking, and it is
also why the ranking's `gap` column must be read as *"unwitnessed"*, not
*"unimplemented"*.

### 4.1 The fixtures

`w9_rel_signed.cpp` and `w9_rel_unsigned.cpp` — **twelve cells**, six relations ×
signed/unsigned, in the band-3 class the W8 gate already accepts (both arms
tail-call a *different* external, so no fold cost model is needed — board #187
stays declined here as it was in W8).

The shape is W8's with as few things moved as possible, so a failing cell
localizes: the scrutinee is the **third** formal, so neither arm wants its
register and no entry-block park is involved. `w9_rel_unsigned` also carries the
corpus's **first non-zero comparison immediate** (7) — the cell that would catch
a `u > 7` → `u >= 8` canonicalization, which would move a byte the port believes
it controls.

### 4.2 The fixture is not vacuous — checked, not assumed

A branch fixture that folds grades nothing, which is the failure mode §5.0 of
`CFG_SHAPE.md` warns about and the reason `Pool.cpp` is the wrong TU to grade on.
So the reference obj was read:

```text
  0000  2f050000  cmpwi  cr6,r5,0        <- signed, first oracle witness
  0004  409a0008  bf     26,+8           Rel::Eq   BO=4  bit EQ
  001c  419a0008  bt     26,+8           Rel::Ne   BO=12 bit EQ   <- first `bt`
  0034  40980008  bf     24,+8           Rel::Lt   BO=4  bit LT
  004c  41980008  bt     24,+8           Rel::Ge   BO=12 bit LT
  0064  40990008  bf     25,+8           Rel::Gt   BO=4  bit GT
  007c  41990008  bt     25,+8           Rel::Le   BO=12 bit GT
  … and in w9_rel_unsigned, `2b050007  cmplwi cr6,r5,7` at every compare
```

All three CR bits, both `BO` values, both compare forms, a non-zero immediate.
The `li r5,0` / `li r4,0` pair also re-confirms the incumbent
**descending-destination** rule at two literals.

### 4.3 The result, and the prediction it refutes

**All twelve came out `Port=Match` on the first differential run**, at all
twelve `gate.sh` lanes.

Registered prediction **E3** said *"at least one of the new cells does NOT come
out `Port=Match` first time"* — taken neat, because an unwitnessed table row is
unwitnessed. **E3 is REFUTED**, and its registered rival R-E3 wins:
`docs/CFG_SHAPE.md` §3.1's branch table **transfers whole**. That is a real
result about the spec, not about this lane, and it is the second time
`CFG_SHAPE.md` has come out of an implementation intact.

### 4.4 One boundary found by bisection, not by guessing

The first draft of both fixtures censused **0/6**. Rather than adjust until it
went green, four probe cells (`work/w-frame/p/probe1.cpp`) isolated the cause:
it is **not** the relation, **not** the signedness and **not** the scrutinee's
parameter position — it is `h3(v1, 0, v1)`, an else-arm that passes **the same
formal to two slots**. `plan_cond_pair` refuses it, correctly: that is a value
wanted at two destinations, and the park is measured at one. The fixtures use
`h3(v1, 0, 0)`; the refusal stands and is now a named boundary rather than a
mystery.

## 6. Why nothing converted — the five refusals, hand-counted

The estimate's §1.1, restated because it is what the next lane needs. Counting
**independent** refusals, per the rule *"if one quantity governs several
boundaries, that is one refusal"*:

**`src/system/negate_test.cpp` is the cheapest TU on the frontier** — two
byte-identical 80-byte framed functions — and it is **9th of 17** on the
published list and **5th** on this lane's own key. Neither key found it; the
disassembly did.

| # | refusal | independent because |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a **framed body containing basic blocks** | `cond_tail.rs` computes its displacement from a *fixed* three-block shape and W8 deliberately built **no fixup list**. This body has 5 blocks, 4 branches, 3 targets, two of them shared. Forces `CFG_SHAPE.md` §6's block IR |
| 2 | **`cmpwi` / `bt`** | one quantity — the compare/branch encoder — governs both boundaries. **This rung closes it**: written before, graded now |
| 3 | the **intra-section unconditional `b`** — true displacement, no relocation | board **#191**. The port's `b` today is a tail call with a section-start placeholder + `REL24`. Choosing the encoding is distinct from computing the displacement |
| 4 | **two `bl` sites in one body** | board **#35**, PARTIAL and explicitly blocked on ">1 call per body". Here they are on exclusive paths — the easy half — but `CallSeq` admits one |
| 5 | the **register plan** — scrutinee in r10, result temp in r11, shared argument hoisted to r3, `mr r11,r3` / `mr r3,r11` across the join | `plan_cond_pair` parks at **r11 and only r11**; `CODEGEN_W6_COMPARE.md` §6 calls the descent to r10 *"demonstrably richer than a descending counter and not characterized"*. An allocation rule with **ONE witness** — the TU's two functions are byte-identical, so it is one shape |

Prereg decline clause **1** (minimum ≥ 4) fires at five; clause **2** (an
allocation or schedule decision with fewer than three witnesses) fires on
refusal 5. The rung ships refusal **2** and declines the other four by name.

   `mmio.cpp`** — w-pair named it "the natural first target" on the strength of
   8 of 11 functions already in class, and it is 9th here and wants

   is wanted by 4 frontier TUs and has no witness. `encode_cmpwi` already
   refuses a literal outside `i16`; the register form is simply absent.
3. **Board #187's ~30-cell fold probe** — still the highest-value follow-up in
   `CFG_SHAPE.md`, and this rung deliberately did not need it. The W9 class is
   band 3 by construction, exactly as W8's is.
4. **The `xboxheap.cpp` schedule.** It is the *only* frontier TU at `gap 0`, so
   if the interleave rule were ever settled it converts with no new instruction
   at all. w-pair killed six candidate rules; that is the shape of the remaining
   work, not a reason to retry the same six.
5. **A same-formal-to-two-slots arm** (§4.4). One value, two destinations. A
   named boundary now; needs the r10 descent, i.e. refusal 5 above.

## 2. Deliverable 1 — the FRONTIER's first cost-ordered ranking

Full document: [`work/w-frame/RANKING.md`](../../work/w-frame/RANKING.md).
Tooling: `work/w-frame/featmap.py`, `analyse.py`, `modectl.py`, `refobj.sh`.

`c2rs gap` prints the frontier ranked by **blocked-function count**. `w-front`,
`w-pair` and `w-cfgimpl` each took a target off the head of that list, each
converted zero, and each independently reported that the key is wrong — board
**#198**, the fourth instance of **#150**'s shape.

**The method.** For each of the 17 TUs: compile the real obj at the **workload's
own flags** (not `c2rs compile`, which board #195 shows hardcodes `/Ox`),
disassemble every code section, classify each emitted function over four axes
(`insn:` mnemonic, `frame:` class, `reloc:` kind, `sym:` family), union over
**every** emitted function — a TU matches only when the whole obj does — and
subtract the vocabulary the port has **demonstrably emitted**: the 102
`Port=Match` fixtures plus the 8 matching workload TUs.

**Spearman ρ between the published key and this one is +0.295.**
`EncryptXTEA.cpp` and `IPP_basicmath_xbox.cpp` are joint-**first** published
and joint-**last** here.

### 2.1 The finding

> **The port has emitted 105 functions byte-exact. 28 are framed. 2 branch.
> ZERO are both.**

Its entire branching capability is two leaf functions from w-cfgimpl's
`cond-tail-pair` rung. **10 of the 17 frontier TUs need the product**, across 13
functions.

That is w-cfgimpl's *"the wall is the general framed-function class"*, sharpened:
the wall is not frames — the port emits 28 framed functions — and it is not
branches. It is **frames × blocks**, a cell of the cross-product with no witness
anywhere in the port's history, and everything that makes a framed body
multi-block is un-witnessed at once (block layout inside a frame,
branch-displacement fixup against a materialized epilogue, the intra-section `b`
encoding of board **#191**, callee-saved allocation across blocks).

**And it is not the whole wall.** Seven frontier TUs need no framed-and-branching
function and are blocked by something else each: schedule (`xboxheap`), the four
folds w-cfgimpl measured (`xboxmem`), the EH critical path (`Main`), leaf CTR
loops (`Sort`, `Pool`, `IPP_basicmath`), leaf float division plus a straddling
`lis`/`lfs` (`Biquad`). **22 of the missing constructs occur in exactly one TU.**

### 2.2 The key's blind spots, registered before the numbers and fired by them

The prereg §2 registered two, both pointing the same way (TUs look *cheaper*
than they are). Both fired, on cells where the truth is independently known:

| cell | key says | truth | source |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `xboxheap.cpp` | **gap 0** | unemittable — diverges at **instruction 0** on instruction *order* | w-pair |
| `xboxmem.cpp` | **gap 1** | **seven** independent facts across two functions | w-cfgimpl §4, 10-cell grid |

**Say this plainly, because it is what the key is worth: the feature-union
ranking EXCLUDES CORRECTLY AT THE TAIL AND DOES NOT RANK THE HEAD.** Everything
it puts at `gap ≥ 6` is genuinely six or more constructs away and no lane should
look there. Its top three are all TUs whose real distance is *derivation* or
*schedule*, which no obj-side classifier can see, and its own author's target
(`negate_test.cpp`, §6) is **5th** on it. **Do not pick a target off its head
without reading the disassembly.** A ranking honest about where it fails is
worth more than one that claims the head.

## 4. What was built, and why it is not a consolation prize

Reading `crates/c2-core/src/codegen/cond_tail.rs` produced a **correction to
this lane's own ranking**, and it is the reason there is a rung at all:

> **`bt` and `cmpwi` are already written.** `branch_sense` maps all six
> relations to `BO_TRUE`/`BO_FALSE`; the emitter picks `cmpwi` for a signed
> operand and `cmplwi` for an unsigned one. **Neither had ever been graded by
> the real `c2`.**

Every W8 fixture tests `v1 == 0` on a **pointer** — `Rel::Eq` (→ `BO_FALSE`
only) on an unsigned operand (→ `cmplwi` only) against the literal **0**. So
five of `branch_sense`'s six rows, the whole signed-compare path and every
non-zero comparison immediate were held up by a unit test comparing the port's
table **to itself**, which cannot fail for the right reason. `STATUS.md` trap 5
— *"absence reads as success unless something forbids it"*, recorded twelve
times — and board **#137**'s shape.

`port_vocab` is measured from objs the port has *demonstrably emitted*, so
`insn:bt` and `insn:cmpwi` are honestly absent from it. **The ranking is right
that they are missing from the port's evidence and wrong to imply they are
missing from its code.** That correction travels with the ranking, and it is
also why the ranking's `gap` column must be read as *"unwitnessed"*, not
*"unimplemented"*.

## 4.5 The sweep — which other productions has the oracle never seen?

Asked for at the funnel, because §4's defect shape is **invisible everywhere it
exists**: code that is written, untested against `c2`, and passing a
self-referential unit test emits nothing wrong — it simply never runs, and no
byte compare, no census disagreement and no `mismatch` can see it.

### 4.5.1 Method — derived from the source by coverage, never from a list

A hand-written list is the defect: *a table test proves completeness only over
the list it was written from*, and that exact failure is on this project's
record. So the list is generated. `work/w-frame/sweep.py`:

Build the harness with `-C instrument-coverage` and collect **two** profiles:

* **A = GRADED.** Coverage of only those runs whose obj was **byte-compared and
  matched**: the 105 `Port=Match` fixtures under `c2rs perf`, **plus each of the
  12 `scripts/lanes.txt` flag lanes restricted to that lane's own matching set**,
  **plus** the 8 matching workload TUs at the workload's flags. Every one of
  those runs reports `mismatch 0` over a 100 %-match population, so every region
  executed under A is graded by the oracle.
* **B = REACHED.** Coverage of `c2rs gap` over **all** 222 fixtures at **all 12**
  lanes. This includes the 117 `NotImplemented` ones, whose codegen paths run as
  far as the refusal and are never byte-compared. **B over-credits on purpose.**

Then `never in B` = never executed at all; `in B but not in A` = the **suspect
band**, executed only on a refusal path or under a build the port declined —
which is exactly where `branch_sense`'s five rows lived.

**Both bounds are reported because neither alone is honest.** A alone
under-credits and B alone over-credits, and the method caught itself doing both:
the first A missed the workload TUs and falsely accused `dyninit_thunk_text`; the
second missed the gate's per-lane runs and falsely accused **24** `/O1`
register-allocation regions in `leaf/compare.rs`. Each correction is a
measurement of the instrument, and each shrank the band.

### 4.5.3 The one production the sweep found, and it is graded now

**`leaf/compare.rs (Rel::Le, signed)` — the `a <= 0` zero fold** — with
`encode_orc`, which nothing else in the crate calls:

```text
   neg   r11,a
   orc   d,a,r11        <- the only `orc` the port can emit
   srwi  r3,d,31
```

Its own comment reads *"/O1: as for `>` above — the `orc` consumes the dying
`neg`"*: the scratch `d` is **11 at `/O1` and 10 otherwise**, a rule with a
witness at `>` and **none of its own here**. `wcf_neighbours.cpp::ctl_cmp` is
`a > 0` and is the entire reason the `>` arm is graded; nothing anywhere was
`a <= 0`.

**Why `w6_rel_k.cpp` could not have caught it, which is the transferable part:**
all twenty of its bodies compare against a **non-zero** literal (5, −3, 1,
32767, −32768). It is thorough on the *relation* axis and *literal-magnitude*
axis and holds the *zero* case fixed at "absent". **A fixture family can be
exhaustive on the axis it varies and totally blind on the one it holds fixed** —
and looking at it, it reads as complete.

`w9_cmp_zero_le.cpp` grades it. Both register variants reproduce against real
`c2`: `/Ox` → `orc r10,r3,r11`, `/O1` → `orc r11,r3,r11`. **The fitted rule
transfers**, so this closes green rather than red — the same outcome as §4.3,
and the second time this lane registered against a spec and lost.

### 4.5.4 A second finding, from the four cells that would not go green

Four sibling arms — `(Rel::Gt|Lt|Le|Ge, unsigned)` — were also never executed. I
added their source spelling (`a > 0u`, `a < 0u`, `a <= 0u`, `a >= 0u`) to the
fixture. The obj is correct and `Port=Match`, and **the four arms still did not
execute.**

That is a measurement, not a failure: **`c1xx` canonicalizes these upstream**, so
the IL `c2` is handed never contains them. `a > 0u` arrives as `Rel::Ne`
(`addic`/`subfe`), `a <= 0u` as `Rel::Eq` (`cntlzw`/`rlwinm`), and `a < 0u` /
`a >= 0u` are folded to constants (`li r3,0` / `li r3,1`) before any comparison
survives. **The port models four cases its only IL producer cannot emit.** They
are not a grading gap — they are unreachable, and no fixture can ever cover them.
Left in place and *recorded* rather than deleted: the record is what stops the
next sweep re-finding them.

### 4.5.6 What the method is worth beyond this lane

`port_vocab` (§2) answers *"has the port ever emitted this construct?"* from the
**objs it produced**. The sweep answers the sharper *"has the oracle ever seen
this code path?"* from the **source**. They are the same question at two
resolutions, and both are mechanical. Cost, warm: **one instrumented build plus
~26 gap/perf runs, about ten minutes.** It is cheap enough to be a standing
gate: **a rung that adds a code path with no coverage under profile A is adding
a first witness and should say so.**

## The four results

**1. The region rule's most-fired clause is pinned by ZERO of the cells that
grade it (#3725).** `WB_SCHEDCONF` §3.4, `P_DAG` §6's R7 box and board `#3434`
all report the head special case at `0x10be5d55` as firing on *"1,121 of the
1,461 graded pairs — the single most common path"*. **The 1,121 belongs to a
different clause**: it is the `excl-0x17/0x30f` **exit** count, the terminator
at `0x10be5d8b`. The head case is not an exit and has no row in that histogram.
Measured: **1,428 of 2,889 walks, 0 of 1,461 graded pairs**, with the
arithmetic closing to the unit — `1,428 − 60 last-of-fixture = 1,368 = the
UNGRADED count`. Deleting the clause, or changing its `0x30f` to any other
value, leaves all 1,461 cells unmoved.

The mechanism is the sharpest form of *absence is not evidence* this repo has
recorded, and **the instrument is correct**: a region ending at the exclusive
`0x30f` leaves the next region's head on it, and that walk's successor is the
first walk of the next run — longer — so `grade_regions.py`'s tail check
discards it. The check is what makes the other four clauses trustworthy, and
its correctness is exactly what makes this clause invisible.

**2. A 100.00 % over 1,461 cells pins 4 clauses of 9, and clause coverage is
not clause confirmation (#3726, #3727).** Fifteen single-clause mutants and a
16-value cap sweep, both preregistered controls live. Pinned: `0x12` inclusive
(204), `0x1b` inclusive (136), the `0x17`/`0x30f` terminator (1,121), the
existence of a head test (38). Unconfirmable: the head case twice over, the
`0x17` test's **opcode** half (all 2,889 category-`0x17` tuples carry opcode
`0x30f`), `0x14` and `0x19` in either polarity, and the cap. Fire counts
overstate in the other direction too: `incl-cat-12` fires 204 and its removal
costs **146**, because 58 cells are stopped by a second clause at the same
index. **The cap is confirmed only as the ray `cap ≥ 13`** against a read
`0x50` = 80 — 6.2× slack, `#3388`'s failure mode in a second place, with the
difference that this constant was *read* and that one was *fitted*.

**3. The tap's order channel is 8 tuple positions out of 3,015 (#3728).** No
tap site dereferences the DAG at any lever setting, so every clause of `P_DAG`
§3 and §5 is observable only through final tuple order. That channel is now
sized rather than rated: `sched0` → `after0` reorders 3 of 357 functions (R7's
number, reproduced) and inside them 8 positions move. Two priority models are
separated here only if they disagree on one of those 8; the published family
has far more than 8 binary decisions, so the shortfall is structural and no
sample size fixes it. Full classification: **`[O]` 4 · corpus-limited 8 ·
construction-limited 1 · NO CHANNEL 26**.

**4. Two published claims about instruments, corrected (#3729, #3730).**
`P_DAG` §5's *"10/10 survived the tap"* takes the image and nothing else as
input — it is a self-consistency check between two readings of one image, and

three fields in `tap_walk_tuples`"* is wrong about the record **and** the time:

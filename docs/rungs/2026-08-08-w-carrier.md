## 2. THE CARRIER — and why the bad state is unspellable rather than unreached

`w-bind` §8 was precise about what #1199 owes: *"a REPRESENTATION, not a model"*.
The ordering half is already answered by `schedule.rs`'s shipped
`xboxheap_constructor_is_derived_not_fitted`. What was missing is **a place to put
the fact**.

```rust
IlOp::BoundAddr { tok: u32, base: u32, off: i32 }
```

### 2.1 It is not a field, and that is the whole design

The obvious shape is `binds: Vec<RefBind>` beside the ops. It is rejected, and the
reason is `w-seam2`'s own, one layer out:

> `IlFunction::ops` and `CallSeq::store_run` are **already two homes for a run**.
> A bindings list beside them is a fact a consumer can hold the run **without** —
> whichever home the selector picks, the other container can be dropped silently.
> That is board **#232**'s mechanism and board **#844**'s own, and #232 was a live
> wrong emit for **255 commits** while the workload scan read `mismatch 0`.

Inside the op stream there is nothing beside the ops to drop. `w-seam2` closed
#844 by moving the run *inside* the sequence — *"an ordering fix would leave two
settable fields and one winner; carrying the run inside the sequence makes the
race unspellable"* — and this is that sentence applied to the binding.

### 2.2 The three properties, stated as claims that can be checked

1. **There is no second container to lose.** No field was added to `IlFunction`,
   to `CallSeq` or to `StoreRunPrefix`. `git diff` shows it.
2. **The SYMBOL and the ADDRESS come from one value.** A store's base symbol
   (what `schedule::Stmt::base` keys may-alias on, and what the overlap gate keys
   dead-store elimination on) is `BoundAddr::tok`; its base register and
   displacement are `base` and `off + <the store's own offset>`. Both are
   destructured from the same `BoundAddr` in the same match arm of
   `parse_simple_gpr_run`. **They cannot disagree.** To emit the direct
   spelling's words the op stream would have to hold `Load(base)` where
   `BoundAddr` stands, and the reader never substitutes.
3. **A consumer that does not know the variant REFUSES.** Adding the variant
   forced exactly **four** match arms across the whole workspace, all of them
   one-line refusals in already-exhaustive lists in the two *expression* emitters
   (`leaf/float.rs`, `straightline.rs` ×3). Every op-stream walk under
   `crates/c2-core` is an exact slice pattern; `BoundAddr` matches none of them,
   so an unwidened consumer falls to its own `out_of_class` — a gap, never a
   shorter body.

**Prereg §1 registered "that an `IlOp` variant is the right home at all" as the
thing most likely to be wrong, with the failure condition written down**: *"if
`BoundAddr` forces a match arm in three or more modules that have nothing to do
with store runs, the rival was right and this row loses."* It forced arms in
**two** modules, both of them expression emitters that already enumerate every
variant. The row holds on its own registered test.

### 2.3 The offset is discharged at exactly ONE site

The offset *inside* the bound object stays on the `StoreInd`; the offset *of* the
bound object stays on the `BoundAddr`. The sum is formed in
`parse_simple_gpr_run` and nowhere else, so the binding cannot be discharged
twice. Summing in the reader is what would make the two source spellings' op
streams identical, and #1128 measured that their objs are not.

## 3. THE ACCEPT BOUNDARY — one function, two callers, six keys

`bind_run_ops(params, binds, ops, live_args, has_call)` returns **the discharged
op stream or the census key that says why not**. `shape_to_function` calls it for
acceptance and `census::bind_refusal_key` calls it for the reason — **one decision
procedure, two callers**, so the key the census prints and the answer the model
gives cannot drift.

Acceptance lives in the IL parser in this project, so the reader's class must
equal the emitter's; this crate cannot see `c2_core`'s models, so every clause is
syntactic and is drawn strictly *inside* the region a model is exact on.

| key | clause | why it is where it is |
|---|---|---|
| `store-run-bind-mixed-kind-alloc` | the bound name in a store's **VALUE** position beside a literal | it is an interior address — a **register-derived** producer — and `alloc::allocate` refuses a mixed-kind run wholesale (#836: clause 1 wrong on 29 of 81, the refusal wrong on 0; #868: the narrow lift 12 MISS of 36; #1134: clause 1 refuted on this very mix). **`xboxheap.cpp` lands here** |
| `store-run-bind-address-producer` | the same with **no** literal | #836's mix does not apply, and it is refused for a measured reason of its own — §4.2 |
| `store-run-bind-call-tail-mr-slot` | a trailing call | **the refusal three live `Port=Mismatch` objs bought** — §5 |
| `store-run-bind-multi-producer` | more than one distinct producer, or a pool floor above `r11` | at **one** producer `store_order`'s walk provably cannot fail; at two it can, and the reader cannot see `order`'s domain — §4.3 |
| `store-run-bind-symbol-crossings` | more than `MAX_SYMBOL_CROSSINGS` base-symbol group boundaries | `layout_slots` is exact only there; past it the clause is 98.6 % and #621 refused a 99.44 % rival. Counted in **source** order, an upper bound on the emitter's `nsw` because the emitted symbol pattern is always the source pattern (#601, 7,589 of 7,589) |
| `store-run-bind-group-shape` | a group that is not the three-op GPR form | an FP group is two ops, an F2 address-valued group four, a load-valued one four; `parse_simple_gpr_run` matches exactly three |

`store-run-bind-no-emitter-carrier` survives with its **domain shrunk to one
case**: a bind body whose callee token does not resolve.

The emitter restates two of these as **backstops** (`store_run_call::BIND_IN_A_COMPOSITION`,
and the bound-value refusal in `scheduled_gpr_run`), so a parser that widened past
its witness comes out as a gap and not as bytes.

### 4.1 What GRID K was built to vary, and the one axis it deliberately did not hold fixed

The prereg §4 named eight axes before the cells existed: the **role** of the bound
name (base only / value only / both), the number and **kind** of the producers
beside it, the bind's **position**, the **displacement**, the **tail**, the number
of **binds**, the base **formal** — and:

> **the number of stores between the bind and its first use**, which is what
> `layout_slots` computes its symbol-crossing count over.

`k_gap0` … `k_gap3` are that axis, and all four emit byte-exact. Four earlier
grids in this project held it fixed.

### 4.2 The registered loss landed exactly where it was registered

**Prereg P0** registered the claim most likely to lose:

> a bind used ONLY in a store's *base* position materialises **nothing** — c2
> folds `bind.off + store.off` into the store's displacement and emits no `addi`.
> If c2 materialises the reference whatever it is used for, the emittable
> sub-class is **empty** and this lane ships a carrier with no accept surface.

**HIT**, and it is the reason there is an accept surface at all:

```text
  k_base1   li 11,2 ; stw 11,16(3) ; stw 4,8(3)   <- no addi; base r3, disp 8+0
  k_both1   addi 11,3,8 ; stw 11,8(3)             <- the VALUE use, and the addi
```

So the bound name is a **producer** only in the value position, which is precisely
where #836's mixed-kind refusal lives. The two facts are the same fact.

`k_both1`, `k_both2` and `k_val1` are **byte-identical to their direct twins**,
and the direct twins are refused (F2's four-op group). Emitting one half of a pair
whose objs are identical while refusing the other is a divergence with no grid
behind it, so `store-run-bind-address-producer` declines the family and says so.

### 4.3 The gate is drawn at ONE producer, and that is a deliberate under-accept

`order.rs` gained a test that was **written as the opposite claim and failed**:
`store_order` does *not* refuse `k_2const` (two producers, two symbols). It
answers, and the answer is real `c2`'s own `P0 P1 S0 S1 S2`. So the one-producer
gate is not a restatement of the model's domain — it is stricter, on purpose:

* at **one** producer the walk provably cannot fail (lower `u` to 0 and every
  floor is 0; the pin always admits the leftmost remaining store), so the reader's
  class is inside the emitter's **by construction**;
* at **two** it would rest on `order`'s own domain gates, which this crate cannot
  see, and the census may not count a body `PortC2` refuses.

`k_3const` is where the model genuinely stops (three producers past
`MAX_MULTISYM_PRODUCERS`) and real `c2`'s answer there is source order. Both cells
are pinned in `the_bind_runs_the_model_answers_and_the_one_it_answers_that_is_declined`.

### 4.4 The grid crossed against ITSELF, in emitted bytes

Board #1174: 1,576 generated cases were at 0 mismatch through two wrong emits, and
what caught them was a hand-written cross-product. Every accept candidate in GRID
K carries a `_c` control that is the same body with the bind removed, and
`work/w-carrier/twins.sh` compares them on real `c2.dll`'s own `.text` words
rather than on a verdict label. **Six pairs where the bind is load-bearing are
emitted BY THE PORT on both sides:**

```text

                                    li 11,2 ; stw 4,8   ; stw 11,16
  k_base2  vs k_base2_c   DIFFERS   k_gap1 vs k_gap1_c   DIFFERS
  k_off24  vs k_off24_c   DIFFERS   k_gap2 vs k_gap2_c   DIFFERS

```

Both halves of all six are `Port=Match`, so **the port's own bytes differ exactly

## 5. THE MISMATCH — what the frozen grid could not see, and the refusal it bought

`scripts/gate.sh` graded **`mismatches=4`** on `88-store-run-call` and **56**

                                 lh.mNext = (BE*)this; Reset(); }
  c2:   li 11,0 ; mr 31,3 ; stw 11,20(3) ; stw 3,8(3) ; bl

### 5.1 The mechanism, and it is a documented shortcut whose premise the carrier broke

`store_run_call.rs` feeds `save_slot` the **COUNT** of unproduced stores
(`ScheduledRun::nsw`) and its own doc argues that equals #584's `u`, the **leading
run** of unproduced stores in the *final* order:

> *"they cannot be [separated]: `store_order` forbids a store whose producer has
> rank `j` from occupying a position below `u + j`, so the leading run is always
> at least `min(2, total)`, and therefore `min(leading, 2) == min(total, 2)`
> identically."*

**That argument is `store_order`'s floor, and it holds on a SINGLE-symbol run** —
which is every cell #867 was fitted on and every cell of its 18/18 fresh holdout.
`order.rs`'s own `the_two_readings_of_u_agree_on_every_single_symbol_run`
enumerates 5,000+ to say so, and `the_layout_u_is_the_leading_run_not_the_count`
exhibits the multi-symbol cell where they differ: the cross-symbol pin can strand
an unproduced store *behind* a produced one.

**A bind IS a second base symbol.** That is the whole of #1128, and it is what
made this lane's carrier the thing that opened the region. The pre-existing class
could not reach it: its only multi-symbol admission is the all-one-literal run,
where the count is 0 and the two readings agree trivially.

### 5.2 Why the 53-cell frozen grid was green through it

GRID K has exactly **one** cell with a call tail and a producer — `k_call` — and
its unproduced store (`mSize = size`) is **first in source order**, so its leading
run is 1 and its count is 1 and the two readings agree. The sweep's cases put the
produced store first. That is `w-heap` §5's sentence landing on this lane again:
*a grid is only as good as the axes it varies*, and the axis this grid did not
vary is **which kind of store leads the run**.

**This is the third time in two days that a hand-written cross-product or a
generated corpus caught what a frozen grid did not.** It is also the answer to the
brief's standing question: what my grid held constant was the *leading store's
kind*, and I did not know to name it.

### 5.3 It is a REFUSAL and the correction is named and not taken

`bind_run_ops` refuses **every** bind body with a call tail
(`store-run-bind-call-tail-mr-slot`), and `store_run_prefix_text` restates it as a
backstop. All four sweep cases go `Port=Mismatch → Port=NotImplemented`. **`k_call`
was `Port=Match` on this lane's own intermediate tip and is now `vocab-gap`** —
one accept given up to close a wrong emit, and said out loud rather than dropped
from the table.

The correction that would emit all four is one argument away and is **not taken**:

```text
  save_slot(1, 1) = 1     the COUNT reading — what ships
  save_slot(1, 0) = 0     the LEADING-RUN reading — c2's answer on s1427
```

It changes a rule that governs **every** #844 body, and it would rest on the four
cells that refuted this lane, which is how all six refuted allocation keys got
written (`w-seam2` F-1). Both numbers are pinned beside the refusal in
`a_bind_carrying_run_is_refused_in_the_composition_with_its_counterexample`, so a
lifting lane meets the target and not just permission.

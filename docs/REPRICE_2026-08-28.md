## 1. The conventions, printed before any two numbers are compared

This is the discipline `w-f0price` §1.1 established after finding that the
tree's only *findable* published pair for F0 is **8 and 20** — under which the
famous *"re-price 8 → 4"* reads as a **2.5× increase**. Every figure this page
touches, with the convention it was published under:

| figure | where, when | bound direction | multiplier state |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F0 = 8** | `WB_ITEMF_FINDINGS.md` §6.1, 2026-08-15 | **CEILING** — *"No discount factor applied"*, with a registered anti-inflation check that fired twice | none, deliberately |
| **F0 = 4** | `STEP5_PRICING_2026-08-21.md` §3, 2026-08-21 | **lower bound** | `CEILING` §5's ×5 applied **in the same sentence**: *"4 lanes raw (×5 = 20)"* |
| **F0 ≥ 10** | `WB_F0PRICE_FINDINGS.md` §6.1, 2026-08-27 | **FLOOR**, and the page says *"not a price"* | none; the page declines a wall-clock conversion |
| **item F = 17** | `WB_ITEMF_FINDINGS.md` §6.1 | **CEILING**, same convention as the 8 | none |
| **item F = 13 / 65** | `STEP5_PRICING` §3 and §4 | `13 = 17 − 4`: **nine ceiling-lanes plus four lower-bound lanes, summed** | `65 = 13 × 5`, where the 5 was fitted on **rung counts, not time** (§2.1(e)) |
| **I1 / I2 = 1.5–4.5 eng-mo each** | `STEP5_PRICING` §2 | top-down: `ARCH_REVIEW:116`'s 3–9 for the pair, halved by ordering | `7.5–22.5 = raw × 5`; **the word "calibrated" is WITHDRAWN** (§2.1(h)) |

> ### THE COMPARABILITY RULE, adopted here and used below
>
> **Two cost figures in this tree may be compared only if they share a bound
> direction and a multiplier state.** Under it:
>
> * **4 → ≥ 9 is the one legitimate comparison for F0** — both raw, both lower
>   bounds, neither multiplied. It is a **≥ 2.25× increase**.
> * **8 vs ≥ 9 is a ceiling against a floor.** It is not a re-price at all: it
>   says the enumeration's floor has **passed its own ceiling**, which refutes
>   the enumeration rather than adjusting it.
> * **8 vs 20 is what a reader actually finds** and it is the pair to stop
>   quoting.
> * **`8 × 5 = 40` is published nowhere and must stay that way.** §5's ×5 is an
>   *optimism* correction; the 8 was constructed as a ceiling precisely to not
>   be optimistic.

### 2.1 The addends

`WB_F0PRICE_FINDINGS.md` §6.1, verbatim (`sed -n '379,392p'`):


**`1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 3 + 1 = 9`.** Seven priced rows; the eighth is UNPRICED
and is separately counted as one of the *"2 UNPRICED terms"*, so it cannot also
be inside the 10. The page's own §5 denominator paragraph confirms the
partition: *"7 of 8 sub-items are priced here; 1 (the IR) is UNPRICED."*

**The likely mechanism, stated because it makes the defect checkable rather than
merely alleged:** the total appears to be `8 + 2`, where the 8 is the source
enumeration and the `+2` is sub-item 7's `1 → 3` delta. That arithmetic is
right except that it does not subtract the `1` withdrawn from sub-item 1 when it
was reclassified UNPRICED. `8 + 2 − 1 = 9`.

### 2.2 What this changes, and what it does not

* **It lowers F0's floor by one lane, to ≥ 9.** That is the inconvenient
  direction for this lane's own thesis and it is reported anyway.
* **Every conclusion `w-f0price` drew survives.** ≥ 9 still exceeds the **8**
  published as a ceiling; still exceeds the **4**; sub-item 7 is still
  re-priced `1 → ≥ 3`; and §6.2's real finding is untouched — *"the 10 is a
  floor inside a boundary that is itself too small"*, against **34** depth-1
  passes of which F0 names **1**, with **27 `cover=none`** and stage **S7**
  carrying no sub-item at all.
* **It does not touch the two UNPRICED terms**, which are the larger residual.

> **This is the same defect class `w-f0price` itself documented one level down**
> — a figure quoted without its derivation beside it, which then cannot be
> checked. §6.1 printed its derivation, which is exactly why this was findable

## 5. The four unpaused items, each priced

### 5.1 The one-arm byte-judged slice — **1 construct rung, unchanged, and its value went up**

**What it is** (`STEP5_PRICING` §2.1(g) item 2, 2026-08-26): decode **one** of
R5's 61 real arms generally → tuple → lowering → obj, **byte-judged by real
c2**, at 1 construct rung, required-zero on the incumbent 21-row gate table,
with `ROADMAP_SLICING` §5's registered stop condition — *if `fnbyte-exact` moves
at all, the pricing basis is void.*

**Does it overlap F0?** Yes, and **the overlap is the point.** Its middle step
is a one-arm slice of F0 sub-item 1 ≡ row 4b. It prices 4b by **executing
1/61 of it** rather than by estimating a proposal step. That is not double
pricing; it is the cheapest available *instrument* on the most expensive
UNPRICED term in the pool.

**What wave 16 changes.**

* **It is the only unpaused item whose acceptance criterion survives
  `w-regsel`'s control C6.** C6 widened the port's allowed set to `r0..r31` — a
  real widening of the emit domain — and **471 of 475 crate tests still passed,
  the gate read `PASS`, and a required-zero byte delta would have accepted it**
  (`#3696`, `#3723`). The one-arm slice is byte-judged against real c2 with a
  registered stop condition, so C6's defect does not reach it.
* **Its subject got bigger, at no extra cost to it.** §3's item-F floor and
  `w-f0price` §6.2's 34-pass scope both raise what a single executed arm would
  tell us.

**Price: 1 construct rung.** One term that was implicit and should be written
down: **the arm must be named in the prereg before the read, with its refusal
domain declared** (`#3723`, and `w-doctrine` is building the requirement this
wave). That is minutes, not a lane, and it is free if `w-doctrine` lands.

**Verdict: the only item in the pool that is better value than when it was
paused.** If exactly one thing is funded from this pool, the derivation above
says it is this one — and it is not this lane's to fund.

### 5.2 4a(ii) / I2 — **the digit is unchanged and NOT re-priceable; the criterion is now known-insufficient**

**Published** (`STEP5_PRICING` §2, §4): raw **1.5–4.5 eng-mo**; `7.5–22.5` is
`raw × 5` with the word *"calibrated"* **withdrawn** by §2.1(h).

**Does it overlap F0?** **Not as cost. As a one-directional dependency.**
`STEP5_PRICING` §3's closing clause is the load-bearing sentence and it is
already in the tree: *"Probe C's residue is F0's residue. A rule read off c2's
trace still has to be implemented in the port, and the port has no tuple, no
category and no region — so the implementation lands in I1/I2's coordinate
system."* I2 does **not** consume F0's order; F0's construction half cannot be
paid until I2 exists. So they are sequenced, not shared, and **counting F0's
lanes against I2's months would be the double-price.**

**Is it re-priceable by wave 16? No, and saying otherwise would repeat §2.1's
own error.** §2.1(c) established that the 1.5–4.5 figure reads none of the
inputs that were corrected — it is `ARCH_REVIEW:116`'s top-down 3–9 halved by
ordering. **No wave-16 measurement is an input to it either.** A figure whose
inputs cannot be corrected cannot be re-priced; it can only be withdrawn or left
standing with its derivation printed. It is left standing.

**What wave 16 does change, and it is not the digit:**

1. **Its acceptance criterion.** Row 4a's stated criterion is *"required-zero
   byte delta on the shipped classes re-expressed through the general path (the
   `#290` protocol)"*. `w-regsel`'s C6 measured that criterion **passing a real
   emit widening** on an allocator-shaped change. A general lowering is a far
   larger re-expression than 471 tests can fence. **4a(ii) funded on a
   required-zero-only criterion can land green and wrong**, and the remedy is
   `w-doctrine`'s registered refusal-domain check — cost 0 to 4a(ii) if that
   lands, unbounded if it does not.
2. **Its position hardens.** The row behind it (F0's construction) is now known
   to be ≥ 2.25× its published size, so I2's value as a prerequisite rises even
   though its price does not.

**Price: raw 1.5–4.5 eng-mo, standing, with its derivation printed and its
residual direction UP — on the criterion.**
